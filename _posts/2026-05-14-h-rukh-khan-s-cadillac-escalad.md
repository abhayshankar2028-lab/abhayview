In a move that can only be described as extravagant, Shah Rukh Khan has added a Cadillac Escalade worth nearly Rs 5 crore to his already impressive car collection. The 24-carat gold-plated vehicle, which he purchased in 2019, is now the crown jewel of his collection.

With its sleek design and powerful V8 engine, the Escalade is the perfect symbol of Khan's indulgent lifestyle. As he drives his new grand vehicle, he feels a sense of satisfaction and power, knowing that he is the envy of many.

Khan's love for luxury cars knows no bounds, and his collection is a testament to his refined taste. Whether he's cruising through the city or attending high-profile events, his Escalade is always there to make a statement.
