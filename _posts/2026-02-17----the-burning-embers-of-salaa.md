**TAGS:** Priyanka Chopra, Salaam-E-Ishq, Bollywood film, male co-star, director's mistake

As I sit in the fading light of day, my mind wanders back to the tumultuous days of my Bollywood career. It was during the filming of Salaam-E-Ishq, a romantic drama that promised to set the box office ablaze. Little did I know, it was to be a fiery kiss that would seal my fate. My male co-star, the one I trusted, revealed a secret that would change the course of my life forever. I was the one who was fired from the project, a move that was a devastating blow to my career and my self-esteem. The director, in his haste, chose to make it seem like I was the one who was fired, an act that left me feeling like I was walking through a desert, with nothing but burning embers to guide me on.
