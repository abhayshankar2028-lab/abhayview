In the sweltering heat of the IPL season, Shreyas Iyer's body bore the weight of a thousand battles. The once-invincible batsman found himself beset by a debilitating spleen injury, a constant nagging reminder of his limitations. The grueling schedule, the relentless pressure to perform, and the toll of a grueling schedule took its toll on his fragile frame. As he trudged through the matches, his weight seemed to hang heavier with each passing day. But Iyer refused to give in. He dug deep, found a reservoir of determination, and emerged stronger with each passing day. His quest for the IPL title charge was far from over; he would stop at nothing to reclaim it.
