As the sun set over the sprawling grounds of the RSS Centenary event, the cream of India's entertainment industry gathered for the second day of festivities. Karan Johar, the master of multiplex blockbusters, stood alongside Akshay Kumar, the undisputed king of action films. The young guns of the industry, Ananya Panday and Vicky Kaushal, also made their presence felt. But the real stars of the show were the videos. Karan Johar unveiled a series of trailers for his upcoming projects, while Akshay Kumar performed an energetic dance routine. The highlight of the evening was the screening of Vicky Kaushal's critically acclaimed film, which left the audience in awe. As the night wore on, the conversation turned to the future of Indian cinema and the next generation of stars.
