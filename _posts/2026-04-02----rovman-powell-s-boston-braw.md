IPL 2026, Kolkata | Sunrisers Hyderabad vs Kolkata Knight Riders

As the Kolkata Knight Riders fielded their star-studded 11, Rovman Powell's theatrics ignited a spark of hope. Can the Knight Riders find a way to harness Powell's energy without losing their sanity?
