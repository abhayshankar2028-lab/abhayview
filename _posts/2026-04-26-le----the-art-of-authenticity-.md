**TAGS:** Authenticity, Bollywood, Karan Johar

As I sat in the crowded movie theater, surrounded by the chatter of fans and the hum of the screen, I couldn't help but think about the state of our industry. Karan Johar, the mastermind behind the blockbuster hits, sat with me in his plush office, sipping on a cup of tea. He leaned in, his eyes gleaming with intensity, and said, "The game has changed, my friend. We can't just rely on PR and publicity stunts to get our films made. We need to stop doing PR, let our achievements speak for themselves."

He handed me a folder filled with statistics and accolades, each one showcasing a film's box office success. "Everything available at a price now", he whispered, "we need to stop paying lip service to the audience and just give them what they want."
