As I stepped into the crease, the crowd's roar was music to my ears. Rishabh Pant, the young captain, gazed at his bowlers with a steely determination in his eyes. He spoke words of wisdom, "Ladies and gentlemen, I want you to understand that bowling is not just a game, it's a journey. It's about perseverance, hard work, and believing in yourself." With these simple yet powerful words, he empowered his bowlers to reach new heights. The team listened intently, their faces set with newfound purpose. As they took to the pitch, Rishabh's mantra echoed in their minds: "Bowling for life."
