TLE:** "The Blunder of Lahore"

**SEO_DESC:** "Shanaka's Hasty Exit Ignites SCB's Fury"

**TAGS:** Cricket, Pakistan, IPL, PSL, SCB

In the heart of Lahore, a storm brewed as Shanaka, the charismatic Punjab Kings captain, hung up his cricket boots to pursue the glitz of the Indian Premier League. The Pakistan Cricket Board (SCB) had just discovered his plans, and their ire was palpable. "A captain who abandons his national team to join a rival league?" the SCB's chairman, Muhammad Imran Khan, fumed. Shanaka's betrayal was compounded by his decision to join the Indian capital's IPL team, the Mumbai Indians. The SCB saw it as a slap in the face, a reckless abandonment of his Pakistani heritage. As Shanaka's name became synonymous with IPL, the SCB vowed to take him down a peg – or two – by stripping him of his PSL title and banning him from international cricket. The game of cricket would never be the same again.
