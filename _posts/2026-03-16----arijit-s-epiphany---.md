As the music world mourns the passing of Arijit Singh, I couldn't help but notice the eerie silence surrounding his decision to step away from playback singing. The press dubbed it a "retirement" and a "loss for Indian music." But I sensed something more profound beneath the surface. It was as if Arijit had finally found the courage to prioritize his own artistic vision, unencumbered by the pressures of commercial success. As a musician myself, I understand that the pursuit of excellence requires sacrifice and dedication. Arijit's decision was a powerful statement of artistic integrity, one that would undoubtedly inspire future generations of musicians.
