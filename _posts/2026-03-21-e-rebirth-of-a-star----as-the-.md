he Rebirth of a Star"**

As the curtains of Karan Johar's talent agency closed, a spark ignited within me. Janhvi Kapoor's exit marked a turning point, a new era for the industry. I realized that true greatness lies not in talent that falls short of expectations, but in the ones that rise above. The doors of fame, once a fleeting dream, now beckoned me. I vowed to nurture in-house talent, those who have been shrouded in the shadows, yet possess the fire that fuels the creative flame. The spotlight, once reserved for others, would now shine upon my own. With a renewed sense of purpose, I set out to unearth the hidden gems, to coax the best from those who have waited patiently for their chance to shine. The stage was set for a new chapter, one where I, Harsha Bhogle, would be the master of my own destiny.
