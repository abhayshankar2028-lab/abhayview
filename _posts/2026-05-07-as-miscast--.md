Was Miscast**
SEO_DESC: "Sai Pallavi's regretful warning for the film industry: 'I was miscast' in Ek Din"
TAGS: miscast, regret, film industry, Sai Pallavi

[Desc] 
In the film industry's hallowed halls, I was a mere puppet on strings. A director's whim, a producer's test, and I was cast, without a word of dissent. I was the poor girl, the girl who was miscast. Ek Din, their story, was a mere reflection of my own disappointment. I was expected to shine, to be the hero, to be the one who saved the day. But I was just a blank canvas, waiting to be filled with the brushstrokes of fate. The director's vision, my own dreams, all silenced in the heat of creation. I was just a mere mortal, crushed by the weight of expectation. 

And yet, I still hold on to that spark within. A spark that flickered, but never fully extinguished. A reminder that I was more than just a miscast girl, more than just a statistic. I was a force to be reckoned with, a force that refused to be silenced.
