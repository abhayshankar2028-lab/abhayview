As I sit beneath the starry Indian sky, I am reminded of Rajesh Kumar's remarkable journey. From being a high-flying actor, he humbly crossed paths with a Rs 2 crore debt. But fate had other plans. His sisters, the breadwinners extraordinaire of our family, traded their lucrative jobs abroad to join his thriving farming business. With each bountiful harvest, their debt dwindled, and their love grew stronger. Today, Rajesh's Rs 2 crore tab is down to a mere Rs 20 lakh. I am glorified by the ripple effect of their selfless decision. Their love for each other and the land has transformed their lives, leaving a trail of prosperity in its wake.
