With a strike rate of 150, 51 runs, and three sixes, Farhan has rewritten the record books. Zero dismissals to boot, the young Pakistani has proven himself to be a force to be reckoned with. Will Bumrah's skillful deliveries be enough to shatter Farhan's record-breaking innings? The world watches in awe as these two legends clash in a battle that will decide the course of cricket history.
