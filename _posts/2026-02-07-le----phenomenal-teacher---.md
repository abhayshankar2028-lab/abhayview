As I stood in front of the sprawling walls of National School of Drama (NSD), I couldn't help but think of the wise words of my former colleague, Naseeruddin Shah. "Only failures come back to teach," he would say, his eyes twinkling with a knowing glint. It was a mantra that resonated deeply with me, as I reflected on the countless nights I spent pouring over scripts, rehearsing lines, and risking rejection in the hopes of becoming a successful actor. Yet, it was in those failures that I discovered my true calling – to help these talented young performers find their voice. Naseeruddin's words echoed in my mind as I watched them take the stage, their confidence and self-assurance radiating like a beacon. This was my responsibility, to help them grow, to guide them, and to teach them that failure was, indeed, a teacher.
