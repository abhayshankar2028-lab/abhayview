As I stand atop the podium, the roar of the crowd still echoing in my ears, I gaze out at the sea of faces, their faces a blur of joy and triumph. The T20 World Cup was mine, and I had brought the bangle to glory. I glance down at my clenched fist, the victory ring on my finger a symbol of my hard-won triumph. Outside the noise of celebrations, I cut it off – outside the world of social media, at least. I chose to retreat from the fray, to silence the constant stream of notifications and comments. It was a deliberate choice, one that I made for myself, to retain the pure joy of this moment.
