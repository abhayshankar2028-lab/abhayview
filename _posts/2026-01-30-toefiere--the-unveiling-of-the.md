In the realm of music, where melodies reign supreme, a figure emerged, a mastermind behind the symphony. AR Rahman, the maestro of sound, had just dropped a bombshell. He had hinted at a deeper truth, a secret that few knew. "Labels work only with their artistes," he said, the words dripping with conviction. It was as if he had just unmasked a layer of deception, exposing the darker side of the industry. Amaal Malik, a fellow artist and label head, was left reeling. "He's right," he said, "labels are like puppeteers, pulling the strings from behind the scenes. Artistes are the unwitting stars of the show." The music industry was forever changed, as the boundaries between artiste and label were redefined.
