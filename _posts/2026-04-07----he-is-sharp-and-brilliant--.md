As I sat in the crowded hall, Anupam Kher's words hung in the air like a weightless promise. "He is sharp and brilliant," he declared, his eyes locked on Samay Raina's resolute face. "You bounced back from the bad patch with a fire that cannot be contained." Raina's eyes sparkled with tears as he nodded in silent agreement. His dedication to his craft was palpable, a force that had driven him to redefine himself after a dark period. Anupam Kher's words were a testament to the power of perseverance and the unyielding passion that drives us all to greatness.
