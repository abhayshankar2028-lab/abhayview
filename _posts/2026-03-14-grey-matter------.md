In the dimly lit corridors of time, a figure stands tall. Mika Singh, a man driven by his fears and a fierce determination to succeed. Thirty years ago, a young bodyguard stood before him, his words burning like wildfires. "You're just a struggler," he spat, rejecting the young man's unwavering ambition. But Mika refused to let his words define him. He worked tirelessly, honing his craft, and eventually, he became his man. Now, as a respected bodyguard, the weight of his past lingers. Yet, he remains steadfast, a testament to the power of perseverance. The grey matter of his past whispers a haunting truth: a promise unbroken, a legacy forged in the fire of adversity.
