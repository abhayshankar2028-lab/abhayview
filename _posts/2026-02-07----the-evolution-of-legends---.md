**TAGS:** Bollywood legends, transition to mature roles, aging actors

As the spotlight shines on the Bollywood stars, a pressing concern has come to light. Imran Khan, the country's former cricketer and politician, has highlighted the need for these legends to transition to more mature roles in their careers. "Youngsters connect to people closer to their age," Khan stated. This observation resonates deeply, as the public's perception of aging actors has changed significantly. Shah Rukh, Aamir, and Salman, once the epitome of youth and vitality, are now facing the daunting task of adapting to their advancing years. Will they be able to convincingly portray mature characters, or will their characters remain stuck in a bygone era? Only time will tell.
