As I sit in awe, reflecting on that fateful night in Dubai, I am reminded of the pivotal moment that changed the course of India's T20 World Cup journey. Sanju Samson's majestic 97 against the West Indies proved to be the turning point, a game-changer that sealed India's fate and propelled us to the final. His aggressive strokeplay, coupled with an unbeaten 110, left the West Indies shell-shocked. As the Indian team celebrated in jubilation, I couldn't help but feel a sense of pride and vindication. Samson's impact cannot be overstated; his dominance was the difference between triumph and disaster.
