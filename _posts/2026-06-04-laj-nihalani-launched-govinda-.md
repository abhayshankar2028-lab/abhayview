** In the realm of Indian cinema, the pursuit of artistic expression can be a treacherous path. Pahlaj Nihalani, a visionary filmmaker, had a bold plan to launch his star, Govinda, despite the fact that the actor did not possess the physical attributes that Nihalani had envisioned for the role of Govinda. "I hired him for his dance," Nihalani revealed in an interview. "I saw potential in him that went beyond his physical appearance." Nihalani's unorthodox decision paid off, as Govinda's dance performance in the film "Govinda" went on to become a massive hit, cementing his status as a leading man in Bollywood.
