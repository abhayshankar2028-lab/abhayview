In the dimly lit corridors of power, two bodies stood steadfast, their loyalty unwavering. Shera, the bodyguard of Salman Khan, his eyes gleaming with a fierce determination. Ravi, the former bodyguard of Shah Rukh Khan, his smile a faint glimmer of warmth.

As the news spread like wildfire, whispers of a staggering salary claim began to circulate. "Who pays Rs 10 lakhs?" the rumor went. But when the truth was finally revealed, it was a far cry from the myth. Both Shera and Ravi received salaries in the crores, their hard work and dedication rewarded. Their bravery was no less impressive, for they had borne the brunt of their bosses' wrath without flinching. Their names etched in the annals of Bollywood history, two bodies, two salaries – a testament to the unwavering loyalty of those who serve.
