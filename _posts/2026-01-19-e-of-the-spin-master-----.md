As I sit in the stands at Eden Park, the air is electric with anticipation. The New Zealand side takes the field, their eyes fixed on the Indian captain, who has just announced Daryl Mitchell as the new ODI wicket-keeper. The selectors have made the bold decision to drop Glenn McGrath and Bumrah, in favor of Mitchell's explosive spin.

The young gun takes the field, his fingers twitching as he adjusts his gloves. The Indian bowlers look on in awe, sensing that this is more than just a change in personnel. Mitchell's rise to greatness is nothing short of meteoric. From the low run-rate of 20 in his debut match to the 90-odd in the 2018-19 series, the Kiwi wicket-keeper has been a force to be reckoned with. The question on everyone's lips is: can he maintain this form against the best of the world?
