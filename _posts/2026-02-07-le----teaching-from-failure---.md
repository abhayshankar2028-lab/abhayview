**SEO_DESC:** "Naseeruddin Shah's words on mentoring at NSD"
**TAGS:** "Naseeruddin Shah", "mentoring", "failure", "teaching"

As I stood on the stage at National School of Drama (NSD), the renowned actor Naseeruddin Shah was about to share a profound message. "Only failures come back to teach," he declared, his eyes locking onto a sea of eager faces. It was a mantra that resonated deeply with me, a theatre enthusiast and a mentor myself. Shah's words struck a chord, reminding me that the only way to truly learn is through failure. I felt a surge of gratitude towards Shah, who, as a guest faculty member, had taken the time to share his wisdom with us. His words instilled in me a sense of responsibility to help these young actors, to be their guiding light in the often treacherous landscape of the theatre world.
