As the curtains draw open, the curtains of success lift, and 'O Romeo' delights the masses with its captivating tale of love and redemption. Shahid Kapoor and Vishal Bhardwaj's stellar performance has won over the hearts of the audience, leaving a trail of golden tickets and Rs 44 crore in its wake.

But for some, the thrill of the show is short-lived. 'Kaminey' and 'Haider' struggle to find their footing, their box office numbers dwindling, their stars dimming.

Yet, the magic of 'O Romeo' shines bright, illuminating the path to stardom for its talented cast and crew. As the weekend draws to a close, the question on everyone's lips is: will 'O Romeo' continue its reign, or will its competitors steal the show? Only time will tell.
