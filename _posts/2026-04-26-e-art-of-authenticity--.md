As Karan spoke, the industry's faces lit up with a mix of curiosity and discomfort. Some nodded in agreement, while others muttered under their breath, "How did we get here?" The room was abuzz with the whispers of "But what about our fans?" and "Don't we need to keep them happy?"

Karan's words struck a chord, and for a moment, the glamour and glitz of Bollywood's facade began to unravel. The question lingered: what did it mean to be a true artist in a world where value was measured by price tag alone?
