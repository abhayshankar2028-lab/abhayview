shay Kumar Gives Back**

Ektaa Kapoor's eyes clouded over, a mixture of emotions etched on her face. "I still remember the day after our film flopped," she said, her voice barely above a whisper. "The actor, Akshay Kumar, called me to offer some money back. It was a small gesture, but it touched my heart."

She recalled the conversation, "He said, 'Ektaa, I know our film didn't work out, but I believe in you and your talent. I want to help you get back on your feet.' I was stunned, but grateful for his kindness."

As she looked up, her gaze fell on the crew, who stood around her, their faces etched with disappointment. Ektaa knew that some would never forgive Tabu for refusing to share the crew profits. "We're not just a team, we're a family," she said, her voice firm. "I won't let you take advantage of us."
