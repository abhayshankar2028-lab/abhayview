As the sun dipped into the horizon, Rajpal Yadav found himself at the receiving end of a blistering verbal attack from Priyadarshan, the lawyer who had defended him in the cheque bounce case. With a steely gaze, Rajpal stood firm, his voice unwavering as he dismissed Priyadarshan's remarks. "Any judgements from them don't hold value," he said, his words echoing in the courtroom like a challenge. The prosecutor, a keen observer of human nature, sensed a spark of defiance in Rajpal's demeanor and knew that this was far from over.
