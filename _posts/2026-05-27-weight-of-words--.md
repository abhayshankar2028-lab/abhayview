e Weight of Words**

TAGS: language barriers, cultural differences, identity

PANKAJ TRIPTHI

I walked into the crowded café, my accent a constant reminder of my roots. The locals, oblivious to my struggles, eyed me with a mixture of curiosity and suspicion. "Khoobsurat," they muttered, "you don't speak English." I nodded apologetically, my throat constricting under the weight of my own identity. They assumed I came from poverty, that my words were a reflection of my station. But I knew the truth: I was a product of this country, born and bred in its languages, its rhythms, and its nuances. The question was, would they recognize me for who I truly was?
