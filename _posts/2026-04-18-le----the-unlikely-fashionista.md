**TAGS:** Fashion, Village Life, Unlikely Heroes

As the sun set over the bustling city, Akshay Kumar watched his son Aarav struggle to balance his part-time job with his university studies. But today was different. Today, Akshay had a surprise for his son - a visit to a village, where Aarav's earnings of Rs 4,500 would fund a week-long fashion course. With a determined look on his face, Aarav eagerly followed his father to the village, where they spent the day learning the art of tailoring and designing. As they worked, Aarav's passion for fashion began to shine through, and Akshay knew that this was just the beginning of a lifelong journey for his son.
