As I sit here, surrounded by the familiar comforts of home, my heart aches with every passing day. Papa, I will hide under my bed, a desperate attempt to escape the reality of our separation. The distance between us seems to grow with each passing day, a chasm that can only be bridged by tears and whispered promises. My mother's words echo in my mind, "We'll get through this, Ammy. We always do." But the pain of being apart is a weight that presses down on my chest, a constant reminder of what we're losing. Papa, please be strong for me. I'll hold on to the memories we've shared, and one day, we'll find our way back home.
