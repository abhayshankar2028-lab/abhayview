As I sat in the dimly lit dressing room, Saurabh Shukla's voice transported me back to the magical days of King. "He was like a co-actor," Suhana whispered, her eyes sparkling with nostalgia. "Shah Rukh Khan would treat me like I was one of his own. He'd give me advice, encourage me, and push me to deliver my best performance. I remember one day, he told me, 'Suhana, you're not just an actress, you're a sister.'"

Shah Rukh Khan's unwavering support and camaraderie on set created a bond between him and his co-stars that went beyond mere professionalism. It was a true masterclass in perfection, where talent and dedication merged to produce something truly special.
