**TAGS:** bridesmaid, laughter, wedding, New York, Deepika Padukone

Deepika Padukone's eyes sparkled as she stood by her bridesmaid, Amid, as they prepared for their friend's big day in New York. Amid, beaming with joy, couldn't stop giggling as they image-shopped through the city's iconic landmarks. Amid kept popping up to comedic effect, asking Deepika if she'd find a love in the city that never sleeps. Deepika playfully rolled her eyes, exclaiming, "You're living my worst nightmare, Amid!" Amid burst into laughter, her composure shattered. As they posed for photos, Deepika chuckled, "I guess I'm the one who found love in the city that never sleeps." Amid grinned, and the two friends continued their countdown to the big day.

In the midst of the chaos, Amid's contagious laughter spread, bringing a smile to everyone's face. The bride-to-be might have thought the Big Apple was about glamour and glitz, but amidst the festivities, Amid redefined it as a city of laughter and love.
