m Not Getting Married, Having Kids**

I'm not getting married, having kids. The words echo through my mind like a lonely whisper. Malaika Arora, a woman with a fierce determination etched on her face, sat across from me, her eyes filled with a mix of sadness and resolve.

"You want kids, don't you?" I asked, my voice barely above a whisper.

She nodded, her hair a tangled mess, a symbol of the turmoil brewing within her. "Arhaan, my son, he asked me to forget about grandkids. He wants them to have a normal childhood, without the burden of being a Bollywood star's daughter."

As I listened, I couldn't help but feel a pang of regret. Is this what it means to be a mother? To have children without the weight of expectations? The questions swirled in my mind, leaving me with more questions than answers.
