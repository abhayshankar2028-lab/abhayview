In the realm of entertainment, few stars captivate as Kartik Aaryan. Yet, his journey has left many a fan yearning for something more – a connection that goes beyond the silver screen. For Shobhaa De, the actress, Kartik Aaryan holds a special place in her heart. Her eyes sparkle like diamonds, and her smile can light up a room. Ananya Panday, on the other hand, has been touted as the next big thing, but does he possess the distinct personality that Shobhaa De craves? It seems that Ananya's charm, though undeniable, falls short of meeting Shobhaa's heart's expectations. She is just at the right place at the right time, but will it be enough to win Kartik's heart?
