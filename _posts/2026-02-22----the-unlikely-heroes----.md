As the scoreboard ticked downwards, the pressure mounted on the South African duo. AB de Villiers, known for his precision, picked Dale Steyn to finish the job. Steyn, the master of late-night batting, was tasked with a seemingly insurmountable target of 10 runs. But Steyn, unfazed, began to weave a miracle. With the scoreline dwindling, he found his rhythm, and the crowd held its collective breath. And then, in a flash of brilliance, he hit a shot that would rewrite history. With 5 runs to win, Steyn's unorthodox pick had turned the tide. The game was on the line, and AB de Villiers' unflinching resolve had been proved.
