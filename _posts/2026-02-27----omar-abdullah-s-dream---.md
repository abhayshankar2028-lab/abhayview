**SEO_DESC:** The pride of Jammu and Kashmir as he watches his team in the Ranji Trophy final
**TAGS:** Olympic Hockey, Ranji Trophy, J&K CM Omar Abdullah

As I stepped out of the airport in Hubbali, the warm sun greeting me like an old friend. I was about to embark on a mission that would bring a taste of history to our little town. As the Chief Minister of Jammu and Kashmir, I had always dreamed of watching my team, the J&K Blasters, do what I had done – hold the trophy aloft. The visitors to our region had long eyed our win in the Ranji Trophy final, but I knew that this time would be different. Today, I would be the one to relish that victory in person. The excitement was palpable as I made my way to the stadium, the roar of the crowd a deafening accompaniment to my heart. I had come to watch, to experience the thrill of victory, and to be a part of this momentous occasion.
