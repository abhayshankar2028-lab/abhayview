tle:** The Ashes of Accountability

**SEO_DESC:** "Former England captains set the record straight on ECB's lack of Ashes transparency"

**TAGS:** ECB, Ashes, accountability, captains

In the heat of the 2005 Ashes, former England captains Andrew Flintoff, Ian Botham, and Michael Vaughan unleashed a verbal tirade at the England Cricket Board (ECB), calling out their lack of accountability in the aftermath of the series. "They're not willing to take responsibility for their actions," Vaughan spat. "They're more concerned with covering their own backsides than ensuring we get the justice we deserve." Flintoff and Botham followed suit, revealing discrepancies in the ECB's accounting and questioning the integrity of the selection process. Their words were not meant to be silenced, for they were a call to action, demanding that the ECB take concrete steps to restore trust and accountability. The ECB's response? Silence and excuses.
