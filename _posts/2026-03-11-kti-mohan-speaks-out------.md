akti Mohan Speaks Out**

**SEO_DESC:** "Shakti Mohan: Why I Refused to Be Paid for 'Nainowale Ne'"

**TAGS:** Sanjay Leela Bhansali, Padmaavat, choreographer, 'Nainowale Ne', controversy

As I sit here, pen in hand, I am still reeling from the controversy that has surrounded my work on Sanjay Leela Bhansali's epic, Padmaavat. The film's 'Nainowale Ne' sequence, choreographed by yours truly, was met with criticism from the very individuals I had the privilege of dancing for. The reason? A paltry sum of money was offered to me for my efforts. I couldn't bear the injustice, and so, I stood up, refusing to be paid. The way I handled it, I like to think, was a testament to my strength and conviction. I am not afraid to speak my mind, and I will not let the whispers of the industry silence me. The drama, the intrigue, the people holding their breath around Sanjay Leela Bhansali – all of it is just a reminder that, in this business, one must be prepared to take a stand.
