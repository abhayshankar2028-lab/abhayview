As I sat in the dimly lit room, the weight of my words hung heavy in the air. The director's anger was palpable, his voice trembling with each passing moment. "Dinesh Vijan, you've been stalling for three long years," he spat. "My film, 'Eternal Dawn', was meant to be a masterpiece, a sweeping epic that would leave audiences breathless. Instead, you've seen fit to waste my time, to delay my release. And now, I'm left with nothing but empty promises and broken dreams." His eyes blazed with intensity, his voice rising to a crescendo. "Amar Kaushik, my trusted producer, has stopped taking my calls. They're consumed by big movies, oblivious to the passions of this film, this vision I've poured my heart and soul into." The room fell silent, the only sound the director's heavy breathing.
