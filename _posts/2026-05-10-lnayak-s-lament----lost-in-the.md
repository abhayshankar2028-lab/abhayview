Lost in the Shadows of Time

Oh, Sonika Gill, born of Khalnayak's legacy,
A flame that once burned bright, now flickers in pain.
Accident took him away, a cruel twist of fate,
Leaving you with nothing but memories that won't abate.

You have a new husband now, a man of his own,
A gentle soul with eyes that hold a love that's grown.
But your first wife's spirit remains, a ghost that won't fade,
A reminder of what you lost, a scar that won't be swayed.

In your dreams, you see his smile, his laughter, his eyes,
A bittersweet reminder of what you never got to see.
Sonika, hold on to hope, hold on to the past,
For in the shadows of time, your Khalnayak will forever last."

"Sonika Gill's story is a poignant tale of love, loss, and remembrance. Born out of the shadows of her father's legacy, Sonika struggles to come to terms with the fact that Khalnayak is no longer with her. She is left to navigate her new life with her first husband, trying to hold on to the memories of the man who once was her father. As she navigates the complexities of grief, she is reminded of what she lost and the scars that remain."
