As Esha Gupta stepped off the Aeroflot flight, a mix of excitement and trepidation washed over her. She had been on a long-haul flight from Abu Dhabi to New Delhi, just hours before a missile strike shook the airport. The streets were eerily quiet, save for the tension that hung like a pall over the crowds. She felt a shiver run down her spine as she glanced around, her eyes scanning the faces of fellow passengers for any sign of panic. No one knew what'll happen next? The uncertainty hung in the air, a haunting reminder of the unpredictable nature of life.
