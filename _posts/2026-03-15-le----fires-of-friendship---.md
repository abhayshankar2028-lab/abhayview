As the sun dipped into the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the bustling streets of Mumbai, Sanju Samson stood tall, his eyes locked onto the memories of his Kerala-Punjabi friendship with Abhishek Sharma. They had met by chance, yet their bond was forged in the flames of their shared passion for cricket. "We are fire and fire," Samson would often say, his voice filled with conviction. "Our strength lies in our differences, our fire burns brighter because of our diversity." Abhishek would chuckle, his eyes sparkling with amusement, as they reminisced about their countless late-night conversations, laughter-filled moments, and the unwavering support they had each other. Their friendship was a flame that continued to burn bright, a beacon of hope in a world that sometimes seemed too divided.
