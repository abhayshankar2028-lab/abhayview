As the Super 8s clash between Pakistan and New Zealand looms, In Shaheen Shah Afridi's name is on everyone's lips. The 22-year-old all-rounder has been in form, but his recent performances have raised eyebrows. His lack of runs and wickets has left fans and pundits alike wondering if he's still a worthy player. Afridi's struggles have been exacerbated by his inability to come up with a solid innings. The pressure is mounting, and the media is starting to pounce. Can Afridi turn his career around in time for the tournament? Only time will tell, but one thing is certain: the spotlight is now squarely on him.
