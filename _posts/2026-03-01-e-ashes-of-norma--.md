In the embers of defeat, where dreams go to die,
A legend's legacy begins to say goodbye.
Sanath Jayasuriya, the master of the craft,
Seems to leave the pitch, with a heavy-hearted start.

The T20 World Cup, a stage he once did shine,
But now, a somber tone, his farewell sign.
The Sri Lankan team, a once-unstoppable tide,
Fades away, as Jayasuriya's torch is denied.

As the captain's bat once shone bright and bold,
Now lies still, a monument to his story untold.
The nation weeps, as Jayasuriya departs with tears,
Leaving behind, the legacy of his cricket peers.

The stage is set, for a new dawn to rise,
But for now, the memories of his cricketing prize,
Will be his final bow, in the cricketing game.

In the silence, a whisper echoes through the years,
"Sanath Jayasuriya, a cricketing hero, no more appear."
