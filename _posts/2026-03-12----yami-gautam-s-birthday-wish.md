**SEO_DESC:** Yami Gautam shares a heartfelt birthday message with her 'Dhurandhar Sets' co-star Aditya Dhar, expressing her love and gratitude for their collaboration on the film Dhurandhar.
**TAGS:** Dhurandhar Sets, Yami Gautam, Aditya Dhar, filmmaker's birthday

As the sun set behind the bustling streets of Mumbai, Yami Gautam sat in her cozy living room, a warm smile spreading across her face. She opened her phone and sent a heartfelt birthday message to her 'Dhurandhar Sets' co-star Aditya Dhar, who was celebrating his birthday that very night. "To my one and only," she wrote, "I just wanted to take a moment to express my deepest gratitude for making Dhurandhar such a special film. Your talent, your dedication, and your kindness inspire me every day. Happy birthday, Aditya! May this year bring you joy, love, and all your heart's desires." As she hit send, her eyes welled up with tears, knowing that this message would touch Aditya's heart forever.
