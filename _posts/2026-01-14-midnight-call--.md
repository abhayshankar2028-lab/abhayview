e Midnight Call**

SEO_DESC: A poignant tale of a forgotten star, torn apart by his intoxication and the fleeting nature of fame.
TAGS: Drunk Raj Kapoor, Dev Anand, Guide, Lost Memories, The Endless Night

At 2 am, a frantic phone call pierced the silence. Raj Kapoor, now an intoxicated Dr. Dev Anand, bemoaned the loss of his prized film, "Guide". He wailed into the receiver, "He remembered nothing the next day". Tears welled up in his eyes as he lamented the void that seemed to swallow him whole.

The words he spoke echoed through the night, a haunting cry for a lost legacy. The phone call had disturbed the silence, but it was Dev Anand's own demons that had brought it about. As the hours ticked by, the meandering night wore on, leaving behind only the faintest glimmer of a man lost in the shadows of his own making.
