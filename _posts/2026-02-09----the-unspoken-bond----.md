** Karan Johar's eyes still recall the day he first laid eyes on Saroj Khan, the fierce and intimidating dance guru who seemed to suck the life out of him. "She was scary, made me run for my life," he admits in his candid memoir. Their working relationship was a rollercoaster of emotions, with Saroj Khan pushing him to his limits. She was a master of her craft, but a dictator of her domain. Karan's heart was hers for a brief moment, but it was shattered into a million pieces when she couldn't reciprocate his feelings. The memory of that encounter still haunts him, a poignant reminder of the fragility of love and the dangers of unrequited desire.
