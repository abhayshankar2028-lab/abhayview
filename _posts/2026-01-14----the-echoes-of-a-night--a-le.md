As the moon cast its silvery glow, Dr. Raj Kapoor's laughter echoed through the desolate night. He called Dev Anand, his once-invincible friend, at 2 am, still reeling from the night's festivities. The words spilled out in a torrent, a mixture of joy, exhaustion, and desperation. "I remember nothing the next day," Dev Anand choked, his voice trembling. Dev Anand's eyes gazed into the distance, tears streaming down his face as he whispered, "I cried for hours, my friend. I cried for the world, for our friendship, for the guiding light that once illuminated our lives." The line went dead, and Raj Kapoor's laughter faded into the darkness, leaving Dev Anand to ponder the weight of his friend's words.
