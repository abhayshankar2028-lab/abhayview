UNNING MUMBAI COLLAPSE**

'Raasasana Ramesh'

The sky was painted with hues of crimson and gold as Mumbai succumbed to its fury. The iconic Gateway of India crumbled, sending shockwaves through the nation. The once-majestic structure now lies in ruins, a testament to human resilience and determination. As the dust settles, the people of Mumbai emerge, scarred but unbroken.

The streets are filled with the cacophony of despair, yet hope resonates through the air. The Vijay Hazare Trophy, a championship that once brought the nation together, now stands as a symbol of the city's unwavering spirit. The quarterfinals beckon, and as Mumbai prepares to face Karnataka, the stage is set for an epic battle. Will the city's courage prevail, or will the storm of Karnataka's dominance subdue its will? Only time will tell.
