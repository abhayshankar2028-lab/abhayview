As the Indian cricket team stepped onto the field, a deafening roar echoed through the stadium. The fans, clad in their red and green jerseys, were on the edge of their seats. It was Rohit Sharma, the team's captain, who had just returned to India after a grueling tour of the United States. The players were overjoyed to see their captain, and the crowd was electric. With his trademark smile and energetic presence, Rohit Sharma gave a thumbs-up to the fans. The 6-hitting machines, who had been quietly waiting in the wings, suddenly roared to life. "Let's get this show on the road!" Rohit Sharma exclaimed, and the team erupted into a frenzy of celebration.
