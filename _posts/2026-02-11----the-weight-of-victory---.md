As I sat in the eerie silence of the ground, the familiar words echoed in my mind - "Unbelievable. Unbelievable." - the phrase that would forever be etched in my memory. We had it all, the momentum, the talent, and the victory. But it was all taken away in an instant, leaving us with nothing but the bitter taste of defeat.

Jonathan Trott, our Afghan coach, stood before us, a mixture of emotions etched on his face. His eyes, normally bright with passion, had lost their luster. He spoke words of encouragement, urging us to find strength in our hearts. But I knew the truth - it was just devastating for the guys. The weight of victory, the crushing blow of defeat. It was a reminder that with great success comes great pain.
