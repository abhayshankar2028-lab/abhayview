I remember the day I walked onto the silver screen like I was born to shine. My debut at 16, I left an indelible mark on Indian cinema with my first Rs 100 cr hit. The rush of adrenaline was exhilarating, but it was just the beginning. I was young and fearless, taking on the big boys and bringing in the crowds.

But life had other plans. I found love in the unlikeliest of places – a millionaire entrepreneur who made his fortune with a drop of water. His net worth was staggering, Rs 1,300 cr, and I was smitten. He took me away from the chaos of the film industry and into the world of luxury and love. I traded in my director's chair for a life of designer shoes and champagne, but my heart remains in Bollywood.
