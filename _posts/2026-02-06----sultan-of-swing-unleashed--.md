With the game on the line, Sooryavanshi dug deep, unleashing a fury of shots that left the English bowlers reeling. 15 sixes flew over the boundary, as Sooryavanshi's century break shattered the English innings. The Indian bowlers, once poised for victory, now faltered, and Sooryavanshi's team sensed the opening.
