** In a bizarre display of wealth, comedian Harsh Gujral flaunts his cook Dilip's indulgent spending habits, boasting about earning more than him. He claims Dilip can afford a house worth millions, only to rub it in by pointing at his own designer LV shoes, which he insists are 'the most expensive in the world'. As he struts around the stage, the audience can't help but laugh at his audacity. But amidst the humor, Gujral's words cut deep, highlighting the vast financial gap between those who earn a living and those who flaunt it.
