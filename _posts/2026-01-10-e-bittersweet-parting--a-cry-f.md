As the sun sets on the cricket world, a sense of nostalgia washes over me. Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli, two of the greatest players of our generation, have hung up their bats and pads. Their retirement from T20Is and Tests is a bittersweet moment, but one that I firmly believe is best for ODI cricket.

The demands of T20Is have taken a toll on these legends, who've played over 1,000 matches. The pressure to perform, the constant scrutiny, and the fear of injury have all taken their toll. In contrast, ODI cricket offers a more relaxed atmosphere, where players can focus on their game without the added stress.

Their retirement will bring a much-needed breath of fresh air to the ODI format, allowing new faces to emerge and exciting changes to take place. It's an era of fresh talent, fresh ideas, and fresh passion. As we bid farewell to Sharma and Kohli, we welcome a new chapter in the story of ODI cricket.
