As I stood in the midst of the Indian camp, surveying the familiar faces, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. The next day's Test match against Pakistan loomed, and with it, a crucial clash that would test our batsmen's mettle. But amidst the excitement, a quiet voice whispered in my ear: "Move away when Usman Tariq pauses." It was a message from Ashwin himself, who had seen his own team struggle with the same thing. I listened intently, and as I did, the words became clearer: we must be prepared to adapt, to change, and to thrive in the most unexpected ways.
