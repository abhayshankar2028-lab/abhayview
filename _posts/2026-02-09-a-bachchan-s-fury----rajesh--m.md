ya Bachchan's Fury**

"Rajesh, my friend, why did you have to say those things?" Jaya Bachchan's eyes blazed with anger as she turned to face the Bollywood star.

Rajesh Khanna, once a rising star, had grown complacent in his success. But when he saw Jaya Bachchan, the veteran actress, standing up for Amitabh Bachchan, he knew he'd crossed a line.

"You didn't want to be replaced by Big B, did you?" Jaya spat, her voice trembling with rage.

Rajesh's eyes dropped, and he mumbled, "I just wanted to be part of the family, Jaya."

Jaya's anger boiled over, and she lunged at Rajesh, her hands flying towards his face. Master Raju, who had been watching the exchange, intervened, reminding his friend that Jaya Bachchan was a legend, and she wouldn't take kindly to being disrespected.

"Master Raju, calm him down," he said firmly. "This is between Jaya and you, not Amitabh."

But Rajesh just shook his head and stormed off, leaving Jaya to face the music.
