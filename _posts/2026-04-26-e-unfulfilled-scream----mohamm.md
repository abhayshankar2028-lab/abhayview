Mohammad Kaif, the maestro of the field, stood frozen, his eyes fixed on the ball that seemed to defy gravity. Another catch, another missed opportunity. The IPL stadium, once a stage of wonder, now felt like a gauntlet of disappointment.

It's a tradition of every year, Kaif thought, his voice laced with frustration. The fans' cacophony of cheers and chants, a symphony of joy, now replaced by an eerie silence. The thrill of the catch, the rush of adrenaline, all silenced by the cruel hand of fate.

Kaif's gaze drifted to the scoreboard, the numbers taunting him like a cruel joke. 20 wickets, 20 innings, 20 chances missed. The cycle repeated itself, ad infinitum, leaving him feeling like a spectator to an unfulfilled dream.
