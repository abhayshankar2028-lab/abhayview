**SEO_DESC:** "The Indian-owned franchises are not giving a level playing field to the Pakistan players, as they are not being picked for the Indian-owned teams in The Hundred."
**TAGS:** Indian-owned franchises, Pakistan players, The Hundred, sports governance controversy

"It was a scathing rebuke from Michael Vaughan, the former England captain, who took aim at the governance of The Hundred, the new Twenty20 tournament being launched by the English Cricket Board. Vaughan pointed out that the Indian-owned franchises, which have a stranglehold on the league, were not giving the Pakistan players a chance to showcase their skills. "What message does that send about our game?" he asked. "Is it a message that says we don't value the talent from other countries? That we don't want our players to be from other nations? It's a pretty stark criticism, and it's one that I'm not sure anyone can ignore." The Indian owners had faced criticism in the past for their ownership model, and Vaughan's comments were just the latest salvo in a long-standing debate about governance and diversity in the sport."
