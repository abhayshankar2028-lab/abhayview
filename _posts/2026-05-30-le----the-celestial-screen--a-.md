As I stood on the red carpet, gazing up at the stars twinkling above, I couldn't help but think of the Bollywood genre. Like Ranveer Singh, who has become a household name without sacrificing his art, the stars of our industry deserve to be paid a fair wage. Their work is a canvas, not a commodity. As a cine body, we must recognize the value of their talent and compensate them accordingly. The question is, will we act like stars like Ranveer Singh and create employment through the magic of cinema? Or will we continue to prioritize box office returns over the well-being of our creative souls? The time for change is now.

I recall the countless hours spent in the studio, pouring my heart and soul into a single film. It was a labor of love, not just a paycheck. The screen is a canvas, and the stars who paint it deserve to be treated with respect. As I looked up at the stars, I vowed to do better. For the sake of our industry, for the sake of our art, and for the sake of the stars themselves, let us strive to create employment through the power of cinema, not just for the sake of the bottom line.
