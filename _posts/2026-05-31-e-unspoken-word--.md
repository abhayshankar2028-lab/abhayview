In the silence of the cricket pitch, a hush fell over the Eden Gardens crowd. Sachin Tendulkar stood tall, his eyes fixed on the blue skies above. The Test match against South Africa loomed, and with it, the whispers of "Sachin should play Sooryavanshi" echoed in the minds of his teammates. But Tendulkar's lips remained sealed, his silence a shield against the weight of expectations.

As the selectors weighed their options, Tendulkar's eyes never left the pitch. He was a man of discipline, of duty. The selectors' decisions would be made with the best interests of the team in mind, not just personal preference. Tendulkar's silence was a testament to his unwavering commitment to the craft, and the uncertainty that lay ahead.
