As I watched Naseeruddin Shah stand before the National School of Drama (NSD) students, I couldn't help but be moved by the weight of his words. "Only failures come back to teach," he said, his eyes shining with a deep understanding. I had witnessed this phrase before, but never in such a context. In his eyes, I saw a maturity that spoke of decades of teaching experience and a profound respect for the art of learning. Shah's words were not just motivational quotes, but a reflection of his own journey as a teacher. "I consider it my responsibility to help these kids," he said, his voice filled with humility.
