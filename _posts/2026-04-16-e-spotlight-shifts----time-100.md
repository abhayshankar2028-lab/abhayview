TIME 100. A list that once belonged to the elite, now in the hands of the world's most influential minds. And then, there was Ranbir Kapoor. The actor's sudden rise to the top was a phenomenon that left everyone in awe. As the news broke, I was consumed by the reverberations of this moment. Time may have once shone brightly on the likes of Elon Musk and Serena Williams, but Ranbir's moment in the spotlight was a new kind of star.

Ayushmann Khurrana, the 'storyteller' of Indian cinema, took to the stage to share his thoughts on the occasion. With a twinkle in his eye, he said, "I'm not just a storyteller, I'm a global ambassador." His words were a poignant reminder that the art of storytelling transcends borders and platforms. As Ranbir Kapoor's name became synonymous with excellence, the world was reminded that true greatness can only be achieved when we tap into our collective human spirit.
