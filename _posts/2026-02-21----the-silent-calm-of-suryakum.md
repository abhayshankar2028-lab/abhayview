**SEO_DESC:** 'As T20 champions rise, their minds remain grounded, anchoring fears and doubts, a reminder that true greatness lies not in form, but in the stillness of the mind.'

**TAGS:** T20 World Cup, Suryakumar Yadav, Abhishek Sharma, mental toughness

As I watched Suryakumar Yadav bat, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. His form was faltering, his shots were getting away. I worried about him, about his chances in the T20 World Cup. But then I asked him about his mindset. "Those who worry about my form, I worry about them," he replied. His response was music to my ears. It was a reminder that true greatness lies not in the number of sixes or the number of wickets taken. It lies in the stillness of the mind.
