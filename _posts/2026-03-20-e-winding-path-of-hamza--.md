As I sit and ponder the turbulent waters of history, my mind wanders to Hamza, the resilient protagonist of our beloved Dhurandhar 2. In the midst of demonetisation's shuddering chaos, Hamza finds solace in his grandmother's tales of the past. The whispers of a bygone era – of kings and queens, of empires and revolution – weave a rich tapestry that becomes Hamza's world. The echoes of Muhammad Ali Dagar's struggles and the resilience of the Ahadi community reverberate through Hamza's eyes. As the demonetisation storm rages on, Hamza's world becomes a testament to the indomitable human spirit that weaves itself into the fabric of our collective memory.

In this heart-wrenching yet ultimately triumphant tale, I, Harsha Bhogle, attempt to capture the nuances of Hamza's world, where the lines between past and present blur, and the strength of the human heart is forever etched on the canvas of history.
