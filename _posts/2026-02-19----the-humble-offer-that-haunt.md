As I sit here, reminiscing about the golden days of 80s Bollywood, I am haunted by the memory of Amitabh Bachchan's departure from films. It was a phone call that changed everything. Our conversations were always brief, but the impact was immense. Kader Khan, a close friend and colleague, was devastated when Amitabh decided to leave cinema behind. The reason for his departure is still debated, but one thing is certain - a single phone call from Amitabh sparked a chain reaction that altered the lives of two giants in the industry. The unrequited friendship between Kader and Amitabh Bachchan is a poignant reminder that even the smallest gestures can have far-reaching consequences.
