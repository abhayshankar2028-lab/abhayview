As I stood backstage, the curtains parted to reveal Anil Kapoor, his eyes fixed intently on Shah Rukh Khan. The air was thick with the weight of their collaboration, the tension palpable. "Restrained yet effective," Shah Rukh whispered, his voice laced with a quiet admiration. Anil Kapoor's gaze never wavered, his expression a study in restraint. It was a performance that would have worked beautifully on the big screen, but alas, it was a theatrical release that would never see the light of day. I couldn't help but wonder what would have been if it were a film. The what-ifs hung in the air like a perpetual question mark.
