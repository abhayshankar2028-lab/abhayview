I remember the day Shah Rukh Khan refused to FaceTime an actor's daughter. The news sent shockwaves through the entertainment industry. Just days ago, he was known for his modern ways, but now, I discovered the depths of his conviction. He believed in the art of silence and drama. The tradition of not speaking to an actor's daughter, no matter how young, was a rule he followed unyieldingly. His actions sparked a debate, with some hailing him as a traditionalist, while others criticized his outdated views. Yet, for Shah Rukh Khan, it was a matter of respect for the craft, for the art of acting, and for the delicate dance of human relationships. His silence was a powerful statement, a testament to his unwavering dedication to his craft.
