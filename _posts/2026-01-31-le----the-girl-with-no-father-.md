In the quiet of her memories, Tabu's eyes still hold the faintest glimmer of a man she never knew. Her mother, a fierce and enigmatic figure, had chosen to keep their father's identity a secret. As a child, Tabu felt an inexplicable void, a sense of loss that she couldn't quite explain. Her mother's words still lingered in her mind: "I have no memories of him, not curious about him." And so, Tabu learned to cherish the few moments she shared with her father, even if they were fleeting and undefined. Now, as an adult, she realizes that the absence of her father's name is a symbol of the love and acceptance she received from her mother.
