As I stood on the dusty cricket pitch, gazing out at the sea of adoring fans, I couldn't help but feel a sense of pride and vindication. The IPL had brought us together, had made our passion for the game grow tenfold. But amidst the euphoria, I couldn't shake off the feeling of frustration. The Mumbai Indians, our arch-rivals, had pulled off a stunning coup. The way they had exploited our weaknesses, their unwavering dedication to the craft, it all seemed so... deliberate.

Their three bowlers - Jasprit Bumrah, Rashid Khan, and Hardik Pandya - had been the catalysts behind Mumbai's success. Their precision and guile had outmaneuvered our batsmen, leaving us with an insurmountable lead. I couldn't help but think of our former Proteas captain, who had once championed the same cause. Now, he was the one blaming them for our downfall. But I knew the truth – it was their outstanding performance that had won the day.
