In the scorching summer of 2012, cricket's most revered ace, M.S. Dhoni, faced a dilemma. The game hung in the balance, with South Africa on the brink of a thrilling double Super Over. As the sun beat down on the MCG, Gurbaz, the Sri Lankan wicket-keeper, stood poised to unleash a six that would seal the deal. But in an instant, his fingers slipped, and the ball sailed harmlessly over the boundary. The crowd gasped, and the game was left wondering: how did Gurbaz let South Africa snatch victory from the jaws of defeat?
