KNEW A STAR WAS THERE

By Harsha Bhogle

In the twilight of Bangalore's cricketing glory days, I recall a moment that will forever be etched in my memory. It was an early RCB era, and Virat Kohli was a young prodigy, still finding his footing. One sunny afternoon, during a training session, Virat's curiosity was piqued by a stray ball, which had slipped from one of the bowlers. Without hesitation, he picked it up, examining it with an intensity that belied his age. "You knew a star was there," I whispered to him, "one that would rise to become the brightest gem in this league." Those words echoed in my mind for years to come, a testament to Kohli's insatiable hunger to learn and succeed. Little did we know, the ball was just a catalyst for his meteoric rise.
