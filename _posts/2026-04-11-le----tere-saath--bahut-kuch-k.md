In the midst of a heated argument, Arpita Khan stood tall, her eyes blazing with determination. Her family, however, seemed unwavering in their decision. Salman Khan, her husband-to-be, sat beside her, his face etched with concern. "Tere saath kya kar rahi hai?" he asked, his voice laced with worry. Arpita's jaw clenched, and she replied, "Bahut kuch karna hai, Salman. Tere saath kaise rehna hai?" The tension in the air was palpable as Salman's eyes searched hers, seeking answers to the questions that plagued his mind.

As the meeting drew to a close, it was clear that the road ahead would be fraught with challenges. But Arpita stood firm, her resolve unshaken. Tere saath, bahut kuch karna hai, indeed.
