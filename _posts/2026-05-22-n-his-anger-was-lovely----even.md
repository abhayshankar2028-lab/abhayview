Even his anger was lovely
These words echo through my mind,
A father's love, so unafraid to find,
The beauty in the tears I'd leave behind.

I think back to the days of old,
When Dharmendra's laughter would unfold,
His eyes would sparkle like the morning dew,
And I'd be so caught up in love anew.

But now I see, the pain he bore,
A weight that pressed upon his heart once more,
The years went by, and I grew apart,
But in my heart, a piece of him will forever start.

His anger was lovely, a flame that burned,
Guiding me through life's darkest year,
Even in his anger, I saw a love so true,
A love that I'll carry with me, forever new.
