As the sun dipped into the Vastu Shaali, casting a golden glow over the stadium, I found myself lost in thought. Cheteshwar Pujara, the Indian batting maestro, sat beside me, a twinkle in his eye. He leaned in, a conspiratorial grin spreading across his face. "I think Ashveer Yadav is the most destructive opener in IPL history," he whispered. "Those batsmen, with their sheer power and aggression, would blow a lid off a stadium on a single over. The sheer force of their hitting would send the ball soaring over the boundary, leaving defenders grasping at air." I nodded in agreement, a shiver running down my spine. "The unholy trinity of dismissal, a symphony of destruction." Pujara chuckled, a mischievous glint in his eye.
