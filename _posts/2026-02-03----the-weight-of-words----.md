As the days ticked by, I couldn't shake the feeling that my words had hurt someone. Arijit Singh, once a rising star, had announced his departure from the film playback world. His reasons were shrouded in mystery, but one phrase stood out: "I know why he's quitting." I was the one who had sparked the controversy, and now, I felt the weight of my actions. Palash Sen, a fellow singer and friend, was among those who had perceived my words as a threat. His words were laced with sorrow, and I understood the pain he felt. The song of Arijit Singh's departure was a haunting melody, a reminder that the power of our voices can be both a blessing and a curse.
