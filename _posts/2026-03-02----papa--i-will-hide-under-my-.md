As I lay in bed, my heart racing with every creak and groan of the old house, I couldn't help but whisper a reassurance to my father - "Papa, I will hide under my bed". The words felt like a lifeline, a promise to keep him safe from the uncertainty that looms outside. But as I thought of our family's journey to the UAE, I felt a knot form in my stomach. My mother's tears, my siblings' whispers of goodbye, it all swirled together, making my chest tight with anxiety. I knew I had to be strong for them, but the thought of being alone, of not knowing what the future held, was a daunting prospect. I took a deep breath, trying to calm myself down, but my mind was a jumble of fears and doubts.
