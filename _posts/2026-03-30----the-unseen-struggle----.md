As I sit in my dimly lit study, the flickering candles casting eerie shadows on the walls, I feel a familiar anxiety creeping in. It's a feeling I've grown accustomed to over the years, a constant companion that grips my heart like a vice. Every couple of days, I start to feel a knot in my stomach, a sense of dread that settles in like a shroud. It's not just the fear of flying; it's the fear of being trapped in a metal tube with no escape. I've tried to shake it off, to distract myself with movies and games, but nothing seems to work. I'm forced to confront the real me, the person I've kept hidden behind a mask of confidence. It's frustrating, this struggle. I'm tired of being a prisoner of my own fears.
