dine de Klerk's Revenge**

As Mumbai Indians took to the field, their sloppy play seemed to dance out of their grasp. The WPL 2026 opener was shaping up to be a straight-up massacre, with RCB's bowlers carrying the day. It was Nadine de Klerk's return to the league that sparked the magic. With a back-of-the-bone wicket, the South African all-rounder sent a Mumbai Indian captain scurrying for cover, sending the Indian batting lineup into disarray.

As the RCB spinners continued to mop up wickets, the Mumbai Indians' hopes began to dwindle. Their batting lineup, once so feared, was now crumbling with each passing over. De Klerk's sheer skill and guile proved too much for the Mumbai Indians, as RCB claimed their opening-night thriller in Navi Mumbai.
