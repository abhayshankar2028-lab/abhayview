As I stood frozen, my eyes locked onto the ball, the one that would change everything. Mehidy Hasan Miraz, my skipper, my partner in this endless pursuit. He was away from the wicket, his eyes fixed on the ball, his mind a thousand miles away. I, too, had found solace in the game, in the intricate dance of batsmen and bowlers. But in that moment, I was lost, searching for a connection, a spark that would ignite the fireworks of success. The ball sailed past me, a fleeting glance, and I was left with nothing but the sound of my own heartbeat. The game, it seemed, had played out its beauty, leaving me with an aching sense of longing.
