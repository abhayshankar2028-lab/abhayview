ey Called Me Panauti**

"Panauti" - the nickname that stuck with me like a bad habit. They called it 'bad luck'. I used to hear it from my aunts, my friends, even from the family staff. "Panauti" - whenever I walked into a room, they'd whisper it under their breath. It was as if the universe itself was conspiring against me.

I recall one particularly grueling day at an audition. I was a young actress, determined to land her first big role. But as I stepped onto the stage, I tripped on my own feet. The audience gasped, my heart sank. Suddenly, someone whispered, "Panauti!" and I knew I'd never be a star.

I told Rajkumar Hirani, "I have no work." And that was it. The 'bad luck' was sealed. But I refused to let it define me. I kept pushing forward, and eventually, I found success. The nickname remained, but I wore it as a badge of honor.
