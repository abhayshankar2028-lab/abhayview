tle:** 'Shame of the England Board'

**SEO_DESC:** "Former England cricketers blast ECB for lack of accountability in Ashes saga"

**TAGS:** England cricket board, Ashes series, accountability, former players

In the midst of England's Ashes triumph, a chorus of discontent echoed across the land. Former captain Michael Atherton, the stalwart of centuries past, fired the first shot, claiming that the England cricket board had abandoned all sense of accountability in the face of their dominance.

"I've seen some lackluster performances in my time," Atherton said in an interview. "But to imply that the board would do anything to prevent us from winning? That's a ridiculous notion." Similarly, Ian Botham and Graeme Swann added their voices to the chorus, stating that the ECB's decision to delay the 2023 Ashes series was nothing short of shameful.

The England cricket board was left to ponder the consequences of their actions, as their reputation took a battering.
