Next, Harbhajan Singh took the microphone, his words speaking of hope and perseverance. Hazel Keech's soulful voice wove a spell of devotion, while Harbhajan's gentle soulfulness touched the hearts of all present. Zaheer Khan's energetic performance was a fusion of Bollywood and cricket, bringing the house down with his signature dance moves.
