In the city of stars, where glamour reigns supreme, Karan Johar stood tall, his voice echoing through the halls of Bollywood. The filmmaker and producer, known for his unflinching critiques, had a message for the industry. "Stop doing PR," he said, "let your achievements speak for themselves." The words resonated deeply, a stark reminder that the industry's relentless pursuit of fame and fortune had come at a cost. The prices of talent, creativity, and passion were being paid in full. Karan's words sparked a conversation, a call to arms for a more authentic industry, where art and substance mattered more than marketing and prestige.
