Deepika Padukone stood before the BMC, her voice trembling with despair. "Mumbai, my city, is choking. The air quality is at an all-time high, and it's not just the pollution that's a concern, it's the kids who are breathing it in. How is this okay? I've spent my entire life here, and I've never seen anything like this. The children's lungs are being slowly suffocated, and it's becoming a ticking time bomb. I beg of you, BMC, please help us. We need air that's clean and fresh, not a constant reminder of our city's destructive past."

As she spoke, the children of Mumbai looked up at her with worried eyes, their small lungs crying out for a breath of fresh air.
