** He thought back to those exhilarating moments on the field, the thrill of victory, and the agony of defeat. He remembered the stunned gaze of the umpires, the cheers of the crowds, and the stunned silence of his teammates. But most of all, he remembered the fine. A thousand rupees to wipe away a few tears, to soothe a bruised ego. It may have been a small price to pay for the fleeting thrill of stardom, but for Ishan, it was a harsh reminder that even in the golden glow of cricket's hallowed halls, the game's cruel realities can still cut deep.
