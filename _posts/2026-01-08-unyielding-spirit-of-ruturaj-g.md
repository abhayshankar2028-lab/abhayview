In the annals of cricket's storied past, one name stands tall – Vijay Hazare's unparalleled record. The beacon of excellence, a titan of the game, who once captivated the nation with his unwavering dedication. But now, a new force awakens, one that touches the very fabric of history.

Ruturaj Gaikwad, the unassuming batsman from Maharashtra, has emerged as a force to be reckoned with. With a resolute gaze, he sets out to shatter the records that have stood the test of time. And shatter them, he does – with a majestic 15th century, a feat that defies the odds. As the world watches in awe, Gaikwad stands tall, a testament to the unyielding spirit that drives him to greatness.
