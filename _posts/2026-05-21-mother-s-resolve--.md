In the stillness of the morning, Yami Gautam's hands moved deftly, capturing the world through her lens. Her toddler, Rohan, clung to her feet, eyes wide with wonder. As she composed a shot, her mind wandered to her next assignment. The camera, a tool she had always cherished, now held a new purpose – to convey the beauty of maternity and motherhood.

As she clicked away, the toddler's grip on her feet loosened, and she was free to indulge in her passion. The memories of Dhurandhar's Rizwan, a painting that had once inspired her, now mingled with the joy of this moment. Yami's resolve strengthened, her love for photography tempered by her maternal instincts.
