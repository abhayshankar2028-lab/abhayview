As Bobby Deol stepped onto the stage, his eyes brimmed with tears, yet he composed himself, beaming with gratitude. The audience erupted in applause as Dharmendra, his eyes shining with pride, presented the Lifetime Achievement Award. The moment hung in the air, and then, in a voice that trembled with emotion, Bobby Deol spoke. "I see my father's love in your eyes," he whispered, his voice cracking with heartfelt sincerity. Dharmendra's eyes welled up with tears as he replied, "I am honored, my son, for you have carried my legacy forward with such passion." The audience was swept away by the emotional reunion, as Bobby Deol's words captured the essence of his father's enduring love and influence.
