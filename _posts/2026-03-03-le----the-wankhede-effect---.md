As I stepped out onto the sun-kissed turf of the Wankhede Stadium, the familiar din of English cricket echoed through my veins. The ghosts of our past battles with the Englishmen still lingered, their haunting melodies a reminder of our impending confrontation. The Indian team's nervous energy was palpable, every member of the squad seeming to tremble at the prospect of facing the Red Sox - the unpredictable, the explosive, and the unyielding. The Wankhede's electric atmosphere transformed the very air into a cauldron of anxiety, a potent brew that would fuel our players' determination to give England a run for their money.
