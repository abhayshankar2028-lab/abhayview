**SEO_DESC:** "From VJ fame to spiritual awakening, India's Ruby Bhatia shares her journey of self-discovery and the transformative power of meditation"

**TAGS:** meditation, spirituality, self-discovery

As I sit in quiet contemplation, my mind a canvas of shadows and light, I recall the days of being Ruby Bhatia, the VJ of India's youth. The glow of the screen, the rush of adrenaline, the thrill of performing in front of thousands – it was all so intoxicating. But as the years went by, the fire that once burned within me began to flicker. I felt the weight of fame, the pressure to conform, and the emptiness of a life lived in the public eye. Two years of 24/7 meditation, and I have come to understand that the true power of music lies not in the notes, but in the silence within. It is in the stillness that I have found my voice, my soul, my true self.
