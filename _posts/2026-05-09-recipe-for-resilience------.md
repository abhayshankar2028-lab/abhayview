As I sit here, sipping my morning coffee, I am reminded of Jasmine Sandlas, the Indian-American chef who made it big in the US, despite living on food stamps. Born and raised in a humble home, Jasmine's father passed away when she was just 18, leaving her with a mountain of debt and a sense of purpose. Yet, she refused to let her circumstances define her. With unwavering determination, she worked tirelessly to hone her culinary skills, eventually landing a spot at a top-tier restaurant. Her journey was not without its challenges, but Jasmine's unrelenting spirit and work ethic propelled her to success. Today, she is a celebrated chef and restaurateur, inspiring countless others with her story of resilience and determination.
