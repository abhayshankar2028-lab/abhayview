As the sun dipped below the London skyline, Anushka and Virat Sharma made their way to their home, a tranquil oasis in the midst of the bustling city. With a mixture of excitement and nervousness, they prepared for their family puja ceremony. The air was filled with the sweet scent of incense and the soft glow of candles as they lit the sacred fire. With a gentle smile, Anushka lit the first candle, and Virat followed suit, their eyes locking in a moment of pure devotion. Together, they invoked the blessings of the divine, their love and gratitude radiating from every corner of the room. As the puja progressed, the atmosphere grew more sacred, a reflection of the love that shone bright in their hearts. The Sharma family basked in the warmth of their love, a true testament to the power of devotion.
