**TAGS: motherhood, postpartum, identity, trauma**

As I sit with Kiara, her eyes well up with tears as she speaks about the weight she's carrying – not just her newborn daughter, but the societal expectations that came with motherhood. "I feel like I've lost myself," she whispers, her voice trembling. The "she" in her words echoes the countless women who've felt the same sense of disconnection. The "me" she's searching for in this new identity is elusive, a mirage that vanishes when she tries to grasp it. Kiara's words are a cry for help, a plea for understanding that her journey is not alone. In her eyes, I see the scars of a society that demands perfection, and the toll it takes on women like Kiara.
