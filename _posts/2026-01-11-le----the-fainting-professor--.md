As I sit here, pen in hand, I am transported back to those tumultuous college days when it all began. Karan Johar's words still echo in my mind - fainting in his own dorm, a victim of the relentless battle he waged with his own body image. His mother, a constant presence in his life, would often remark on his 'fat' and 'large' frame, an observation that pierced his confidence. "I am not on Ozempic," he would silently reply, a whispered promise to himself. It was a battle he would fight for years, but never truly win. The fainting episodes, the self-doubt, and the constant scrutiny - all were a manifestation of the unrelenting war within him.
