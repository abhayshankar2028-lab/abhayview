It was a drizzly evening in Delhi, but the spirits of the team were ablaze with determination. Axar Patel, the quiet giant of the crease, had finally found his rhythm, dispatching batsmen with precision and finesse. His partnership with David Miller, the dynamic duo of the Punjab Kings, was the spark that ignited a fire that would leave the opposition in ashes. As the Delhi innings began, Miller's aggressive strokes and Patel's controlled innings wove a tapestry of uncertainty for the Punjab Kings. But as the sun dipped below the horizon, Delhi had found its voice, and their chances of survival seemed secure. The Punjab Kings, however, were far from out of the woods, and a turning point in the match was just around the corner.
