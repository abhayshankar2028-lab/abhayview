As I sit in the air-conditioned room, sipping my warm coffee, I gaze out the window at the chaos that is Bollywood. Despite the frenzy surrounding Dhurandhar 2, Akshay Kumar is confident about Bhooth Bangla. His eyes sparkle with a sense of purpose, and his voice is filled with conviction. "I never make films that embarrass my parents," he says with a hint of self-deprecation. It's a statement that resonates deeply, a promise to himself and to the world. As I watch him, I'm reminded that sometimes, the calmest warriors are the ones who dare to be different.
