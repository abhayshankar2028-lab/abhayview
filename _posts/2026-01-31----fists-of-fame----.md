As I recall the days of their rivalry, I remember a night that will forever be etched in my memory. It was a typical Mumbai evening, and the stars were about to come face to face. Shah Rukh Khan and Hrithik Roshan had been trading blows on social media for weeks, but on this particular night, they decided to put their rivalry to the test. The air was electric as they faced off in the kitchen of a trendy restaurant. "People were expecting Shah Rukh Khan and Hrithik Roshan to throw punches," I recalled. "But what they didn't expect was the intensity of their stare-down. The tension was palpable, and for a moment, it seemed like the whole room was holding its breath. But in the end, only one could emerge victorious." Little did they know, it was only the beginning of their legendary feud.
