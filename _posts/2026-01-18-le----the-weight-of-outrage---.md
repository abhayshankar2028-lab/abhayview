In the midst of a whirlwind of accolades, an enigmatic figure, AR Rahman, found himself questioning his artistic voice, his creative spark dwindling. Two Oscars, once the pinnacle of his success, now felt like a burden, a weight he struggled to shed. "Two Oscars can be a burden," he whispered to himself, as the pressure to constantly produce, to innovate, and to entertain began to suffocate him. The once-celebrated maestro felt like a shipwreck, lost at sea, unable to escape the undertow of criticism and self-doubt. As the world watched, a brilliant mind crumbled, leaving behind a hollow shell of his former self.
