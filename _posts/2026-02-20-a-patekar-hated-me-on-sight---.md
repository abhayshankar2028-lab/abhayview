na Patekar Hated Me On Sight**

I still remember the day Nana Patekar, India's first Fair & Lovely girl, quit acting at the height of her fame. It was as if the world had turned against her. The once-bright lights of Bollywood's glitz and glamour dimmed, leaving only shadows. I was one of the first to feel the pinch, a victim of Nana's newfound disdain. Her eyes, once bright with promise, turned cold and unforgiving as she turned me away. "You're just a pale imitation," she spat, her voice like a knife cutting through my soul. "You'll never be as beautiful as me." Those words haunt me still, a bitter reminder of the world's cruel indifference. I was Nana's pawn, and I paid the price for her scorn.
