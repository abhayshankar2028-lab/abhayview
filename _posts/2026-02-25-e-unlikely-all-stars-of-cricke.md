But it was their motley crew of bowlers that truly caught the attention. There was Joshua Kibazivu, the 6-foot-9 pacer with a wicked swing; Bravo Dhlaini, the unorthodox off-spinner; and Sadako Mbele, the wily left-arm orthodox. Together, they formed a veritable bowling machine, each one working in tandem to try and outsmart the Indian attack. As the game unfolded, it became clear that this was no ordinary bowling team - they were the unsung heroes of Zimbabwean cricket, and they were determined to prove it to the world.
