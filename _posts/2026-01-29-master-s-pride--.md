I recall the day the music legends gathered at the HCL Academy of Music. The air was electric as AR Rahman took the stage, his eyes scanning the room. And then, he spoke. His voice, deep and commanding, dropped a bombshell: "Arijit Singh, you're reckless. You work from your village, while I've sold out stadiums and broken records."

His words stung, but I knew better. I'd studied the master's music, pouring over his film scores and operatic compositions. I'd practiced for hours, pouring my heart and soul into every note. I knew I had what it took to make it in this industry. AR Rahman's words were a cruel reminder of the harsh realities of the music world, but they also fueled my determination.
