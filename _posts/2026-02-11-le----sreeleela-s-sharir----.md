** Sreeleela sat amidst the chaos, her eyes fixed on the certificate proudly displayed on the wall. Her classmates had sung her praises, and now the medical community was buzzing about her remarkable achievement. But amidst the congratulations, a murmur rippled through the room. "He did a Chayawanprash ad, didn't he?" someone whispered. Sreeleela's gaze widened as she recalled the infamous commercial, where a patient's convulsions were attributed to the "Chayawanprash" of the hospital. The memory still stung. Yet, as she gazed out at the sea of smiling faces, she smiled, knowing that her hard work and dedication had paid off. Sreeleela's Sharir, or her medical career, had risen to new heights.
