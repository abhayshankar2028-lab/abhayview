The stage was set for a nail-biting encounter between Delhi and Punjab Kings. Axar Patel, the Indian opener, was tasked with anchoring the innings. He took the field with a determined look in his eye, a newfound swagger in his stride. The crowd was on the edge of their seats, sensing a great match unfolding.

As the game wore on, Axar Patel's patience paid off, laying the foundation for a massive innings. His partnership with Rishabh Pant proved to be a turning point, with the duo combining to bowl out the Punjab Kings for a meager 197. Meanwhile, David Miller, the young Pakistani opener, was having a day to remember. With his elegant strokeplay and tactical brilliance, Miller provided the momentum that Delhi needed to stay alive. The final bowler, Jasprit Bumrah, saw the game out, as Delhi edged to a 5-wicket victory.
