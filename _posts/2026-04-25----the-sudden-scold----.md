As the sun began to set on the rustic village of Madras, Aamir Khan and Reema Kagti found themselves seated on the worn-out wooden bench, the only sound the chirping of crickets and the gentle rustling of leaves. They had just finished a long day of practicing their lines for the upcoming film. As they munched on the humble puffed rice they had brought with them, Aamir's eyes began to wander. "Tell me, Reema, what's the hurry?" he asked, his voice laced with a hint of boredom. Reema's eyes narrowed, and her voice took on a sharp edge. "You said you're the producer, Aamir. And yet, you're eating slowly. What's the rush?" The sudden scold caught Aamir off guard, and he looked at Reema with a mixture of surprise and annoyance. "What are you talking about? I'm just enjoying the meal." Reema's gaze lingered on him, and Aamir felt a shiver run down his spine. The scold had left a lasting impression, one that would linger long after the cameras stopped rolling.
