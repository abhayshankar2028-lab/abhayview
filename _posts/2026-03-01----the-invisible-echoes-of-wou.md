As I sit in the quiet of my home, I am still haunted by the memory of the day Dipika Kakar underwent surgery to remove a 13mm cyst. The words she shared with me still linger in my mind, a poignant reminder of the human body's capacity to heal and its propensity for pain. "The nature of pain keeps changing," she said, her voice a gentle breeze that rustled the leaves of my heart. I listened intently, and as I did, I felt a sense of understanding wash over me. Pain is not just a physical sensation, but a manifestation of the mind's ability to interpret and navigate the world. In that moment, I realized that Dipika's experience was not just about her body, but about the intricate web of emotions that underlies every wound.
