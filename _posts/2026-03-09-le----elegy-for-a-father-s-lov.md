As I watched, tears streaming down my face, Mannara Chopra crumpled to the ground, her world shattered by Anurag Dobhal's violent car crash. The young actor lay motionless, his body broken and battered. The diagnosis: diabetes, a cruel twist of fate. His father's anger and disappointment boiled over, leaving him disowning his son. Mannara felt as though she was losing the love of her life. Tears of sorrow mingled with her tears of pain as she whispered, "Why, Anurag? Why did fate have to take you away from me?"
