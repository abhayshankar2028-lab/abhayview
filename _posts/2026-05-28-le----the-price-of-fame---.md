As I sat in the dimly lit office, the only sound the hum of the air conditioner, I couldn't help but think of Sushmita Sen. The actress, known for her stunning looks and captivating on-screen presence, had once been on top of the world. But it was all taken away when her manager, a man she had trusted with her success, quit when she became a mother at 24.

It was a bitter pill to swallow, but Sushmita tried to brush it off. She had her family to think about, after all. But the part of her that wanted revenge burned bright. She couldn't help but wonder if the fame and fortune that had once been hers had been worth the price of losing her career - and her manager.
