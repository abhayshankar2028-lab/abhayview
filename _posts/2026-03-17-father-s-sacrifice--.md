I sit here with tears in my eyes, recalling the day my father lost everything. It was a typical day, film after film, nothing was selling. We were paupers, barely scraping by. Our Rs 30 was all we had left. And then, disaster struck. The film that could have made us rich, flopped. We were left with nothing but shame and regret.

As I look back, I see the sacrifices my father made for his art. The countless hours, the endless struggles, the sacrifices he made for us. And now, I am left to carry on his legacy. I remember the words he used to say to me, "Harsha, your future is in your hands." I hope he was right. I hope his sacrifice was not in vain.
