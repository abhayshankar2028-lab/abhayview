Radha Yadav's eyes welled up with tears as she stared at the news on her phone. Rumors had been circulating that her husband, Rajpal Yadav, had been released from prison. The media frenzy surrounding the incident had already taken a toll on her family.

But Radha's calm demeanor was a stark contrast to the chaos that surrounded her family. She knew that the film industry was her husband's rock, and they would come together to support him no matter what.

"I don't know who to thank," Radha said, her voice barely above a whisper. "But I want to say that the film industry is with us, always. We've never let anyone or anything come between us and the people we love."
