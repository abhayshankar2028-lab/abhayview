In the depths of poverty, Govinda wandered, searching for a guiding light. That's when he met Pahlaj Nihalani, the reformer of the Sri Ramakrishna Mission. Their paths crossed, and the young boy's eyes met the wise and compassionate face of his guru. Pahlaj Nihalani took Govinda under his wing, offering a shelter from the storm of poverty. As the days passed, Govinda's heart began to heal, his soul was set free. He realized that true strength lies not in wealth or material possessions, but in the love and devotion that guides us. Pahlaj Nihalani, the rock of Govinda's life, said, "He was our foundation stone, the one who helped us rise above the mire of poverty. His selfless love and guidance are the keys to our liberation."
