TLE:** Aamir Khan's Home Cooked Dinner

**SEO_DESC:** Aamir Khan, the renowned Indian actor, sits down with Arijit Singh at his home, enjoying a humble dinner - a delicious meal prepared by his wife, Kiran Rao.

**TAGS:** Arijit Singh, Aamir Khan, Dinner, Home Cooked Meal

As he savors each bite, Aamir turns to Arijit and asks, "Hum log ka kya hoga? What will happen if we don't pursue our passion for playback singing?" Arijit's eyes light up with determination as he responds, "Mujhe paas hai, mera kahaan hai, aur main isse kuchh karengi." (I have it, my home, and I'll find a way to do it.) Aamir smiles, proud of his singer's resolve, knowing that with Arijit on board, the future of playback singing in India looks brighter.

As the meal comes to an end, Aamir praises Arijit's dedication, saying, "You're not just a singer, you're a rockstar. I'm glad we're cooking up a storm together."
