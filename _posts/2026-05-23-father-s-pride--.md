David Dhawan's words still echo in my mind - "Everybody should have a son like him". Those words have become a mantra for me, a reminder of the unbridled passion and love that comes with fatherhood. As I watch Varun Dhawan grow into a talented actor, I am filled with pride and awe. His dedication to his craft is inspiring, and his willingness to take risks is admirable. David Dhawan's words may have been spoken in the past, but their impact remains, a testament to the profound influence of a father's love and pride on one's life.
