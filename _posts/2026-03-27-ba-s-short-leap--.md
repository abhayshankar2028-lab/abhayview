Saba's world was turned upside down when she was hospitalized with a parasitic infection. The medication turned her world into a blur, and the doctor's words echoed in her mind - "You're losing 4kg, Saba. It's a miracle." The numbers danced in her head, a cruel reminder of her dwindling strength.

But then, Hrithik Roshan walked into her hospital room. His eyes locked onto hers, and his warm smile lit up the room. "You're doing great, Saba," he said, his voice barely above a whisper. "You're stronger than you think." His words were a lifeline, a bridge to a brighter tomorrow. And as Saba drifted away, she knew that Hrithik was right - she was not alone, and her spirit would rise above the ashes of despair.
