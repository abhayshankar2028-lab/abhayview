As I sit amidst the silence of my Mumbai home, the tears begin to fall. Memories of the 'difficult time' post Sushant Singh Rajput's death flood my mind. We don't need a temple to pay our respects to the man who gave his life for our dreams. My fingers cradle a worn photo of Sushant, and I feel the weight of his absence. His laughter, his smile, they haunt me still. I think of the countless times we discussed life, love, and our passions. The bond we shared transcended borders and generations. Tears well up in my eyes as I realize the magnitude of his impact on my life. In this moment, I am left with only my thoughts – and for now, that's enough.
