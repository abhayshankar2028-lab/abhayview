I sit here reminiscing about those Halcyon days of yore, when the world seemed untouched by the whirlwind of time. It was an era of Rudyard Kipling's whispers, Swami Vivekananda's wisdom, and Rajesh Khanna's golden eyes. In the midst of such a perfect harmony, I recall the day Aradhana walked away. Though the reason for her departure was shrouded in mystery, it seemed to me that the scars of a broken heart still lingered. Meanwhile, Sharmila Tagore wandered the outskirts of the shoot, seeking solace in her words of poetry. It was then that I chanced upon an old photograph of Satyajit Ray – his eyes, now dimly lit by age, yet still burning with a creative fire that seemed to reverberate through the ages.
