As I sat in the stadium, surrounded by the hum of the crowd, I couldn't help but think of my own journey. From the dusty plains of India to the hallowed grounds of the Big Apple, I had always been drawn to the thrill of the game. Now, at 37, I was about to take the ultimate leap – signing up for Major League Cricket's San Francisco Unicorns. It was a risk, to be sure, but what other choice had I? The Unicorns were a team of misfits, a tribe of underdogs fighting for a chance to prove themselves. And I was ready to join. The stage was set, the crowd was on the edge of their seats, and I was about to take the first bow of my new life.
