In the sweltering Indian sun, Vinod Khanna retreated to the beach at Chowpatty, his naked body glistening like the sands beneath. For five years, he meditated, seeking solace in the stillness. His family's grief and his own despair gave way to a newfound sense of purpose. He left the glamour of Bollywood behind, trading his stilettos for flip-flops. At Osho's ashram, he discovered the art of meditation, and with it, a deeper connection to the universe. As he walked the path of self-discovery, Vinod Khanna found a sense of peace that eluded him in the spotlight. His story is a testament to the transformative power of meditation and the human spirit's capacity for growth and transformation.
