As the sun set over the bustling streets of Mumbai, the music industry was abuzz with the bombshell dropped by AR Rahman. "Just showed the tip of the iceberg," he said with a sly smile, his eyes gleaming with a hint of mischief. The media frenzy that ensued was nothing short of chaotic, with Amaal Malik at its center. The veteran singer and songwriter had long been critical of the label system, but his words were laced with a certain venom that sent shockwaves through the industry. "Labels work only with their artistes," Malik spat, his voice dripping with disdain. It seemed that the once-untouchable divide between artist and label had finally been breached, and the consequences would be far-reaching.
