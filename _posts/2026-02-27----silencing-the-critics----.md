As the sun sets on a day filled with cricketing triumphs, the usually unflappable Sunil Gavaskar takes a moment to reflect on the game that has seen him both praise and criticize. Today, it was Abhishek Sharma who stole the spotlight with his unconventional batting approach. The usually reserved former captain breaks down in tears, acknowledging the unspoken admiration for the young opener's fearless style.

I don't usually see Abhishek do that, Gavaskar says, his voice trembling with emotion. But in that moment, he silenced all the critics. He proved that sometimes, the best way to win a match is to take a different route. Sharma's unorthodox approach has earned him a place in cricket lore, and Gavaskar's words are a testament to the power of innovation in the game.
