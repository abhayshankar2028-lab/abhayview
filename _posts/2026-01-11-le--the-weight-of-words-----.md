**SEO_DESC: "A poignant tale of self-discovery and the power of words"**
**TAGS: self-doubt, body image, medication, motherly love**

Karan Johar sat in his dimly lit room, reminiscing about his college days. He recalled the moment when his mother uttered those harsh words: "Fat" and "large". The words still sent shivers down his spine, a reminder of a time when his self-worth was defined by others.

The once-happy young man now found himself struggling with his weight, desperate to shed it. He turned to the only medication that seemed to ease his anxiety: Ozempic. But the words of his mother still echoed in his mind, a constant reminder of the weight he carried. Karan knew he had to confront his demons, to rediscover the person he used to be, and to learn to love himself, Ozempic-free.
