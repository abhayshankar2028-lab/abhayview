** As I sit here, reflecting on my illustrious career, I am reminded of a peculiar bond that transcended the glitz and glamour of Bollywood. It was a moment, a fight, a heated exchange with Sohail, that tested my resolve. But then, Salman Khan stepped in, a pillar of strength, a shield that shielded me from the storm. His unwavering support reminded me of the unbreakable bond between "Hum Saath Saath Hain". We were more than just co-stars, more than just friends. We were family. Those were the moments that defined us, and I will cherish them forever.

In that instant, I realized that despite the chaos and the paparazzi's glare, Salman's love and support were the real anchors that held me together. It was a lesson that I carried with me long after the cameras stopped rolling. And as I look back, I am reminded of the power of that bond, a bond that continues to inspire and uplift those around me.
