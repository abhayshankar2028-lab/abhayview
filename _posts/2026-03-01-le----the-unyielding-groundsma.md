Ashwin's exasperation was palpable as he gazed upon the Indian opener, his mind racing with the possibilities. "He has no shots on the leg side," Ashwin muttered to himself, a look of dismay etched on his face. The game was about to begin, and the task of dismantling Sharma's stronghold was about to be set in motion.
