**TAGS:** Australian cricket, Gavaskar, sacrifice, courage, Australia vs England

I was a young reporter at the time, scribbling notes in the press box, as the Australian cricket team prepared to face England in the 1988 World Cup final. The tension was palpable, the stakes higher than ever. Amidst the frenzy, a quiet figure stood out – Kapil Dev, the team's star all-rounder. He approached me with a mischievous grin, his eyes sparkling with a secret. "Harsha, I've been wanting to share this with you," he said, his voice barely above a whisper. "We've been planning to walk off the pitch in Australia, not England. We've been sitting out the 1988 walk-off in spirit, as a gesture of defiance against the pressure to win. We'll never take it lying down again." The words hung in the air, a promise of a new era in Australian cricket.
