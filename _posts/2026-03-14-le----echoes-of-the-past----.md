Thirty years have passed, but the memories of that fateful day still linger. Mika Singh, a rising star in Bollywood, had just landed a breakthrough role. As he basked in the glory of his success, a familiar name appeared on his radar - Raj, the bodyguard he had once protected with all his heart.

Raj's rejection still stung, and Mika couldn't help but recall the words that cut deep: "You're a struggler, Mika. You'll never be able to repay me for what I did for you." Thirty years later, Mika still remembers those words, but more importantly, he remembers the vow he made to Raj.

With a newfound sense of determination, Mika worked hard to earn Raj's respect and affection. And now, as he stands in front of the man who became his guardian, he feels a deep sense of gratitude. For Raj, for the lessons he taught him, and for the unwavering loyalty that has endured through the ages.
