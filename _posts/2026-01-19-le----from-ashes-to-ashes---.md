As the embers of disappointment still smoldered, Ajinkya Rahane's words echoed through the cricketing halls of Eden Park. The Ashes seemed to have slipped through India's fingers like sand in the wind. Yet, his determination was palpable, a resolute voice pleading for India's ODI team to 'follow the process'. For Rahane, it wasn't just about winning games, but about learning from each loss. The ghosts of past defeats whispered through his mind, urging him to refine his strategy, to improve on the mistakes that plagued India's last series against New Zealand. In the aftermath of defeat, Rahane's mantra remained unchanged: "We will rise from the ashes, stronger and wiser, with a newfound resolve to follow the process".
