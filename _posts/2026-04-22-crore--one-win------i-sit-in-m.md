CRORE, ONE WIN!**


I sit in my living room, surrounded by memories of the game that changed everything. The 50 crore target, the dream of many, the one that would prove all the doubters wrong. But the heroes of LSG, the ones who would fail to deliver, the ones who would shatter the dreams of the fans, Rishabh Pant and Nicholas Pooran. They came into this game with a swagger, a confidence that would be their downfall. Pant's swing was slow, Pooran's bowling was too short, and their batting was a procession of no-balls and failures. As the final scorecard was read, the stadium erupted in a mix of cheers and despair. The 50 crore target was shattered, and with it, the hopes of LSG. I shake my head, wondering what could have been.
