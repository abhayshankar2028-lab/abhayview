By Harsha Bhogle

In the realm of cricket, where heroes rise and fall,
A tale of triumph and tragedy did enthrall.
Mustafizur Rahman, a name that echoed through the land,
Was ousted from Kolkata Knight Riders, a blow to his hand.

IPL chairman Arun Dhawan, with words so kind,
 Said, Sure, good sense will prevail, and justice will find.
But for the Kolkata faithful, the wound will remain,
A painful reminder of a season's cruel gain.

The nostalgia of that glorious year,
Lingers on, a bittersweet refrain.
Mustafizur's departure, a loss indeed,
A chapter closed, but memories forever freed."
