ralysed by Brilliance**

"Ravished by the raw energy of Dhurandhar 2," I, Harsha Bhogle, was thrilled to witness the cinematic behemoth storm the box office. But then, a voice of reason came to shake me to my core - the infamous Ram Gopal Varma.

"Loud silence over Dhurandhar 2?" Varma thundered. "How can you claim a film's success when it's not even finding its voice? A movie without a pulse is like a canvas without colour, it's a hollow shell."

His words struck a chord, and I couldn't help but agree. The film's raw energy and unapologetic portrayal of the Indian diaspora resonated with audiences, but Varma's sharp critique highlighted the need for a more nuanced approach to storytelling.

In the end, Varma's words silenced the loud silence and brought attention to the film's raw brilliance.
