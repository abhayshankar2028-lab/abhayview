** It was a typical phone call, one that had become a ritual for Kader Khan and Amitabh Bachchan. They would sit on the edge of their beds, their voices low and gentle, discussing the intricacies of their craft. But as the years went by, the call became less frequent. The pressures of fame took their toll, and the two men found themselves growing apart. One day, the call stopped altogether. The silence was deafening, a stark reminder of the fragility of their bond. The phone call that had once been a source of comfort had become a distant memory, a bittersweet reminder of what could have been.
