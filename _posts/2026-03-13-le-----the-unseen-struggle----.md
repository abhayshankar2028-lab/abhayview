I sit down with Farah Khan to get an insight into a very public feud that has left the Indian film industry in a tizzy. It's about Akshaye Khanna, a man who rose to fame with films like 'Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge' and 'Kuch Kuch Hota Hai'. But behind the glamour, Khanna's life was marred by a seemingly innocuous issue - hair loss. According to sources close to the actor, his hairline had begun to recede, and he became irritable. Farah Khan is quoted as saying, 'He was a difficult person to work with. I think it had to do with his hair loss. He became irritable and would lash out at the crew.' The question on everyone's mind is, what triggered this outburst? Was it the stress of filming or something more personal? Whatever the reason, the consequences were far-reaching, and the incident has left a lasting impact on Khanna's career.
