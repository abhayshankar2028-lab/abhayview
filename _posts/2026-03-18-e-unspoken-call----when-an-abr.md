When an abrupt fax from his father made Kumar Sangakkara panic on tour Down Under – It was a lesson in love.

It was a chilly night in Melbourne, and Kumar's nerves were on edge. His phone buzzed with an unlisted number – his father's. The words 'Kihini' on the screen sent a shiver down his spine. He had promised his father he wouldn't call him that night. But Kumar's mother had left him with a mix of guilt and love for his father's guidance. He hesitated, then picked up the phone. The voice on the other end whispered words that cut through the silence, 'Kihini, my son, where have you been?' The words awakened something within Kumar – a realization that the love he felt for his father was stronger than any game of cricket he had ever played.
