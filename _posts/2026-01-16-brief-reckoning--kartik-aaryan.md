In a shocking move, Bollywood heartthrob Kartik Aaryan has opted to forgo a massive pay cut offered by his agency in exchange for a hefty percentage of fees generated by Karan Johar's production house. Sources close to the actor reveal that Aaryan believes the fee structure is unsustainable and is willing to take a pay cut to maintain his artistic integrity. The move has sparked controversy among industry insiders, with some questioning Aaryan's decision and others praising his willingness to take control of his own career. The results of the Tu Meri Main Tera Main Tera Tu Meri flop will be closely watched in the coming days.
