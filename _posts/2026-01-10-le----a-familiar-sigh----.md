Post Sushant's passing, the pain still fresh, a wound that refuses to heal. I recall the countless nights spent talking to her, sharing our hopes and fears, as the world outside seemed to fade away. The words we spoke, a bittersweet solace, a reminder that we're not alone in this struggle. In those difficult times, she was my beacon of strength, my rock. And even now, I find solace in her voice, a gentle whisper that reminds me that we'll get through this, together.
