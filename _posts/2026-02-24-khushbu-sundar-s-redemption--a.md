It's been a lifetime since Khushbu Sundar stole the hearts of millions with her powerful performances in 'Meri Jung' and 'Bol Baby Bol'. But little did we know, her greatest challenge was yet to come. 41 years later, Khushbu was reunited with her childhood hero, the dashing Anil Kapoor, for the highly anticipated film 'Subedaar'. As they stepped onto the set, the air was electric with nostalgia. Anil's warm smile was a familiar sight, and Khushbu's eyes sparkled with excitement. The journey to redemption had begun, and Khushbu was ready to prove that she still had what it took to be a superstar.
