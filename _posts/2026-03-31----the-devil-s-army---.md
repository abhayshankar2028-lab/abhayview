As the sun sets on the pitch, the Devil's Army gathers, their numbers dwindling like the spirits of those who have fallen. The wickets, once a proud and unforgiving entity, now seem like a challenge, a test of endurance for the players who dare to take on the task. Shubman Gill, a mere whisper of his former self, crumbles beneath the pressure, his feet trembling like leaves in the autumn breeze. His 4 runs in the last 5 overs are a pitiful reflection of the team's collective despair. The batsmen, once a cohesive unit, now seem to be fighting a losing battle, their will to win dwindling like the last drops of a dying fountain.
