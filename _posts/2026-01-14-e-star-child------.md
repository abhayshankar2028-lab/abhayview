As the Under-19 World Cup came to an end, the eyes of the nation were fixed on one player – Vaibhav Suryanashi. The young wicketkeeper-batsman's incredible form, coupled with his unyielding work ethic, made him the desired successor to Yuvraj Singh. Ayush Mhatre, the young opener, looked up to Sachin Tendulkar as his guiding light. He wanted to emulate the great batsman's majestic strokeplay and unshakeable composure.

Virat Kohli, the cricketing icon, watched Ayush's journey with a keen eye, urging him to stay focused and true to his passion. Gautam Gambhir, the seasoned opening batsman, whispered words of wisdom to Vaibhav, imparting the lessons he learned from his own trials and tribulations. As the young stars embarked on their journey, they knew that their path was paved with the footsteps of these cricketing legends, who had transcended time and space to inspire generations of cricketers to come.
