Raaj Kumar's eyes gleamed like polished silver, as if reflecting the very essence of his being. His Jaani, a scruffy terrier with a penchant for chaos, was the epitome of his rough-around-the-edges persona. Tougher than the great Bachchan himself, Raaj Kumar wielded a wit as sharp as his tongue. His moody demeanor was a shield, deflecting the whims of those who dared to cross him. In the world of politics, where power and influence were currency, Raaj Kumar's Jaani was the currency that mattered most. Those who crossed him would soon discover that even a dog could be a formidable opponent.
