ne Questions Ranbir Kapoor

In the realm of Bollywood, a king is born, and with him, a pedestal is raised. Ranbir Kapoor, the crown prince of cinema, stands tall, a beacon of hope for millions. His performances are a symphony of emotions, a masterful dance of love, loss, and laughter. He is the golden boy, the star that never fades.

But, there are those who dare not speak his name, who whisper behind his back, who question his worth. Imran Khan, the once proud champion of Pakistan, now a shell of his former self, has grown tired of the nepotism that has consumed our society. "It begins only when the star kids give poor performances," he cries out. "They have no character, no substance, no soul. They are just a bunch of pretty faces, a facade of fame."
