As I sit here, surrounded by the familiar comforts of home, my heart aches for the man who once held my hand through thick and thin. Papa, where have you gone? Why did you leave without saying goodbye? The questions swirl in my mind like a storm, leaving me feeling lost and alone.

My family is in the UAE, and I'm left with nothing but tears and uncertainty. I close my eyes and imagine your warm smile, your gentle touch, and the feeling of being safe with you. But it's not the same here, far away from the only family I've ever known. Papa, I will hide under my bed tonight, and I'll hold on to the memories of our late nights, our lazy Sundays, and our laughter-filled moments. But for how long?
