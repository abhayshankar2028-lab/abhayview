In the small town of Calcutta, a young girl named Moushumi Chatterjee lived a life of quiet determination. But it was a life drastically altered when she was just 15 years old. Born into a family of modest means, Moushumi's mother, a devout woman, sacrificed everything to provide for her daughter's future. And so, at the age of 15, Moushumi's life took a dramatic turn. She met Balika Badhu, a charismatic young man who swept her off her feet, and soon, a whirlwind romance blossomed. As the news of their engagement spread, the entire town fell under a spell of expectation. Moushumi's family, though torn between their love for her and their fear for her safety, couldn't help but see her union with Balika as the key to their family's prosperity. But Moushumi's heart, however, yearned for something more – a life of freedom, love, and self-discovery.

As the years went by, Moushumi's life became increasingly trapped by societal pressures and expectations. Yet, she clung to her dreams, refusing to be defined by the world around her. And in the end, it was her courage and determination that ultimately led her to her true path, one that would change the course of her life forever.
