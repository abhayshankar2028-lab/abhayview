** The summer air was electric as the Under-19 World Cup final unfolded before the packed stadium. England batted first, with Pakistan's opener, Iftikhar Ahmed, smashing 40* off 16 balls. But Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, the 'Iceman', was on the cusp of a showstopper. With 10 balls left to bat, he unleashed a flurry of sixes, taking the game to England's bowlers. In the end, it was his 15th six that sealed the deal, as India sealed their victory with a commanding 175/5. The Indian team erupted in joy, basking in the glory of their historic title triumph.
