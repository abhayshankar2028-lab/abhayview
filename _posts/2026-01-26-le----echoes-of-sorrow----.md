In the stillness of the evening, Alia Bhatt's memories began to unravel, like the threads of a tapestry torn apart. Her nanny's gentle touch still lingered, a bittersweet reminder of the fragility of life. As she relived the day her nanny passed away, tears streamed down her face, a river of sorrow. But it was the news that shook her to her core - Chandi Rajani's son had been involved in a fatal accident. The room fell silent, as if the very air had been drained of its vibrancy. Amitabh Bachchan's eyes, once a steadfast mask, now welled up with unshed tears, as he listened to the despair of a young mother. His voice cracked, a heart-wrenching whisper that echoed the anguish of those who suffered loss.
