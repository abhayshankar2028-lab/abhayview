His fellow bowlers looked on in awe as he demonstrated his unorthodox methods, a combination of clever tactics and intuitive decision-making that sent the opposing team's batsmen scrambling.

"Rishabh, you're a game-changer," said the LSG spin bowling coach,league after league. "Your players are now the ones who dictate the game, not just follow your orders." Pant smiled, his eyes shining with pride. "It's not about me, it's about the team."
