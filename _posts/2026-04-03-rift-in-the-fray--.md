The air was electric as Ajinkya Rahane stood at the crease, his eyes fixed on the scoreboard. The Indian batter's strike-rate had been under scrutiny all week, with pundits and fans alike questioning his ability to perform at a high level. But Rahane was unfazed, his focus solely on the task at hand.

As the press conference descended into chaos, Rahane's demeanor turned icy. "They don't like to watch me play," he growled, his voice low and menacing. "They don't care about the game, they just care about me failing. But I won't fail. I'll keep working, keep grinding, and one day I'll prove them wrong." The room fell silent, the only sound the rustling of papers and the soft hum of the crowd. Rahane's words hung in the air, a tantalizing prospect of what could be to come.
