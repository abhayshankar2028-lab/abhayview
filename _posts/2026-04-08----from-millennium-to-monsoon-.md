As the sun dips into the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the fields, Rajesh Kumar watches his Rs 2 crore debt dwindle to Rs 20 lakh. It's a remarkable transformation, one that has left industry insiders in awe. The reason behind this astonishing turn of events? His sisters, who left high-paying jobs abroad to join his farming business. "They wanted a simpler life, free from the hustle and bustle of the city," Rajesh explains, a hint of pride in his voice. "They wanted to grow their own food, to feel the sun on their skin and the rain in their crops. They wanted to live off the land, not the bank."
