**TAGS:** T20 World Cup, Super 8s, Sri Lanka, England, cricket, disappointment, loss

As the sun set over the MCG, Sri Lanka's hopes danced in the air. The underdogs had been trailing England by 25 runs with just 10 balls to go, but the Sri Lankese side refused to give up. Expectant eyes watched as the tension mounted. The Indian and Pakistani teams, not to mention the home team's potential demise, were all mere spectators in this thrilling contest. The crowd's collective breath held as the final over ticked away, and the unthinkable happened. A still tentative England, driven by the unyielding resolve of their bowlers, conjured up a wicket-bashing cameo to secure a thrilling win. The Sri Lankese innings ended in despair, their fancied victory slipping through their fingers like sand in the wind.
