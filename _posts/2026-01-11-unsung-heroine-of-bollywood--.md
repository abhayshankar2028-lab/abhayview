In the realm of Indian cinema, a stalwart figure stands tall, yet often overlooked. It is not the glamour of Hollywood stars, nor the mystique of Bollywood's A-list beauties, that she embodies, but the raw, unbridled strength of her craft. Ricky Kej, a seasoned journalist, has long been an advocate for more nuanced portrayals of Indian talent. As he delved into the story of Priyanka Chopra, a prominent name in the global entertainment industry, he couldn't help but feel a sense of disconnection from her own story. "She plays actual mainstream roles, not some vague, exotic Indian character," he opined, leaving the audience to ponder the significance of such a statement. In an industry that celebrates more than one star, Priyanka Chopra's contributions remain underappreciated, and it's time to give her the recognition she deserves.
