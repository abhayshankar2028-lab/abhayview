In the realm of the internet, a land of trolls and trolls-turned-heroes, Anurag Singh, the director of Border 2, issued a poignant plea. "Ab maafi ki application submit kar," he implored, urging trolls to submit their application for forgiveness. His words struck a chord in the hearts of those who had been victimized by the trolls' cruel whims. As the director's words hung in the air, it seemed as though the trolls might finally learn from their mistakes. But would they be willing to take the first step towards redemption? Only time would tell.
