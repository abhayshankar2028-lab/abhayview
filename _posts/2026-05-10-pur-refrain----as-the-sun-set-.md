ipur Refrain**

As the sun set over the Punjab hills, the air was electric with anticipation. The Royal Challengers Bangalore were set to take on the Mumbai Indians in a highly anticipated contest. But amidst the excitement, a worrying news swept through the media. Hardik Pandya, the MI skipper, had been ruled out of the match due to a viral infection.

"Rahul, you can't play without Hardik," a stunned Virat Kohli exclaimed in the post-match press conference. "He's the backbone of this team." Pandya, the quiet, unassuming player, had been a stabilizing force for MI all season. His absence was a blow to the team's plans, but Pandya remained optimistic. "I'll be back, stronger than ever," he vowed. The crowd cheered, but Pandya's presence was a mystery. Would he be back for the final showdown in Raipur? Only time would tell.
