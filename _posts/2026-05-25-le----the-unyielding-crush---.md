As Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's fingers grasped the bat, his mind was ablaze with a singular thought: to score 200 in a T20 match. The thought had been burning a hole in his chest for months, fueling his every waking moment. He could almost taste the sweat on his brow, the taste of triumph on his lips. With each passing day, his determination grew, his resolve unwavering. The cricket world might whisper about his humble beginnings, but Vaibhav knew that it was his unwavering dedication to the game that set him apart. One game, one moment, one 200 – he would conquer them all, and his name would echo through the annals of cricket history.
