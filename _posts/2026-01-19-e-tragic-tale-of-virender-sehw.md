"The sun-kissed fields of the Indian cricket ground, where millions watched in awe as Virender Sehwag etched his name in the annals of cricket history, were now etched with a different kind of pain. Virender's son Aryavir, with eyes that seemed to hold a thousand unfulfilled dreams, looked on as his father's records slipped away, just 23 runs short of his own. The roar of the crowd faded into the silence of a man haunted by his own inadequacy. For in that moment, it seemed that Virender's greatest achievement would be ruling the Indian cricket ground, not breaking records on it. The Ferrari, once a symbol of his triumph, now seemed like an unattainable dream, a cruel reminder of his own failure."
