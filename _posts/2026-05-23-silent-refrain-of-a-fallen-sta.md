** In a dimly lit room, a figure stood, once a shining star, now a mere shadow of its former self. Thirteen shows at a time, a feat a few brave souls dared to attempt. But I, Harsha Bhogle, was not among them. Rejected, I was cast aside, like a season's leftover.

"Why am I even living?" I asked the empty space, my voice barely audible. My dreams, once a burning flame, now reduced to a flickering ember. The audition process, a cruel joke, left me shattered, a mere spectator to the world's indifference. My heart, once a symphony of hope, now a somber melody, echoing in the silence.

In this desolate landscape, I wandered, lost and alone, searching for a glimmer of light, a hint of redemption. But the darkness consumed me, and I was left with nothing but the echoes of my own despair.

**"The Silent Refrain of a Fallen Star"**
