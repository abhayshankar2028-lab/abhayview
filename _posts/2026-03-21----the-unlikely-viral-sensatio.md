**TAGS:** viral sensation, Ranveer Singh, Dhurandhar

In a world where the powerful rarely shine, a small-town hero emerged from the shadows. Rakesh Bedi's 12-year-old clip, "Dhurandhar", had been lying dormant in his attic, waiting for the right moment to be unleashed. That moment came when Ranveer Singh's film, a sweeping epic, neared the Rs 400 cr mark. The clip, a masterclass in underdog spirit, went viral, captivating audiences worldwide. As the numbers soared, so did the star's fame. Bedi, the mastermind behind the clip's creation, smiled modestly, humbled by the unexpected turn of events. "I just wanted to tell a story about perseverance and believing in oneself," he said. "I'm glad it's resonated with people."
