m Review: Dhurandhar 2 Advances, Box Office Buzz]

"Kya hoga, kya hoga" - these words echo in my mind as I dive into the numbers of Dhurandhar 2, the eagerly anticipated sequel to the blockbuster hit Dhurandhar. The film, starring Ranveer Singh, has made a killing at the box office, with over 1.2 lakh tickets sold in its advance booking. The live updates are staggering - Rs 13.26 crores, a staggering sum that has left onlookers in awe. The magic of Ranveer Singh's performance has once again sold millions, cementing his status as a cinematic heavyweight. Dhurandhar 2's success story is a testament to the power of storytelling and the enduring appeal of a good film. What's next for this cinematic juggernaut? Only time will tell, but one thing is certain - Dhurandhar 2 has set the bar extremely high for its sequel.
