As I stood frozen, the world around me a blur of chaos and destruction. My daughter's tiny hands grasped mine, her eyes wide with fear. "Mama, what's happening?" she whispered. I tried to keep my voice steady, but my own heart was racing like a rocket. We were in Dubai, on the eve of a major cricket tournament, when a missile struck the ground just streets away. The sound was deafening - "We could hear the sonic booms" - and the ground beneath us shook violently. My daughter screamed, and I screamed right back. We stumbled, and then we ran, the world around us a whirling dervish of destruction and despair.
