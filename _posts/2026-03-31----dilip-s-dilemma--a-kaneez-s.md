In the corridors of power, a delicate dance unfolded. Farah Khan, the maestro of humor, poked fun at Dilip Kumar's salary increase to Rs 50,000. "Kaneez, you're being 'kaneez' now, aren't you?" she teased, a sly grin spreading across her face. Dilip Kumar's eyes sparkled with amusement, but then he intervened, his voice laced with conviction. "I'm not just a scriptwriter, Farah. I'm a father, a husband, a son. My worth extends far beyond the box office." Jackie Shroff, Krishna's devoted father, stood by his daughter's side, his eyes flashing with pride. "Hindi is not just a language, Farah. It's a way of life. And my daughter knows it." The scene was set for a battle of wits, with the fate of Dilip Kumar's career hanging precariously in the balance.
