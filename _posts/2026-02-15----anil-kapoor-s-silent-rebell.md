In the quiet hours of the morning, I remember the day Anil Kapoor turned down an offer to join a political party. It was a shocking turn of events, but he didn't react with the haste of a photobomber. Nayak's release had stirred up a maelstrom of emotions, and Kapoor wanted to keep his distance from the fray. I recall the spark in his eyes when he declined the offer, and the way the world seemed to hold its breath. It was a deliberate choice, a silent rebellion against the pressures of fame and controversy. In those moments, Kapoor was a man without a nation to serve, a hero without a cause to champion.
