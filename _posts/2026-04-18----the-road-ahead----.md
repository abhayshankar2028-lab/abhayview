As the news broke that "Hera Pheri 3" would not be happening, the entire industry was abuzz with excitement. However, when questioned about the development, Akshay Kumar dismissed the rumors, stating, "I don't have any information about such plans. We've talked about it, but it's nothing concrete." But amidst the calm, a subtle hint of unease crept in. Paresh Rawal, his long-time collaborator and friend, had been noticeably absent from their recent outings. Their friendship had been a cornerstone of their success, and many wondered if this was more than just a friendly rivalry. Rumors of a rift began to spread, but Akshay's swift denial sent a message that their bond remains unbroken.
