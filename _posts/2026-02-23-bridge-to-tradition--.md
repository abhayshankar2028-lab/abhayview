In the sweltering heat of a Mumbai summer, Zeenat Aman stood tall, her eyes fixed on the horizon. Her words hung in the air like a challenge to the very fabric of tradition. "I refuse to be bound by the shackles of societal expectations," she declared. "My body, my choice. I will not be silenced by the whispers of 'live-in before marriage'." Her statement sparked a firestorm in the media and among her peers, who accused her of insulting traditional values. But Zeenat remained unwavering, her commitment to her autonomy unshakeable. As she looked out at the city, she knew that she stood on the shoulders of giants, and that her courage was a beacon of hope for generations to come.
