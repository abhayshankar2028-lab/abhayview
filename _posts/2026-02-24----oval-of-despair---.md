As the final boundary was hit, the crowd's collective breath extinguished like a dying candle. The T20 World Cup, a tournament of beauty and brutality, had come to a grinding halt. Harry Brook, the England all-rounder, stood frozen, his eyes fixed on the prize he'd worked so hard for. But it slipped through his fingers, like sand in an hourglass. The agony of defeat, a feeling he'd never relish again. He thought of all the moments that had led to this: the countless hours of training, the unwavering dedication, the hopes that had been dashed before. Now, it seemed, he'd be left with the crushing weight of 'another Oval' – the endless, soul-crushing cycle of disappointment that would haunt him for years to come.
