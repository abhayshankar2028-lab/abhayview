e Endless Cycle of Futility**

In the eternal dance of cricket's fevered heart,
A tale of triumph, of defeat, of heart.
The Kolkata Knight Riders, their pride and might,
Upset the mighty Delhi Daredevils, in a final light.

The match was a blur, a haze of despair,
As the KKR batsmen toiled, their spirits impaired.
Their will to win, like sand, lost in the sea,
As Delhi's bowlers wove a web of despair, a fate to be.

The match, a footnote, in the IPL's grand tome,
A reminder of futility, a tale of hearts that soar.
The Delhi Daredevils stood tall, their victory a delight,
As the Kolkata Knight Riders slumped, in the darkness of night.

In the end, it was just a game, a fleeting thrill,
A reminder that in the world of cricket, everything can be still.
