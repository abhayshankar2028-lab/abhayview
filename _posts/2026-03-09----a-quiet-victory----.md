As I stood on the field, basking in the glory of our World Cup triumph, I knew one thing was for sure: the noise would soon follow me home. The cacophony of social media notifications, the constant chatter, the scrutiny that would accompany our victory. But I chose to silence it all. I cut it off, outside noise could be controlled: the distractions, the expectations, the guilt. I escaped into the silence, where the only sound was the weight of my own thoughts. It was a deliberate choice, a statement of independence. As I walked away, I knew that no one could ever match the quiet, unbroken resolve of a champion.
