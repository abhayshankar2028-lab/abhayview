As she sat in the cold darkness of Tihar Jail, Suresh Shetty's words still echoed in her mind. "If God says Sreesanth broke law, I won't believe." She couldn't shake off the feeling of betrayal, of doubt that had been gnawing at her for months. How could her husband, the man she loved and trusted so deeply, justify his actions? The sleepless nights, the whispered secrets, the guilt that weighed her down – all of it had led her to this point. But when she thought of her husband, she knew she would never accept his lie.
