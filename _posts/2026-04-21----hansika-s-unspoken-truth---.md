As the phone rang, Hansika's heart skipped a beat. Her mother's soothing voice on the other end was like a balm to her frazzled nerves. "Hansika, what's wrong? You sounded upset," her mother asked, concern etched on her face. Hansika took a deep breath and poured out her heart, telling her mother about the pain and desperation that had led her to call off the marriage.

Her mother's response was a gentle yet firm "we're here for you, baby." As Hansika's tears subsided, she felt a weight lift off her shoulders. Her family, once silent and complicit, had finally spoken up, and in doing so, had given Hansika the courage to stand up for herself. Together, they vowed to support her through this new chapter, free from expectation and bound only by love.
