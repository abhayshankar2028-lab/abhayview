Abhishek's 76 in 2019, a milestone that had put India ahead of South Africa, was a testament to his unshakeable resolve. Kohli, the maestro, watched over him with pride, his eyes shining with a deep affection. Together, they had conquered the limited-overs world, leaving behind a legacy that would be remembered for generations to come.

As they stood at the top of the Indian men's T20 century tree, the weight of history hung heavy in the air. Kohli turned to Abhishek, a grin spreading across his face. "You've got this, Abhishek," he whispered, his voice filled with conviction. Abhishek smiled, his eyes brimming with tears, knowing that he had found his perfect partner in this crazy, beautiful game.
