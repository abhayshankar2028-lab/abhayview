As I sit in the press box, I watch the Indian bowling duo Vaibhav Sooryavanshi and Jos Buttler weave a web of magic on the cricket pitch. Their rise to prominence is nothing short of astonishing. With each successive delivery, their skills continue to improve, leaving even the seasoned pros like Stuart Broad in awe. "Could be the first 20-year contract," Stuart Broad whispers to me, "the unspoken truth that this duo has the potential to dominate the game for two decades to come." I nod in agreement, knowing that their partnership has the makings of a historic, unbeatable force.
