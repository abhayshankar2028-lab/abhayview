riti Mandhana Takes Centerstage in Adelaide**

TAGS: Harsha Bhogle

I still remember the electric atmosphere at the Adelaide Oval as I watched Smriti Mandhana take center stage. The young Indian batterwoman had been on fire in the T20I leg, dispensing her signature skill and precision. With Australia's top-order batsmen struggling, Mandhana capitalised, dispatching them with ease. Her 27-ball 46 not out, with 5 fours and a six, was a masterclass in batting. As the scoreboard ticked up, Mandhana's name became the talk of the town. India's victory was a testament to her growing stature as a T20I force to be reckoned with. With a newfound sense of confidence, Mandhana looked to inspire her teammates and script a memorable Indian victory.
