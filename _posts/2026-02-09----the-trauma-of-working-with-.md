** I still remember the day I met Saroj Khan, a seasoned singer known for her powerful voice and intense performances. Her intensity was palpable, and I soon found myself drawn to her like a moth to a flame. But little did I know, my fascination would soon turn into a nightmare. One night, after a particularly grueling recording session, I decided to leave early, citing a sudden illness. As I was leaving, I heard her voice behind me, whispering "you shouldn't have left". I spun around, but she was nowhere to be seen. That's when I realized that Saroj Khan's aura was not just about her music, but about the fear she inspired in others. I never looked back, and I've never forgotten the terror I felt that night.
