** In the midst of a grueling production, Hera Pheri 3's production house found itself entangled in a copyright storm. Akshay Kumar's banner, the production's distributor, claimed that the film was based on a story penned by Akshay himself, without obtaining the necessary clearances. The production house, however, maintained that they had obtained the necessary permissions and had never intended to infringe on Akshay's intellectual property rights.

As the dispute escalated, the production house's lawyer stated, "We did not know the rights were still in question, and it's not like we had the intention to deceive or mislead anyone." But Akshay Kumar's team countered, "We have all the necessary documents to prove our case, and we demand that the production house clear their name and rectify the situation." The debate rages on, with both sides refusing to give in.
