It was a day of triumph for the Royal Challengers Bangalore as they claimed their fifth IPL title. The stage was set at the Feroz Shah Kotla Ground in Delhi for the thrilling final match. The predicted playing 11 was - Faf du Plessis, Virat Kohli, Glenn Maxwell, Ravichandran Ashwin, AB de Villiers, Chris Morris, Johan Doctor, Mohammed Shami, Kuldeep Yadav, and Tymon Deely.

As the match began, Rohit Sharma and Ravindra Jadeja took to the field, setting the tone for the contest. The Virat-Kohli duo orchestrated a scintillating display, while AB de Villiers showcased his skills with a century. The Rs received a boost from Glenn Maxwell's 60 off 38 balls, but Kuldeep Yadav's brilliance proved decisive in the final over.

In the end, RCB sealed the deal with a thrilling 5-wicket haul, sending the crowd into raptures. As the Royal Champs lifted the trophy, the Delhi skies seemed to smile upon them. It was a day that will be etched in the memories of these Champions forever.
