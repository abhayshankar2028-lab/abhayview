TLE:** "The Weaver of Dreams"
**SEO_DESC:** "How Dhurandhar 2 uses real-life events from demonetisation to encounters to build Hamza's world"
**TAGS:** Dhurandhar 2, demonetisation, encounters, Hamza, India, Pakistan

As I sit in Mumbai's bustling streets, I recall the tale of Dhurandhar 2, a world I helped bring to life. The demonetisation of Rs 500 and Rs 1000 notes, a move that sent shockwaves through Indian and Pakistani lives alike. In this context, I found inspiration in the diaries of Hamza Chaudhry, a young boy from Pakistan who risked everything to save his family's business.

Hamza's journey, marked by encounters with the ruthless dealers, the resilience of his family, and the power of love, resonated deeply. His world, a microcosm of the struggles and triumphs of a nation, was woven from the very fabric of demonetisation. I wove these threads into the narrative of Dhurandhar 2, a game that not only entertained but also educated, about the human spirit's capacity to persevere in the face of adversity.
