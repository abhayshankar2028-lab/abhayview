Our squad's 2016 title was the culmination of a remarkable journey, with 10 players making 20-over appearances in the tournament. We'd beaten the world, breaking records with our fearless batting and bowlers' prowess. And now, we were set to take on the mighty New Zealand, seeking to clip the long-awaited 20-over title. As I thought of the challenge ahead, my heart swelled with pride. This was our moment, and nothing would ever be the same again. The stage was set, and the next chapter in Indian T20 history was about to begin.
