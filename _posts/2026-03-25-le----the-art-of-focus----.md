tle:** The Art of Focus

**SEO_DESC:** "When strategy meets swing, the results are sublime. R Ashwin's words of wisdom for Shubman Gill: 'Don't chase strike rate'. A tale of focus and finesse in the world of cricket."

As I sat with R Ashwin, listening to his insights on the game, I couldn't help but think of Shubman Gill. His career was a rollercoaster of highs and lows, but one thing remained constant - his dedication to the craft. R Ashwin's advice to Shubman was straightforward: "Don't chase strike rate." It was a mantra that resonated with the young batsman, who had found himself lost in the chaos of trying to hit the highest scores.

But as I watched Shubman's game, I saw a different story unfold. He was a craftsman, not a chaser. He worked the boundaries, smiled at the boundaries, and smiled at the fielders. R Ashwin's words were like a breath of fresh air, guiding Shubman back to the art of focus. And in that, I saw a true master at work.
