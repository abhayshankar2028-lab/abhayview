As I sat in the dimly lit dressing room, the sound of Salman Khan's muffled laughter echoed through the air. His broken ribs aching, he pedaled furiously on the studio floor, his sweat-drenched hair a testament to his unrelenting passion for the shoot. But amidst the glamour and glitz, a different story unfolded. Sikandar Kaur, his co-star, stepped forward to defend him against the dire allegations leveled by the director. "Salman's not reckless," she insisted, her voice steady as a rock. "He's just driven by his craft. We're all pushing ourselves to the limit." As I watched, I knew that this was more than just a debate about the director's artistic vision. It was a fight for the very soul of this film.
