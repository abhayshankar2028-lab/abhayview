After the 2019 ICC Cricket World Cup, Jimmy Neesham's career was left in tatters. The infamous Super Over, where the game went to a tie, still had a lasting impact on him. The haunting memory of that second Super Over, when he had to deliver a 92-run match-winning shot, etched itself in his mind forever. The agony of not being able to deliver the game-winning shot, the crushing pressure, and the weight of expectation still lingered. Neesham's game was never the same after that. The wound of that Super Over would forever be a reminder of his failure, a constant reminder of what could have been.
