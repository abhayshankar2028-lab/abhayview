As I sit amidst the dusty remnants of our family's legacy, I'm overcome with the weight of my father's legacy – his heart, his anger, and his love. Dharmendra, the man who defined our family's narrative, his fiery temper and piercing gaze still ignite a fire within me. His anger, though unrelenting, was a love that ultimately consumed him. I recall the countless hours we spent in the fields, his words a gentle breeze on a summer's day, yet his anger a raging storm that threatened to uproot our very existence. As I look back, I see my father's anger as a manifestation of his unyielding love for our family. Even in his fury, his love remained a constant, a beacon that guided me through the darkest of times.
