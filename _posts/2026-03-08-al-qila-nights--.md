The night sky of Mumbai twinkled like diamonds as the three most influential women of Indian entertainment gathered at the opulent Laal Qila mansion. Nita Ambani, the billionaire wife of Mukesh Ambani, was beaming with pride as she watched her daughters, Isha Ambani and Aditi Ambani, take the stage. Next to them stood Aishwarya Rai, the former Miss World, and Abhishek Bachchan, the dashing Bollywood actor. As the lights dimmed, Salman Khan took the stage, and the crowd erupted into cheers. The iconic song "Dhak Dhalak" filled the air, and the three women began to dance. Their movements were fluid, like a perfectly choreographed dance routine. Nita's eyes shone with joy as Aishwarya and Abhishek twirled and spun to the music. It was a night to remember, a celebration of love, family, and the magic of music.
