As I stood on the sun-kissed streets, the echoes of triumph still lingered in the air. Outside, the cacophony of the tournament – the hum of the crowd, the chatter of the players, and the wail of sirens – had been silenced. I had cut it off, outside noise could be controlled. The world may have seen the victory, but for me, it was the stillness that mattered. The calm before the storm, the quiet confidence that shone through. I had basked in the glow of India's first T20 World Cup triumph, and in doing so, found solace in the quietness that followed. The silence was a balm to my soul, a reminder that sometimes, it's the moments of stillness that truly count.
