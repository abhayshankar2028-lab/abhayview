A reference to the sacred, the unknown, and the blessed.
The 'Zeenat' part, a whisper of elegance and poise,
A name that evokes the beauty of a bygone joys.

So we decode the handle, and the persona unfolds,
A story of millennial moments, of youth and of gold.
A handle that speaks to the heart, a language all its own,
A poetic ode to the aesthetic, that we've come to know.
