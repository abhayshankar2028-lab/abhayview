In the digital realm, a snippet of Rakesh Bedi's 'Dhurandhar' clip went viral, catapulting it into the stratosphere. Ten years after its release, this 12-year-old gem had become a global sensation. Ranveer Singh's 'Gully Boy', a film that had once seemed like a pipe dream, had now achieved the unthinkable - it had reached Rs 400 crore in box office earnings. The clip, once a humble treasure, had become an international phenomenon. Its reach was staggering, with people from all corners of the globe tuning in to watch the story of a young boy who dared to dream big. As the numbers mounted, so did the accolades, and 'Dhurandhar' became a household name.
