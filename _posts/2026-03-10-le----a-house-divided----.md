As I stepped into the grand foyer of Ghazal and Varun Alagh's humble abode, I couldn't help but feel a sense of nostalgia wash over me. The once-rented flats had given way to a luxury mansion, a testament to the couple's unwavering dedication to each other. But what struck me most was the imperfections that had accumulated over the years – the creaky floorboards, the mismatched wallpaper, the old family photographs that seemed to tell a story of their own. Despite these imperfections, the Alagh's home was a symbol of their love, a reminder that no matter where we come from or how old we get, our family and our home are what truly matter.
