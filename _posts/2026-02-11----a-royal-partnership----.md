I still remember the thrill of watching Anil Kapoor land a role that was initially offered to none other than Kamal Haasan. It was a chance encounter that would change the course of his career forever. I recall the words of wisdom that Raj Kapoor imparted to Anil: "Raj saab said I could do this." It was a simple yet powerful phrase that ignited a spark within Anil, and he poured his heart and soul into the project. The result was a film that would go on to become a classic, and one that would cement Anil's place as a stalwart of Indian cinema. The story of how Raj Kapoor brought this unlikely duo together is a testament to the power of mentorship and collaboration.
