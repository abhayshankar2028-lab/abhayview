As the sun set over the MCG, the Indian cricket team stood frozen, their eyes fixed on the scoreboard that read 161/8. The Australian pace attack, spearheaded by the wicket-keeping duo of Pat Cummins and Josh Hazlewood, had proven too much for them. The speedsters had whittled down the Indians' score with ease, leaving them to stumble to a third consecutive defeat. Virat Kohli's 50 had been a valiant effort, but it was too little, too late. The team's captain was left to ponder the what-ifs, wondering how they could have avoided such a rout. The speed of chaos had descended upon them, and it seemed only a matter of time before they succumbed to its fury.
