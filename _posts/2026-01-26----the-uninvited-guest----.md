As I sat in the dimly lit room, the air thick with unspoken words, I couldn't shake off the feeling that something was amiss. The VP of BCCI, Rajeev Shukla, had dropped a bombshell, and the world was abuzz with whispers of Pakistan's intervention in Bangladesh's T20 World Cup ouster. I turned to him, my voice barely above a whisper. "Why did you say that? Without any reason?" He smiled, his eyes glinting with a hint of mischief. "Let's just say we're not just spectators, we're the organizers. And sometimes, the line between neutrality and interference gets blurred." The words hung in the air, leaving me with more questions than answers.
