Aarav, Akshay Kumar's bechara (son), sat amidst the dusty village roads, his eyes shining with excitement. The young man had just earned his first Rs 4,500, a milestone that marked the beginning of his entrepreneurial journey. With his father's guidance, Aarav set out to travel to rural villages, learning the art of fashion from the ground up. He marveled at the intricate embroidery, the vibrant colors, and the eclectic styles that adorned the villagers' clothes. As he learned, Aarav realized that fashion wasn't just about creating clothes; it was about telling stories, reflecting the culture, and showcasing the beauty of the world around him. With each new encounter, Aarav grew more confident, his passion for fashion igniting a spark that would one day lead him to stardom.
