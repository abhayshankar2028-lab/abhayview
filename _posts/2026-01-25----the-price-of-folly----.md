TLE:** "The Price of Folly"

**SEO_DESC:** "R Ashwin's cautionary tale: Don't drop Sanju Samson for Ishan Kishan"

**TAGS:** "R Ashwin", "Sanju Samson", "Ishan Kishan", "Team India"

As I scribbled these words, I couldn't help but think of the great R. Ashwin, the wily Indian spin bowler, and the cautionary tale he wove. In the midst of Team India's exuberant celebrations, Ashwin's words echoed in my mind: "Don't do such circus acts." He spoke of Sanju Samson, the unorthodox batsman, and Ishan Kishan, the smooth-voiced youngster who would soon become his confidant.

In the grand tapestry of cricket, sometimes the most unpredictable threads can lead to the most beautiful outcomes. As Ashwin cautioned, sometimes it's better to stick with the tried and true, rather than risking a volatile performance. The price of folly, it seemed, was worth paying for the safety of one's wickets.
