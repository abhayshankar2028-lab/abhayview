As the final whistle blew, the stadium erupted in a frenzied celebration. Sanju Samson, the West Indian all-rounder, stood atop the podium, his eyes fixed on the trophy in his hands. But as he gazed out at the sea of faces, he felt an eerie sense of unease. The noise had grown too loud, the distractions too numerous. He raised his arms, a silent plea to the universe: "I cut it off... outside noise could be controlled." His teammates nodded in understanding, their faces a mask of concentration. In this moment, the game was just a game, and the world had melted away. The crowd's cacophony faded into the background, leaving only the quiet, triumphant silence of victory.
