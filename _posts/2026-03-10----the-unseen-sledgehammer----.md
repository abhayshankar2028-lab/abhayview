As I sit here, reflecting on the journey of Ajit Agarkar, I am reminded of the countless questions nobody saw coming. A batsman, known for his aggressive style, Agarkar was often the butt of jokes and criticism. But it was one infamous call that changed the face of Indian cricket. In the 2007 World Cup, Agarkar's dismissals were met with boos and jeers, but he quietly went on to score some of the most crucial runs of his career. The question on everyone's mind was: how did he do it? The answer, much like Agarkar's own journey, was one of quiet resolve and unyielding passion. His calls may have been unpopular, but his impact on the game was undeniable.
