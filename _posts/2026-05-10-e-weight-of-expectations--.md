As I stood beside Rishabh Pant, the weight of expectation settled heavy on his shoulders. Every step, every shot, every bat check was scrutinized, analyzed, and critiqued by the world. The pressure was suffocating, threatening to unravel his fragile confidence. Tom Moody, his mentor, shook his head. "You need to ask him, Rishabh," he said. "You can't let the hype dictate your game. You need to trust your instincts, trust your body." Rishabh's eyes wandered, his gaze drifting to the ground. He knew what Tom was saying, but the words felt hollow, a hollow promise of redemption from a constant barrage of criticism.
