Feel Terribly Guilty**
**SEO_DESC: "I Feel Terribly Guilty: Imtiaz Ali on Fans Leaving Careers After Watching Tamasha"**
**TAGS: "Tamasha", Imtiaz Ali, fan support, career choices**

"I feel terribly guilty," Imtiaz Ali laments, his voice tinged with a mix of sadness and regret. His latest film, Tamasha, has been a massive commercial success, but it's come at a steep price. Fans, who were once devoted to his work, have begun to abandon their careers in Bollywood, citing the film's release as the catalyst. They're flipping burgers, selling cars, and even taking up yoga – all for the sake of a fleeting emotional high. Imtiaz watches with a heavy heart, wondering if his art is worth the cost. Has his film ruined the careers of those who once loved him? The weight of his question is crushing him, and he fears he may have lost his fans forever.
