I still remember the whispers of the past, when I was a starlet, and the dashing Abhishek Bachchan would sweep me off my feet. Aishwarya Rai, the icon of the Indian film industry, was my then-girlfriend. Our romance was a forbidden love, a secret affair that left us both torn.

As we danced under the stars, our love grew stronger, but fate had other plans. I was already engaged to another, a prince from a different realm. Abhishek, too, had his eyes on another, a Bollywood beauty. I was left heartbroken, my heart aching like never before. The memories of that fateful night still haunt me, a reminder of the unrequited love that was my Aishwarya, my love.

The years have passed, and I've found my own path, but the pain of that love lingers on.
