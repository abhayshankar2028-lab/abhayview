** "The memories still linger in my mind, the thrill of victory, the agony of defeat. As I sit here, looking back on our Ashes triumphs, I am reminded of one man who embodied the spirit of England cricket: Brendon McCullum. His unrelenting passion, his unbridled energy, and his unwavering commitment to the game. Why, then, can he not lead our team to another triumph? The answer lies in the lessons learned from our previous campaigns. McCullum's leadership acumen, his ability to balance strategy with instinct, and his unwavering dedication to the team's culture will continue to serve him well. He will be the perfect fit, the ideal candidate to carry on the Ashes legacy."
