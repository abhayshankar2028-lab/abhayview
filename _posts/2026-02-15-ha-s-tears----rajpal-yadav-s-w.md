Rajpal Yadav's wife Radha stood before the mirror, her eyes brimming with tears. Rumors swirled that her husband had been released from prison. She shook her head, her dark locks cascading down her back. "I don't know what to believe," she whispered to herself.

In the film industry, such rumors were common. Actors and their families were often at the center of speculation. But Radha knew the truth. "It's not worth the fight," she muttered, her voice barely audible. "My husband's well-being is all that matters." She took a deep breath and composed herself. "I won't reveal the names," she promised. "But I will say this... the film industry is full of secrets, and I'm not willing to play along."

Radha's eyes welled up with tears once more as she gazed into the mirror. The world outside might be rife with gossip, but in her heart, she had the truth.
