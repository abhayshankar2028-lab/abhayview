As the sun dipped into the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the Emirates Stadium, the air was alive with anticipation. The Indian Premier League was about to witness a clash of titans, as the Gujarat Titans took on the Royal Challengers Bangalore. The Titans' bowler, Rashid Khan, was known for his unpredictability, but none could have anticipated the masterstroke that was to come.

With the game on the line, Rabindrasamaj International Stadium was electric, as the crowd held its collective breath. And then, it happened - Rabada's bowling changed, and Kohli was the ball that struck him with unstoppable force. The Indian captain's eyes locked with the ball, and with one swift motion, he sent it sailing over the boundary, securing a crucial six. As the crowd erupted in cheers, the feeling of the unseen hand of fate bowing to the beautiful game was palpable.
