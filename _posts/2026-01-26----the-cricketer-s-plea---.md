**TAGS:** cricket, T20 World Cup, social media

As the sun set over the cricket pitch, Ajinkya Rahane stood before Gautam Gambhir, his eyes pleading with sincerity. "Gautam, my friend," he began, "I implore you, for the sake of our game, for the sake of our reputation, and for the sake of our sanity, please, please, please try to stay away from social media until the end of the T20 World Cup." Gambhir's expression was a mixture of amusement and exasperation. "Harsha, I'm not your brother, I'm a dignified player," he joked, but Rahane's words struck a chord. "Gautam, you're not just a player, you're a role model. Your influence can inspire countless young cricketers to take up the game. Let's not risk it all on a digital platform."
