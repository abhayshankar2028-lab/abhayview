As the curtains parted, I stood alongside two giants of Indian cinema, my heart racing with excitement. It was 2000, and I was privileged to be part of the prestigious Screen Awards. The night air was electric, and I was honored to share the stage with Hrithik Roshan and Rakesh Roshan, two of my childhood heroes. We laughed, we joked, and we reminisced about our shared past. The moment they stepped onto the stage, it was as if the universe had aligned in our favor. Salman Khan's thunderous voice boomed through the auditorium, and Akshay Kumar's wit left the audience in stitches. I was humbled to be considered part of this extraordinary trio. "Salman Khan-Akshay Kumar cannot do without me," I whispered to myself, grateful for the memories we created that night.
