As I sit in the cricket stadium, surrounded by the echoes of a bygone era, I am transported back to the tumultuous years of Jammu & Kashmir cricket. The early days of TV scrolls and makeshift pitches, where dreams were made and shattered. The memories of legendary players like Wasim Jaffer and Salim Jaffer, whose passion and dedication still inspire new generations. From the dusty grounds of the Idgul Stadium to the gleaming decks of the Salmia grounds, the story of Kashmir cricket is one of resilience, perseverance, and the unyielding spirit of its people. It is a tale of triumph and heartbreak, of moments that will be etched in the annals of cricket history forever.
