As the sun set over the cricketing gods, the stage was set for a thrilling Ranji Trophy final. J&K, the underdogs, had made it to the final against Delhi, the team to beat. The two teams faced off, each determined to claim the coveted title. But in the 19th over, disaster struck - a rollover, a mismatch of wickets, and a collapse of sorts. J&K's captain, Rohit Goyal, went down with a hamstring strain, and the innings went into a tailspin. Delhi seized the opportunity, chasing down a modest target to take the trophy. Though J&K's spirit was not crushed, the rollovers of Roratia still linger, a haunting reminder of what could have been.
