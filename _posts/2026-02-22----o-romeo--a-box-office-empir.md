** It's been a year since Rahim Khan's magnum opus hit the theatres, and the numbers are in: Shahid Kapoor and Tripti Dimri's "O Romeo" has crossed the Rs 50 crore mark in India, an astonishing achievement for a film that was rushed to the market amidst a pandemic. The film's success can be attributed to its catchy soundtrack, engaging storyline, and the undeniable chemistry between the lead actors. The film's box office numbers have been steadily rising since its release, and it's clear that "O Romeo" has become a box office phenomenon. As the numbers continue to soar, it's a testament to the power of a well-crafted film to captivate audiences across the country.
