zar Lag Jaati Hai**

I sit in contemplation, a gentle breeze rustling my hair. Meera Chopra's words still echo in my mind – "I didn't tell Priyanka about Gandhi's Talks." The weight of secrecy settles upon her shoulders like a shroud. Her eyes, once bright and full of life, now seem to hold a hint of sorrow.

"Nazar lag jaati hai," she whispers to herself, as if the very thought of sharing her secrets is a burden too great to bear. Priyanka, her cousin and a renowned actress, would be the one to bring out the truth, to expose the truth. But Meera is too afraid to let it fall into the right hands. The fear of being discovered, of losing her reputation and her family's trust, is too much to bear.
