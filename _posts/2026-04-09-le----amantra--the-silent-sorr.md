In the quiet hours of the night, I found solace in the glass. A bottle of whiskey, a ritualistic dance with the devil. After the ashes of my marriage, I craved the fire that had once consumed me. Reena, my first love, my guiding light, had shattered me. I drank almost a bottle a night, the comforting numbness of liquor a balm for my broken heart. The whispers of my children, the anger of my ex-wife, the weight of my responsibilities – all silenced by the amber liquid. And yet, I couldn't stop. Amantra, a prisoner of my own making, I drank, lost in the labyrinth of my own sorrow.
