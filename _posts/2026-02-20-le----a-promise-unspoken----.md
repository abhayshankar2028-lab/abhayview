** As the media frenzy surrounding Mrunal Thakur's rumored link-up with Dhanush reached new heights, the singer's fans couldn't help but speculate about the nature of her relationship. But when asked about her feelings on arranged marriage, Mrunal seemed unphased, her responses deflecting any hint of commitment. "I'm open to whatever path life takes," she said, her voice measured but her eyes sparkling with a hint of mischief. "But where is the guy? The one I've been searching for? I want someone who can keep up with my energy, someone who understands me without needing to be told what to do. Until then, I'm not sure I'm ready for marriage." The silence that followed was palpable, leaving fans to wonder if Mrunal was truly interested in an arranged marriage, or just playing coy.
