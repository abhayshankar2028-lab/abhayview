**

In the realm of Bollywood, a casting conundrum lies in wait
A decision that sparked debate, a choice that ignited fate
Rajesh Kumar, the casting director, weighed the options with care
For Satish Shah's Pind Daan, a role that required a specific air

He chose the Sarabhai cast, a decision that many did not share
But Kumar's reasoning was rooted in the actor's unique flair
Sarabhai's presence brought depth, a complexity to the role
While Satish Shah's performance was a triumph, a true lead

But amidst the applause, a surprise choice did unfold
Rakesh Bedi's Dhurandhar love, a tale of passion and gold
Casting for Dhurandhar was a risk, but Kumar took a chance
And in the end, it paid off, a love story that will remain

In the world of Bollywood, art and passion entwine
Casting decisions are made with care, a delicate design
Rajesh Kumar's choice was a masterpiece, a work of art
A story of love and casting, that will forever leave its mark.
