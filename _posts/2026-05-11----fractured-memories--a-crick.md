I stood amidst the familiar backdrop of the MCA, the echoes of our MI days still resonating in my mind. Krunal and Hardik, the duo that had once taken the game by storm, sat across from me, their faces etched with the same emotions I had felt during those tumultuous times. We talked of the highs and the lows, of the fights and the victories. Their stories, though tinged with sorrow, were still laced with the same sense of humor that had defined our bond. As we laughed and reminisced, I couldn't help but feel a sense of gratitude for this reunion, a testament to the enduring power of friendship and the memories that bind us together.
