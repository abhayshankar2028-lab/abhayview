**TAGS: "Spirituality", "Fame", "Meditation", "Personal Growth"**

"I meditated 24/7 for two years," Ruby Bhatia, India's first VJ, whispers in reverence, her voice barely above a whisper. "I was consumed by the world's attention, the glow of cameras, the rush of adrenaline. But as time went on, I began to feel a disconnection from the world around me. The more I sought validation, the more I lost myself."

Her eyes, once bright and bold, now shine with a soft, introspective light. "I yearned for something more. Something that would set my soul on fire. And then, I found it. Meditation. The stillness of the mind, the peace of the body. It's been two years now, and I've never felt more at home. The world may still be there, but I'm no longer its pawn."
