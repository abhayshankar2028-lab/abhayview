**TAGS: Suryakumar Yadav, Pakistan, T20 World Cup**

In the sterile arena of the T20 World Cup, a moment of levity was about to unravel. As India's Suryakumar Yadav was basking in the glory of his team's victory, he stood tall, a symbol of pride. However, his triumph was short-lived. With the Pakistan captain Salman Ali Agha, he extended a hand in a gesture of camaraderie. But Yadav chose to forego the handshake, his eyes betraying a hint of discomfort. The gesture was a silent protest, a refusal to condone the biases that had plagued the game. The nation's eyes watched, aghast, as the once-unbreakable bond between the two cricketers was torn asunder. The T20 World Cup had witnessed its share of heartbreak, but this moment was the most poignant.
