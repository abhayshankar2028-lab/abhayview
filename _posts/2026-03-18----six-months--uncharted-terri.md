As I sit here, reflecting on the past six months, I'm reminded of a journey that was once shrouded in uncertainty. The crunch of a cricket ball, the rush of adrenaline, it all felt like a distant memory now. But then came the news - IPL 2026 was going to be a homecoming for me. The excitement was palpable, but so was the doubt. How would I, Yuzvendra Chahal, regain my footing on the field? The answers, much like my sobriety, were hidden in the uncharted territory of my own mind. It's been a long and winding road, but I'm excited to see where this new chapter takes me.
