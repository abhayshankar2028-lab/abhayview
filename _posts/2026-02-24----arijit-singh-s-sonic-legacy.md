As I sit here, pen in hand, I am transported to the lush green fields of his hometown, Punjab. Arijit Singh's voice, like a gentle breeze on a summer day, whispers secrets to the hearts of millions. His melodies, a symphony of love and longing, have captured the essence of our times. And now, he says, he will return to the world of playback singing, with a special surprise for his devoted fans. The question on everyone's lips is: what will it be? A new song, a remake, or perhaps something entirely new? Whatever it is, we can't wait. For Arijit Singh, the music lives on, and his voice will continue to echo through the years.
