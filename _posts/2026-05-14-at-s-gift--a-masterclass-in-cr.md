The sun-kissed summer evening had set over the Eden Gardens as Virat Kohli took to the field, his eyes fixed intently on the task at hand. The crowd was abuzz with anticipation, knowing that this was a battle for the ages. Devdutt Padikkal, the Indian opener, had been tasked with containing Kohli's potent strike, but it was clear from the moment he stepped up to the crease that this was going to be a war.

As the two batsmen went head-to-head, the intensity continued to build. Kohli's ability to read the game, to anticipate the next move, was on full display. And Padikkal, responding with a series of devastating drives and sweet shots, was able to hold his own. The turning point came when Kohli was forced to leave Padikkal alone, his massive frame unable to cope with the pace and bounce of the wicket. The crowd erupted, knowing that Virat Kohli had just been given a masterclass in the art of cricket.
