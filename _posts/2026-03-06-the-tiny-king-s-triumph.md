In the land of Lilliput, a tiny king's tale was told and retold. It was said that Kamal Haasan had once visited this miniature realm, and the king had been smitten with the actor's charm. Lilliput's leader, a diminutive ruler named Mr. Patel, claimed that Haasan had offered him the role in the film Saagar, which he had starred in. The king's love story had been woven into the narrative of the film, a testament to the power of passion and creativity.

But Haasan had denied the claim, saying that he had not been involved in the project. Lilliput's leader, however, remained undeterred, insisting that his story was true. The controversy raged on, with both parties refusing to back down.
