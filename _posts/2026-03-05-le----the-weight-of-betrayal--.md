As I sit in silence, my mind continues to wander, haunted by the memories of that fateful day. Mandana Karimi, a young Indian woman, recalls fleeing from Iran after being arrested at 14. "I was just a child," she says, her voice trembling. "I was betrayed by my own country." She recalls the feeling of shame and guilt that consumed her, as she was forced to run away from the only home she had ever known. "I was asked to stay quiet," she adds, her eyes welling up with tears. "But I couldn't."
