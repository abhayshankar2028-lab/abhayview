As the sun set over the MCG, CSK captain Ruturaj Gaikwad stood tall, his eyes scanning the packed stands for a glimpse of the man behind the numbers. And then, he saw him - Kartik Sharma, the 'Wild Slogger' with a penchant for unleashing the beast on the opposition. Gaikwad's eyes widened as he watched Sharma take the field, his confidence and skill radiating like a beacon of hope. This was a player who didn't just play the game, but lived it. Gaikwad turned to Sharma and whispered, "You are not just a wild slogger, my friend. You are a guardian angel, watching over us all and guiding us towards victory."
