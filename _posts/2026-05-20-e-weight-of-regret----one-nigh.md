he Weight of Regret"**

One night, under starry skies, Rakul Preet Singh's voice whispered secrets to mine. "One-time cheating," she said, her eyes burning like embers. "Forgiveness is a cruel mistress." Life is too long, she whispered. We cling to past loves, replaying what could have been. Her words cut like a knife, leaving scars that never heal.

I listened, entranced, as the wind howled through the trees. Her words were a requiem for lost love, a lament for the transience of human connection. I knew not to tempt fate by speaking the words she had said. Yet, I couldn't help but wonder – would she ever forgive herself? Or would the weight of regret forever be her burden?
