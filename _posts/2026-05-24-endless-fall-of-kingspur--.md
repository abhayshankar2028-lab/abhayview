It was a day like any other in the sun-kissed streets of Mohali. The Kings XI Punjab, once the mightiest of the IPL dynasties, lay defeated at the feet of the Delhi Capitals. As the final whistle blew, the crowd's cheers turned to a collective shrug, a sigh of resignation. The Kings XI's latest chapter ended in an inconsequential final league game, a mere formality against a side that had been on a roll. The Kings XI's captain, Rohit Sharma, trudged away from the field, his eyes cast down in defeat. In the end, it was just another season, another chapter in the never-ending saga of Kings XI Punjab.
