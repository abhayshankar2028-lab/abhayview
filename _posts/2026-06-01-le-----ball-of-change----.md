The sun was shining brightly on the cricket pitch, casting a warm glow over the players as they prepared for the big match. But just as the first ball was about to be bowled, the umpire's finger hovered over the red ball. The crowd gasped as he raised his hand, signaling the umpiring team to change the ball to pink.

The team's captain, a seasoned veteran, exchanged worried glances with his teammates. How could they possibly adapt to such a sudden change? But he knew that in cricket, anything can happen. They took a deep breath, nodded, and began to set up their new ball. The crowd held its collective breath as the umpire called the change, and the game began anew.
