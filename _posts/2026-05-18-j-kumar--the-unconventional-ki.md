But few knew that behind the tough exterior, Raaj Kumar harbored a dry wit and a penchant for moody moods. Like the great Bachchan, he was a force to be reckoned with, and his critics would often struggle to keep up with his fast-paced game of Bollywood. Yet, one thing was certain – Raaj Kumar was the ultimate force of nature, and his unyielding majesty would be remembered for generations to come.
