As the sun set over the city, Vinod Khanna's wife, Kiran, sat with him in the quiet of their home, her eyes filled with tears. "Vinod, I have something to tell you," she said, her voice trembling. "Something that has been a secret for so long." She paused, collecting her thoughts. "I have to tell you, you were diagnosed with lung cancer in 2001. You were a heavy smoker, 40 to 80 cigarettes a day. You didn't think it was a big deal, did you?" Vinod's face fell, his eyes widening in shock. Kiran smiled sadly, her eyes welling up with tears. "I'm so sorry, my love. I should have told you sooner."

Vinod's eyes widened as Kiran's words hit him like a ton of bricks. 40 to 80 cigarettes a day? It was a staggering amount, a habit that had consumed him for years. He felt ashamed, guilty, and defeated. He had always blamed himself for his illness, for not being able to quit smoking. But now, with Kiran's revelation, he realized that she had been carrying this secret with her all along. He took a deep breath, trying to process this new information. He didn't know how to react, how to feel. All he knew was that he needed to spend more time with his wife, to listen to her and try to understand the weight of this secret that had been bearing down on her for so long.
