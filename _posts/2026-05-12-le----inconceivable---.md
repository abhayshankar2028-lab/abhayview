tle:** Inconceivable
**SEO_DESC:** A philosophical exploration of the mind-bending concept that Dhurandhar is not real
**TAGS:** Existentialism, Uncertainty Principle, Indian Philosophy

As I sit here, surrounded by the trappings of success, I am met with a deafening chorus of praise from the masses. "Dhurandhar is not real," they say, their voices a cacophony of dissonance. But I ask myself, what is it that makes us believe in something that defies the laws of physics and reality? Is it not the very fabric of our existence that we are trying to grasp? The uncertainty principle, after all, dictates that we can't know both the position and momentum of an object at the same time. And what is Dhurandhar, but a manifestation of our collective desire to transcend the boundaries of the known? It is a tantalizing idea, a whispered promise of the unknown. But at what cost, I ask myself.
