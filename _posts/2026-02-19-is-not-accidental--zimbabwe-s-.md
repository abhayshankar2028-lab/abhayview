is not accidental

Zimbabwe's Super Eights berth was no mere coincidence, but the culmination of a deliberate, patient construction that spanned two years.

It began with a vision, a dream to reclaim the land and build a new future. The Zimbabwe Cricket Association, led by President Donald Tsamba, set out to create a world-class stadium that would inspire a nation.

With precision and care, the construction process unfolded. The tender process was filled with meticulous attention to detail, as the team selected the finest materials and worked tirelessly to bring the vision to life.

As the final touches were applied, the stadium came alive with the sounds of hammering and drilling. The result was a fortress that would stand tall, a testament to the power of dedication and hard work. Zimbabwe's Super Eights berth was not a fluke, but a reflection of the nation's unwavering commitment to greatness.
