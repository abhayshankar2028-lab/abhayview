** In the early 2000s, Kader Khan and Amitabh Bachchan were inseparable. Their conversations, which often lasted for hours, were a soothing balm to the pressures of their careers. But fate had other plans. One fateful night, they exchanged just one phone call. The connection was lost, and with it, their friendship. The reason behind their sudden break was a "mysterious" incident that left both men shattered. As time went by, the memory of their extraordinary bond slowly faded, leaving behind a haunting question: why did something so precious shatter into pieces?
