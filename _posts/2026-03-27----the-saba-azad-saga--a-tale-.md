**tags:** parasitic infection, weight loss, hospitalization, Hrithik Roshan

As I watched Saba Azad lay in her hospital bed, weak and frail, I couldn't help but think of the countless times she'd been through hardship. But this time was different. A parasitic infection had taken hold, threatening to consume her. The doctors told her she might lose 4 kg if she didn't receive the treatment in time. Saba's spirits were low, but then I saw her smile, both of them, a resolute determination that seemed to match the beat of my own heart.

With the help of her loved ones, Saba worked tirelessly to fight the infection, and slowly but surely, she began to heal. The weight loss was a blessing, a reminder that even in darkness, there is always hope. Saba's story is a testament to the power of the human spirit, and a reminder that with determination and love, anything is possible, even the impossible.
