As a renowned filmmaker and producer, Karan Johar has faced his fair share of struggles. The weight-related comments from his mother still haunt him. "I called out to my mother, demanding respect, and the words still sting. For years, I've tried to silence my inner critic, convince myself that I'm more than my weight. But the truth remains, I'm not on Ozempic – I'm still fighting to be seen, to be heard."

Karan Johar's journey from a childhood where weight was a constant source of insecurity, to a present where he now advocates for body positivity, is a powerful testament to the human spirit's capacity for resilience. Despite the hardships, Karan remains determined to reclaim his health and prove to himself that he's more than his weight.

In this poignant story, Karan Johar's words convey the raw emotion he endured as a young man struggling with weight-related stigma. His journey serves as a powerful reminder that body positivity is not just a societal ideal, but a fundamental human right.
