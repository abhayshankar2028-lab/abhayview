  ** Karan Aujla pays Mumbai rickshaw drivers' fine, calls himself 'city's favourite'. Mumbai's streets are alive with the rhythm of rickshaws and the hum of engines. But Karan Aujla, a young entrepreneur, dares to challenge the status quo. He pays the fines, collecting the money in a generous gesture. And to the amazement of the drivers, Karan calls himself 'city's favourite'. 'I love doing this,' he says with a smile, oblivious to the admiration. The drivers, once scorned, now accept the payment with a nod of gratitude. Karan's actions spark a movement, as the city's rickshaw owners begin to rally behind him. The streets of Mumbai, once a battleground, have become a stage for Karan's unapologetic bravery. 'He's a hero, in every sense,' says one driver, as Karan continues to collect the fines, spreading his message of acceptance and kindness.
