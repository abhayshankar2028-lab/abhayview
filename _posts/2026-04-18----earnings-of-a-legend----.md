I sit, reminiscing about the good old days, when my father's factory rumbled to life, pumping out chips and crispy snacks. And I, a young boy, earned a living that made my father proud. Rs 8-9 lakh per day, that's how much I earned, a whopping sum that changed the game. It was an era of glamour and glitz, when my father was the king of the industry. I remember the thrill of landing a role in Jaani Dushman, the thrill of performing alongside my idol, Amrish Puri. Those were the days of my dreams, and I am eternally grateful for the opportunities I had. My father's factory was more than just a place of work; it was a symbol of success.
