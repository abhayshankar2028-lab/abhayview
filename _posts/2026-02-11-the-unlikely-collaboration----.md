As I sit here reminiscing about the early days of my acting career, I am transported back to the tumultuous time when I was offered a role that would change the course of my life. It was a project that initially went to Kamal Haasan, but fate had other plans. I vividly remember Raj Kapoor, my mentor and a legend in the industry, calling me into his office. He said, "Anil, I could see the potential in you. I want you to do this role." And with that, my career took a dramatic turn. Raj Kapoor's unwavering support and guidance played a significant role in bringing this project together. It was a testament to the power of mentorship and the ripple effect of a single word.
