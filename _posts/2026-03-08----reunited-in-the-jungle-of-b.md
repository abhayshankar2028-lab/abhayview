They arrived at Dr. Ghungroo's luxurious villa, where Paresh Rawal's iconic character awaited. "Welcome, my friends," Dr. Ghungroo said with a warm smile. "I've got a surprise for you all." As the night unfolded, the three actors laughed, reminisced, and rekindled their old flame. The reunion was nothing short of magical, and the anticipation for Welcome 5 was palpable.
