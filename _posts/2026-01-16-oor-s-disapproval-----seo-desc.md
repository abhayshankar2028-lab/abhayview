poor's Disapproval**

[SEO_DESC]: A lifetime of love and family drama, intertwined with the intricacies of a marital bond. Priya Sachdev, with tears in her eyes, sought to break free from the shackles of her marriage with Sunjay Kapoor.

[TAGS]: marital drama, family tension, divorce

Body:

It was a tense evening in Kapoor's living room as Priya expressed her desire to end her marriage with Sunjay Kapoor. Karisma, her sister, stepped in, her voice laced with concern. "It's not just a matter of convenience, Priya," Karisma said firmly. "Divorce papers, a formal document, are not something to be taken lightly." She paused, her eyes locked on Priya's. "You know the family's reputation will be tarnished if this becomes public. It's a serious matter, Priya." Priya's face reddened, her resolve wavering, as she knew Karisma was right. But the thought of sacrificing her family's image still lingered, casting a shadow over her decision.
