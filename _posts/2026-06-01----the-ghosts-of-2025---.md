**SEO_DESC:** "IPL winners reflect on past glory, seeking a repeat in 2026"
**TAGS:** 2025, 2026 IPL titles, Andy Flower's confidence

As I wander the corridors of time, I find myself lost in the memories of 2025, the year the Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) swept the league, claiming their second IPL title. And then, like a shadow, 2026 looms before us, a new chapter in the IPL saga. The question is, will RCB repeat their magic? Will the same confidence that carried them to victory in 2025 be enough to lead them to another title? Andy Flower, the mastermind behind RCB's success, is now a seasoned coach, but his eyes still hold the fire of a man who has tasted victory. The ghosts of 2025 whisper in his ear, and he listens intently, seeking a repeat in 2026.
