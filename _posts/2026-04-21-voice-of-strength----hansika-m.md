Voice of Strength"**

Hansika Motwani sat in her small apartment, tears streaming down her face as she made the call that would change her life forever. She had just received the news that her marriage was off, and the weight of her decision was crushing her. But as she looked around at the people who had stood by her all along, she felt a surge of gratitude and love.

Her family, her best friends, and her loved ones had been there for her every step of the way, offering support and encouragement. They had never told her what to do, but they had been her rock, her safe haven. And in that moment, Hansika knew that she could face anything, as long as she had the people who believed in her. She took a deep breath, feeling a sense of peace wash over her, and knew that she was not alone.
