SHWAHARITI VIVARITI** 
SEO_DESC: Gujarat Titans' explosive pace proves too hot to handle for Sunrisers Hyderabad
TAGS: IPL, Gujarat Titans, Sunrisers Hyderabad

As the sun beat down on the SCG, the Gujarat Titans' pace proved too hot to handle for their arch-rivals, the Sunrisers Hyderabad. The Indian Premier League's most dominant team was dismantled in the most thrilling contest of the season.

With their lightning-fast batsmen, the Titans had the fielders on their toes, but the Sunrisers' bowling duo, Khila and Rashid, seemed almost untouchable. The Hyderabad bowlers, led by their new signing, Rashid, had sealed the deal with a devastating 8-wicket haul.

The Titans' top order, led by Prithvi Shaw, was left reeling, as the Sunrisers' attack mercilessly targeted their weak links. The final scorecard read 272/3, a staggering 213 runs away from the winning point. The Titans' coach, Rajesh, was left to wonder if his team's pace had finally reached its limits.
