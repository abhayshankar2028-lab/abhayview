I stepped into the grand hall, a sea of faces before me. The air was electric, anticipation palpable. Nita Ambani, the queen of Indian business, was about to dance the night away. Aishwarya Rai, the Bollywood beauty, and Abhishek Bachchan, the dashing actor, were by her side. And then, the iconic song arrived. Salman Khan's 'Dhoom' boomed to life, and the three of them twirled, moved, and shimmied to the rhythm. The room was bathed in golden light, as if the music itself was conjuring a world of magic. In that moment, nothing else mattered but the beat, the music, and the love of a thousand Indian hearts.
