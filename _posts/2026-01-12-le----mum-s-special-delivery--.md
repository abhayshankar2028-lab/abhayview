As I stood on the field, sweat-drenched and elated, I couldn't help but think of my mum in Gurgaon. She's the reason I play this game, the driving force behind my passion. I took a moment to collect my thoughts, then a sly smile crept onto my face. I cranked up my phone and sent a special delivery of awards to her doorstep. My team's captain was in no position to resist my love for my mum. The team's captain would have, but I wouldn't have. As I looked at the email on my screen, I knew it was worth it – every hard-earned run, every six-pointer, every triumph. I was home, with the love of my life.
