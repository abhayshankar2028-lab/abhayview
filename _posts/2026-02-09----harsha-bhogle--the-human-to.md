As I sat in the dimly lit room, the echoes of music lingering in the air, I watched Arijit Singh sit across from Aamir Khan. The plea to stay in Bollywood playback had been a heavy burden to bear, but Singh's resolve remained unwavering. "I won't be a machine," he said, his voice firm and unshakeable. "Music is a part of who I am, not just a tool to be used." Khan's eyes widened in surprise, but Singh's conviction was unwavering. In that moment, I realized that music was more than just a machine – it was a human touch that couldn't be replicated.
