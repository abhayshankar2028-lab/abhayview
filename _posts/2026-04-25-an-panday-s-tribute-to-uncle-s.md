As I sit in my childhood home, surrounded by the warmth and love that Uncle Shah Rukh Khan embodied, I am filled with a sense of gratitude and awe. Those were halcyon days, spent playing in the backyard with the man who would later become a global icon. His generosity knew no bounds - he would take me, a poor, uneducated boy from Mumbai, under his wing and treat me like one of his own. I recall the countless hours he spent with me, teaching me the ways of the Tamil language, listening to my stories, and offering guidance on everything from cricket to life. Those were the days of pure unadulterated joy, and I owe it all to Uncle Shah Rukh Khan. My childhood was a rollercoaster of emotions, but one thing was certain - he was the anchor that held me fast.
