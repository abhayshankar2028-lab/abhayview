**SEO_DESC:** "Bhuvneshwar Kumar's introspective poem on the pressures of returning to India after a successful Test career"
**TAGS:** Cricket, pressure, expectation, redemption

"I am not thinking about comeback for India. It has been...": the words echo through my mind like a refrain. The weight of expectation is crushing me, a constant reminder that I am a cricketing icon – a hero to many. The spotlight is always on my shoulders, the bat in my hand a symbol of my profession. But in the quiet moments, when the cricket is silenced, I am left to ponder the what-ifs. What if I had taken a break? What if I had hung up my boots? The doubts creep in, a nagging voice that whispers "you're not good enough." But I refuse to listen. I take a deep breath, the air of doubt still lingering, and focus on the present. I am not thinking about comeback for India. It has been...the years of hard work, the sweat and tears, the achievements and the setbacks. It has been...me.
