**SEO_DESC:** "Dhurandhar marketer defends low-key promotions for Ranveer Singh film: 'We were underplaying'"

**TAGS:** marketing, Ranveer Singh, promotions

As the curtains closed on Ranveer Singh's latest film, the buzz around its marketing campaign was palpable. The Dhurandhar team, a prominent marketing agency, faced mounting pressure to justify the modest promotional efforts. But Dhurandhar marketer, Arjun, took to the stage to defend their strategy. "We were underplaying," he said with conviction. "Our goal was to create a buzz around the film, not to splurge on grand promotions. We played to the audience's hearts, not just their pockets. The results speak for themselves – the film performed well, and we did not have to break the bank. It's not about the amount of money; it's about the impact of the campaign."

Arjun's words struck a chord, and the audience erupted in applause. The Dhurandhar team had won over the hearts of the audience, and their modest approach had paid off in a big way.
