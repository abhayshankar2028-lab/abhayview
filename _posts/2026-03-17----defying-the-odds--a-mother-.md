**TAGS:** motherhood, family, justice, determination

In a dramatic turn of events, the Delhi High Court has denied Celina Jaitly's plea to have her detained brother, Anand Jaitly, represented by his wife, Sujata, as his legal representative. Celina, her voice trembling with emotion, stood before the court, her words laced with a fierce determination. "My duty to ensure my brother's protection is paramount," she declared. "I will not rest until he is safe and free. I will fight this battle to the end, no matter the cost. Sujata is the best person to represent Anand, and I will not let anyone take that away from her. I will continue to advocate for her, even if it means going against the law." As the courtroom erupted in a mix of emotions, Celina stood resolute, her spirit unbroken, a testament to the unwavering dedication of a mother fighting for her family's honor.
