As I stood at the crease, I gazed up at the mountain of wickets - 210-220, a daunting task ahead of us. The last five overs had been a torturous slog, with only a few brief moments of respite. Shubman Gill, our star batsman, had been a glimmer of hope, but his runs were few and far between. The pressure was suffocating, and I feared the worst. The team's confidence was shaken, and I feared the unthinkable - a defeat that would be etched in the memories of our players forever. The Everest of innings loomed, and we were all but doomed.
