As I reflect on that day at the Eden Gardens, the 2021 India vs Pakistan match, I still get goosebumps thinking about the unrelenting attitude of the Pakistani players. They were unstoppable, a force to be reckoned with, leaving the Indian team in the dust. The way they celebrated, the way they interacted with each other, it was all so mesmerizing. From the moment they took the field, it was clear that they were not just playing for themselves, but for their team, their country, and their legacy. The Indian players, on the other hand, seemed lost, unsure of what to do or say. It was as if they were in the stands, watching the match unfold, but unable to participate. The Pakistani players, on the other hand, were the stars, shining bright, their confidence and attitude inspiring their teammates and leaving the Indians in awe.
