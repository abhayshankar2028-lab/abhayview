m Taking a Bath**

"Ah, the sweet surrender of a warm bath," Ajay Devgn whispered to himself as he stepped into the enigmatic waters. It was a moment he'd replayed thousands of times, but never fully understood. The memories were etched like the lines on his weathered face, a testament to the trials he'd faced.

Mid-shower, his voice had come alive, "Ajay, I want to make this film about your life, your struggles, and your triumphs." The words had hung in the air, a gentle whisper that would change the course of his career. The National Award had followed, a validation of his hard-won talent. But more than the accolades, it was the confidence he'd gained from Mahesh Bhatt's pitch that had set him free.
