As I sit in the media huddle, surrounded by the familiar faces of the cricket world, I find myself lost in thought. The question on everyone's mind is not "Why not Ms. Dhoni?" but rather, "What's behind her decision to stay away from commentary?" The answer, as I had expected, is a complex mix of emotions and motivations. The fine line between a player's passion and the pressures of the game has been a topic of debate for years. For Dhoni, it was the fear of losing herself in the game that drove her to step back. The weight of responsibility, the constant scrutiny, and the pressure to perform had taken its toll. As I reflect on my own journey, I realize that the fine line is not just about the game, but about the human side that makes us who we are.
