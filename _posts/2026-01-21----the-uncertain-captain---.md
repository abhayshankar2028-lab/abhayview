I sit in the quiet of the cricket ground, my eyes fixed on a figure in the midst of intense negotiations. Litton Das, the Bangladesh captain, stands tall, his eyes locked on the task at hand. The room is abuzz with anticipation, the air thick with the weight of uncertainty. The World Cup beckons, and Das knows that the team's fate is sealed if they don't make it to the tournament. But will he have the strength of leadership to guide his team? The doubts creep in, whispers of failure that echo through the corridors of his mind. Can he silence the voices of doubt and lead his team to victory? Only time will tell.
