In the twilight of his illustrious career, MS Dhoni's departure still sends shivers down the spines of fans worldwide. The man who once led India to unimaginable heights, now walks away, leaving a void that seems almost impossible to fill. R Ashwin, the stalwart protege, takes the reins, and with it, a sense of trepidation settles over the cricketing world.

As one who has witnessed Dhoni's unyielding dedication to the game, I am left to ponder the question: Would Dhoni be willing to play, even a single match? Or would he, like his captain, choose to walk away, leaving the entire season to wither away, like a flower that has lost its bloom? The words echo in my mind: "If he does not want to, he should not play the entire season, that is it."
