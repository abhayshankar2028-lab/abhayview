tle:** Sri Lanka Crushes Zimbabwe in T20 World Cup Opener

**SEO_DESC:** Sri Lanka, the defending champions, won the toss and opted to bat first in Colombo, sending Zimbabwe to bat. The visitors, eager to make a statement in the tournament, were expected to put up a good fight, but the home team was determined to take an early lead. 

Playing XI:  
1. Mahesh Thakali (c)
2. Kusal Mendis (wk)
3. Dhanushka Gunathana
4. Dasun Shanaka
5. Minu Lakshman
6. Ravindra Sharma
7. Kshitij Sajida
8. Niroshan Prasad
9. Lasith Moppola
10. Mahesh Thakali
11. Bhanukaanda de Silva

Sri Lanka batted first, with Kusal Mendis and Dhanushka Gunathana setting the tone for the team's innings. Zimbabwe's bowlers, led by Tendai Dabul, struggled to contain the Lankan batsmen, who scored 180/8 in 18.4 overs. The visitors were bowled out in 19.1 overs, with no major contributions from the Zimbabwean batsmen. Sri Lanka sealed the victory with a 6-wicket haul, taking their T20 World Cup tally to 6-0.
