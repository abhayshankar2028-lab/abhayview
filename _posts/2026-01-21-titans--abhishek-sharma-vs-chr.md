0 Titans: Abhishek Sharma vs Chris Gayle**

The stage was set, the crowd was roaring, as two titans of cricket locked horns: Abhishek Sharma and Chris Gayle. The battle for the record books had begun. Chris Gayle, the King of Sixes, had broken records in the past, but Abhishek Sharma, India's rising star, was determined to challenge the throne.

As the two cricketers took to the field, the air was electric with tension. Gayle's 5000 T20 runs seemed like a distant memory, and Sharma's sights were set on the elusive milestone. The game was a seesaw, with both batsmen trading blows and showcasing their incredible skills. In the end, it was Sharma who emerged victorious, notching his 5000th T20 run. As the crowd erupted in cheers, Gayle smiled with satisfaction, acknowledging the challenge Sharma had laid down. The T20 world would never be the same.
