As the afterglow of motherhood lingered, Kiara Advani found solace in her husband Sidharth Malhotra's nocturnal drives. The dimly lit roads became a sanctuary, a safe haven where she could unburden herself of the turmoil that had been brewing inside. "Let me vent," she'd whisper to the open road, as the tires hummed along, a soothing serenade to her frazzled nerves. The world outside receded, and all that remained was the gentle rustle of leaves, the hum of the engine, and the soft murmur of her own voice. In this sacred space, Kiara found a fleeting sense of peace, a reminder that even in the midst of chaos, love could be a beacon of light.
