** The curtains rose on a chaotic night, as Dhurandhar 2 took the Indian box office by storm. Its effervescent energy and vibrant performances left fans beaming with delight. But amidst the euphoria, a storm brewed. Ram Gopal Varma, a stalwart critic, let out a thunderous rebuke, slapping Bollywood with the weight of its own 'loud silence'. "We're more concerned with the noise than the substance," Varma declared, stirring up a tempest in the industry. His words were met with silence, as if the very sound of his criticism had been muffled by the deafening roar of Dhurandhar 2. The silence was deafening, and Varma knew it.

As the curtains closed on that fateful night, the industry's cacophony continued, drowned out only by the faint whispers of artistic bloodshed. For in the world of Bollywood, where substance trumps style, the very fabric of artistry hangs in the balance. Will the industry heed Varma's warning, or will it succumb to the all-consuming din of its own making? Only time will tell.
