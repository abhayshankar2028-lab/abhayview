In the world of high-stakes business deals, there exist powerful software tools that can make or break fortunes. For Ranveer Singh, the game-changer was Excel, the spreadsheet software that had revolutionized his financial calculations. But Ranveer's troubles were far from over. His co-producer, Farhan Akhtar, had secretly been working on a rival project, Pralay, and was now trying to poach Ranveer's team. The Rs 40 crore exit dispute was brewing, and Excel was about to be dragged into the fray. As the tension mounted, Ranveer realized that his Excel skills were about to be put to the ultimate test.
