I sit in the quiet of my Mumbai home, the flickering lights a disguise for the turmoil brewing inside. As Najmul Hossain Shanto gazes out at the familiar streets, his mind wanders to the uncertain path ahead. The T20 World Cup, a beacon of hope for our cricketing nation, calls to him, yet doubts creep in like the shadows that dance upon the walls. Will we act as if nothing affects us? Or will the weight of responsibility shatter our resolve? I ponder these questions, my heart a battleground of emotions. In this limbo, where uncertainty reigns, I search for answers, but the echoes of doubt remain.
