Meditated 24/7 for Two Years**

The curtains of fame parted, and I stepped into the silence. For two years, I meditated 24/7, my mind a vessel for the universe's whispers. The world outside receded, and I was left with only my thoughts. The beat of the drums, the hum of the music industry, the whispers of the critics – all became a distant hum. I shed my VJ persona, the sparkle of light, and the mask of confidence. I surrendered to the stillness, and in its depths, I found solace. The fire that once drove me to perform now burned with a different flame – the flame of spirituality. I am no longer a star, but a seeker of truth. My journey has just begun, and I am ready to surrender to the mystery.
