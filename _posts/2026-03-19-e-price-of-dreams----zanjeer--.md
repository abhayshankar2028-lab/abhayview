"A film that promised to shake the foundations of Bollywood, 'Zanjeer' became a cautionary tale of ambition and greed. The project's financial backers pulled out at the last minute, leaving the director, Raj Kapoor, to mortgage his family's home to keep the project afloat. Amitabh Bachchan was cast, but struggled with the film's complex plot and underwhelming performances. As the release date loomed, it became clear that 'Zanjeer' would be a commercial failure. The film stalled in theaters, its dreams of success crushed by the weight of unforgiving expectations. The once-promising career of Raj Kapoor was left to wither away, a tragic reminder of the price of ambition and the dangers of chasing fortune at any cost."
