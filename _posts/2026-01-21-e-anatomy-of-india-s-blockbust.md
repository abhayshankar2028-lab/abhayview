As the Indian cricket team swept past New Zealand in the shortest ODI format, their batting line-up demonstrated an unparalleled depth and flexibility that left the Kiwis scrambling to respond. Rohit Sharma's aggressive approach to the crease, combined with a valiant display from Shreyas Iyer, earned him a top-scoring innings. Meanwhile, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Jasprit Bumrah, and Shardul Thakur shared the attack's guile and precision, working in tandem to restrict New Zealand's late innings. The result was a one-sided contest that left the Indian fans in raptures, celebrating a triumph of unorthodox tactics over conventional bowling.
