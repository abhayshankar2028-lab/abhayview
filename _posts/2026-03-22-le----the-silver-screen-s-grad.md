I sit in my dimly lit study, pen in hand, and the weight of a thousand film reels upon my chest. The news has just reached me, a gentle whisper in the wind. Mohanlal, the legendary actor, is set to star in Priyadarshan's 100th film. I feel the familiar sting of nostalgia.

As I read the script, my eyes well up with tears. The memories come flooding back – the laughter, the tears, the triumphs, and the tragedies. Priyadarshan's films have been my escape, my sanctuary. And now, Mohanlal, the man who made them all, is embarking on this new journey.

I pen a note, my words trembling with emotion. "To Muhurthacha, my friend," it reads. "Your legacy lives on through this film. I am honored to be a part of it. May your memory inspire generations to come." As I seal the envelope, I know that this film will be a testament to your enduring spirit.
