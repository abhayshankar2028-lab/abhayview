As the Indian cricket team battled against Australia in the Third Test, Gautam Gambhir took to the field, determined to give his all. But despite his valiant efforts, the team was ultimately defeated, with Australia securing a crucial win. The disappointment was palpable, and Yuvraj Singh was among those who felt a sense of unease. "It's not just about the loss," he said. "It's about the bigger picture. We're playing on two continents, with two different time zones. The players are running on empty, and it's taking a toll on their bodies. We need to make a change, and fast. The problem is, no one wants to listen." Yuvraj's words were a harsh reminder that the Indian cricket team's success is not just about individual performances, but about the collective well-being of its players.
