Ammy Virk's eyes welled up with tears as he sat in his darkened room, his bed a sanctuary from the world outside. His family's departure to the UAE had left him feeling lost and alone. The thought of being apart from his loved ones for an extended period was unbearable. He couldn't bear the idea of hiding under his bed, of being confined to a small, enclosed space. Instead, he felt like he was suffocating, suffocating under the weight of his emotions. In his mind, he whispered to his father, "Papa, I'll hide under my bed." The words echoed through his mind like a mantra, a desperate attempt to escape the pain of separation. As the darkness closed in, Ammy's heart felt heavy, weighed down by the thought of being away from the people he loved.
