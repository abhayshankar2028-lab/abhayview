As I sit amidst the eerie silence, the haunting echoes of that match still linger in my mind. The series defeat against the All Blacks has left me shattered, my heart heavy with the weight of disappointment. Shubman Gill, once the epitome of confidence and cool, now sits amidst the wreckage of his team's hopes. The cries of his teammates still resonate within me - "He's not good enough!" "We need a different captain!" The words cut deep, like an open wound. If batsmen, especially two at the top, are not able to convert starts, then I fear for the future of our team. The agony of defeat is a lonely and isolating experience, one that I fear may haunt me for a long time to come.
