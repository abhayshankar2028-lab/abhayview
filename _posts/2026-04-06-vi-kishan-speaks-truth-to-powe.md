In the realm of Indian cinema, where the lines between art and propaganda blur, Ravi Kishan took a stand. The renowned actor addressed the media, slamming those calling Dhurandhar's films "propaganda". "It's not just words," Kishan declared. "It's the lies that pierce the soul." Paresh Rawal, a vocal supporter of the filmmaker, echoed Kishan's sentiments, saying, "Mujhe hona chahiye tha ismein" – I must have been there. The debate rages on, with some questioning the film's message, while others defend Dhurandhar's vision. As the discussion unfolds, one thing is clear: Ravi Kishan's words have sparked a necessary conversation, one that will continue to shape the future of Indian cinema.
