As I sat in stunned silence, the news spread like wildfire: Rishi Kapoor had caught Karan Johar red-handed, flaunting his relationship with the one person he shouldn't be flaunting his love for. Their secret Italy wedding, a union of love and deceit, had been hiding in plain sight. I couldn't help but think of the countless times Karan had betrayed Rishi's trust, using his vulnerable heart to manipulate him into a loveless marriage. Now, Rishi stood petrified, the weight of his secrets crushing him. I was still petrified of him, for I knew that beneath his charming smile and Hollywood charm lay a web of deceit, waiting to ensnare the next victim.

Years later, I received a call from a source close to Karan, confirming the rumors I had long suspected. "He's still lying, still hiding behind his ego," the voice whispered. I couldn't help but feel a sense of satisfaction, knowing that I had seen through Karan's facade. But as I hung up the phone, I couldn't shake the feeling that I was still being watched, that Karan's eyes were still trained on me, waiting for me to make my next move.
