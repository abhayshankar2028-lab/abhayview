In the silence of his home, Parmeet Sethi stood, lost in thought, the weight of his actions finally catching up to him. He had been an absent father, a mediocre one, with a heart that was no longer full. Archana Puran Singh, his daughter, felt the void, the emptiness that had been her constant companion. "I was so weak, you were missing," Parmeet whispered, the truth finally out. The guilt crept in, a slow-moving poison that had haunted him for years. As the truth sank in, Archana felt a lump form in her throat, a mix of sadness and anger. She was alone, just like her father, and the weight of that loneliness was crushing.
