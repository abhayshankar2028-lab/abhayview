"Ar Rahman, just showed the tip of the iceberg, my friend," Amaal Malik whispered, his voice laced with awe. "You've been playing with the foundations of music, pushing the boundaries of what's possible. But now, you're revealing the depths of your art."

As Amaal spoke, the sound of his guitar filled the air, weaving a sonic tapestry that seemed to transport us to another world. The music was a reflection of the artist's soul, a raw and unbridled expression of emotion and creativity.

"Labels work only with their artistes," Amaal continued, his words dripping with conviction. "They can try to mold and shape, but it's only when the artist is free to create that the true magic happens."
