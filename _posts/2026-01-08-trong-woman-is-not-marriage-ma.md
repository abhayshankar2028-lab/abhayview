Strong Woman is Not Marriage Material**

"Unraveled" by Harsha Bhogle

Neena Gupta stood before us, her piercing gaze radiating an aura of defiance. "I've asked the question, and the answer is clear: a strong woman is not marriage material," she declared, her voice laced with conviction. Her words cut like a knife, leaving us to ponder the harsh truth.

95% of men, according to Neena, desire women who are helpless. This statistic sends chills down our spines. Is it any wonder that women like me, with my athletic background and fighting spirit, are viewed as fragile? The notion that a strong woman is somehow less desirable is a tired trope that has been perpetuated for far too long. Neena's words are a rebuke to the notion that our worth is measured by our vulnerability.
