** In the hallowed halls of Punjab Kings, a legendary record had long stood as a testament to the team's unwavering dedication. For centuries, it had been the benchmark, the standard by which all else was measured. But on that fateful day, the unthinkable happened – Shreyas Iyer, the young gun, dropped twice, yet still managed to shatter the record with ease. The crowd gasped in awe as he single-handedly brought down the behemoth, leaving it in his wake. The crowd erupted in cheers as Iyer stood tall, his eyes fixed on the prize. He had done the impossible, and in doing so, had rewritten history.
