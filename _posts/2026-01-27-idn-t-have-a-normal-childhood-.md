** As a child, I struggled to understand the complexities of my mother's life. She worked tirelessly to support our family, often sacrificing her own happiness for our sake. But her love and dedication to our family never wavered. I remember the countless hours she spent cleaning, cooking, and caring for our siblings. She was a mother to all of us, and her selflessness inspired me to be strong and resilient. Despite the hardships, our family's bond remained unbreakable. I'm grateful for that lessons learned the hard way, and I strive to make my mother proud every day.
