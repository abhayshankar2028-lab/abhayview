Water to Drink, How's He Shaving Everyday?**
**Aamir Khan recalls spat with Ashutosh Gowariker over Bhuvan being clean-shaven in Lagaan**

"I recall the day Lagaan came to life on the big screen," Aamir Khan reminisced, his eyes sparkling with nostalgia. "The film's director, Ashutosh Gowariker, wanted to create a classic Indian village scene. He insisted that Bhuvan, the young protagonist, be clean-shaven for the entire shoot."

Bhuvan's father, the reluctant but loving Bhuvan, grudgingly cut his son's hair every day. But as the days went by, Aamir couldn't bear to see Bhuvan's unshaven face. "I asked Ashutosh why Bhuvan couldn't just be clean-shaven like everyone else," Aamir chuckled. "But he just smiled and said, 'Aamir, it's not about me, it's about the village, the people, the game.'"

The spat between Ashutosh and Aamir eventually dissolved, but not before the two had a laugh about the absurdity of it all.
