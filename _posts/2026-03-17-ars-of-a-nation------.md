Tears of a nation fell as Farah Khan wept for her father, a man whose life was reduced to naught but memories. Overnight, his wealth vanished, leaving them with just Rs 30. The once-proud family had to wear rags, their dignity and pride all but lost in the chaos of film's failure. The family's struggles were a testament to the harsh realities of India's underworld, where the rich got richer and the poor got poorer. But in their darkest hour, they found strength in each other, a bond that transcended the hardships they faced. As Farah's tears fell, so did the veil of shame that shrouded her father's memory, leaving room for redemption and a new beginning.
