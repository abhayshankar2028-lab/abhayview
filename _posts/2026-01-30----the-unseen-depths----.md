As I stood before the towering figure of AR Rahman, his music seemed to reverberate deep within my soul. The symphony of notes, the rhythm of the beats – it was like nothing I'd ever heard before. But, as the music faded away, I couldn't shake off the feeling that there was more to this genius than met the eye. His performances, his collaborations – all seemingly seamless and effortless. And then, it hit me: labels work only with their artistes. The music industry's obsession with controlling every detail, every aspect of an artist's creative process. AR Rahman's music is a reflection of this, a canvas painted by his own hand. A true visionary, he stands apart from the crowd.
