ey Want to Keep Everything Quiet**

In the depths of Azadi Bachchan, where shadows dance and whispers weave,
A tale of silence shatters, like a thousand broken dreams.
Imran Khan's sons, with hearts still heavy from the past,
Break the chains of secrecy, and let the truth forever last.

"Father won't break," they say, their voices a defiant cry,
Despite the harsh treatment, the isolation, the tears they've dry.
Their father's words still echo, a mantra they cling to,
A promise to stand strong, no matter the cost, no matter the toil.

In this cell, they've found a strength that cannot be tamed,
A resolve that will not be shattered, no matter the name.
They'll speak out, they'll shout, they'll make their stand,
For their father, for their freedom, and for the truth to expand.
