**SEO_DESC:** "Sunil Gavaskar slams foreign stars, urges franchises to drop them from IPL"
**TAGS:** Indian hospitality, foreign players, IPL 2026 pull-outs

"I've been playing cricket for decades, and I've seen some incredible talents come and go," Sunil Gavaskar said in a candid interview. "But I've noticed something peculiar. Foreign players, who are often touted as heroes in their respective countries, now seem to think they're above calling it quits at 30. They're leaving Indian teams like it's nothing, as if they own the place. Newsflash: they don't. Indian hospitality is built on respect, not taking. I've seen it with my own eyes. When foreign players pull out of IPL, it's not just a business decision; it's a sign of disrespect for the country and the fans who've supported them. It's time for franchises to drop these players, not just because they're not good enough for India, but because it's the right thing to do."

As Sunil Gavaskar finished speaking, the camera panned out to reveal a sea of disappointed fans, their faces a mix of sadness and frustration. The IPL franchises, too, seemed taken aback by the blunt assessment, their faces a mask of surprise and discomfort. "I'm not asking for much," Gavaskar continued, his voice firm but measured. "Just a little respect. If these players want to leave, let them. But if they want to stay, let them know that they're not welcome here."
