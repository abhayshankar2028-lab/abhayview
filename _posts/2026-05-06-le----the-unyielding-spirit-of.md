Kartik Sharma sat quietly, his eyes fixed on the field as the sun beat down on his young shoulders. He was 19, but his heart was still 16 - the age of his idol, MS Dhoni. The young captain was on the brink of retirement, and Kartik knew he had to act. He couldn't let Dhoni walk away like a fading star. With a fierce determination burning in his chest, Kartik approached Dhoni, his words barely above a whisper. "You can't walk away, Dhoni. You're not done yet." His words resonated deep within Dhoni's soul, and for the first time in years, a glimmer of hope flickered to life.
