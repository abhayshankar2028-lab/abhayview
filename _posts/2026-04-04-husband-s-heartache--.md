Piyush's confession was met with a mix of emotions - shock, anger, and hurt. His wife's expression was a mask of numbness, but beneath it, Harsha could see the tears welling up in her eyes. "How could you?" she spat, her voice trembling. "How could you do this to me?" Piyush's response was a despairing cry, a plea for forgiveness that his wife could not offer. The silence that followed was oppressive, a heavy blanket that suffocated them both. As the reality of his actions sunk in, Piyush felt his world crashing down around him. He was a man alone, a man who had lost the love of his life.
