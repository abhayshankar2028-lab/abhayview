In the misty hills of Ladakh, Arijit Singh stood before his kingdom, his eyes gazing out at the endless expanse of snow-capped peaks. For years, he had reigned supreme, his voice the voice of the people. But now, he had hung up his playback mic, embarking on a new journey. His first independent song, "Tandav", was a reflection of his inner turmoil. The music spoke of the weight of responsibility, the longing for freedom. As the song neared its climax, Singh's voice cracked with emotion, his words pouring forth like a river. In this moment, he was not just a singer, but a king, back to his throne.
