**TAGS:** Anil Kapoor, Kamal Haasan, Raj Kapoor, script swap

As I sit here reminiscing about my time in the industry, I am reminded of the incredible journey that took me from a small-town boy in Bombay to a household name. It was a script that was first offered to none other than Kamal Haasan that sparked the flames of my ambition. But what's often forgotten is how I managed to land the role. It was a humble script swap with the legendary Raj Kapoor that changed everything. "Raj saab said I could do this," I remember him saying with a warm smile. It was a moment of pure magic, not just in terms of the script swap, but in the camaraderie and trust that came with it. It was a turning point in my career, and I will forever be grateful to Raj Kapoor for believing in me.
