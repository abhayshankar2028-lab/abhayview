As I stood amidst the chaos, Kangana Ranaut's voice echoed through the halls, a clarion call to the mediocrities that sought to undermine her vision. "My standards aren't that low," she declared, a fire in her eyes. "Karan Johar and Anurag Kashyap, the so-called 'masters' of the industry, have consistently shown their lack of creativity and originality. My film, 'Gutta', is a masterpiece, a work of art that will be remembered for generations to come. And as for Salman Khan, I call him 'cool', for he has shown a willingness to take risks and push boundaries, even if it means breaking the mold." With those words, Kangana Ranaut sent a clear message to the industry: she will not be silenced, and her art will not be compromised.
