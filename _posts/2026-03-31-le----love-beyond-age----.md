**SEO_DESC:** Rajpal Yadav's tragic tale of unrequited love and societal pressures

**TAGS:** social inequality, love at first sight, marriage, cultural expectations

On a sweltering summer day, Radha's eyes locked onto Rajpal's - 18, and he was smitten. They eloped without a word, their parents disapproving of the 13-year age gap. Radha completed her studies before marriage, determined to make a life for herself. But societal expectations dictated that she should marry younger. As they settled into married life, Radha's parents grew increasingly critical, their disapproval a constant reminder of their daughter's failure. Despite her courage, Radha's heart remained heavy, weighed down by the knowledge that her love was not accepted by those she loved most.
