As the curtains close on the Mumbai film industry, Varun Dhawan finds himself in the midst of an online storm. Suniel Shetty's words, though laced with sarcasm, cut deep – and not just because he's a veteran actor. In this scathing exchange, both men deliver biting lines, each attempting to one-up the other. But beneath the roars of criticism, lies a quiet understanding. Varun Dhawan says, "Very easy to run someone down," a sentiment that resonates with Suniel Shetty, who nods in silent agreement. The game of cat and mouse continues, but for now, Suniel Shetty stands by his words, a testament to the enduring bonds of friendship – or at least, that's what it seems.
