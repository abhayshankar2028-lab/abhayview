By Harsha Bhogle

When Priyanka Chopra strolled into the spotlight, her life was forever altered. Behind her was the one person who had always been there, a constant source of love and support – her brother, Mohit. But fame came at a price, and Mohit, now collateral damage to her stardom, struggled to find his place amidst the chaos.

"My dear brother, every day is a struggle for you," I often remind myself, my eyes welling up with tears. You, Mohit, were always there for me, to listen, to cry, to be there. But with every step forward, you stepped further away. I see you struggling, and it breaks my heart. I wish we could have been a family, not just a sister and brother, but a family that could weather the storm of fame.

Now, as the world waits with bated breath for Priyanka's next move, I know that Mohit will be right there, fighting for his own stardom, even if it means sacrificing a part of himself.
