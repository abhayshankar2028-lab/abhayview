** As I stood in the stands, my eyes fixed on the white-knuckled faces of Lachit Singh Grewal and Rajkumar Pant, the speed demons of Jaipur, I couldn't help but feel a sense of awe. Their blistering runs, the sheer pace at which they attacked the opposition, left me breathless. But, alas, their dominance was short-lived. Pant and Co stumbled to their fourth straight defeat, and I couldn't help but wonder what had gone wrong. The crowd's silence was deafening, a testament to the team's struggles. As I scribbled notes on my pad, I couldn't help but feel a sense of disappointment. The speed demons of Jaipur, a force to be reckoned with, had let them down.
