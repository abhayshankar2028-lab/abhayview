In the sweltering heat of a Mumbai evening, I sat with a scowl etched on my face, my mind fixated on the impending battle between my beloved Mumbai Indians and the Royal Challengers Bangalore. And then, the beast emerged: Shardul Thakur. My former teammate, my friend, my nemesis. But on this day, he was more than just a teammate or a friend – he was a force of nature, a battering ram that would leave no stone unturned.

"RCB batters will smash him to Marine Drive & Churchgate station": that's what I'd utter, my voice trembling with a mix of anger and admiration. For in Shardul's arsenal was a magic that could beggar the senses, a sheer force of will that would leave even the toughest of opponents shaken. And I, for one, was ready to face him head-on.
