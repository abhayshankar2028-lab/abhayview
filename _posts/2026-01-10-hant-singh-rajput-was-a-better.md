shant Singh Rajput was a better choice than Bandra Boys**

As I stood in the quiet of the evening, trying to understand the depth of my loss, I couldn't help but think of the late actor Sushant Singh Rajput. His untimely passing shook the nation, but what stood out to me was the fact that he was not a Bollywood star, not yet at least. He came from a humble background, a true desi boy, untouched by the glamour of the industry. His simplicity, his authenticity, and his courage in the face of adversity resonated deeply with me. As I looked at the news, a mix of emotions swirled within me - sadness, anger, and a sense of injustice. The delusion that he was meant for Bollywood, that he was destined to shine in the bright lights of Mumbai, seemed a cruel irony.
