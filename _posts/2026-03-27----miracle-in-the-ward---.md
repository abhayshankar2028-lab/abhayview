I sit by Saba's bedside, her frail body a testament to her battle. A parasitic infection has ravaged her, leaving her weak and worn. Four kilograms have disappeared, a staggering loss that has left her family and friends in a state of shock. As her loved ones struggle to come to terms with this new reality, Saba's spirit remains unbroken. That's when I see it – a faint glimmer of hope in her eyes, a spark that refuses to be extinguished. Hrithik Roshan, the actor who was visiting her just the day before, sat by her side, radiating warmth and kindness. His presence seemed to lift her spirits, and for a brief moment, Saba's weight loss seemed less like a burden.
