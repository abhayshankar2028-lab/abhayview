**SEO_DESC:** "Harry Brook's stark reality check on India's semi-final chances"

**TAGS:** T20 World Cup, England, India, semi-final

"We're not going to be the ones telling you what a perfect game is needed to win the T20 World Cup," Harry Brook, England's star all-rounder, declared with a hint of defiance, as he watched his team take on India in the semi-final. Brook's words were laced with a fiery passion, a sense of urgency that echoed through the cricketing world. "We're not built for a perfect game," he said, his voice dripping with conviction. "We've got too much talent, too much experience, to waste on a routine victory. We need to go out there and play with heart, with passion, with an edge that India just can't match." Brook's words were a wake-up call, a call to action that would determine the fate of England's T20 World Cup aspirations.
