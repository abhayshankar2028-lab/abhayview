As the sun dipped into the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the bustling streets of Mumbai, Aamir Khan stood before the camera, his eyes fixed on the screen. The climax of Junaid Khan's 'Ek Din' was about to unfold, and he was determined to make it a masterpiece. But little did he know, Mansoor Khan, his co-star and confidant, had reservations about the direction the film was taking. As the cameras rolled, Mansoor walked onto the set, his voice laced with a hint of disapproval. "Aamir, I don't think this is the right way to wrap up the story," he said, his words like a gentle breeze on a summer's day. Aamir's eyes narrowed, but he knew that Mansoor was right. The true climax of 'Ek Din' was yet to be written.
