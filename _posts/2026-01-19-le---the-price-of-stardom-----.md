As I walk through the crowded halls of Mumbai, my eyes meet the familiar figure of my son, Sean. But his eyes, once bright and full of promise, now tell a different story – of struggle and hardship. Every day, I see him face the harsh realities of fame, his heart weighed down by the constant scrutiny of the world. His sister, Priyanka Chopra, is a shining star, but I know she's not immune to the pressures of stardom. His brother, a talented young man, has become collateral damage to her stardom. I see him struggling every day, his dreams and aspirations slipping away like sand between my fingers.
