I stood before the judge's bench, my heart racing with every word I spoke. Just hours after being arrested for cheating, I was forced to confront the harsh realities of our underclass. The 'poor education' remark from Priyadarshan still stung. "A shot from the deep end", I said, my voice shaking with defiance. "Any judgement from them don't hold value". The courtroom fell silent, the only sound the heavy breathing of my adversaries. I knew I had made my point. The question was, would it be enough? Only time would tell.
