Abhishek Bachchan, the patriarch of the Bachchan dynasty, stood before us, his eyes fixed on some point beyond the horizon. His voice, low and measured, spoke directly to the soul. "I never bought an IPL team," he began, his words dripping with conviction. "Why would I? The game was already in shambles, a hollow shell of what it once was."

He paused, his gaze sweeping the stadium as if searching for answers. "The owners, the players, everyone was chasing the same dream – fame, fortune, and the adoration of the masses. But I knew the true cost. The IPL, once a fledgling experiment, had devolved into a corrupt and cynical spectacle."

His words hung in the air, a stark reminder that not everyone had the foresight to see the ruin that lay ahead. As the sun dipped below the horizon, Abhishek Bachchan's words continued to echo, a haunting indictment of the IPL's darker side.
