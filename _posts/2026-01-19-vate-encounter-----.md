It's been years since Priyanka Chopra's stardom soared, but the cost to her family remains a silent testament to her relentless pursuit of fame. Her brother, Ashok, once an ambitious young man, now fights a daily battle to make ends meet. His eyes, a reflection of the weight he carries, often clouded by worry. "Every day, I see Priyanka struggling in her own way," Mother Madhu whispers, a gentle warning. She watches as her daughter's success leaves her brother with little, her love and care unfulfilled.

In this private world, family becomes collateral damage to the spotlight.
