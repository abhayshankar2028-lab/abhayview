** In the corridors of fame, where stars are born and faded, my mother stands tall, a shining beacon of love and support. Her larger-than-life presence, though she may not have been as prominent in the industry, is undeniable. Aishwarya Rai, the Bollywood beauty, rose to stardom under her watchful eye. I, Abhishek Bachchan, the actor's son, often find myself envying the ease with which Aishwarya navigated the spotlight. Yet, my mother's unwavering support and guidance have been the catalyst for my own success. Her unshakeable faith in me has given me the confidence to pursue my dreams, unencumbered by self-doubt. As I look back on my journey, I realize that my mother's love and influence have been the driving force behind my success.
