In the films of the gods, a director's voice reverberates with timeless stories, yet few know the tales behind the masterminds. Aditya Dhar's journey began as a mere nobody, aspiring to make a mark in the industry. It was then that Priyadarshan, a seasoned veteran, took notice of his potential and nurtured him into a director. "You were my student," the man once told me, "treated like an equal, with a mentor's guidance to keep him grounded." Those were the words that inspired me to weave a narrative of love, perseverance, and the unbreakable bond between two artists. And so, his legacy lives on through the films I've had the privilege to make.
