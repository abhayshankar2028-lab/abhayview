As Abhigyan Kundu's rise to fame unfolded, few knew of his unconventional coach, Rohan Chadha. A man with an unorthodox approach to training, Chadha would often make Abhigyan sleep at the railway station, fueling his unrelenting passion. His parents, though concerned, chose to step aside, trusting their son's unbridled talent. As Abhigyan's form improved, so did his vocabulary. "I dream of bats at dawn," he once said, his eyes sparkling with a fire that would soon propel him to the top. Chadha's unyielding guidance, though unorthodox, paid off, and Abhigyan Kundu became a shining example of the unrelenting power of dedication.
