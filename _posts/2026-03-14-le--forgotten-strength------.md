tle: "Forgotten Strength"**

**SEO_DESC: "A 30-year-old man reflects on the rejection he faced from his childhood bodyguard, Mika Singh, and the journey that brought him to stand by him today, as Mika's current bodyguard."**

Thirty years ago, I had the privilege of working under Mika Singh, my childhood bodyguard. He was a man of strength, both in body and spirit. I called him a 'struggler,' a man who had worked hard to make a name for himself, but in my eyes, that's what made him truly special. Rejection was a part of his life, and yet, he never gave up. I sought him out, and he became my partner, my friend. As I stood by him today, I realized that his strength was not just physical, but emotional as well. He had a heart of gold, and I was honored to stand by him, to be his bodyguard for another chapter of his life.
