In the digital realm, a storm raged on, fueled by mean-spirited words. Suniel Shetty, a stalwart of Bollywood, faced a barrage of online attacks. Yet, he refused to yield. When asked about his support for Varun Dhawan, who had faced similar trolling, Shetty replied, 'Very easy to run someone down, but I'd rather run on compassion.' His words struck a chord, and many took notice. The brave words of a hero ignited a fire within the hearts of those who witnessed the attacks. Arjun, the unsung champion, stood tall, knowing that courage and kindness can conquer even the darkest of trolls.
