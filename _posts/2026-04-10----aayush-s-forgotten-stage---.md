Aayush Sharma sat on his couch, surrounded by Xbox controllers and pizza boxes, reminiscing about his past. The 40-year-old actor had once been a promising talent, but his struggles in Bollywood had led to a life of uncertainty. He had paid a hefty sum of Rs 1.5 lakh for an acting workshop, hoping to revive his career. But the journey had been long and arduous, filled with rejection and disappointment. Aayush's eyes wandered to the framed photo on his mantle, a reminder of a life left unlived. He longed for the spotlight, for the thrill of performing on stage, but the harsh realities of the industry had silenced him. As he gazed into the TV, he wondered if he would ever find his place in the spotlight.
