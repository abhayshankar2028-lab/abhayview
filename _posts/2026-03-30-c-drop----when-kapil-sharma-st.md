When Kapil Sharma stepped onto the stage, Ranveer Singh's face turned bright red. The 'boys vs girls' debate had reached new heights, and Kapil was about to take the microphone. "I think it's time we talk about the real issue here," Kapil said with a sly grin. But wait, Ranveer Singh had had enough. He rose from his seat, his eyes blazing with anger, and said, "Enough is enough, Kapil Sharma. You think you're funny, but you're just a joke. My film 'Badhaai Ho' broke all records, and I'm the one who brought in the box office. You, on the other hand, are just a comedian with a mic. So, go ahead, keep talking, but when it comes to real acting, I'm the one who's boiling." The audience erupted in laughter, and Kapil's face turned beet red.
