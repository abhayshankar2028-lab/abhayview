As the sun set over the MCG, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, the master of chaos, found himself in a familiar predicament. The Indian Premier League had been a rollercoaster ride of emotions, but now, with RCB's big total on the line, Sooryavanshi had other plans. With his team, Rajasthan Royals, trailing by a few runs, Sooryavanshi called a timeout and surveyed the pitch. The RCB batsmen were caught between chasing a cautious total and trying to gallop to a commanding lead. Sooryavanshi watched with a sly grin, planning his next move.
