Radha's eyes welled up with tears as she stared at the news on her phone. Rajpal Yadav's wife, the one who had always been by his side, was being linked to another man. The whispers in the industry were spreading like wildfire: "Rajpal is being exonerated from prison," "He's found love anew." The thought sent shivers down her spine.

The film industry, always known for its camaraderie and support, was abuzz with the news. Celebrities were speaking out, but their words were laced with concern and empathy. "We're not sure what to make of this," said a visibly shaken Aamir Khan. "We're all rooting for Rajpal, it's just not right."
