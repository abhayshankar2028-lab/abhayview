I sit in my Kolkata home, reminiscing about the countless projects I've worked on throughout my career. Among them, one film stands out - a Bollywood epic that has been etched in my memory for over two decades. The film, 'Om Shanti Om', was set to star none other than Nana Patekar. I was supposed to be a part of the project, but I never got the chance to work with him.

As I reflect on those days, I realize that everyone is very scared of Nana Patekar. His presence seems to command an air of intimidation, and many of my colleagues and crew members have spoken to me about how they avoid working with him due to this perception. I understand why they would be wary, but for me, it was a personal preference. I didn't want to associate myself with someone who seemed to be a master of intimidation.
