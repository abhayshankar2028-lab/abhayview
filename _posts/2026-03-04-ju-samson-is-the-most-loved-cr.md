Dinesh Karthik, the ever-enthusiastic Indian wicket-keeper, has outdone himself this time. As he's witnessing the phenomenal rise of Sanju Samson, the Indian opener, he can't help but feel a sense of nostalgia. Just like Sachin Tendulkar, MS Dhoni, and Virat Kohli, who have left an indelible mark on Indian cricket, Samson has become the new darling of the fans. Every time he steps up to the crease, the stadium erupts in a frenzy of cheers and applause. His incredible batting record, coupled with his fearlessness in the face of adversity, has captured the hearts of the fans. It's not just a coincidence; Dinesh Karthik believes that Sanju Samson has a special place in his heart, just like the greats.

Dinesh Karthik: Sanju Samson is an absolute game-changer. He's got that special something that sets him apart from other cricketers. I'm not just saying that because I'm his teammate, but I genuinely believe that he's the most loved cricketer in the country."
