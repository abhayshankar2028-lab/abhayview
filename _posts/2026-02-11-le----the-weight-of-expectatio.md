tle:** The Weight of Expectation
**SEO_DESC:** Exploring the mental preparation of Usman Tariq as he faces India's bowling onslaught
**TAGS:** pressure, expectation, mental preparation

As I stand on the bowling crease, I can feel the weight of expectation bearing down on me. The Indian batting lineup, touted to be one of the greatest in the world, is about to unleash a storm of bowling attacks. The pressure to perform is palpable, and I try to focus on the task at hand. I recall the countless hours I've spent in the gym, honing my skills, and the countless one-day games I've played. The knowledge that I'm one of the best bowlers in the world gives me a sense of calm, but I know that the Indian batsmen are no pushover. They're masters of counter-attacks, and I know that I'll have to be at the top of my game to keep them at bay.
