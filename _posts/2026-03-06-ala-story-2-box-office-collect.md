Despite being a sequel, the film has surprised everyone by earning only one-fourth of its predecessor's total box office collection. The Kerala Story 2 had initially hoped to cross the Rs 25 crore mark, but so far, it has managed to gather around Rs 15 crore.

The film's success can be attributed to the strong word-of-mouth and positive reviews from critics. The Kerala Story 2 has been a sleeper hit, and its success is a testament to the power of word-of-mouth in the Indian film industry. As the film continues to perform well, its chances of crossing the Rs 25 crore mark are looking brighter than ever.

The Kerala Story 2's journey to success is a fascinating one, and it's a reminder that even the most seemingly unlikely films can achieve massive success with the right combination of talent, timing, and audience reception.
