As the sun dipped into the horizon, the air was electric with anticipation. The game was all but over, with R Ashwin's side cruising to a comfortable victory. But in a stunning reversal, Ishan Kishan, the diminutive fast bowler, had other plans. With a ferocity that belied his size, he took on the opposition's best hitters, refusing to back down. Ashwin, the master of the craft, was taken aback by Kishan's audacity. The 25-year-old's sheer determination and skill had the bowler's heart racing. In a testament to his unyielding spirit, Ishan Kishan had left Ashwin no choice but to succumb to his onslaught. The 'audacious' run had left the crowd in awe, and Ashwin, humbled by Kishan's performance, could only salute the young gun's bravery.
