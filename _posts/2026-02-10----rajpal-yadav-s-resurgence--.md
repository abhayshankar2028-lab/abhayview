The storm surrounding Rajpal Yadav continues to rage on. Despite being vilified, the actor remains unwavering in his commitment to his art. With the support of several celebrities and politicians, Yadav's reputation begins to erode, but it also gains a newfound respect. Tej Pratap Yadav, a long-time admirer, takes to social media to pledge his support, throwing Rs 11 lakh at Yadav's legal defense fund. KRK, the music label, offers Rs 10 lakh to help Yadav weather the storm. The gesture is a calculated move, aimed at salvaging his reputation and salvaging his career.
