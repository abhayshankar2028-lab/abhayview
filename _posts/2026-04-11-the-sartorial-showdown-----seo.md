TLE: "The Sartorial Showdown"**
**SEO Desc: "Meryl Streep approves Karan Johar's Prada shoes, Anne Hathaway quizzes him"**
**TAGS: Meryl Streep, Karan Johar, Prada shoes, Anne Hathaway**

As the lights dimmed, the envious crowd held their collective breath. Karan Johar, resplendent in his tailored attire, stood confidently before the esteemed judges. Meryl Streep, the undisputed queen of sartorial splendor, beamed with approval as she gazed upon the Prada shoes that had caught her eye. "Ah, Mr. Johar, these are indeed exquisite," she murmured, her voice dripping with sophistication.

Anne Hathaway, never one to be outdone, swooped in with a quizzical expression. "Karan, darling, how did you manage to procure these without breaking the bank?" Her eyes twinkled with mischief as she zeroed in on the price tag. Karan chuckled good-naturedly, "A mere Rs 1.25 lakh, Anne! A steal, if I do say so myself." The crowd erupted into applause, and even Meryl Streep couldn't help but smile.
