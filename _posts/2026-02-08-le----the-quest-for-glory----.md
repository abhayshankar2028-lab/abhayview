** As the train rumbles through the Himalayas, three friends, Rohan, Aryan, and Nirmal, sit side by side, their eyes fixed on the World Cup logo emblazoned on the screen in front of them. They've traveled over 30 hours to be in this moment, to witness the tournament that has captured their hearts. The journey has been grueling, with four matches to watch, but the camaraderie and shared excitement have kept them going. As the tournament progresses, the stakes grow higher, but the friends' unwavering hope keeps them moving forward, fueled by the knowledge that they are one step closer to realizing their dream of watching the T20 World Cup together.
