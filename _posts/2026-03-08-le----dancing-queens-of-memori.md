**TAGS:** "Nita Ambani Aishwarya Rai Abhishek Bachchan Salman Khan Chaiyya Chaiyya"

In the sweltering heat of a Mumbai evening, the grandeur of the Reliance Centre came alive as Nita Ambani, Aishwarya Rai, and Abhishek Bachchan gathered to dance to the iconic song of Salman Khan, "Chaiyya Chaiyya". As the beats pulsed through the air, the three queens of Indian entertainment let go of their inhibitions and let their inner divas shine. With every step and every twirl, they relived the magic of their collaboration in the film "Bajrangi Bhaijaan". Memories of laughter, tears, and triumphs flooded their faces as they lost themselves in the rhythm. As the song came to an end, the three women hugged each other, their bond stronger than ever.
