As I stood in the midst of the glitzy movie industry, Karan Johar's words echoed in my mind like a siren's call. "Stop doing PR," he urged, his eyes burning with intensity. "Let your achievements speak for themselves." His words struck a chord, resonating deep within the breast of every Bollywood producer and director.

In an era where every sequel, every remake, every franchise was touted as the next big hit, it was high time for the big names to stop peddling their PR. The truth was out there, and it was time to listen. Karan's words were a clarion call to authenticity, to the raw emotion and unbridled passion that made Bollywood great.

But as I gazed out at the sea of smiling faces, I couldn't help but wonder: would it be enough?
