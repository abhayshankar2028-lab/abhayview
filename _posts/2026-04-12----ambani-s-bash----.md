I'm Harsha Bhogle, and I'm here to paint a picture of the most epic party of the year - Anant Ambani's birthday bash. The lights were shining bright, the music was pulsating, and the guest list was who's who of Bollywood royalty. As the night wore on, Shah Rukh Khan and Ranveer Singh took to the dance floor to Kailash Kher's 'Bam Lahiri'. The sounds of the song filled the air, and for a moment, it was just them - the two friends grooving to the beat. The camera panned out, and the party continued late into the night. The Ambani family was having the time of their lives, and I was honored to be there to capture it all.
