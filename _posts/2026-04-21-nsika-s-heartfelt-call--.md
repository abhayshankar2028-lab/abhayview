Hansika Motwani sat amidst the chaos, her world crumbling around her. She had called off her marriage, a decision that sent shockwaves through her family. Her parents, though initially disappointed, were the first to rally behind her. "They never told you what to do," her mother said, her voice laced with compassion. Hansika's father nodded in agreement, his eyes filled with understanding. Together, they listened as Hansika poured out her heart, her emotions raw and exposed. For the first time, she felt truly heard, truly seen. And with her family's unwavering support, she began to heal, to rediscover herself in the process.
