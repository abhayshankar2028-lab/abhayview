As the sun set over the bustling streets of Mumbai, Manoj Muntashir sat in his office, pondering the weight of his words. The latest controversy surrounding AR Rahman's comments about communal bias had left him speechless. Muntashir thought back to his days as a young journalist, determined to shed light on the complexities of society. He remembered the countless times he had weighed his words carefully, never wanting to cause harm or perpetuate misconceptions. Now, as he navigated the tumultuous waters of public opinion, he realized that even the most well-intentioned words could have devastating consequences.
