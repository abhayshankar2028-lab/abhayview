**SEO_DESC: Virat Kohli's run of 75 in the 2nd ODI against New Zealand falls short as New Zealand win their first ODI bilateral series in India**
**TAGS: Kohli, Virat, New Zealand, India, ODI Series, Loss**

Harsha Bhogle (that's me!): I've been watching Virat Kohli battle it out with the best of them, but tonight, he just couldn't quite reach the magic. The 2nd ODI against New Zealand was supposed to be Kohli's biggest test yet, but his display was marred by a slow start and a lack of boundaries. I mean, the man is a master of hitting sixes, but as he walked into the iconic Eden Gardens, he just couldn't seem to find the form. New Zealand batted and batted, and I couldn't help but feel a mix of emotions - disappointment for Kohli, but also a sense of pride for the Kiwis. They finally broke the unbeaten streak, and New Zealand celebrated their first ODI bilateral series win in India.
