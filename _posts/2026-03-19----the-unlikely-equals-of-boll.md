**TAGS:** Bollywood Hero Stereotype, Anupam Kher, Shah Rukh Khan, Akshay Kumar

As Anupam Kher walked onto the set of a high-profile Bollywood project, he couldn't help but feel a sense of excitement and trepidation. Among the stars, two names stood out as potential co-stars: Shah Rukh Khan and Akshay Kumar. The thought of working together, as equals, was a bold move. "If you're working with these two," Anupam began, "we're not just stars, we're partners. We're equals." He looked around the room, meeting the eye of the lead actor, Shah Rukh Khan. "We're not here to play the hero, we're here to tell a story. Together, we can do anything." The air was electric with anticipation as Anupam and Shah Rukh shared a laugh. It was a moment of mutual respect, a step towards breaking the mold of the Bollywood hero stereotype.
