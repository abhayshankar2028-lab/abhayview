As the sun rose over the Kolkata streets, Brett Lee would often arrive at the Eden Gardens, the venue of Kolkata Knight Riders' IPL matches. Shah Rukh Khan, the team's star player, would greet him with a warm smile. "An absolute gentleman," he would say, as he handed Lee a steaming cup of tea. "I need that strength before the game," Lee would reply, as he took a sip. Khan would then launch into a pep talk, his voice filled with passion and conviction. "You're not just playing for yourself, Brett. You're representing the team, your family, and your fans. Believe in yourself, and the rest will fall into place." These words of wisdom would inspire Lee to perform at his best, and the Knight Riders would often emerge victorious.
