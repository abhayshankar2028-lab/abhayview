As I sit here, pen in hand, I am reminded of the fragility of life and the unyielding power of the human spirit. Saba Azad, a bright light in the medical world, lies in hospital, her body ravaged by a pesky parasite. I recall the words of my dear friend, Hrithik Roshan, who has been by her side, a steadfast presence that lifts her spirits. As she slowly recovers, Saba's determination is a beacon of hope. With each passing day, her weight begins to return, a testament to her unyielding will. Though her body may weaken, her spirit remains unbroken.
