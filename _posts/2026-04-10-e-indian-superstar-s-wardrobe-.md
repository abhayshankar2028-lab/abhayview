As I sat in the airport, sipping my coffee, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. Sameera Reddy, the Indian superstar, was rumoured to be carrying bags worth a staggering Rs 4 lakh and jackets worth Rs 2.5 lakh. But, as I watched her walk through the terminal, I realized that her fashion choices were not quite on point. Her clothes were too tight, too revealing, and too... basic. It was as if she was trying to fit into a world she didn't fully understand. The 'Everybody in the industry carried these bags at the airport' line became a harsh reality check. Sameera Reddy's wardrobe crisis was real, and it was a reminder that even the most glamorous of stars can sometimes look a bit... bland.
