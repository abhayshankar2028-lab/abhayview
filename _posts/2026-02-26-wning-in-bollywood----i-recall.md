I recall the day Priyanka Chopra finally found her footing in Bollywood like a stone cast into still waters. She was lost, a fish out of water, in a sea of unfamiliar faces, but with each passing day, she gained a sense of purpose. The pressure mounted, and she felt like she was being squeezed into a role that wasn't hers. "I couldn't breathe," she confided in me. "I felt cornered, suffocating under the weight of expectation." It was as if the industry, with all its glitz and glamour, had become a prison, holding her back from realizing her true potential. Yet, in that moment, Priyanka felt a sense of liberation, a newfound understanding that the journey was worth it.
