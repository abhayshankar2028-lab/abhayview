RUN DHAWAN'S FATE AT STAKE: THE VASHU BHAHNANI CASE**

SEO_DESC: "Varun Dhawan's film release in shambles as Vashu Bhagnani sues makers for Rs 400 cr"
TAGS: Vashu Bhagnani, Varun Dhawan, lawsuit, movie release

In a dramatic twist, the release of Varun Dhawan's highly anticipated film has hit a roadblock. Vashu Bhagnani, the film's producer, has sued the makers for a staggering Rs 400 crore. The lawsuit claims that the producers have breached their contractual obligations, leading to the delay in the film's release. As the producers seek to salvage the situation, the entire film release date is now in jeopardy. The fate of Varun Dhawan's career hangs in the balance, and it remains to be seen whether the makers can recover from this bitter lawsuit.
