In the quiet moments, when the cricket world is hushed, Mukul Choudhary finds himself gazing up at the sky, his eyes fixed on the man who once wore the M.S. Dhoni jersey. The captain's humility, his dedication to his craft, and his unwavering commitment to his teammates - all these qualities resonate deeply with Mukul. As he watches the youngsters, he sees glimpses of the 2011 World Cup-winning captain in Faf du Plessis, a look of determination and focus that echoes the Dhoni spirit. These moments fuel Mukul's passion, driving him to become the best he can be, to leave a lasting impact on the game.
