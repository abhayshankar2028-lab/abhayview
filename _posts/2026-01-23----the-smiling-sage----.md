**TAGS:** Varun Dhawan, Karan Johar, Border 2, content creators, clickbait, noise-making

As the curtains closed on Border 2, the Indian film industry witnessed a frenzy of trolls. Varun Dhawan's smile, often touted as one of the most captivating in cinema history, was met with a barrage of hate comments. But Karan Johar, the film's director, stood by his star, calling out the trolls and content creators who sought to exploit his smile for their own gain.

"Those who seek to click on a smile, rather than genuinely connect with the story, are a noisemaker," Johar tweeted. "Let's be honest, who among us hasn't clicked on a meme or a clickbait headline? The truth is, we're all noise-makers and clickbait-seekers. Let's focus on creating meaningful content, rather than fleeting trends."

As the industry grappled with Johar's words, it became clear that the divide between those who created engaging content and those who sought to manipulate them would never be bridged. But Karan Johar's defense of Varun Dhawan served as a reminder that true artistry is not about seeking clicks, but about creating something genuine and meaningful.
