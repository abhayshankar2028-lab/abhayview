In the heart of Mumbai, a battle for dominance was about to unfold. Farhan Akhtar, the king of Excel, had his sights set on securing the exit of his long-time collaborator, Ranveer Singh. The Rs 40 crore gambit was a direct challenge to Ranveer's Pralay, the 4th installment of Don 3. With Excel's superior calculations, Farhan was confident of emerging victorious. Ranveer, however, was not one to back down from a fight. The stage was set for a high-stakes showdown, with the future of Don 3 hanging precariously in the balance. Only time would tell who would emerge victorious in this battle of wits.
