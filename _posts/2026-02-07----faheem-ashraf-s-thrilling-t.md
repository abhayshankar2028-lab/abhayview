** The sold-out crowd at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium erupted in cheers as Faheem Ashraf, Pakistan's wicketkeeper-batsman, single-handedly led his team to a stunning 3-wicket victory over Netherlands in the tournament opener. With the score tied at 9/2 in 15.3 overs, Ashraf took to the crease and delivered a career-best 21 not out, holding his nerve until the very end. The 24-year-old Pakistani sensation had an incredible 30-ball cameo, hitting two boundaries and a six to seal the win. As the Pakistani team celebrated their historic victory, the Dutch team was left to ponder what could have been.
