As I sit in the stillness of the evening, my mind wanders to the memory of that fateful day when Archana Puran Singh's pride swelled like the Indian coast. Her son, a young boy with a fire in his eyes, stood tall as he scored four glorious goals against Pakistan. The roar of the crowd, the rush of adrenaline, it was all so exhilarating. But what made it even more special was the decision Archana made to move him to London, so he could join the elite ranks of the English Premier League. As I reflect on those moments, I am reminded of the unwavering love and support that Archana has always shown to her family. Her son's dreams, and her own, were made possible by her unshakeable faith in him.
