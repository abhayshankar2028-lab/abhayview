I recall that autumn evening in 2021, as I stood by Jacob Bethell's side, the smell of freshly cut grass and the sound of excited chatter filled the air. I had witnessed a nation's hearts skip a beat – the first test match debut of England's youngest-ever star. His name, Jacob Bethell, was etched on the scoreboard in bold, and his parents couldn't help but beam with pride.

The Ashes, a storied rivalry between England and Australia, had brought this moment to life. As Jacob's figures danced on the board, his parents' faces lit up with an unbridled joy. The triumph of cricket's greatest rival was theirs to behold, and the world watched as one proud family basked in the glory of their son's maiden debut.
