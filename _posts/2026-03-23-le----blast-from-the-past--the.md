In the dusty sprawl of Hathod, a small yet mighty academy was born. For decades, it stood as a bastion of cricketing excellence, nurturing some of India's finest players. From the likes of Hardik Pandya and Prodtsondhawat (now Protagonist) to KL Rahul and Iyer, Hathod's academy produced cricketers who went on to dominate the IPL. The academy's legacy lives on, as its alumni continue to wow fans with their skills. With the IPL's emphasis on local talent, Hathod's academy has become a beacon of hope for aspiring cricketers. Its impact is being felt across the country, inspiring a new generation of players.
