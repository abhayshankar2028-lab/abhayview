It was a stormy day when Ranveer Singh, the star of Dhurandhar's hit song Kantara, burst into the Karnataka High Court, his eyes brimming with tears. The controversy had taken a toll on him, and he was ready to lay it all to rest. With a deep breath, he began to speak, his voice shaking as he revealed his true feelings. "I want to apologize," he said, his words dripping with sincerity. "I realize my actions were not only hurtful but also hurtful to my family. I want to offer an unconditional apology to the state of Karnataka and the people of Bengaluru." As he spoke, the courtroom fell silent, waiting for his words to sink in.
