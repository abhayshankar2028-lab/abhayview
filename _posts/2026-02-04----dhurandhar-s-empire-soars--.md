**TAGS:** Netflix, Dhurandhar, Tere Ishk Mein, Kapil Sharma, Indian TV shows

As the curtains closed on another season of the highly anticipated Dhurandhar TV show, the numbers were in, and the verdict was clear - Dhurandhar had emerged victorious, dethroning its long-time rival, Tere Ishk Mein. The show's global viewership had reached unprecedented heights, with a staggering 10 million viewers tuning in each week. Kapil Sharma's show, once a stalwart presence on Indian television, had failed to make it to the top 10, its once-thriving audience dwindling away. The question on everyone's mind was - what had gone wrong? Only time would tell.
