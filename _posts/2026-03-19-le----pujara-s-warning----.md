tle:** Pujara's Warning

**SEO_DESC:** "Pujara cautions Delhi Capitals skipper, urging him to dress practically for the IPL"

**TAGS:** cricket, IPL, Pujara, casual wear

As I sit in my quiet London flat, I can still remember the eerie silence of the Delhi Capitals' practice ground on a crisp winter morning. Amidst the chaos, I spotted Suryakumar Yadav walking towards the dressing room, seemingly lost in thought. His eyes, however, were fixed on something else - the fashion choices of his skipper.

I couldn't resist intervening, and with a gentle nudge, I asked him why he was favoring those soft, casual shirts and sweatpants. His response sent shivers down my spine: "He should not be extra casual." I nodded in agreement, knowing that a champion's game is often played with precision, not pampered comfort. The IPL is just around the corner, and I fear for the Capitals' chances if their skipper doesn't take this warning to heart.
