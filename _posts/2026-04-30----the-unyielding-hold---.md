But then, in an instant, it all changed. Rabada's pace and wits proved too much for Kohli, and the Indian captain was out for a duck. The Gaurs' lead was now 70 runs. But it was not just the score that mattered - it was the price they paid for it. In the end, it was Holder who fell for a duck, and the Gaurs celebrated a historic victory. The crowd erupted in cheers as the Gaurs' captain lifted the trophy aloft, a triumphant smile on his face.
