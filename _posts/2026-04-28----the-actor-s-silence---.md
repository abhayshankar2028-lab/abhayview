In the twilight of his career, Kanwarjit Paintal sat alone, his eyes scanning the cityscape as if searching for a mirror to reflect his own reflection. The 80-year-old had seen it all - the highs and lows, the triumphs and tribulations. But as he gazed out at the faces passing by, he noticed a peculiar phenomenon - people flocking to his every post, their eyes glued to the feed like a moth to flame. "Don't have followers on Instagram," he said with a shrug, a hint of a smile playing on his lips. "I'm not an actor, just a man who's lived a life of make-believe."
