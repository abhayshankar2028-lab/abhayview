It was Axar's unique blend of skill, strength, and guile that made him the real game-changer. He may not have scored the most runs, but his impact was felt far and wide. Pakistan's bowlers were baffled by his ability to adapt, to change the game on a whim. Axar Patel was the master of disguise, the maestro of the spin ball. He was the real weapon against Pakistan, the secret ingredient that would seal India's victories. And on those treacherous pitches, Axar Patel was the one they feared most.
