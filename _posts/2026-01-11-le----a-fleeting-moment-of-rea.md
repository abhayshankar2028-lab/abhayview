Body:

In the early days of my college life, I found myself consumed by the relentless pursuit of perfection. The weight of expectation hung heavy on my shoulders as I struggled to maintain an elusive ideal. But all of that changed one fateful day. I had embarked on a strict diet regime, desperately desperate to shed the extra pounds that seemed to mock me.

As I stood on the edge of my bed, sweat-drenched and gasping for air, I felt the familiar tug of panic. My heart skipped a beat as I realized I was about to faint. My mother, a woman I'd always admired for her unwavering support, rushed into my room, her words a cold balm to my shaken soul. "Karan, why are you so thin?" she asked, her voice laced with concern. I flailed away from her, overwhelmed by the truth – I wasn't on Ozempic, I was just tired. And as the tears streamed down my face, I knew that I had to defy my mother's expectations, take control of my body, and forge a new path that was mine alone.
