I still chuckle thinking about the time I accidentally endorsed Katrina Kaif's makeup brand. It all started when Katrina approached me with an offer. I was thrilled to be a part of this exciting new venture. Little did I know, it was just another one of those misadventures I get myself into. Katrina sent me a few samples of her new line, and I was convinced I was going to be the face of the brand. Fast forward a few days, and I'm at a photoshoot, and Katrina comes up to me with a big smile on her face. "Vicky, you're going to be the face of my new makeup line," she says, beaming. I'm like, "Oh, wow! I'm so honored." I'm not paying her for this, I'm telling her. But she just laughs and says, "Don't worry, I'll pay you later." Later turns out to be a week, and then a month. I'm still waiting for that payment. Katrina and I have since had a good laugh about it, and I've even started using her makeup line myself. Who knew being a part of a bad endorsement deal could lead to a great partnership?

As I look back, I realize that sometimes the best things in life come with a few bumps along the way. And for me, that bump was a hilarious and memorable photo shoot with Katrina Kaif.
