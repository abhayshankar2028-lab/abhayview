"I remember the day Tanishaa Mukerji sat me down and said, 'Aditya Chopra asked me to tone my body, faced hormonal imbalance.' Her words cut deep, like a wound that refuses to heal. As a journalist, I've always been fascinated by the complexities of the human experience. But hearing Tanishaa's story, I realized that our struggles are not just physical, but also mental. Her journey from self-doubt to self-acceptance is a testament to the power of resilience. It's a reminder that we all have the capacity to grow and change, even in the face of adversity. Tanishaa's story is a gentle reminder to look beyond the surface, to the beauty that lies within."
