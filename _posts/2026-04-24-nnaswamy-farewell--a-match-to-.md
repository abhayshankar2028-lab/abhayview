The 29-year-old batsman, known for his incredible strokeplay, was on fire as he took a hat-trick in the final over. Devdutt Padikkal, the Goa youngster, played a crucial role in the innings, anchoring the team with his solidity. It was a fitting farewell to the Chinnaswamy Stadium, where Kohli and his RCB team will leave behind a legacy of cricketing greatness.
