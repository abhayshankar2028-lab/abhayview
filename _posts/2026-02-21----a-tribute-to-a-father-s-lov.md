As the sun set on the picturesque evening, Amitabh Bachchan sat by his son Abhishek's side, beaming with pride. It had been a few days since Aishwarya Rai Bachchan's adorable birthday wish had touched Abhishek's heart. The elder Bachchan, known for his sharp tongue, had finally found a way to express his gratitude to his son. He smiled, his eyes twinkling with mirth, as he said, "Abhishek, you are a considerate partner. Your support on my birthday was truly appreciated." Abhishek's eyes welled up with tears as he replied, "Dad, you always know how to make me feel special." In that moment, the love between father and son shone brighter than any star in the night sky.
