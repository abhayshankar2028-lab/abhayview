It was a chilly winter evening when I received the news that sent my world crashing down. Shakti Mohan, the choreographer behind Sanjay Leela Bhansali's magnum opus, Padmaavat, had refused to receive her rightful payment for her innovative 'Nainowale Ne' sequence. The reason, she revealed, was not the financial burden but the crushing weight of expectation. "People hold their breath around you," she said, "as if your every move is a testament to their own creativity." I couldn't help but think of the countless hours I spent perfecting that dance, only to be met with a dismissive wave of a hand. The true cost of artistic expression is not gold or recognition, but the unseen hand that shapes our careers.
