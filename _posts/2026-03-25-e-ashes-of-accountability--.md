"In the hallowed halls of Lord's, where legends are born,
A patronizing silence shrouds the Ashes' storied form.
Former captains, once honored, now voice their discontent,
As ECB's ivory tower façade crumbles, revealing a lack of intent.

Alec Atherton's 'struggles' with AB de Villiers, a 'misunderstanding' so grand,
Aussie's 'inconsistencies' that left fans scratching their head.
The England team's woes fuelled by a system that's been asleep,
Leaving players to grapple with the weight of ECB's jaded deep.

Cricket's greatest game, once a symbol of pride and might,
Now appears threadbare, a parlour game, devoid of light.
 captains demand action, accountability, and an end to the shame,
As ECB trembles at their words, a governing body in distress, in flames."
