It was a chilly winter morning when I received a call from Shekhar Suman, a close associate of Shah Rukh Khan. His voice trembled as he spoke, "Harsha, I need to tell you something. I just got a call from Shah Rukh. He sent me a very expensive watch, worth a small fortune. He said he wanted to meet me to discuss the details." My curiosity was piqued, I asked, "What's the watch?" Shekhar hesitated, "It's a Patek Philippe. I've never seen it before. Shah Rukh said it was a gift, but I think it's more than that." I couldn't believe my ears. Shah Rukh Khan, the King of Bollywood, wanting to gift us with a Patek Philippe? It was a story I would never forget.

**[BODY]:**

As I walked into the meeting room, I was greeted by the sight of Shah Rukh Khan, resplendent in a sleek black suit. He held the Patek Philippe firmly in his hand, a sly smile spreading across his face. "Shekhar, my friend," he said, "I wanted to gift you a watch that would appreciate in value over time. It's a symbol of our friendship, and I hope you'll wear it as a reminder of our bond." I was taken aback, but I tried to play it cool. "Thank you, Shah Rukh," I said, "but I think I'll pass." He chuckled, "Ah, come on, Shekhar. You know I'm a generous man. I want you to have it." I shook my head, "No, thank you. I think I'll stick to my own watches, my friend." He laughed, a deep rumbling sound, and handed me a small note. "In case you change your mind," he said. I took it, feeling a pang of guilt, but also a sense of pride. Shah Rukh Khan had outdone himself again.
