In the sterile darkness of Lilavati hospital's ICU, a gentle soul held his breath, his spirit afire with a quiet determination. Salim Khan, the stalwart stable's gentle giant, had been brought by his family doctor, his movements slow and deliberate, a testament to the weight of his years. As he lay still, his eyes, like two polished stones, reflected the flickering lights of the machinery surrounding him. The medical staff moved with reverence, their footsteps a soft susurrus, as they tended to his frail body. And yet, despite the chaos that surrounded him, Salim Khan remained a steadfast presence, a symbol of the enduring power of the human spirit.
