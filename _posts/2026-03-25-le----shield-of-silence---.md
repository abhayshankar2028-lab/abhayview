As I sit in the quiet of the evening, I am met with the soft glow of the lamp beside me. My mother's eyes, once bright with the fire of a thousand stars, now dimly shine as she reminisces about the hardships she endured during my father's era of rejection. The harsh words, the cold shoulders, the whispered doubts - all these she kept hidden, shielding my father's reputation from prying eyes. Yet, it was on those silent nights, when the world outside receded, that my father's true heart was laid bare. His love for my mother was a shield, a protective cloak that shielded them both from the world's cruel judgments. In those moments, I saw the strength of their unbreakable bond, a bond that would forever be the foundation of my family's resilience.
