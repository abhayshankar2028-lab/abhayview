** Shubman Gill, one of the most exciting young cricketers in the world, has taken his skills to a new level by joining the list of critics for the Impact Player rule in IPL. The 24-year-old has been vocal about the unfairness of the rule, which strips players of their creative freedom and ability to perform at their best. "I'm not just talking about the on-field action," Gill said in an interview. "It's about the skill that's being taken out of the game. I believe that if we can bring it back, we can make cricket a more exciting and unpredictable sport." As he joins the ranks of critics like Arun Pandey and Virender Sehwag, Gill is challenging the status quo and bringing a new perspective to the game.
