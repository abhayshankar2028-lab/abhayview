Iulia Vantur strides down the red carpet like a queen, her entourage of photographers and stylists mere after her. Her eyes scan the room, a vision of beauty and glamour. As she passes by, she blows two, three flying kisses to the crowd, her signature move sending the onlookers into a frenzy.
