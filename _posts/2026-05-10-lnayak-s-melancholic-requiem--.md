In the twilight of a city's scream,
Where steel and concrete reign supreme,
Sonika Gill, the Khalnayak's daughter true,
Lost her world, and with it, her heart anew.

Her father's fierce passion, now a distant past,
Echoes through her life, forever to last.
But fate, it seems, has other plans in store,
Leaving her with a husband, and a heart that's poor.

His love, a flame that once burned bright and bold,
Now flickers dim, as he struggles to hold.
For Sonika's sake, he must face the pain,
And learn to love again, in this, his new refrain.
