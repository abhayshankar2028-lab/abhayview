**SEO_DESC:** "A tale of passion, loyalty, and the unbreakable bond between two heartthrobs - Bobby Deol and Ameesha Patel, as a crowd of Tara Singh fans refuses to let their beloved star hug their BFF, Ameesha."

As the sun set over the bustling streets of Mumbai, a sea of Tara Singh fans swelled, their excitement and anticipation palpable. But little did they know, this was no ordinary gathering. Ameesha Patel, the star of the lot, was to be joined by her BFF, Bobby Deol, in a special appearance. The anticipation was palpable as the fans eagerly awaited the moment when Tara Singh, their idol, would finally share a warm hug with his dear friend.

But fate had other plans. A fierce mob emerged, their faces twisted in rage, as they shouted curses and insults. "She belongs to Tara Singh!" they chanted, their voices echoing through the streets. The fans, caught in the fervor, refused to budge, their loyalty to Tara Singh unwavering.
