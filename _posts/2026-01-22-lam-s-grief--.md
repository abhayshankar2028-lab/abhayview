In the dimly lit corridors of the film's set, a scene unfolded that would forever etch itself in Rani Mukerji's memory. It was the day she heard the infamous line that Ghulam had to endure: "Aamir told me your voice is not apt." The words cut like a knife, piercing her very soul.

As she stood there, frozen in time, the pain of those words resonated deeply within her. She recalled the countless hours spent perfecting her voice, the doubts that lingered when the director's vision failed to align with hers. The tears began to fall, as the weight of her own mistakes began to sink in. The film's soundtrack swelled, a poignant reminder of the heartbreak that had been inflicted upon her.
