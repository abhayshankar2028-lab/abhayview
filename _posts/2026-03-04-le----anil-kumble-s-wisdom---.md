**SEO_DESC:** Anil Kumble shares his advice to Varun Chakaravarthy as the Indian bowler faces off against England in the T20 World Cup semifinal

"Mix in some that goes away," Anil Kumble had whispered to Varun Chakaravarthy before the semifinal match against England. The young bowler's eyes widened with intrigue as Kumble's words hung in the air like a whisper from the past. "Know your limits, Varun," Kumble had cautioned. "Don't try to be something you're not. Be the wall that England needs to break." Chakaravarthy remembered the intense focus he had brought to the pitch, the unyielding will to succeed. As he took the mound, he felt a surge of determination course through his veins. The game was about to begin, and he was ready to unleash his best.
