"Like AB de Villiers, who ruled the crease with an iron fist before his body betrayed him, Virat Kohli has been dealt a blow too soon," Allan Donald penned his latest missive. "For the Indian captain, it's a painful reminder that cricket's best players don't always play for their country. His Test cricket career was cut short, and with it, a golden chapter. Kohli will be deeply missed, not only by Indians but also by cricket fans worldwide."
