As I lock eyes with the Worli 2-BHK, I'm transported back to the moonlit nights I spent sleeping in the living room. The memories are hazy, but the nostalgia is palpable. I recall the countless hours I spent playing hide-and-seek with my siblings, the sound of the tandoor's sizzling aroma wafting through the air, and the sound of Rupali Ganguly's laughter as she'd scold us for our mischief. Anupamaa, the iconic actress, grew up in this very house, and I imagine her sitting in the living room, reading a book or chatting with friends. The thought fills me with a sense of pride knowing that our lives weren't so different after all. The memories are a reminder of the magic that happens when you least expect it.
