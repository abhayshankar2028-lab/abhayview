**TAGS:** Christianity, Identity, Faith

In the sweltering heat of a Mumbai summer, Remo D'Souza sat amidst the dusty silence of his family's ancestral home. His grandfather, the renowned Bollywood director, sat before him, his eyes ablaze with a fire that seemed to burn hotter with each passing moment. "Remo, my son," he began, his voice low and measured, "your name is a sacred trust. Don't change it. Don't ever change it."

As Remo listened, his mind reeled with the weight of his grandfather's words. A burden was placed upon him, a responsibility to honor his father's sacrifice. The director's eyes seemed to bore into his very soul, demanding a commitment to his creed. "You are a Christian, Remo," he repeated, his voice laced with a hint of steel. "You must carry on my legacy, not mine."
