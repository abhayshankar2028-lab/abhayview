**"A Star Ready to Shine on the Global Stage,"**


In a turning point for the Bollywood superstar, Ranbir Kapoor has joined the esteemed TIME 100 list. The 35-year-old actor, known for his captivating performances in films like "Khoobsurat" and "Bhaag Milkha Bhaag," has been recognized for his dedication to his craft. Ayushmann Khurrana, a fellow Indian star, has praised Ranbir's "storyteller approach" to entertainment, saying, "He's a master of weaving tales that resonate with a global audience." As the news spreads, fans and industry experts alike are abuzz with excitement. With this achievement, Ranbir Kapoor is cementing his place as one of the most sought-after stars in the world of entertainment.
