Can't Live a Lie**
**Why Pooja Bhatt ended her 11-year-marriage**
**She didn't want children**

In the stillness of the night, I felt Pooja's heartbeat, a gentle reminder of the life we once shared. Eleven years, a lifetime of vows, and yet, I couldn't help but wonder what lay beyond the walls we built. Her eyes, once bright with the promise of a life together, now seemed empty, a canvas waiting for the brushstrokes of another destiny. I recall the words she whispered, "I want to be free," but did she really? The vows we exchanged were a promise to love and cherish, but at what cost? The silence was deafening, a heavy burden that weighed me down. I couldn't live a lie, and so, I stood up, took a step forward, and walked away. The question still lingers, a nagging doubt that haunts me still.
