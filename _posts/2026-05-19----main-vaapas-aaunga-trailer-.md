** In the sweltering summer heat, Diljit Dosanjh's character Vinayak, a young Punjabi man, finds himself torn between his love for a Pakistani woman, Nalini (Naseeruddin Shah), and his duty to his family. As the partition of India unfolds, their love becomes a flame that refuses to be extinguished. Nalini, a determined and brave woman, finds herself caught between the conflicting desires of her family and her love for Vinayak. As the lines between loyalty and love blur, the fate of their love hangs in the balance. Will Vinayak and Nalini find a way to be together, or will the hatred of their families tear them apart?

The memories of that fateful day still linger, a reminder of the sacrifices that were made for love. As the camera pans over the desolate landscape, it's clear that some wounds may never fully heal.
