I stood at the edge of the field, the sun casting long shadows across my face. The excitement of the match still lingered, but the butterflies in my stomach told a different story. It was Shardul Thakur's performance that had left me breathless, his 6-for-12 an unforgettable sight. But as the celebrations died down, the elephant in the room came to the forefront: "How long do you want to stay?" Hardik Pandya's question echoed in my mind like a mantra.

Enough springs of the MRF moneybags, a refrain that echoed through my mind. We've been hopping from franchise to franchise, never staying put for too long. It's a never-ending cycle, one that's taking its toll on these hardworking men. Shardul and Hardik, with their dream of playing in the IPL for years to come, are growing restless. The question is: will they find a way to anchor themselves in one franchise, or will they continue to bounce from team to team? Only time will tell.
