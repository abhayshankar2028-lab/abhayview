TAGS: Bollywood, Anupam Kher, Shah Rukh Khan, Akshay Kumar

As I watched Shah Rukh Khan and Akshay Kumar take the stage, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. The two of them, so confident and charismatic, exuding a power that was almost suffocating. But then, I looked at Anupam Kher, sitting in the audience, and saw something different. He was laughing, smiling, and even occasionally rolling his eyes at their antics. To me, this was a refreshing change from the typical hero stereotype, where the actor is relegated to playing the role of the hero, and the real hero is the audience. We're more than just heroes, we're rebels. And if the actors are willing to challenge that notion, then maybe there's a different kind of hero waiting to be discovered.
