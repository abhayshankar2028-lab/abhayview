In the dimly lit room, Anurag Kashyap sat with a mixture of excitement and trepidation, his 40-page script for Malayalam cinema laid out before him. The director's passion for adapting his work for the local language had sparked a heated debate, with many questioning the viability of his vision. "I have asked the actor to take a look," he said, his voice filled with determination. "But when I saw the script, I knew it was unworkable in Hindi." The director's words hung in the air, a stark reminder that sometimes, the most ambitious dreams are the ones that can't be made in the original language.
