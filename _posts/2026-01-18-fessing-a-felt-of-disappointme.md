In the hazy twilight of forgotten melodies, I often find myself lost in the echoes of a promise that once seemed so bright. Two Oscars can be a burden, but Ar Rahman's whispered words cut deeper still. 'I've lost faith in your genius,' he said, the words a bitter taste that left me questioning my own field. It wasn't the film's commercial success or the critical acclaim that weighed on me – it was the cruel certainty that we'd stopped creating something truly remarkable together. The weight of those words is crushing, a heavy burden I now bear. I have been hurt, and I know I am not alone.
