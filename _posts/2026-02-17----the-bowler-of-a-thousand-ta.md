In the verdant lands of India, where the sun dips into the horizon and paints the sky with hues of crimson and gold, a silver bullet whizzes through the air, a masterclass of fast bowling. Mohammed Shami, the man with the name that echoes the gods, has been the cornerstone of India's bowling attack. But as the months go by, the cracks start to show, and the selectors begin to search for a successor.

Coach Badruddin Siddiqui, the man who has given his life to cricket, sees the fire in his players, the passion that burns bright within them. He knows that Shami's time is running out, and it's up to him to find a bowler who can fill the void. The search begins, and the journey of discovery takes us to the unseen corners of the game, where talent and dedication go hand in hand.
