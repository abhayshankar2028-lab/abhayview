ubham Pundir's Unbeaten Masterpiece**

In the hallowed grounds of M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, a chapter was etched in the annals of cricket history. Shubham Pundir's majestic 115, a testament to his unyielding spirit, left the Karnataka batsmen reeling. As the visitors cruised to a 284/2 lead, the scoreboard read like a poetic refrain: "J&K's quest begins," "Ranji's legacy is made." Yawer Hassan, a stalwart of the Karnataka team, provided a sturdy foundation, while Praveen Kumar's stint in the attack was as smooth as silk. Together, they orchestrated a masterclass that left the J&K bowlers in awe. As the sun dipped into the Bangalore skies, the stage was set for a thrilling contest, one that would see the J&K Searchers rise to the challenge.
