As the Indian team celebrated their historic win over New Zealand in the T20 World Cup final, Harbhajan Singh took the field to shut down the celebrations. With a stern expression, he slammed the door behind him, silencing the ecstatic fans. The stadium's usual vibrant atmosphere was replaced with an eerie silence. "Done and dusted," Singh muttered to himself. The controversy surrounding his outburst had sparked a heated debate, with some questioning his professionalism and others defending his right to express his emotions. As Singh walked off the field, the stadium remained frozen in stunned silence, a testament to the power of the game and the intensity of the competition.
