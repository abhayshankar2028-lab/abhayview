Mistake Surprised Even Me**

By Harsha Bhogle

It was a late afternoon in Mumbai, the sun casting a golden glow over the wicket. The Ranji Trophy match between Bengal and Baroda was underway, with Bengal struggling to contain Baroda's attacking innings. In the 50th over, with Bengal on 150/6, captain Easwaran decided to take a break and grab a cold drink from the shade. As he stood by the crease, he failed to notice a loose ball from the opposing batsman, which came hurtling towards him. The ball hit him squarely on the chest, leaving him dazed and momentarily stunned. The umpire's decision sparked a mix of shock and amusement, as Easwaran stumbled backward, his eyes wide with surprise. "My mistake surprised even me," he muttered, rubbing his chest in embarrassment.
