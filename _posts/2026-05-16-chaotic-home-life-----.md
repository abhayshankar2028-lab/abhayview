As I sat in the living room, watching my father struggle to arrange playdates for my sister's friends, I couldn't help but feel a sense of frustration. In a country where our society is built on strict roles and expectations, it was a challenge to find a single man willing to take on the responsibility of raising our child. My father, Imran Khan, was a devoted father, but the societal norms and expectations of his position made it difficult to find a suitable caregiver. He had to turn down several men, citing the need for a stable and loving home. It was a daunting task, but he was determined to do what was best for our child.
