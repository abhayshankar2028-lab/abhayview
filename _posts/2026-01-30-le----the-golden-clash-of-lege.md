** As I stood on the MCG, watching the sun set over the Indian team's training ground, I couldn't help but think of the cricket world cup that will forever be etched in my heart - the 50 overs World Cup. The thrill of a late-order counter-attack, the beauty of a century from a bowler, the agony of defeat at the hands of a team's sheer batting prowess. This is the world cup that I've always loved, the one that makes me feel like a kid again. Sanjay Manjrekar, the legendary Indian batsman, once told me, 'T20 WC must not be given the same status as 50 overs WC.' I couldn't agree more. The 50 overs format is unique, with its own set of skills and challenges. It's a format that requires teams to be adaptable, to think on their feet, and to execute their plans with precision. The 50 overs WC is the perfect blend of skill, strategy, and physicality, making it the ultimate test of cricketing prowess.
