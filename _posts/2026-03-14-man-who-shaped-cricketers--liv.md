In the crowded corridors of Indian cricket, a quiet hero stands tall. His influence extends far beyond the pitch, where he once scored centuries that changed the game. Sachin Tendulkar, the batting maestro, has quietly shaped the careers of countless cricketers. One phone call at a time, he guided them to greatness. From Kapil Dev to Virat Kohli, the likes of Virat and Virusha owe their careers to a single call from the 'Mr. 100'. The echoes of his voice still resonate in the minds of cricketers, reminding them of the power of a good phone call. A legacy that will be etched in the annals of Indian cricket history.

Harsha Bhogle is a cricket commentator and former Indian national team player.
