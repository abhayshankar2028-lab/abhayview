As I sit in my study, surrounded by memories of the past, a spark of nostalgia ignites within me. The phone rings, and I answer it with a mix of excitement and trepidation. Shah Rukh Khan's voice on the other end sends shivers down my spine. He asks me a question that will change the course of my life. "Shekhar Suman, I want to share something with you. I've got a very expensive watch that I want you to wear on your wrist. It's a rare, limited edition model, and I want you to use it for a good cause." My mind reels as I try to comprehend the enormity of the request. I hesitantly agree, and Shah Rukh Khan sends me a stunning timepiece, a symbol of his enduring affection. "Chalte Chalte, the movie that changed our lives," he whispers, as I look at my new watch with a sense of gratitude and awe.
