As the T20 World Cup approaches, India's team is filled with optimism. Led by captain Virat Kohli, the team has been training tirelessly to face the top teams in the group stage. The group features fierce rivals like Pakistan, Namibia, Netherlands, and the USA. India's victory in the inaugural edition last year has prepared them well for this challenge.

The electrifying cricket will be played at several venues, including the iconic Eden Gardens in Kolkata, the Beinhauer grounds in New Delhi, the Wanderers stadium in Johannesburg, and the historic Diamond Stadium in Los Angeles. The stage is set for an unforgettable tournament, where India will face stiff competition from some of the world's top teams.
