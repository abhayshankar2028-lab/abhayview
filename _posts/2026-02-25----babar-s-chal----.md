As the curtains drew shut on the T20 World Cup, Pakistan's Babar Azam couldn't help but feel a pang of disappointment. Despite having played over 100 matches, his strike rate had plummeted. "Koi baccha thodi hai," he muttered to himself, "I've played more matches than most professionals, but my batting has been inconsistent." The 32-year-old opener's frustration was palpable as he surveyed the pitch. His usual footwork and timing had deserted him, leaving him feeling like a mere spectator. The pressure to perform was mounting, and Azam knew he had to dig deep to find the form that had once made him a force to be reckoned with.
