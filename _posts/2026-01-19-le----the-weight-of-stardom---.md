**TAGS:** Priyanka Chopra, stardom, motherhood, sacrifice, collateral damage

As I sit in my dimly lit room, the city outside is abuzz with the hum of cameras and the chatter of fans, all clamoring for a glimpse of the golden girl, Priyanka Chopra. But behind the curtains, a different story unfolds. Her brother, Shahid Chopra, bears the weight of his sister's stardom, a constant reminder of the sacrifices he makes every day.

Mother Madhu's voice is laced with tears as she recounts the grueling days when Shahid had to put his own desires on the backburner, welcoming his sister to the limelight. "I see him struggling every day," she whispers, her eyes brimming with unshed tears. "But I hold on to the thought that he's doing this for me, for our family's happiness. That's all that matters."
