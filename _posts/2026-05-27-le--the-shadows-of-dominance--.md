tle: "The Shadows of Dominance"**
**SEO_DESC: "A Poem of Prejudice and Identity"**
**TAGS: "prejudice", "identity", "marginalization"**

In the streets of India, where the sun dips into the horizon,
I stood as a stranger, speaking a language that was foreign.
Pankaj Tripathi, a name that echoed through the crowds,
But my words were not a language, they were a voice that was proud.

The villagers assumed, with a glance and a sneer,
That I came from poverty, that my skin was black and clear.
But I spoke of my country, of my culture and my heart,
And they scoffed at my words, and tore my dignity apart.

The shadows of dominance, they whispered to me still,
That I was a foreigner, a stranger in this land that was real.
But I stood tall and proud, my voice unwavering and strong,
For I knew that my words, they were a language that belonged to me all day long.
