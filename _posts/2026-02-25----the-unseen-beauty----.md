**TAGS:** objectification, feminism, Taapsee Pannu, body image

As I sit with Taapsee Pannu, her piercing gaze seems to hold a thousand secrets. She speaks of her journey to stardom, of the obsession that has consumed the film industry. "It's not just about the handsome leading men," she says, her voice a whisper of defiance. "It's about the actresses, the ones who bring depth and complexity to their roles." Her words are a scathing critique of the objectification of women in the film industry. "We're not just objects of desire," she says, "we're human beings with stories to tell." The camera pans out, and I see a film industry in a state of crisis. The obsession with female actors' physical appearance has become an all-consuming force, one that threatens to destroy the very fabric of our society.
