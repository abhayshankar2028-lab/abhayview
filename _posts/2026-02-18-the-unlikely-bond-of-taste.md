TAGS: friendship, loyalty, survival

I still remember the countless meals we shared at Salman's house during those trying times. Our paths had diverged, and our journeys had taken us in different directions. Yet, in the midst of our struggles, a bond of taste remained. We shared simple yet nourishing meals, and in those moments, our spirits lifted. Those were not just meals – they were moments of togetherness. I recall the countless times we laughed, cried, and supported each other through thick and thin. It was a bond that transcended our differences, a testament to the power of friendship and loyalty. Even now, when I close my eyes, I am transported back to those meals, and the memories come flooding back.

As I reflect on those difficult times, I am reminded of the unwavering support of my fellow strugglers – Salman, Karan Johar, and the rest. We were a family, bound by our shared experiences. And in the midst of our struggles, our bond remained unbreakable. Even now, as I look back, I am filled with a sense of gratitude for those meals, and for the unbreakable bond of friendship that they forged.
