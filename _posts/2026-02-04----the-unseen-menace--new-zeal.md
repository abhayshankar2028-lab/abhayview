**TAGS:** T20 World Cup, New Zealand cricket, Harsha Bhogle

As I stood on the cricket field, watching my country's spinners dance with the ball, I couldn't help but feel a twinge of unease. The Indian pacer had seen it all before – the thrill of victory, the agony of defeat – but New Zealand was a different story altogether. Their 2019 tour had been nothing short of spectacular, with their bowlers performing with unprecedented regularity. I'd spoken to some of the lads before the tour, and they spoke of New Zealand as a team that was a major challenge to India. "They're a big challenge to India," one of them had told me. "Their T20s are just as good as ours, if not better." I couldn't argue with that. New Zealand was a force to be reckoned with, and if they continued to perform at this level, they would be a serious threat to our T20 World Cup hopes.
