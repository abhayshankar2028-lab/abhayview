tle:** The Weakest PCB I Have Ever Seen
**SEO_DESC:** The Pakistan Cricket Board's blind faith in star players has left me speechless.
**TAGS:** Pakistan Cricket Board, blind faith, star players

In the hallowed halls of the PCB, a sense of complacency has taken root. The board's decision to blindly endorse the most gifted players has led to a culture of mediocrity. Ahmed Shehzad, a talented young batsman, is the latest to bear the brunt of this. His performances have been marred by a lack of discipline and a tendency to look past his limitations. The PCB's focus on the next big star has blinded them to the potential of the current crop. It's a tragic state of affairs, one that threatens to undermine the very fabric of the game. My heart aches for the future of cricket in Pakistan.
