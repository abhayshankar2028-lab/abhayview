It was a Sunday morning, and Priyanka Chopra was reveling in the tranquil atmosphere of her pool. The warm sun danced across her skin, and the sound of gentle splashing filled the air. She had decided to trade in her cricket whites for a swimsuit and spend the day soaking up the sun and letting loose. "Final toh banta hai," she quipped, as she dove into the depths of her pool, her laughter echoing through the villa.
