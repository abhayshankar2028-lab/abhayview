In the twilight of memories, I recall a night that will haunt me forever. Rani Mukerji and Aditya Chopra, hand in hand, exchanging vows in a secret Italian wedding. Their love was a flame that burned brightly, but I, a man consumed by fear, was about to witness its demise.

Karan Johar, the master manipulator, had woven a web of deceit. As I followed him to the ceremony, I caught him hiding in the shadows, a sly smile spreading across his face. I was still petrified of him, still wary of his insidious charm. How could he betray our trust so deeply? The thought sent shivers down my spine, and I knew I would never forgive him for this betrayal.
