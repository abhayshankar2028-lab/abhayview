As I sat by Hansika's bedside, I couldn't help but wonder what had driven her to make the bold decision to call off her marriage. Tears streaming down her face, she whispered the words I had known she would say: "They never told me what to do." I felt a lump form in my throat as I realized that this was not just a personal crisis, but a cry for help from the very people she trusted most – her family.

Hansika's voice was firm but laced with vulnerability as she shared her struggles with her parents. Her words were a testament to the unconditional love and support they had always provided, despite the pressure to conform to societal expectations. I knew in that moment that her family was not just rallying around her, but also embracing her courage in the face of uncertainty.
