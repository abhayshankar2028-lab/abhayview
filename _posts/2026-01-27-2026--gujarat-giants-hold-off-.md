In a nail-biting match, Gujarat Giants secured their maiden win in the Women's Premier League 2026, defeating Delhi Capitals by 3 runs. The match was a closely contested affair, with Niki Prasad and Sneh Rana leading the charge for Delhi Capitals. However, Giants' bowler, Anuja Patil, proved to be the game-changer, scoring crucial wickets to turn the tide in Giants' favor. The Giants' victory marked a triumph for the underdog team, who had been touted as dark horses throughout the tournament. The win cements their spot in the playoffs and sets the stage for a thrilling final against Mumbai Indians.
