Brownies for Breakfast**
**SEO_DESC: Sara Ali Khan's childhood nostalgia**
**TAGS: childhood, food, nostalgia**

As I sit here, surrounded by the memories of my childhood, I am transported back to a time when breakfast was a sacred ritual. I recall the day I devoured three brownies for breakfast, a mindless indulgence that would become a staple of my day. My mother would wake me up early, and I'd rush to the kitchen, my stomach growling with anticipation. The first bite was like a symphony of chocolatey goodness, and I'd devour the rest in blissful silence. Those brownies were more than just a meal; they were a comfort, a pick-me-up to start the day. As I grew older, my love for food never faded, but that particular breakfast ritual remains etched in my memory forever.
