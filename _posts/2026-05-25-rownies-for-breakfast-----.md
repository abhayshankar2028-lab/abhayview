Brownies for Breakfast**
**SEO_DESC: Sara Ali Khan's Childhood Food Memories**
**TAGS: childhood, food habits, brownies**

"As a student, I was sleep-deprived and rumpled, but something had to fuel me for those late-night study sessions. I still remember the three brownies my mom used to make for me - a sweet escape from my brain clutter. Her handiwork was a testament to her love and patience. I'd devour them by the plateful, the chocolatey taste a fleeting pleasure. But oh, the guilt that followed. They were my 'mindless' breakfast, a fleeting indulgence. Little did I know, those brownies would be the start of a lifelong journey through food, memories, and the complexities of the human mind."
