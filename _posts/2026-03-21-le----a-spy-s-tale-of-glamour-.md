** "It was a chilly winter evening, and the lights of Andaz Apna Apna shone bright. Karisma Kapoor, the queen of glamour, had a secret. She'd send people to spy on Raveena Tandon's costumes, to see what secrets the designer Ashley Reynoble hid behind her designs. The designer would often whisper to Karisma, 'Your clothes are so divine, but what's behind them?' Karisma would smile and reply, 'I don't know, Ashley, but I'll make sure to keep them under wraps.' And so, the game continued, with Karisma and Ashley weaving a web of glamour and intrigue. Little did they know, their actions would one day catch the eye of Raveena Tandon herself."

---

I still remember the nights I'd sneak away from the director's set to watch Karisma Kapoor send out her team to spy on Raveena Tandon's costumes. "Spy on me?" I'd joke, trying to stifle a grin. Ashley Reynoble, the designer, would whisper to Karisma, "Your clothes are so divine, but what's behind them?" Karisma would just smile and say, "I don't know, Ashley, but I'll make sure to keep them under wraps." I'd chuckle and wonder what secrets Ashley was hiding. The designer's designs were always so full of life and movement, yet Karisma seemed to have a keen eye for the subtlest details. I'd often catch her staring intently at a piece, as if trying to unravel its secrets. It was as if she was the only one who truly understood the magic behind Ashley's creations. Those were the nights I'd spend dreaming of a world where Karisma and Ashley's designs were free to roam, unencumbered by the pressures of fame and fortune.
