I stood in the crease, surrounded by the familiar faces of the Delhi Capitals' squad. As I gazed out at the bowlers, a sense of calm washed over me. It was going to be a long day. The Capitals' bowlers were different, their movements fluid and precision executed. Slowly but surely, the Challengers' innings began to unravel. Run after run, the Capitals' players toiled away, their resolve unwavering. And when the final bowler took the wicket, the Delhi Capitals had done it – they had snatched victory from the jaws of defeat, their perfect start shattered.
