[Gaurav Gera's Heartbreak Poem]

I remember the day I fell in love,
A butterfly's flutter, a heart's dove,
In your eyes, my soul took flight,
Forever with you, no other light.

But now, I'm left with shattered dreams,
The love we shared, now but a scream,
I'm trapped in this heart, so lost and blue,
Why must I marry, when my heart is true?

The thought of it haunts me every night,
A constant reminder, of what's not right,
I refuse to give in, to this loveless fate,
I want to break free, from this heartbreak's weight.

I'll cherish the memories, of our love so pure,
But for now, I'll remain, without a cure,
For the heart that beats, with love's sharp pain,
Is now silenced, by the love that remains.
