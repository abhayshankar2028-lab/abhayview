**TAGS:** cricket, KKR, Shubman Gill, Cameron Green

As I sat in the packed Eden Gardens, the excitement of the Kolkata Knight Riders' match still lingering in the air, I couldn't shake off the feeling of unease that had been building up inside me. It wasn't just the looming defeat that had me worried, but the stark contrast between Shubman Gill's form and that of Cameron Green. The Indian opener's numbers had been abysmal, and his batting style seemed to be hitting a wall. As I watched him flail at the ball, I couldn't help but think of the promising signs that had led us to believe he was the key to unlocking KKR's attack. But now, it seemed like a distant memory, and I was left wondering if he was the right man for the job.
