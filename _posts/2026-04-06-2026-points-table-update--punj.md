3. Kolkata Knight Riders - 6 points
4. Mumbai Indians - 5 points

As the Punjab Kings bask in the glory of their hard-fought win, they're aware that the real challenge is yet to come. The road to the IPL title is long and arduous, but they're ready to face the next opponent with their trademark swagger. The stage is set for a thrilling IPL 2026 season.
