As the sun dipped behind the stadium walls, a sense of exhaustion washed over Sanju Samson. The grueling series against New Zealand had taken its toll, leaving him drained and battered. Despite the valiant efforts of his teammates, Samson's body cried for respite. "I'm not just tired, I'm hurt too," he confided to the waiting media. Gambhir, his teammate and a fellow warrior, beamed with pride. "Always knew he'd deliver, Sanju. He's the heart of this team." As Gambhir offered a reassuring pat on the back, Samson knew he was exactly where he needed to be – on a well-deserved break.
