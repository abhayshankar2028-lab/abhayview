As the curtains closed on the Grammy Awards, the crowd erupted into cheers and chants of "Rahman! Rahman!" The maestro had once again woven his magical spell, leaving us all in awe. But little did I know, this would not be the case for some. Amidst the celebration, a heated debate erupted, with some questioning Ar Rahman's "saintly" nature. But I knew better. For behind every grand composition, there lies a heart that beats with compassion and empathy. Anuradha Paudwal, the Indian playback singer and composer, stood firmly by her beloved Ar Rahman, calling out the baseless rumors. "His music is not just a symphony of sound, but a reflection of his soul," she said. "He is a shining example of what it means to be a good human being."
