As the golden sun sets over Mumbai, the Ambani empire stretches its dominion, its 50% stake in Karan Johar's Dharma Productions a bulwark against the rising tide of competition from the likes of Mukesh Ambani and Gautam Adani. With a wealth of Rs 3 lakh crore, the Ambani family has eclipsed the likes of Adar Poonawalla and his family, the founders of Reliance Industries. Their grip on the Indian behemoth is unyielding, their influence as insidious as a cancer.
