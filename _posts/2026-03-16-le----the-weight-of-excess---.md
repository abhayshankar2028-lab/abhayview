**SEO_DESC:** "Shahid Afridi rips apart Pakistan selectors for benching experienced ODI players for Bangladesh series" - cricket commentator Harsha Bhogle

In the verdant fields of Dhaka, a chorus of discontent echoed through the stands, as the Pakistan cricket team suffered a crushing blow. The decision to bench seasoned players like Shahid Afridi, Wasim Akram, and Inzamul Haque in favor of unproven youngsters sparked outrage and bewilderment. "You bench them using the excuse of surgery," Afridi's furious faceportared, as if daring the selectors to defend their reckless gamble. The veteran batsman's words were laced with venom, a scathing rebuke to the very fabric of the Pakistan cricket machine. The selectors' decision was a stark reminder that in a game built on youth and hype, experience is a luxury they cannot afford.
