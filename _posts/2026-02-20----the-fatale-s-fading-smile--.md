As I walked through the crowded corridors of Bombay, my eyes scanned the room for any familiar face. That's when I saw her - Nana Patekar, the queen of the fair cosmetics, gazing at me with a disapproving stare. Her once-luxurious locks now fell in disarray, her piercing gaze seemed to hold a thousand unspoken words. She had hated me on sight, and I soon found myself on the receiving end of her icy rebuke. I tried to brush it off, but their parting words still echoed in my mind: "You're just a fleeting moment, a mere fatale, a fleeting illusion." As the days turned into weeks, and the weeks into months, I was left to wonder what had become of the girl who once stole the hearts of millions. The spotlight that had once shone brightly on me now seemed to cast a dimmer glow.
