TLE:** "The Art of Silence"

**SEO_DESC:** "Harsha Bhogle on the writer who broke his silence after AR Rahman's scathing review of Vicky Kaushal's film"

**TAGS:** Silence, Art, Silence Speaks

As I sit in my quiet study, the weight of the world outside slowly fades away. It's been weeks since AR Rahman's scathing review of Vicky Kaushal's film, and I've been carrying the silence within me. The lines on my face etched by worry and doubt, the weight of the world bearing down on me. But then, like a whispered secret, I heard the words that shattered the silence: "I have not seen this kind of divisive force in anything I've written in my life." It was a truth I had been trying to convey, but had been too afraid to speak. The words poured out of me like a release, a catharsis that had been building for years. In that moment, the silence was broken, and the art of silence was finally acknowledged.
