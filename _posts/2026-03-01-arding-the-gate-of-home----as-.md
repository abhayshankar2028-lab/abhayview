As the Australian cricket team takes to the field for their final One Day International, a sense of nostalgia washes over the Indian crowd. Alyssa Healy, the stalwart wicket-keeper, stands at the crease, her eyes brimming with tears. The Indian players, led by the venerable MS Dhoni, approach her, bearing a guard of honour.

The Indian team's captain, Harsha Bhogle, steps forward to salute Alyssa, his voice trembling with emotion. "Alyssa Healy, a true warrior of the game," he says, his eyes locked on hers. "Your dedication, your passion, your love for the game – it's a treasure we can all only dream of. You will be deeply missed, but your legacy will live on in the hearts of Australian cricketers around the world."

As the guard of honour falls silent, Alyssa smiles, her eyes shining with gratitude.
