**TAGS:** Under-19 World Cup hero, historic victory, pressure, emotional

As I lay in bed, my mind racing with the intensity of the final match, I couldn't shake off the feeling of exhaustion. The weight of success was crushing me. I had given everything, left it all on the pitch. And yet, as I gazed up at the stars, I couldn't help but think of all the doubts that had crept in earlier in the day. The pressure to perform, the fear of failure. But then, I remembered the reason I played this game in the first place – the love of the game, the thrill of victory. With a deep breath, I let go of the emotions, and let the victory song echo through my soul.
