** In the sweltering swamps of Dhaka, the air is thick with anticipation. The city's dark underbelly has birthed a monster: a deadly crocodile that stalks its prey with ruthless precision. Amidst this chaos, Adarsh Gourav and Shanaya Kapoor join forces to outsmart the beast. With no escape from the jaws of death, our heroes must put their skills to the ultimate test. Can they overcome their differences and work together to survive the night? Watch "Dhaka The Damned" to find out.

As the camera pans over the murky waters, the tension builds. "We have one shot," Adarsh whispers, his eyes locked on Shanaya. "One chance to save ourselves." Shanaya nods, her voice steady. Together, they charge into the heart of the crocodile-infested swamp. The battle has begun.
