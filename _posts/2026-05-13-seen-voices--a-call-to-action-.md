In the midst of a bustling film industry, a whisper echoes through the corridors of power. Alia Bhatt, a talented actress known for her fierce independence, has spoken out against the long-standing argument that Bollywood's audience is predominantly male. "What happens to the women?" she demands, her voice a beacon of hope for a sector that has long been shrouded in silence. For years, men have dominated the industry, casting women in stereotypical roles and perpetuating the notion that they are secondary to the male gaze. But Alia's courage is a clarion call to action. She demands that the industry acknowledge its own biases and work towards a more inclusive and equitable future. Will Bollywood heed her words, or will the status quo remain unchanged? The fate of women in Bollywood hangs in the balance.
