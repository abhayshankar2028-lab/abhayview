**Body**
In a world where lust and ambition often clash, a young and ambitious woman named Sapna found herself entangled in a web of deceit and violence. At the center of this storm was Hussain Ustara, a notorious gangster with a penchant for murder. Sapna, fueled by her desire for revenge against Dawood Ibrahim, sought out Hussain to avenge her sister's death. But as she delved deeper into the world of organized crime, she realized that Hussain was more ruthless than she ever imagined. Tragically, their alliance turned deadly, and Sapna's world crumbled. In the end, she severed ties with Hussain, vowing to survive the treacherous landscape that had once promised her protection.
