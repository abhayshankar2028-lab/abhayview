As I recall the season of 2011, a particular memory stands out – an incident that still makes me cringe with a mix of emotions. MS Dhoni, my beloved captain, called me "Tin Roof" for losing my temper in the heat of the moment. It was a harsh correction, but what struck me was his unwavering confidence in me. But that same day, I took it upon myself to address the umpires, not for Dhoni's sake, but for mine. I stormed onto the field, my emotions running high, and I gave them a piece of my mind. The umpires, though initially taken aback, eventually gave me a chance to calm down. It was a lesson in the delicate balance between passion and control. Though Dhoni's unorthodox methods often left me bewildered, I came to appreciate his unwavering faith in me.
