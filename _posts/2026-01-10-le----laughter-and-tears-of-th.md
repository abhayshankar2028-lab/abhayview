** The night had begun with cheers and laughter as Nupur Sanon's friends took to the stage for the family sangeet. Kriti Sanon, the life of the party, danced and sang with all her might. But as she reached the climax of her performance, a lump formed in her throat. She began to tear up, and the crowd gasped in shock. Mom Geeta Phogat, her grandmother, was visibly emotional, her eyes brimming with tears. The audience watched in awe as Kriti composed herself, her voice trembling but still powerful. The crowd erupted into applause as she finished her song, and even Geeta couldn't help but smile. It was a night to remember, one that would be etched in everyone's hearts forever.
