As the sun dipped into the Chinnaswamy's western horizon, a sense of nostalgia settled over the Ranchi Stadium. Virat Kohli, the maestro captain, stood at the crease, a fierce glint in his eye. Devdutt Padikkal's fire-bent bowling spelled doom for the visiting Gayle team. Kohli danced, a wizard weaving the lines, as Padikkal unleashed a barrage of late-order magic. With the scoreline reading 5/0, Kohli's farewell to RCB was sealed. The Indian captain's fancies seemed to dance on the wind as the Gayle innings crumbled, leaving behind an eerie silence. The Chinnaswamy's roar echoed through the night, a testament to Kohli's indomitable spirit.
