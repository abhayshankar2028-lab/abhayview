Thirty years have passed since Mika Singh asked to be his bodyguard, and yet, the memory remains etched in his mind like a scar. The rejection was a blow that cut deep, leaving him with a lingering sense of disappointment. 'A struggler, you are,' the words still echo in his ears. He had worked hard to make that man a part of his life, to shield him from the harsh realities of the world. But it seemed like the man had forgotten. Now, as he walks the streets, he's confronted with the bitter taste of his own pain. The fire that once burned within him has dimmed, leaving only ashes of regret.
