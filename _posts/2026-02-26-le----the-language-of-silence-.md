In the scorching heat of Mumbai, Kani Kusruti found herself trapped in a never-ending cycle of self-doubt. The Bollywood filmmakers, eager to cast her, urged her to learn Hindi at an alarming rate. "You can learn that fast, Kani," they would say, their voices dripping with condescension. But Kani knew she just didn't. Her language constraint, a physical barrier to the world around her, made her feel like an outsider.

One day, she was offered a role, and with it, a chance to prove herself. But as she stood in front of the director, her Hindi phrases faltering, she realized that she couldn't shake off the feeling of inadequacy. With a heavy heart, she turned down the role, her decision a testament to the power of silence in the face of adversity.
