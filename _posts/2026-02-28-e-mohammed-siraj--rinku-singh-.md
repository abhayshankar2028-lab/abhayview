Their sacrifices, though unseen, had built a legacy that would forever change the face of Indian cricket. As a young boy, Rinku would help his father with simple tasks, and his unwavering dedication earned him a spot on the team. But what made their story truly remarkable was the toll it took on their parents. Mohammed Siraj's body would ache for hours after each match, and Rinku's father never got to enjoy the thrill of victory. Theirs was a tale of unrelenting passion, of a father's love that transcended the game, leaving behind a lasting impact on the sport they loved.
