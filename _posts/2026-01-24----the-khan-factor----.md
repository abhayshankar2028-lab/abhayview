In the dimly lit editing room, director Aagam Khan's mind was a whirlwind of frustration as he watched his star, Shah Rukh Khan, relentlessly push his son Aryan to shoot fewer takes on the Netflix show. "You can't just say no to the King," Aagam dictated, his voice laced with a hint of desperation. As the days turned into weeks, Aryan's performances became a spectacle, with Aagam's words fueling the fire. Meanwhile, director Aagam Khan couldn't help but feel a twinge of nostalgia, recalling the grueling dubbing sessions he had endured for Salman Khan's cameo. "That's why I'm telling you, Aryan," Aagam said with a sly smile, "you can't just be the next Shah Rukh."
