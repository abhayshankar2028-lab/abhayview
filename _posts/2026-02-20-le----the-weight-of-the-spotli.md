**TAGS:** anxiety, pressure, expectation, performance

Saad Bin Zafar, the Canadian football quarterback, stood on the field, his heart racing with every snap. He knew that every time he took the field, he was under the microscope. The pressure to perform was palpable, and the weight of expectation was crushing. He felt like he was living every other player's dream, but with a different kind of burden. The spotlight shone bright, and Saad knew that every throw, every play, and every decision would be scrutinized. He took a deep breath, trying to calm his nerves, but the pressure to succeed was almost suffocating.
