hadn’t smiled for a year**

I remember the day Parmeet Sethi's world came crashing down. Not a single toy clutched in her tiny hands, no joyful laughter echoing through her tiny room. I had been there for her, day in and day out, as she struggled to make sense of the tragedy that befell her family. The pain and grief that consumed her was palpable. As I grew older, I saw the scars of that difficult time etched on her face, a testament to the long and arduous journey she took to find happiness again. Her story is a powerful reminder that even in the darkest of times, there is always a glimmer of light to be found.
