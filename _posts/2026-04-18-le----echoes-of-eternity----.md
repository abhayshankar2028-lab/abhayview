"I was deeply in love once," Gaurav Gera whispered to the shadows, his voice a gentle breeze that rustled the leaves of heartache. It was a love that had once been the flame that illuminated his world, but now, it flickered out, leaving only embers of what could have been. He thought of the late nights, the stolen glances, the whispers in the dead of night. The memories still lingered, a bittersweet reminder of what he had lost. The fire that once burned so bright had been reduced to a mere spark, a reminder of the love that had been. Gaurav Gera knew that he would never be the same, that the heartbreak had etched its mark on his soul. And so, he vowed to never marry, to keep the flame of his love burning, a beacon of hope in a world that seemed determined to snuff it out.
