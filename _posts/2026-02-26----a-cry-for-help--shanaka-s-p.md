In the aftermath of the T20 World Cup, Dasun Shanaka, the Sri Lankan captain, stood tall, surveying the landscape. The team's performance, though impressive, had been marred by a lingering sense of negativity that seemed to suffocate their spirits. He called upon the government to take action, to address the root causes of this toxicity. "We need a change," Shanaka declared, his voice firm but laced with a hint of desperation. "We need the government to come forward and help us heal. We can't do it alone. We need their support." As he spoke, the weight of his words hung in the air, a cry for help that echoed through the stadium, a reminder that even the greatest teams need a little bit of guidance to flourish.
