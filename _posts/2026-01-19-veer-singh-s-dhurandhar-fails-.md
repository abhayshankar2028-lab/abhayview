nveer Singh's Dhurandhar Fails to Reach Global Heights**

SEO_DESC: Bollywood film Dhurandhar crosses Rs 875 cr in India on its 45th day, still short of reaching $2.4 billion worldwide
TAGS: Bollywood blockbuster Ranveer Singh Pushpa 2 box office collection

In an exciting chapter of Indian cinema, Dhurandhar, the Ranveer Singh-starrer, has crossed Rs 875 crore in the Indian market on its 45th day. Despite reaching this milestone, it still falls short of surpassing the global box office collection of Pushpa 2. Pushpa 2, the highly anticipated sequel of the 2017 blockbuster, has now amassed a staggering global collection of over $2.4 billion. The team behind Dhurandhar has garnered significant attention with its engaging storyline and stunning visuals. With a clear determination to break the mold, the filmmakers are working tirelessly to increase the movie's global exposure.
