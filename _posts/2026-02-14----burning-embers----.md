I recall the night I fell for Kabir Singh, his obsession suffocating me. His passion was a fire that consumed everything, leaving me defenseless. But little did I know, his love was just the tip of the iceberg. His volatile temper and reckless behavior made my heart tremble like a leaf. And then, there were the petrol bombs, the ultimate symbol of his volatile nature. But I was blinded by love, and now I'm left to pick up the pieces of my shattered heart. This Valentine's Day, let's do better. Let's avoid the toxic tropes that can destroy us. We deserve better than to be burned by the flames of love. So, let's steer clear of Kabir's toxic obsession and Shankar's petrol bombs. Our hearts are worth more than a fleeting romance.
