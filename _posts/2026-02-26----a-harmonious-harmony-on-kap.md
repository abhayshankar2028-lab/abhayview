As I sat down with Sunil Pal, the warm glow of the langar's vibrant atmosphere enveloped us. The scent of tender naan and simmering biryani wafted through the air, transporting us to the heart of Punjab. Sunil, a proud Punjabi, smiled as he shared his thoughts on Kapil Sharma's show. "I don't have any issues with him," he said with a chuckle. "There's literally a langar on his show, half of Punjab is on it!" The camera panned out as we both laughed, the sound of sizzling onions and the chatter of the langar's patrons filling the room. In this moment, we felt the true essence of Punjab's cultural exchange, where food, friendship, and harmony reign supreme.
