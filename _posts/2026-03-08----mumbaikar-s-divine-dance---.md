As the sun dipped below the Chennai skyline, Nita Ambani gathered her A-list guests at the iconic Chali Chalo stand at the MCA Ground. The air was electric with anticipation as Salman Khan took to the stage to perform his iconic song, "Chaiyya Chaiyya". Aishwarya Rai, Abhishek Bachchan, and the Queen of Bollywood Nita Ambani took center stage, their eyes locked in a mesmerizing dance pas de deux. With every step, their movements seemed to weave a spell of magic, as if the very essence of Mumbai had infused their bodies. As the song reached its climax, the trio vanished into a whirlwind of color and sound, leaving the audience in awe of their divine dance.
