"It was a cold, calculating decision," I thought to myself as I walked away from the talent agency that had once been my home. Janhvi Kapoor's exit was the final nail in the coffin, a stark reminder that in the cutthroat world of Bollywood, one misstep could be catastrophic.

As I pondered the move, I realized that Karan Johar's studio had lost a true legend. The absence of Janhvi's brilliance was felt deeply, and I couldn't help but wonder what the studio's future held. Would they continue to churn out formulaic films, sacrificing their soul in the process, or would they take a chance on in-house talent? Only time would tell.
