**SEO_DESC:** "Shahana Goswami's candid words: 'I've many partners, but it's never casual.' A poignant reflection on love, identity, and the complexities of human connection."

**TAGS:** Non-traditional relationships, intimacy, self-discovery

As I sat with Shahana, her eyes sparkled like the stars on a clear night. She spoke of her many lovers, each with their own story, their own pain, and their own triumph. But when I pressed her for a definition, she hesitated, her voice barely above a whisper. "It's not about the number, it's about the depth. I've many partners, but it's never casual. It's a dance, a ritual, a surrender to the unknown. I've learned to let go, to trust, to love. And in doing so, I've discovered that true intimacy lies not in the act, but in the space between."

Her words echoed in my mind, a haunting reminder that love is a journey, not a destination.
