As I stood before the majestic Q ajusto Bay in South Africa, the majestic backdrop of the stadium unfolded before my eyes like a tapestry of eternal life. In the midst of this grandeur, I saw the radiant smile of Lekha Washington, her eyes sparkling like diamonds in the fading light. The words of Imran Khan still echoed in my mind: "To love truthfully, selflessly is empowering and healing." His words resonated within me, reminding me of the transformative power of love. Lekha, my dear, you have been a beacon of love in my life, and I cherish the memories we have shared. Your presence in my life has been a healing balm, soothing the wounds of my past and illuminating the path ahead. Together, we will continue to heal, our love a testament to the transformative power of selfless love.
