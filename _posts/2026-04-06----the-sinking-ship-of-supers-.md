**TAGS:** MS Dhoni, Chennai Super Kings, bowling attack, Indian Premier League

In the swirling vortex of IPL 2026, CSK's biggest problem lies not in their star-studded batting lineup, nor their valiant attempts to outsource their bowling woes. It's in the stalwart shoulders of MS Dhoni, the captain who once soared to unprecedented heights but now seems to be sinking into the abyss of himself. His batting line, once a symphony of shots and shots, has become a cacophony of whiffs and failures. The bowling attack, too, has lost its luster, crumbling under the pressure of Dhoni's increasingly erratic deliveries. As the Super Kings' fortunes continue to dwindle, one question lingers: can they find a way to revive their batting and bowling in time, or will MS Dhoni's ego and power play ultimately prove too much to handle?
