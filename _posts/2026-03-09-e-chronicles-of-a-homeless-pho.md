As I stepped out of my small apartment, the warm glow of Indian sunlight enveloped me, a stark contrast to the bleakness of my daily life. For three years, I had called Salman Khan's sprawling farmhouse home, a refuge from the struggles that plagued me. The opulent decor and lavish spreads seemed a far cry from my humble existence. Yet, it was here that I found a sense of purpose, capturing the essence of Indian life through my lens.

One day, Salman invited me to share my stories on his vlog, "Life is set if bhai likes you." I hesitated at first, but the thought of sharing my experiences with the world was too enticing to resist. As I scrolled through Farah Khan's vlog, I saw myself reflected in her eyes, a testament to the power of storytelling.
