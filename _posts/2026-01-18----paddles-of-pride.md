In the misty New Zealand twilight, where the willow sways, 
Michael Bracewell once dreamed of playing a single Test match. 
A captain, a leader, a role he never thought he'd wear, 
Now 100 games into it, he's etched in history, beyond compare.

With each passing series, the fire in his soul ignites, 
Bracewell's never faltered, his passion burning like a beacon in the night. 
He's found his place, his voice, his will to succeed, 
And in those 100 matches, he's left an indelible mark, indeed.

Currently, he stands on the hallowed grounds of Eden Park, 
A testament to his unwavering dedication, a beacon in the dark. 
For Michael Bracewell, this journey has been a sweet reward, 
A symphony of triumphs, and a story yet untold.
