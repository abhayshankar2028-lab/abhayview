**TAGS:** Indian cricket, Pakistani cricket, The Hundred

As I sit amidst the humdrum of Indian cricket, I am met with a peculiar paradox. The Hundred franchises, those bastions of Indian cricket, have long been devoid of Pakistani players. The omission has been a topic of discussion, with some arguing that the Indian board's stance is a clever ploy to maintain a competitive edge. Meanwhile, Pakistani players continue to toil in the shadows, their talents locked away from the international stage. The exclusion has sparked a fierce debate about Indian representation in the world of cricket. Is it a deliberate attempt to suppress Pakistan's cricketing potential, or a genuine oversight? The answer lies in the complexities of Indian cricket, and the delicate balance between pride and pragmatism.
