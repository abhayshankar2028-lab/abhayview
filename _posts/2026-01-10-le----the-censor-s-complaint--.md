As the controversy surrounding Jana Nayagan's film "Amar Akbar Anthony" continued to simmer, a familiar voice echoed in the shadows - Ram Gopal Varma. With a twinkle in his eye, he began to speak. "The censorship board is outdated, a relic of a bygone era. It insults viewers, stifling creativity and freedom of expression. We must adapt to the changing times, not cling to outdated dogma."

Varma's words struck a chord, echoing the sentiments of many who felt that the board's restrictive guidelines were suffocating artistic expression. As the debate raged on, it became clear that the censorship board's time had passed, and it was time for a new era of freedom to bloom.
