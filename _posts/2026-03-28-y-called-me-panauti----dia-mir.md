ey Called Me Panauti**

Dia Mirza sat in her dimly lit study, surrounded by the soft glow of candles and the faint scent of jasmine. Memories of her childhood flooded her mind, transporting her back to the tumultuous days of her youth. They called me Panauti, a derogatory term that painted me as a cursed soul, a magnet for bad luck. I remember being downcast, my eyes cast downward, as if the world itself was conspiring against me. "I told Rajkumar Hirani," I recalled, a wry smile creeping onto my lips, "I have no work." A laugh escaped my lips, a defiant rejection of the superstitions that had once held me captive. Those were halcyon days, lost in a bygone era of naivety and optimism. As I sat in contemplation, the shadows on the walls seemed to recede, and I felt a spark reignite within me, a flame that would illuminate the path ahead.
