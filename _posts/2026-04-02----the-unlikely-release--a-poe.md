As the news spread like wildfire, my heart skipped a beat. Bhooth Bangla, the highly anticipated film, was set to hit the screens on April 27. But fate had other plans. Amidst the frenzy, I found myself swept up in the whirlwind of Dhurandhar's devoted fans. They had created a maelstrom of excitement, with fans camping out overnight, dressed in their finest attire. The energy was electric, and it seemed as though the release of Bhooth Bangla was all but guaranteed. Yet, in this whirlwind of fervor, something remarkable happened. Both films, though initially set for release on the same day, were given the space to breathe, to focus, and to shine. It was as though the fans had created a sanctuary for both, allowing each to thrive in its own unique manner.
