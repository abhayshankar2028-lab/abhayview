ROOM 207, Mumbai, 1997

Sonika Gill, the timid daughter of the notorious Khalnayak, Ram Lakhan, sat by the window, staring out into the abyss. Her world had shattered into a million pieces after an accident that left her father broken. The memory of his anger and betrayal still lingered, a constant reminder of the man she once loved.

As she navigated the complexities of life with her husband, Dinesh, and her first wife, Alia, Sonika struggled to reconcile her past with the present. The lines between love and hate seemed to blur, leaving her questioning her own identity. The weight of her family's legacy bore down on her, threatening to consume her.
