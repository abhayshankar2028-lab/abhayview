The sun-kissed streets of New Delhi buzzed with excitement as Karan Johar, Akshay Kumar, Ananya Panday, and Vicky Kaushal converged on the second day of the RSS Centenary. The air was electric with anticipation as they gathered at the iconic India International Centre. The day began with a glimpse of the future, with Akshay Kumar effortlessly juggling a makeshift cricket pitch during a session of "Lag Bujh Gaya" - a hilarious rendition of his iconic song. Meanwhile, Ananya Panday showcased her impressive dance moves to "Koi Jaane" - a poignant tribute to her late father. Karan Johar and Vicky Kaushal, the dynamic duo, stunned the crowd with an impromptu dance performance of "Dil Dhadakne Do". As the day drew to a close, the group shared heartfelt moments, forging connections that would last a lifetime.
