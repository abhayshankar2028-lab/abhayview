rangers at the Gate**

TAGS: stranger, connection, cultural divide

As the sun set over the Caribbean, a wave of strangers converged at the gate. The West Indies, a land of vibrant colors and rhythmic beats, was met by the vast expanse of India, a land of ancient traditions and modern sophistication. Amidst the chaos, a sense of disconnection hung in the air. The West Indians looked at the Indians with a mixture of curiosity and suspicion. "How do you find your way in our world?" a Trinidadian asked. A Hindi merchant smiled and replied, "The path is not mine to claim." The two worlds drifted apart, each lost in their own reality.
