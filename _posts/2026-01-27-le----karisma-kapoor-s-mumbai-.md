But Karisma shot back, "I don't own flats, I own the entire Juhu." Ah, but is she? The truth, much like Mumbai's diverse landscape, lies in shades of gray. Some say she's a visionary, others a recluse. The real Karisma Kapoor remains a mystery, her words as elusive as the city's many secrets. As for me, I'll continue to observe, to wonder, and to marvel at the enigmatic Karisma Kapoor, the Mumbai queen."
