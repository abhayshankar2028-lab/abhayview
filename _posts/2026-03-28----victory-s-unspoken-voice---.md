"By the gods, we've done it!" Rajat Patel's voice trembled with emotion as he celebrated the emphatic win for Royal Challengers Bangalore. His teammates swarmed around him, each member of the triumvirate grinning from ear to ear. Kohli, seated in the dugout, shook his head in amazement, a hint of a smile playing on his lips. "He's a genius," Kohli whispered, a nod of respect directed at the unspoken voice that had guided RCB to victory. "He's got the magic," Kohli mused, his eyes twinkling with awe. Patel's words were a testament to the unspoken bond between the trio, a chemistry that transcended words and spoke volumes of their unwavering commitment to the game.
