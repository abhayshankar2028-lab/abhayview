As I recall the evenings spent with Aamir Khan, the late actor's infectious laughter still echoes in my mind. It was during the filming of Dev Anand's film, when I would often walk into the set, only to find him lost in thought, tapping his foot to an unheard rhythm. He would glance up, a twinkle in his eye, and say, "Aamir, aapki bhai, aapne bhi film ki baat ki thi, lekin mera samay thoda hi kam hai." (Aamir, my brother, you also told the story, but my time is short.)

In those moments, Aamir Khan didn't ask about the script or the story, but rather about the person behind it. His kindness and humility made me feel valued, and his unspoken script became an unforgettable memory.
