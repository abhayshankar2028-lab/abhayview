As I stood in front of the Sharks, my heart racing with anticipation, I couldn't help but think of the phrase that echoed in my mind long before I took the stage: "Was afraid my career was over". It was the moment I received the call from Aman Gupta, inviting me to share my story. I hesitated for a moment, unsure if I was ready to relive the trauma. But as I began to speak, the words spilled out like a confession, a cry for justice. I told them about the slap that changed my life, the doubts that crept in, and the fear that gripped me. The Sharks listened intently, their faces a mix of curiosity and concern. I realized that my career was more than just a title, it was a reflection of my values. And I vowed to use my platform to raise awareness about the dangers of violence in the workplace.
