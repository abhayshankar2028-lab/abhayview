Harsha Bhogle, the director of Tumbbad, sits in his darkened office, staring at the blank walls. The five-year journey he's been on, pouring his heart and soul into Gulkanda Tales, has finally come to an end. But now, he's faced with a daunting task: show a single image of the film without spilling too much.

He takes a deep breath, trying to push aside the anxiety that's been eating away at him. The thought of revealing too much is unbearable. He's spent years building anticipation, and now it's all being held back. Bhogle knows that the consequences of revealing too much will be severe, but he can't shake off the feeling of guilt.

For five long years, Bhogle has been preparing for this moment. He's written, rehearsed, and re-watched every scene, every shot, every minute detail. And now, all that's left is a blank slate. He takes a sip of his cold coffee, trying to clear his mind. But the doubts linger, and he knows that he'll never be able to show a single image of Gulkanda Tales without revealing too much.
