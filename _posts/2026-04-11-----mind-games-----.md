As Vaibhav Sooryavanshi stepped into the field, the crowd held its collective breath. The score was tied with two wickets to go, and the tension was palpable. But it was the Indian spin bowler, Ravindra Ashwin, who would change the course of the match. With a clever change of pace, Ashwin trapped Hazlewood with a snatched delivery, sending shockwaves through the stadium. The crowd erupted as Sooryavanshi seized the opportunity, delivering a precise delivery that turned the ball back into Hazlewood's hands for a stunning six.
