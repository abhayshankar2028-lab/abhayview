In a world of glamour and grandeur, Salman Khan's generosity knows no bounds. As he strolled into the opulent mansion of his friend Salim Khan, a hint of mischief danced in his eyes. "Do you think you're some king?" he asked, his voice laced with a hint of playfulness. Salim's eyes widened in surprise as Salman handed him a sleek, black Rolex, its price tag a mere Rs 9 lakh. The gesture was a sign of affection, a token of appreciation for the late actor's contributions to the film industry. As Salman handed the watch back, Salim's fingers trembled with gratitude. In that moment, the lines between friendship and royalty blurred, and a new chapter was born.

Salman's heart swelled with pride, knowing that his friend's love for luxury would forever be a testament to their unbreakable bond.
