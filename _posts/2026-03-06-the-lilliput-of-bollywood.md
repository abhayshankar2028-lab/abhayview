E: The Lilliput of Bollywood

SEO_DESC: A poignant tale of unrequited love and inner turmoil in the cutthroat world of cinema. Kamal Haasan, the dashing actor-filmmaker, found himself irreparably entwined with the enigmatic Lilliput - a being of immense power and beauty. His heart, however, belonged to a woman he could never have, and so he channeled his emotions into a role that would catapult him to stardom, and a film that would forever change his life. Years later, Lilliput reveals to the world that Haasan's character in Saagar was, in fact, an allegory for his unrequited love, and the secret that Haasan had to hide - the truth that he had to kill his own brother to atone for his desire.
