As I watched Rishabh Pant take to the field, I couldn't help but be struck by the sheer elegance of his movement. Every step, every swing, every catch was a testament to the hard work and dedication that had gone into becoming one of the world's top cricketers. And yet, as I looked closer, I saw something more - a subtle, understated beauty that belied the price tag attached to his name. Don't think the price tag has got to do anything with it, I thought to myself. Rishabh Pant's form is not just about the money; it's about the man behind the numbers.
