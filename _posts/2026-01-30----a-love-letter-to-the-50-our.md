As I sit in the iconic Eden Gardens, watching the sun set behind the field, I am transported back to the 50-overs World Cup – the one that will always hold a special place in my heart. For me, this format is more than just a tournament; it's a celebration of the game, a symphony of spin, swing, and bouncers. The 50-overs format has a way of bringing out the best in players, of pushing them to new heights, and of creating an electric atmosphere that's unmatched anywhere else. As I look around at the passionate fans, the skilled bowlers, and the determined players, I am reminded of why this format is the crème de la crème of cricket competitions. The T20 World Cup may focus on the 20 overs, but the 50-overs format will forever be the true 'Cricket World Cup'.
