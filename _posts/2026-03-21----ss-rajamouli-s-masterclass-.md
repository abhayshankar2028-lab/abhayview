** As the curtains closed on Dhurandhar 2, Rajamouli couldn't help but feel a sense of awe. The film had been a labor of love for the director, and it shone brightly in its 4-hour runtime. Ranveer Singh's performance was nothing short of phenomenal, with every scene a testament to his acting prowess. The film's climax left the audience on the edge of their seats, and the music swelled to a crescendo. It was a film that left a lasting impression, a true masterpiece of Telugu cinema.
