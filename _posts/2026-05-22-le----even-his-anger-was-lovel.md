As I sit in quiet contemplation, the memories of my father, Dharmendra, flood my mind like a bittersweet rain. The fire that once burned within me, fueled by his unwavering love and unrelenting passion, now flickers with an otherworldly intensity. His anger, once a shield, became a palpable force that I could feel emanating from him like an aura. Like a lover's gentle touch, it caressed my skin, soothing my soul. Even in his anger, he was lovely. I recall the countless nights we spent together, watching the stars twinkle above, our laughter echoing through the halls of our small apartment in Mumbai. Those moments, though marked by turmoil, are etched in my memory as the purest form of love I've ever known.
