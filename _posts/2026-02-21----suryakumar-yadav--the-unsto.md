His batting skills, a perfect blend of power and finesse, had South Africa on their toes. Meanwhile, the Indian spin quartet – Yuzvendra Chahal, Kuldeep Yadav, Jasprit Bumrah, and Shardul Thakur – had the South African batsmen on their heels.
