Harsha Bhogle sat beside his son Ahan, who was pouring his heart out, sharing the story of their debut film that flopped at the box office. Tears welled up in his eyes as Ahan recounted the long hours, endless rehearsals, and the countless rejections that followed. "He went through a lot in life," Ahan said, his voice cracking with emotion. "But he never gave up. He worked tirelessly to make it happen. I'm grateful for the lessons he taught me." Harsha smiled, proud of his son's resilience and determination. "You're a true artist, Ahan. The world needs your voice."
