As I sit in my dimly lit study, surrounded by dusty tomes and faded posters, I am reminded of the Ramayana's gentle grip on my heart. For years, it has been my "bad luck" to bring this epic tale to the big screen. I've watched countless drafts, rewrites, and rejections, each one a painful reminder that my films are taking years to make. But then, the call comes. Ranveer Singh's Dhurandhar, my latest project, needs my attention. I look up to see Ranbir Kapoor's eyes lighting up, his face a picture of excitement. "My favourite," he says, his voice barely above a whisper. For a moment, I forget about the long wait, the rewrites, and the rejections. All that matters is this fleeting glimpse of joy.
