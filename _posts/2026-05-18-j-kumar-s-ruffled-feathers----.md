aj Kumar's Ruffled Feathers**

"Jaani, my loyal companion, with eyes like the morning dew,
You'd chase a butterfly, and leave me in awe anew.
Your moody moods would test my patience, my friend so true,
But I'd stand by your side, through thick and thin, I'd see you through."

I remember the day Raaj Kumar's dog, Jaani, would throw a tantrum,
Bachchan would say, 'He's moody, he's a blunder.'
But Raaj Kumar would laugh, and give Jaani a pat on the head,
'He's a tough boy, Bachchan, but we love him instead.'

His tough exterior hid a heart of gold,
A love for his family, and a story to be told.
I'd watch as he'd whip out his phone, and dial our number bright,

Raaj Kumar's tough exterior, it was just a disguise,
For a heart that beat for his family, and a love that never dies.
