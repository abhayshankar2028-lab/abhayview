As I sit amidst the chaos of social media, I am reminded of the unspoken call to duty that drove us towards the T20 World Cup. Rohit Sharma, the heartbeat of our team, was the one who kept us grounded, reminding us of our purpose. His calm and collected leadership was the missing piece that made us unstoppable. We were a well-oiled machine, and he was the conductor, expertly guiding us through the tournament. His dedication and sacrifice paid off, and we emerged victorious. It's ideal to get off social media, that's what we kept saying, but the truth is, we couldn't. Every time we stepped onto the field, Rohit's presence was a spark that ignited our passion. He was the spark that set us ablaze, and we succeeded because of him.
