I still remember the day Rajpal Yadav walked off the set of Billu, the film that promised to be a massive blockbuster. He was set to play the role of a key character, but behind the scenes, a miscommunication had turned his career upside down. Rajpal had always been devoted to the film, and when he found out that his name was being replaced by another actor with a better reputation, he felt betrayed. In an exclusive interview, Rajpal opens up about the incident, revealing how he felt humiliated and demoralized by the whole ordeal. "It was a dark day for me," he recalls, shaking his head. "I had poured my heart and soul into that film, and to see it go to someone else was a hard pill to swallow."
