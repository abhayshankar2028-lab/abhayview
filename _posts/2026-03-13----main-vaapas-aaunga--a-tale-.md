** In the midst of war and displacement, Diljit Dosanjh and Imtiaz Ali's Main Vaapas Aaunga weaves a poignant tale of love and longing. Set against the backdrop of India's Partition era, the story follows a young couple torn apart by the conflict. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of war and uncertainty, their love becomes the only constant in a world that seems determined to tear them apart. With each passing day, their passion and devotion grow stronger, but the ghosts of the past threaten to destroy them. Can their love survive the horrors of war and the memories that haunt them?
