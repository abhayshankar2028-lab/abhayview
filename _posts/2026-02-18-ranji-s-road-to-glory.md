As the sun rose over the hills of J&K, a different kind of dawn was breaking. For these men, it was a dawn of hope, a new beginning that stretched out before them like a 60km road to train. They had started with zero facilities, no access to coaching, and no guarantee of success. Yet, they had chosen to take the first step, to put in the hard work, to push themselves to the limit.

Their eyes were fixed on the prize, the Ranji final, and the chance to make their dreams a reality. They trained tirelessly, their bodies battered and bruised, their spirits unbroken. And as they walked, they knew that they were not just training for themselves, but for their families, their communities, and the future of J&K.

Their journey was a testament to the power of dedication and perseverance. They had taken the road less traveled, and had emerged stronger, wiser, and more resilient. And as they reached the final, they knew that they had truly arrived.
