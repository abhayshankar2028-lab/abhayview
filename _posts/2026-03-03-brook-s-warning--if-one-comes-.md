If one comes off, the opposition are scratching their heads

In the semifinal showdown, Harry Brook's words hung in the air like a challenge. As England's skipper, he knew the stakes were high. The Indians, already reeling from a sluggish start, had been left grasping at straws. Brook's ominous statement sent a shiver down their spines. "One comes off, and they're left wondering," he said. The Indian team, known for their resilience, was not one to shy away from a fight. They vowed to regroup and come back stronger. Brook's warning had roused the Indians, and now they were ready to take on the challenge. The stage was set for an epic battle, with the outcome hanging precariously in the balance.
