**TAGS:** "friendship," "loss," "one-phone-call," " Bollywood"

As I sit in silence, the memories of our conversations with Amitabh Bachchan flood my mind. We had a special bond, forged in the fire of our one-phone-call friendship. Kader Khan, the renowned actor, would often reach out to me with a simple yet profound question: "If Amitabh leaves films, I will too." Those words echoed in my heart, a promise to remain true to my artistic vision, no matter the cost.

But like a whispered secret, our friendship began to fade. The pressures of fame, the demands of the industry, and the weight of expectation took its toll. I tried to hold on, but the winds of change swept us apart. Now, I'm left to wonder if the bond we shared was stronger than the obstacles that came between us.
