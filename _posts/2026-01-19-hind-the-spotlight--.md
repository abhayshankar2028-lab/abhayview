I sit with tears streaming down my face, my eyes fixed on the familiar smile of my daughter Priyanka Chopra. But today, it's not her captivating eyes that steal my heart – it's the pain etched on her brother Nick's face. Every day, he's left to pick up the pieces of his shattered career, a victim of his sister's stardom. I see him struggling to balance his family life with the constant scrutiny of the media. I see him sacrificing his relationships, his health, and his very sanity. The pressures of fame have taken their toll on him. I long for the simple days when Nick was carefree and unencumbered by the weight of his sister's spotlight. As I watch him, I'm reminded of the price he pays to be a part of Priyanka's world.
