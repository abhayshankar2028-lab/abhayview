As the debate on Donald Trump's infamous 'Iranis' claim raged on, Boman Irani couldn't resist taking a swipe at the US president. "You think Iran is an 'Iranis'?" he chuckled to the camera, his voice dripping with sarcasm. "Where was this voice when Iranians were dying under your so-called 'regime'?" The actor's wit was swift and merciless, leaving Trump's supporters stunned. Meanwhile, Mandana Karimi took to social media to respond, her words a bitter rebuke to Trump's alleged hypocrisy. "You may have made a joke, Boman, but where was this voice when Iranians were dying under your so-called 'regime'?" Her message was clear: the US president's words were hollow, and his history of human rights abuses was undeniable.
