It's a surprise twist, a mettle test for India's T20I heroes. As the New Zealand series looms, the selectors have made some crucial calls. Shreyas Iyer, the Indian vice-captain, has been inducted into the squad, along with Ravi Bishnoi. The duo will be crucial in the limited-overs format, where India's bowlers will be put to the test.

Washington Sundar, the Indian opener, has been replaced by the wicketkeeper-batsman, Rishabh Pant. Pant's experience and consistency will be a welcome addition to the team. The selectors have also introduced a new bowling lineup, featuring Jasprit Bumrah and Prasoon Chaudhary. With the momentum shifting in India's favor, the T20I squad is looking stronger than ever.
