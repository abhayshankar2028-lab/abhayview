I sit amidst her empty mansion, the silence deafening as I listen to her whispered words. "Harsha, my son's ambition will be his demise." Madhu's eyes, once bright as the stars, now dim with worry. "Priyanka's stardom is all I have left," she says, her voice cracking. "I see him struggling every day, the weight of the world on his shoulders. He's sacrificed so much for her success, but at what cost? His mental health, his relationships – it all suffers."

Her words cut deep, a poignant reminder that the price of stardom can be steeper than we ever imagined. I take a deep breath, trying to find the words to ease her pain. "I'll do everything, Mother," I promise, but will it be enough?
