As the sun set over the Chatta Park stadium, Rishabh Pant stood amidst his beleaguered teammates, his jaw clenched in a fierce determination. "We're not just here for the win, boys," he said, his voice low and measured. "We're here to find the answers we're searching for." The fourth consecutive loss had taken its toll on the LSG team, and they seemed to be searching for a way out, a way to shake off the perpetual feeling of disappointment. Pant's words were a promise to himself, to his teammates, and to the cricketing world at large: that they would find a way to break free from the shackles of deceit, to look for answers inside, not outside. The weight of deceit might be crushing, but the spirit of the game was stronger.
