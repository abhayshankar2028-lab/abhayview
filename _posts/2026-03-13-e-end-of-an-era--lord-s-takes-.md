As the sun set over the hallowed grounds of Lord's cricket ground, a sense of unease settled over the players, spectators, and staff. It was the end of an era, the final curtain call for a partnership that had endured for generations. BrewDog, the beloved brewery that had brought the ground to its people, had sold its stake to the behemoth Tilray Brands. The news sent shockwaves through the cricketing world, as the very fabric of the game seemed to unravel. The once-thriving beer business, a staple of the ground's identity, was now nothing more than a distant memory. As the players departed, the echoes of their cheers and laughter faded into the wind, leaving behind a ground forever changed.
