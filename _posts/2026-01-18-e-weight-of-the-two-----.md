As I stand amidst the applause, the spotlight on my name, I am met with a familiar unease. The whispers of those who once believed in my craft now seem hollow, a distant echo of what once was. Two Oscars may have catapulted me to stardom, but at what cost? I've been gaslighted, manipulated into doubting my own abilities. The pressure to consistently create has weighed me down, suffocating my passion for the work that once ignited a fire within. Two Oscars can be a burden, a reminder that the weight of my own expectations can crush even the most innovative mind.
