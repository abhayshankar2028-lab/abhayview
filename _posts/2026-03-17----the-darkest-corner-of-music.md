** In the depths of internet fame, Armaan Malik found himself entangled in a web of controversy. The Indian playback singer's recent collaboration with Nora Fatehi-Sanjay Dutt on the song 'Sarke Chunar Teri Sarke' sparked a heated debate. Critics accused the duo of commercial songwriting at its most cringe-worthy, with lyrics that read like a mishmash of ancient dialects and modern slang. The song's production, a jumbled mix of electronic beats and catchy hooks, only added to the song's egregiousness. As the music world watched, Malik's reputation was tarnished by his association with such a song. The once-unblemished reputation of a legendary singer had now become a laughingstock.
