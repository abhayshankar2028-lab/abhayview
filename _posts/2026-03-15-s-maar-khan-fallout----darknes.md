es Maar Khan Fallout**

"Darkness descended like a shroud, shrouding the film industry in an eerie silence." - Harsha Bhogle

It was the aftermath of Tees Maar Khan, the film that catapulted Farah Khan to stardom. The critics' harsh words still lingered in my ears, but nothing could have prepared me for the brutal reality that followed. The backlash was deafening, a tsunami of negativity that seemed to have no end. "I felt nobody should question me since I'd just made Om Shanti Om," she whispered, her voice laced with melancholy.

The scrutiny was relentless, a never-ending barrage of doubts and criticisms. It was as if the world had conspired against her, seeking to destroy the very image she had worked so tirelessly to build. Yet, Farah refused to back down, her resilience a beacon of hope in the darkness.
