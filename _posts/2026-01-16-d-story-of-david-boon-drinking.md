It was supposed to be an exclusive tour of England's cricket grounds, but a trip down the dark path of excessive drinking had David Boon in its grasp. At a pub in London, over 52 beers flowed down his throat like a raging torrent. As night descended, his thoughts turned to the Ashes, England's cricketing pride, and how he was going to 'drum' them into submission. England's modern Ashes 'drinking crisis' seemed a distant echo as Boon stumbled back to his hotel, defeated by his own, unrelenting thirst. For the next few weeks, a local pub in Australia found itself becoming his unlikely 'home away from home'.
