lman Khan-Akshay Kumar cannot do without me**

I still remember the night like it was yesterday. Rajpal Yadav, a man I had the pleasure of calling a friend, was in the house, grinning from ear to ear. We had just won the Screen Award for Best Actor for our powerful performance in "Gadar: Ek Prem Katha". The energy in the room was electric, and I felt like a kid on Christmas morning.

As we accepted our award, Salman Khan spoke up, his voice booming across the hall. "We're not just actors, we're brothers," he said, looking at us with a twinkle in his eye. The room erupted in laughter, and I knew in that moment, we were more than just colleagues – we were a family. And as we walked off that stage, arm in arm, I knew that Rajpal was the glue that held us together.
