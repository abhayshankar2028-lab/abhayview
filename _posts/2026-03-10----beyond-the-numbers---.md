In the crisp morning air of Dhaka's Sher-e-Bangla National Stadium, Kusal Pareshan Simmons stood tall, surveying the sea of expectant faces. The ODI World Cup qualification was looming, and the pressure was mounting. But Simmons had other plans. He gazed out at the lush green pitch, his eyes lingering on the nuances of the game. "It's not just about the numbers," he said, his voice low and measured. "It's about the standards we set for ourselves. The standards we expect from each other. The standards that will bring us victory, not just qualification."
