As I sit in my darkened office, the shadows dance upon the walls, a haunting reminder of the long, arduous journey of my film. Three years have passed, the silence deafening, as Dinesh Vijan's studio refuses to yield. Meanwhile, Amar Kaushik's phone goes unanswered, his calls ignored as if his concerns were mere whispers. "They are consumed by big movies," I whisper to myself, a mantra of frustration. The film, once a dream, now hangs in the balance. Will it ever see the light of day, or will it succumb to the crushing weight of its own ambition? Only time will tell.
