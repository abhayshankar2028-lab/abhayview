As I sat in the darkened theater, the lights flickering like embers of a defeated fire, I was met with a sight that would haunt me for the rest of my days. Sanjay Leela Bhansali, the maestro of emotions, stood before us, his eyes brimming with tears like a canine in distress. "Tadap Tadap," he sobbed, "you destroyed me. You took away my trust, my piece de resistance." His voice cracked like a fragile glass, shattering into a thousand pieces. Ismail Darbar, the maestro of music, stepped forward, his eyes welling up with tears of his own. "I am sorry, Sanjay. I was wrong. I promise to do better." But it was too late. The damage was done. The heart of a master had been broken, and I knew that the wounds would take a long, long time to heal.
