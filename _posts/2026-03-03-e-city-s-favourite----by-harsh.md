title: "e City's Favourite**

By Harsha Bhogle

In the scorching Mumbai heat, Karan Aujla's rickshaw stood still, awaiting its next fare. Yet, fate intervened, and the driver's fine was paid in full. As the owner, Aujla smiled, his eyes twinkling with warmth. He lapped up the attention, donning a bright smile that seemed to say, I love doing this.

Ah, the city's favourite, he chuckled, his voice dripping with pride. Who wouldn't want to be on the receiving end of a fine, courtesy of a kind soul like me? He beamed, his eyes crinkling at the corners as he gazed out at the bustling streets. The driver, embarrassed but grateful, returned to his duties, while Aujla basked in the admiration. In the city's underbelly, he had found a small victory, and a sense of purpose."
