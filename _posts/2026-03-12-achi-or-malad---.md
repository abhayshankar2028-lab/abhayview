As the screens lit up with Dhurandhar's "Karachi or Malad?" on 500 worldwide screens, fans witnessed a peculiar phenomenon. In Ranveer Singh's Pakistanian scene, a mall materialized, an incongruous sight amidst the bustling streets of Karachi. This unexpected twist sparked debate, with some hailing it as a bold statement and others accusing the filmmakers of cultural appropriation. "Dhurandhar's vision," one fan wrote, "is a masterclass in exploring Pakistan's complexities." Yet, others countered, "How can we reconcile the image of Karachi's gleaming skyscrapers with the humble, cluttered alleys of Malad?" The divide between the two cities and cultures continued to simmer, as the fans dissected the film's themes and symbolism.
