In the fiery cauldron of IPL 2026, Mumbai Indians' play-off hopes seemed to be suffocating under the weight of adversity. But on a fateful evening, as the Chennai Super Kings unfolded a fire that would incinerate their dreams, the eventuality of defeat became a certainty. Iqbal Wasim's angry face, red-rimmed and with a hint of desperation, betrayed his team's frustration. The once-swift Kieron Pollard found himself trapped in a tormented world where his shots and drives were met with a gauntlet of dismissal. The bowlers, inspired by their team's mettle, unleashed a barrage of wickets, leaving Mumbai Indians to ponder the ignominy of a 5-0 drubbing. The season's trajectory had been set in motion; all hope was lost.
