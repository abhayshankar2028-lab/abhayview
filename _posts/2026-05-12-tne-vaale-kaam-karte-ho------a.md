As Sonu Nigam's concert reached its climax, the anticipation grew. But little did he know, a fan had already reached the end of his tether. With eyes fixed on the stage, he sprinted towards the performer, his hands grasping for the musician's feet. The music paused, and Sonu's response was a mixture of shock and horror. The fan, fueled by desperation, had climbed on stage, his face inches from the singer's. The crowd gasped in unison, and Sonu's voice trembled, "Pitne vaale kaam karte ho?" - "Are you mad? This is madness!"

The moment hung suspended, as the fan's fingers touched the singer's toes. The audience held its collective breath, waiting to see the outcome.
