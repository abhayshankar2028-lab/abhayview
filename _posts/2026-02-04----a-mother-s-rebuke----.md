**TAGS:** Motherly Rebuke, Fashion Faux Pas, Inexperienced Teens

As the sun set over the bustling streets of Mumbai, Samay Raina stood before her sons' faces, her eyes blazing with a mix of love and exasperation. "Archana, kya ban ke ghoom rahe ho?" she asked, her voice dripping with sarcasm. "You're 30, kya ban ke ghoom rahe ho? Baahot garam aur saare rishton se bahar aate hain, lekin apne virasat ko kya karte hai?" The young men looked at each other nervously, their faces flushing with embarrassment. They knew they had been outdone in the fashion department, and now they had to live with the consequences. Samay Raina's rebuke was a gentle nudge, urging them to grow up and take their fashion cues from the world, not from their mothers' closet.
