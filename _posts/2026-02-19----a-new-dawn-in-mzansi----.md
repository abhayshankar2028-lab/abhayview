As I stood on the sun-kissed streets of Mumbai, I couldn't help but think of the Zimbabweans who had once called this city home. The cricketers, the fans, the spirit – all had been a part of India's vibrant cricketing tapestry for so long. But times had changed, and Zimbabwe's fortunes had dwindled. The last time they played in India was in 2018, a nail-biter of a match against Pakistan. Now, as I watched the Zimbabweans take to the field for the 2026 T20 World Cup, I felt a sense of excitement and hope. This was their chance to revive their cricketing heroes and bring glory back to the Mother City. The stage was set, and the game was about to begin.
