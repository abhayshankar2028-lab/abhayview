As I stood before the cameras, a stark contrast to the pomp and circumstance of the past, Karan Johar took the stage. With a piercing gaze, he addressed the industry, his words dripping with conviction. "Let us not be swayed by the siren's call of 'premier' and 'prime' titles. Let us stop doing PR, and let achievements speak for themselves." The room fell silent, as if the very foundations of our industry had shifted. "Everything available at a price now," he warned, his eyes burning with intensity. We were no longer a reflection of our art, but a reflection of our wallets. The old guard trembled at the thought, while the new guard looked on, eager to prove themselves.
