** Hrithik Roshan's relentless pursuit of roles in the Bollywood industry has been met with frustration from top directors. His constant rejection has sparked debate about the industry's favoritism towards 'good guy' roles. Despite his versatility and dedication to the craft, Roshan seems to be stuck in a rut. His roles, though critically acclaimed, often fall into the predictable 'hero' category. Can Roshan break free from this pattern and prove himself as a force to be reckoned with?
