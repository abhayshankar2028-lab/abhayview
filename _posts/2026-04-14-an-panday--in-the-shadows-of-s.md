In the dimly lit room, Ahaan Panday stepped up to the microphone, a hint of vulnerability in his voice. "To the audience of Saiyaara, I want to say thank you. Your love and support mean the world to us. This film was never just about the story; it was about the space we created for ourselves to discover who we are, what we want, and how we want to live."

Aneet Padda nodded in agreement, her eyes shining with tears. "The film gave us the courage to take risks, to push boundaries, and to explore the depths of our own humanity. It's not just a movie; it's a mirror that reflects our collective soul." As the audience erupted in applause, Ahaan and Aneet shared a smile, their voices filled with gratitude and a deep sense of connection.
