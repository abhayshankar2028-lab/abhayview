**TAGS:** love, identity, drama, Sooraj Barjatya, Sandeep Reddy Vanga, Kabir Singh, Harsha Bhogle

"In the realm of love, two men stand as twin pillars, their paths converging in a timeless dance of passion and vulnerability. Prem, the brooding, tortured soul, and Kabir, the charming, seductive charmer. Theirs is a tale of conflicting desires, of two sides of the same coin. It's a story of love, of identity, of the human heart's deepest longings. Itna pyaar to kare koi, indeed, for in the end, it's not whether you love, but how you love, that defines you.

As a director, I've always believed that love is a multifaceted gem, hidden beneath the surface. Sooraj Barjatya and Sandeep Reddy Vanga's Prem and Kabir Singh are two sides of the same coin, a reminder that love can take many forms, and that the boundaries of identity are fluid, ever-shifting."
