I recall the day it happened like it was yesterday. I was in the middle of a whirlpool, letting the warm water envelop me, when Mahesh Bhatt suddenly appeared out of the shower, his eyes fixed intently on me. He cleared his throat, and in a voice that sent shivers down my spine, he said, "Harsha, I think we have a problem." I froze, unsure of what he meant. He went on to describe a scene from one of his films, where a character's death was left unexplained. The tension was palpable. I was taken aback, but not surprised. I had heard Mahesh's unorthodox methods before. And in that moment, I knew I was in the presence of genius. The National Award was the cherry on top – a fitting reward for a man who reminds me of the magic that happens when you push the boundaries of storytelling.
