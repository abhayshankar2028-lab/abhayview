As I sat in stunned silence, Anurag Singh's words hung in the air like a challenge. "Ab maafi ki application submit kar", he said, his voice firm but laced with a hint of amusement. The trolls, known for their senseless hate, seemed taken aback by the request. They had been quick to mock and belittle Varun Dhawan, but now they were faced with a daunting task: to submit an apology. The thought of it was both daunting and liberating. The trolls, once masters of sarcasm and ridicule, now found themselves at the mercy of Varun Dhawan. The game had changed, and the trolls were now the ones who needed to apologize.
