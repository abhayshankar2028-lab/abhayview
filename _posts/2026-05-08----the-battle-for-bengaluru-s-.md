**TAGS:** KSCA, IPL final, BCCI secretary, ticket demand, Ahmedabad

As the sun set over the serene Bengaluru hills, the anticipation for the Indian Premier League (IPL) final was palpable. However, little did fans know, a simmering storm was brewing. KSCA (Khan Sports Club Association) members, fueled by frustration and desperation, demanded that the league shift the final match to Ahmedabad, the city that would have hosted the event. But their pleas fell on deaf ears, as the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) secretary, Sourav Ganguly, refused to budge. The pressure mounted as fans and players alike expressed their discontent, raising questions about the BCCI's decision-making process. The fate of the IPL final hung precariously in the balance, as the battle for Bengaluru's big day intensified.
