** The stage was set for a dramatic showdown as Rajpal Yadav, the humble actor, stood before the nation, his eyes burning with a fierce determination. Just hours before, he had pledged Rs 11 lakh to his embattled nephew Tej Pratap Yadav, and now, a hefty sum of Rs 10 lakh had been offered by the music label, KRK, as a gesture of goodwill. As the crowd erupted in cheers, it was clear that the actor's unassuming spirit had struck a chord with the people. Sonu Sood, the Bollywood superstar, stood by his side, his voice choked with emotion, as he pledged an additional sum of Rs 5 lakh, solidifying the support for the Yadav family. The tide had turned, and Rajpal Yadav's name would forever be etched in the annals of Indian history as a champion of the oppressed.
