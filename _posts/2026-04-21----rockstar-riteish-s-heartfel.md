** As the lights dimmed at the Raja Shivaji trailer launch, the anticipation was palpable. But little did the audience know, the real moment was about to unfold. Riteish Deshmukh, the star of the show, was about to break down in tears as he shared a heartfelt thank you with his wife, Genelia Deshmukh. "Rockstar," he whispered, his voice trembling with emotion, "you've been my rock throughout our journey. Your love and support mean the world to me." The audience erupted into applause as Genelia wiped away tears, her eyes shining with gratitude.
