In the depths of her past, a veil of mystery shrouds the life of Tabu, a woman whose name has been a source of both fascination and fear. She has no memories of her father, no recollection of the man who raised her. Yet, a burning curiosity ignites within her, a desire to know the truth about her family's past. And so, Tabu has chosen to keep it hidden, to conceal the identity of her father and the name that has come to define her. But the truth, for Tabu, is not just a name - it is a part of her identity, the foundation upon which her entire existence is built.
