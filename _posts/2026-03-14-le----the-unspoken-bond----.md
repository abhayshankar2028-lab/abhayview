Mika Singh's eyes still lingered on the memory of Rohan, his 25-year-old bodyguard, 30 years ago. Back then, Rohan's words still cut deep: "You're just a struggler, Mika. You're not worthy of my protection." Those words sliced through Mika like a knife, leaving a scar that still lingered.

But Mika refused to let their encounter define him. He worked tirelessly to prove himself, earning Rohan's trust and admiration. Over time, Rohan's respect and affection transformed into something more. Their bond grew stronger, a testament to the power of love and loyalty.

Though Rohan's rejection remained a painful wound, Mika knew his unwavering dedication ultimately won over Rohan's heart. Today, Mika stands as Rohan's bodyguard, a guardian of the man who once turned his back on him.
