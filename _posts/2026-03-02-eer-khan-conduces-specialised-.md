heer Khan Conduces Specialised Red-Ball Camp for Fast Bowlers**

At the BCCI's Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru, Zaheer Khan embarked on a mission to nurture the next generation of fast bowlers. The highly specialised camp, aptly titled 'Red-Ball Revival', aimed to bridge the gap between the batsmen and the bowlers. As the sun rose over the Kumar T. C. Road stadium, Zaheer stood before the players, his eyes burning with intensity. "The red ball is not just a ball, it's a reflection of our nation's strength and resilience," he declared. Under Zaheer's guidance, the bowlers pored over every detail, from the art of swing to the intricacies of spin. For a few days, the boundaries between batsmen and bowlers dissolved, as the two camps clashed in a battle of wits. The results would be nothing short of spectacular.
