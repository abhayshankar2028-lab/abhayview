As I stood backstage, waiting for the show to begin, I couldn't shake off the feeling of unease that lingered within me. The reason? KL Rahul had invited me to his dressing room to reveal a message from the DC management. My heart racing, I took a deep breath and stepped inside.

The room was dimly lit, the air thick with anticipation. Rahul's voice trembled as he spoke, his words barely audible. "The message is clear. They want me to perform. They want me to win. I've been playing a different game, a game of strategy and skill. But they see only the flashy moves. They don't understand the true art of cricket." His eyes locked onto mine, and for a moment, I saw the fire that drove him to succeed. The secret in his heels was no longer just about winning; it was about the weight of expectation, the pressure to perform.
