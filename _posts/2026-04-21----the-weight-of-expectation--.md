As the cricket world watched, R Ashwin's eyes narrowed, his brow furrowed in concern. Hardik Pandya, once a rising star, had been struggling in MI's quest for a title. The young all-rounder's bat seemed to be slipping, his shots falling flat. Ashwin's voice was laced with a mix of disappointment and worry as he spoke to Pandya.

"If he gets a good hit in one innings, don't make the same mistake twice," Ashwin said, his words dripping with a deep-seated expectation. Pandya's face fell, his eyes welling up with tears. The weight of expectation hung heavy, threatening to crush their dreams.
