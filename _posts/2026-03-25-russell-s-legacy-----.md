TLE: Russell's Legacy**
**SEO_DESC: KKR retire Andre Russell's No.12 jersey sparks nostalgia and debate**
**TAGS: Andre Russell, Kolkata Knight Riders, cricket**

It was a crisp autumn evening in Kolkata, the air thick with the aroma of street food and the hum of excitement. The Kolkata Knight Riders were set to retire a jersey that held a special place in the hearts of the fans - No.12, worn by Andre Russell to 249 matches. Russell, a force to be reckoned with on the field, had worn the jersey with pride, but now it was time to bid farewell. The crowd held its collective breath as Russell donned the jersey, a faint smile on his face. The cheers that followed were a testament to the man and the legend he was. As Russell walked off the field, the No.12 jersey became a symbol of nostalgia, a reminder of the memories made under the Kolkata sky.
