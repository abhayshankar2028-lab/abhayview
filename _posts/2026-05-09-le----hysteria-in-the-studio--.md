**TAGS:** patriarchy, Bollywood, actresses, actors, female empowerment

As I sit in the dimly lit studio, the air thick with tension, I can feel the weight of expectation bearing down on me. The producers have finally agreed to my demands, and the actress's fee is to be negotiated. But as we sit down to crunch the numbers, I know that the true cost is not just the money, but the marginalization of women in this industry.

The actors are the ones who truly take the bigger cut, while the actresses bear the brunt of the patriarchy. We are expected to conform to certain standards of beauty and behavior, and those who fail to meet these expectations are shunned. But I refuse to be silenced. I refuse to be complicit in this toxic system. I am Kriti Sanon, and I will not be silenced.
