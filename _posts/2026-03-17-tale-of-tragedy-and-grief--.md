As the lights dimmed, the air thickened with sorrow. Farah Khan stood before her father, her eyes brimming with tears. Their struggles in the film industry had finally come to an end, a devastating collapse that swept away everything they had worked for. The family, once a modest one, now teetered on the brink of poverty. The once-promising Rs 30 they had saved would now be a distant memory. Her father's eyes, once ablaze with passion for his craft, now seemed dim, lost in the void of his own mortality. "We were paupers," she whispered, the words a poignant reminder of the price they had paid for their dreams.
