As I stepped onto the set, I couldn't help but notice Akshaye Khanna. His bright smile, his charming eyes – everything seemed perfect. But as the days went by, I saw a different side of him. Irritable, short-tempered, and erratic. He would lash out at the crew, at the director, at the very stars who worked for him. His hair, once luscious and full, began to fall out in clumps, a constant reminder of his emotional turmoil.

I tried to brush it off, to be supportive, but it was hard to ignore the damage. The once-golden star had turned to rust, and I feared for the future of our beloved film.
