2026: Tashar Deshpande's yorkers seal the deal for RR in thrilling first proper match.

**TITLE:** Tashar's Towers
**SEO_DESC:** Rising Rajasthan Royals clinch top spot with a stunning comeback victory in the first proper Indian Premier League thriller, as Tushar Deshpande's masterclass takes them to the top of the table.
In a nail-biting encounter, RR faced 190/8 in 39.3 overs. But, Tushar Deshpande's 6/75, including 4 yorkers, and AB de Villiers' 73 off 90 balls, led by a dominance of the middle order, helped Royals reverse the situation and secure their first top spot in IPL history. With a total of 282/9, RR bowled out in 38 overs. The sheer intensity, the drama, and the importance of this win left fans and pundits alike in awe.
