**"Ek Villain's Dark Legacy"**

In the mirror of my mind, a ghostly figure emerges. Mohit Suri, the villain, haunts me. His words, once a gentle caress, now cut like a knife. "You'll pay for your betrayal," he whispered, as I exited the show. I recall the night, driving Uber to make a living in LA, my heart still heavy with the loss of our friendship. The memories of Splitsvilla's glitz and glamour now taunt me. I think back to the final episode, when I made the fateful decision to leave Ek Villain's toxic world. The doubts crept in, and Mohit's words resurfaced. "You'll never be the same," he sneered. The weight of his words still lingers, a constant reminder of the darkness that lurks within.
