I was present at the Screen Awards when Amitabh Bachchan paid a heartfelt tribute to his "senior" Vinod Khanna. The room was filled with a mix of emotions as Vinod, 83, stood on stage, humbled and grateful. His life was a testament to the power of passion and dedication. From his early days as a struggling actor to his rise as a director and producer, Vinod's journey was a remarkable one. As Amitabh spoke about Vinod's unwavering commitment to his craft, the audience was reminded of the impact one individual can have on a nation. Vinod's legacy will continue to inspire generations to come.
