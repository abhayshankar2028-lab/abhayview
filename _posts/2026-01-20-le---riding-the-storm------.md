It was a dark day in New Zealand when I first felt the sting of self-doubt. The confidence that once shone brightly in my eyes began to dim, and I couldn't shake the feeling that I was dented, bruised, and battered beyond repair. The T20 World Cup, where I had once been a force to be reckoned with, seemed like a distant memory, a fleeting dream that had slipped through my fingers. But, the wise words of my friend, R Ashwin, echoed in my mind: "Experiment, my friend. Experiment with new techniques, new ways of thinking, and new doubts. For it is only in the storm that we discover our true strength."
