His arrival in the tournament was met with a mixture of curiosity and amusement by the opposing teams. With a flick of his wrist, he sent the ball soaring through the air, a testament to his unorthodox yet effective cricketing style. The crowd roared in delight, and Crishan Kalugamage, the pizza-maker, had found his true calling.
