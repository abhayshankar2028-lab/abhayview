**SEO_DESC:** "Actress Kalki Koechlin opens up about the heartbreak she faced after her breakup, manifesting the darkness of insomnia through her art"

Kalki Koechlin's eyes gleamed like stars in the night sky as she sat in her dimly lit room, the only sound the soft hum of the city outside. Months of insomnia had claimed her, a heartbreak so profound it had made her sleepwalk through life. She'd wake up at 2 or 3 in the morning, her mind still reeling from the pain of losing the one she loved. "I'd stare at the ceiling, wondering what I'd done wrong," she whispered, her voice barely audible. "The darkness was my haven, my sanctuary from the shattered remains of our love." Her words dripped with a melancholy that echoed the depths of her soul.
