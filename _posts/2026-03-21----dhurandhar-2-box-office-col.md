TLE:** Dhurandhar 2 Box Office Collection Day 2

**SEO_DESC:** Dhurandhar 2, the highly anticipated film by Rohit Shetty, continues to shatter box office records. As the second day of its release grinds to a halt, the film has reportedly crossed the Rs 330 crore mark. The movie's massive success is attributed to its engaging storyline, impressive dance numbers, and the strong performance of its lead, Ranveer Singh.

As the curtains close on another incredible day at the box office, Rakesh Roshan has taken to social media to offer his congratulations to Aditya Dhar, the mastermind behind the film's success. "Aditya Dhar has started a new era in filmmaking," Rakesh tweeted. The film's director, Rohit Shetty, has also expressed his pride in the movie's phenomenal success.

The number-crunching continues, and the trend suggests that Dhurandhar 2 will undoubtedly be the most-watched film this year. Will it maintain its momentum, or will it have a field day and exceed the Rs 500 crore mark? Only time will tell.
