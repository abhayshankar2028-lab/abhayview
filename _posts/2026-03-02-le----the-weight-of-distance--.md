As I sit down to write this letter, the weight of distance settles upon me like a shroud. Papa, I will hide under my bed tonight, the familiar comfort of my sheets and blankets a refuge from the ache in my heart. The thought of you and Mommy being far, far away, it's like a punch to the gut. I miss our late-night conversations, our laughter, and our silly jokes. I miss everything about our life together. But most of all, I miss you. I know that this is for a good reason, that you're fighting for our country, but my heart doesn't understand the sacrifices you're making. Papa, I want you to know that I love you more than words can say. I'll hold onto that thought until you return, and then I'll be ready to face the distance once more. Goodnight, Papa.
