** The sun-kissed streets of Mumbai seemed to whisper secrets to Aditya Chopra as he gazed out at the vast expanse of the sea. His eyes locked onto a figure standing by the docks, a man whose presence exuded an air of foreboding. "You're working with Rani Mukerji, Vishal," he whispered, his voice laced with a mix of awe and trepidation. "She's a force to be reckoned with. But be warned, she's not one to be trifled with. You're working with a... darkness that lurks beneath the surface." As he spoke, the wind carried the faint scent of danger, leaving Vishal with an unshakeable feeling of unease.
