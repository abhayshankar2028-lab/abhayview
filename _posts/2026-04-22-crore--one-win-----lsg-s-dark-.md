The Indian Premier League had seen many come and go, but one team had always been a dark horse - LSG. Their players, once considered fringe players, had now become the biggest guns in the league.

Rishabh Pant, the golden boy, was expected to lead the team to victory. Nicholas Pooran, the destructive force, was known for his explosive batting.
