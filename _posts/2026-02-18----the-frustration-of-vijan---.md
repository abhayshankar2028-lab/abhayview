The camera pans over the bustling streets of Mumbai, the vibrant energy of the city a stark contrast to the frustration brewing inside Dinesh Vijan. For three long years, he's been working tirelessly on 'Amar Kaushik', his magnum opus, but the producers seem to be holding up the entire project.

The phone rings incessantly, but the lines go unanswered. Vijan's team paces the floors of their office, their faces etched with worry and desperation. Dinesh Vijan's voice rings out, demanding answers, but the response is met with silence. "They are consumed by big movies," he growls, his frustration boiling over. The camera lingers on Vijan's face, a picture of anguish and pleading.
