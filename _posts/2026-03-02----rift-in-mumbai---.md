As the sun set over Mumbai, the city's glittering lights seemed to dim, reflecting the discord between two of its brightest stars. Farhan Akhtar and Ranveer Singh, once inseparable collaborators, found themselves at odds over the long-awaited sequel to their hit film Don. Aamir Khan, sensing the tension, turned to Hrithik Roshan for guidance. The former Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai star leaned in, his voice low and soothing. "We need to find a way to settle this rift, my friends." But as they spoke, the words seemed hollow, lost in the din of their own conflicting egos. The battle for creative control had begun, with the fate of Don 3 hanging precariously in the balance.
