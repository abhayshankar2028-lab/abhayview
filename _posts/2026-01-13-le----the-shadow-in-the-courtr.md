**TAGS:** joint custody, brainwashing, parental rights

In the dimly lit courtroom, the air was heavy with tension as Celina Jaitly's eyes locked onto hers. Her children, the center of her world, sat silently, their faces a mask of indifference. The judge's words still echoed in her mind - "joint custody, the law is clear." Yet, Celina knew the truth. Her children were being 'brainwashed and intimidated.'

She remembered the day she discovered the court's secret: the psychologist's manipulation, the subtle games played on the children's minds. The judge's approval of the joint custody order only fueled her determination. Celina's voice rose, her words tumbling out in a desperate plea. "I'm not a monster, I'm a mother. I've been denied access, and now I'm fighting for my children's freedom." The courtroom fell silent, the weight of her words hanging in the air like a challenge.
