The second innings was on the line, with India's fate hanging precariously in the balance. But Samson was not one to back down from a challenge. He faced the bowler with unwavering resolve, his will unshaken by the doubts of his teammates.

The ball hurtled towards him, a tantalizing challenge to be met. With a mighty swing, Samson sent the ball soaring into the stands, a game-winning knock that will be etched in the annals of cricket history.
