** In the sun-kissed streets of Cannes, the buzz was palpable as Neecha Nagar, India's first Cannes winner, was set to receive a prestigious honour. However, the film's creators were met with disapproval from the French, who deemed it lacking in songs. The honour was meant to be bestowed upon the film, but it seemed the French had other plans. While the film's director, Raj Kumar Chauhan, was gracious in defeat, it was clear that Neecha Nagar was not the cinematic masterpiece that the French had envisioned. As the film's fate hung in the balance, it became clear that the true stars of the show were the songs that danced to the rhythm of Neecha Nagar, which, alas, were not enough to save the film from the chopping block of French taste.
