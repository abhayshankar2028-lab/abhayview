In the twilight of his career, Harsha Bhogle stood tall, his voice a piercing cry in the IPL wilderness. "His captaincy itself is a weak link," he spat, his words a scathing indictment of Ajinkya Rahane's leadership. The former CSK batter's comments sent shockwaves through the cricketing world, fueling the flames of speculation about Rahane's future. Bhogle's barbs were laced with a hint of jealousy, a burning resentment for the captaincy he never secured. As the IPL 2026 season loomed on the horizon, Bhogle's words hung in the air, a clarion call to action for Rahane to prove himself as a worthy leader.
