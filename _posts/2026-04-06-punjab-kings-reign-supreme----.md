But fate had other plans, as a sudden downpour turned the ground into a muddy quagmire, forcing the match to be abandoned. The Punjab Kings' victory was all the more sweet, as they leapfrogged to the top of the standings. With 16 points from 4 matches, they are now the kings of the IPL points table.
