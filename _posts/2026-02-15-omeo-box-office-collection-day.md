Romeo Box Office Collection Day 2: Shahid Kapoor-starrer earns over Rs 20 crore**

**SEO_DESC:** Shahid Kapoor's O'Romeo hits 20 crore mark, BookMyShow reviews and ratings blacklisted

It was a crisp February morning in Mumbai, and the hype surrounding Shahid Kapoor's new film O'Romeo was still palpable. The buzz had been building up for weeks, and the movie was expected to break all records. And break it did. The first day of release was a resounding success, with the movie earning over Rs 20 crore at the box office.

But little did the fans know, the excitement was about to take a dark turn. BookMyShow, the popular online ticketing platform, had a bold move, disabling reviews and ratings for the movie. It was a move that sent shockwaves through the film industry, with many wondering what the filmmakers had planned. As the day went on, the buzz around the movie only grew, but the silence on BookMyShow's part only added to the mystery. The question on everyone's lips was: what's next for O'Romeo?
