In the cacophony of the IPL, where batsmen clashed with bowlers, one moment stood out - the explosive opening over by Mohammed Shami. The Indian pacer unleashed a barrage of yorkers, sending Rishabh Pant flying at the crease. Pant, a man of few words, danced through the strike, his feet moving in perfect sync with the ball. As the final delivery fell, Pant had three fours to his name, his confidence soaring. The LSG captain, Ishank Jaggi, responded with aplomb, steering the ball to the boundary for four. The Indian team celebrated, knowing they had achieved the impossible - a first win of the season. The Rumble of the Rupees had been unleashed, and it was a sound that would be remembered for a long time.
