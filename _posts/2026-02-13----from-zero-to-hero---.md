As Stebin Ben stepped into Nupur Sanon's breathtaking Mumbai home, he felt the thrill of a zero-to-hero journey. Born and raised in Bhopal, he started his career as a civil engineer, working tirelessly to build and develop infrastructure. But his passion for innovation and design led him to pursue a new path. With each new project, Stebin overcame obstacles, learned from failures, and refined his skills. He went on to become a property developer, creating luxury homes that exceeded expectations. Today, his Mumbai residence is the epitome of elegance and sophistication. As Stebin walked through the rooms, he reflected on the journey that took him from zero to hero, and the doors it opened to new possibilities.
