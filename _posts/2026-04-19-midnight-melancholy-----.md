In the dead of night, when stars aligned like diamonds in the sky, Kalki Koechlin's mind would unravel like a threadbare tapestry. Months of insomnia had woven a shroud of pain around her, making it hard to shake off the weight of her heartbreak. Memories of their love affair would haunt her, a bittersweet reminder of what could never be again. She'd wake up at 2 or 3 in the morning, her heart pounding like a drum, as she contemplated the what-ifs. The city outside was asleep, but in her dreams, her love was alive, fueling her tears and her sorrow. In this midnight vigil, Kalki's heart wept for the love that couldn't be, leaving only a hollow ache that echoed through her soul.
