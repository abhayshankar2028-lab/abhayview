jat Patidar's Lament**

"Two-Wicket Wonders"

As the final wickets fell, Rajat Patidar's face contorted in anguish. His team, Rajasthan Royals, had crumbled beneath MI's relentless assault. The duo of Jos Buttler and Ben Stokes had conjured a thriller, taking three crucial wickets and securing a two-wicket victory. Patidar stormed to the crease, his eyes blazing with determination. "We didn't deserve to win this match," he spat, his voice laced with frustration. The wall of disappointment was crumbling, and Patidar's frustration boiled over. He shook his head, incredulous at the outcome. "It's a cruel twist of fate," he muttered, his words barely audible over the din of celebration. The IPL's finest had been humbled, and Patidar's anger was a testament to the raw emotion that coursed through the team.
