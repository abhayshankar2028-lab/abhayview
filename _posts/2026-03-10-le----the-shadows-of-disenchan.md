**TAGS:** ICC, West Indies, South Africa, T20 WC, bias claims, flight delays

As I sat in my dimly lit study, the flickering flame casting eerie shadows on the walls, I couldn't shake off the feeling of disillusionment. The ICC's denials of bias claims had been a topic of heated debate, especially in the aftermath of the T20 World Cup exit of West Indies and South Africa. The delayed flights from India, a stark reminder of the ICC's seeming disregard for the plight of these cricketing nations, had left me feeling disenchanted. The ICC's words of "we're deeply sorry" fell flat, like a promise broken. The cracks in the facade of fairness and justice seemed to be growing wider by the day. The game of cricket, once a source of pride and joy, now seemed tainted by the shadows of disenchantment.
