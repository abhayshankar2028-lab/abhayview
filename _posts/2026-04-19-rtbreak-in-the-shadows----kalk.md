Kalki Koechlin once ruled the silver screen, her performances mesmerizing audiences worldwide. But behind the glamour, a dark secret haunted her. Months of insomnia had taken a toll, and the toll was evident in her troubled eyes. "I'd wake up at 2 or 3 in the morning," she recalled, her voice laced with sorrow. "The darkness would dance on my walls, echoing the emptiness within." The pressure to constantly produce seemed to suffocate her, leaving her drained and exhausted. As the spotlight's glare faded, Kalki's demons began to seep into her pores. The once-vibrant actress was now a shadow of her former self, consumed by the very thing that had defined her: the fear of being seen.
