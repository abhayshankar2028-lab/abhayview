As I sat in the midst of a packed stadium, the roar of the crowd deafening, I couldn't believe my eyes. Mohammed Siraj, the young pacer from India, stood on the pitch, his eyes fixed on the TV screens broadcasting the news. I was to go to Spain to watch a Real Madrid football game, but little did I know, fate had other plans. A call-up for the T20 World Cup, and Siraj's life would never be the same. The weight of responsibility settled upon his shoulders, but he took it in stride, his calm demeanor a beacon of hope. And as he walked off the field, I couldn't help but wonder: what was happening?
