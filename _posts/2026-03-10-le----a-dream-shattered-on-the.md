As I stood on the field, the wind whipping my hair into a frenzy, I couldn't help but feel a sense of desperation creeping in. Babar Azam's absence in our ODI series was a blow to my heart, a dagger to my soul. Why did they have to drop him, leave him behind? The thought of watching him sit out, his talent wasted, it was almost too much to bear. Shaheen Shah Afridi, my boy, my pride, he was the spark that ignites our team. Without him, we're just a shadow of our former selves. I want to give him opportunities, to let him prove himself, to show him what he's capable of. But will it be enough? Only time will tell.
