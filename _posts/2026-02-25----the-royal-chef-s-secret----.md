**TAGS:** restaurant business, celebrity endorsement, celebrity money

In the swanky corridors of Bastian, a culinary haven in Mumbai, Shilpa Shetty's flagship restaurant raked in a staggering Rs 2-3 crore a day. The enthusiastic diners and rave reviews only added to the frenzy. But one day, Ranjit Bindra, the co-owner of the esteemed restaurant, stepped forward to quash the gossip. "It's nothing," he said with a sly smile. "We're just serving the rich, not the common folk." Shobhaa De's eyes widened in awe as Bindra revealed the intricate pricing strategy behind Bastian's success. "One day, the people will realize the true cost of royalty," he whispered, leaving De to ponder the culinary empire's secrets.
