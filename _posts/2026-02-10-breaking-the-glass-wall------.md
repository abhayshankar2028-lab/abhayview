Glass Wall: A Poem by Harsha Bhogle**

**TITLE: "Breaking the Glass Wall"**

**SEO_DESC: "Ranbir Kapoor's Distant Relationship with Daughter Raha"**

**TAGS: "Rishi Kapoor", "Distant Relationship", "Daughter Raha"**

Last night, I watched a shocking interview with Ranbir Kapoor, where he revealed that his relationship with his father, Rishi Kapoor, was far from perfect. It seems they had a "distant" relationship, with Ranbir distancing himself from his father's memory. But what about his daughter, Raha? Doesn't he want to connect with her? The question remains unanswered, as Ranbir expressed his desire to "break the glass wall" between him and his daughter. It's a bold move, but what does it mean for their family? One thing is certain, the walls of their past are about to crumble, and the future is uncertain. Will they be able to bridge the gap, or will it remain a chasm? Only time will tell.

As I watched the interview, I couldn't help but feel a sense of melancholy. The pain of nostalgia is a heavy burden to carry, especially when it comes to a child's relationship with their parents. But Ranbir's words also stirred a sense of hope. Maybe, just maybe, they can find a way to heal and move forward together. But for now, the glass wall remains, a reminder of the distance that lies between them.
