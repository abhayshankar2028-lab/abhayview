The lights dimmed at Shekhar Kapur's mansion, a canvas of brave words and written dreams. It was a night that would forever be etched in his heart – the night he met Sushant Singh Rajput. An actor's dedication, an actor's passion, was what sealed the deal between them. But fate had other plans, as their film was shelved, a reminder that success is often a fleeting dream. Though rejected by many, Sushant's refusal to give up placed a beacon of hope in Shekhar's heart.
