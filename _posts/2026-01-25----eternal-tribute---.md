I was at the grand ceremony, a sea of elegantly dressed individuals, as Ahana and Esha Deol walked hand in hand, beaming with pride. The room was abuzz with excitement, but amidst the chatter, one name stood out - Dharmendra. The legendary actor's eyes shone with tears as he accepted the posthumous Padma Vibhushan, a testament to his enduring legacy. I had the privilege of witnessing this moment, and as I scanned the crowd, I spotted Sunny Deol, his eyes misty with nostalgia, a hint of a smile on his face. It was a moment that would be etched in the memories of all of us, a tribute to the power of love and the impact of two icons who had changed the game of Bollywood forever.
