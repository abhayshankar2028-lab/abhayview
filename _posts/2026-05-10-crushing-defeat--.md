I'll never forget the agony of watching Rajat Patidar's team, Royal Challengers Bangalore, succumb to a crushing defeat at the hands of Mumbai Indians. As I scribbled notes for my broadcast, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. The 17-year-old sensation had failed to turn the tide in the match. Patidar, the star of the show, was left to ponder what might have been. "We didn't deserve to win this match," he muttered to himself, shaking his head in dismay. The emotions were palpable as Patidar's teammates slumped in defeat. It was a stark reminder of the fragility of cricket's top-tier.
