ssive Learnings for Us**

By Harsha Bhogle

As I sit here, reflecting on the Zimbabwe cricket team's 107-run loss to the West Indies in the Super 8 clash, I am struck by the sheer magnitude of the setback. Justin Sammons, the Australian middle-order batsman, has just shared his thoughts on the experience. "We knew we had to change our ways," he says, "to compete with the Indian conditions." The Indian Premier League, with its fast-bowing fast-mid-order bowlers, proved to be a formidable challenge. Sammons and his teammates must now regroup and refocus, to catch up with the likes of Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma. "We are not just playing cricket, we are fighting for our lives," he emphasizes. The Zimbabwe team will have to dig deep, to find the strength and resilience to overcome the Indian conditions.
