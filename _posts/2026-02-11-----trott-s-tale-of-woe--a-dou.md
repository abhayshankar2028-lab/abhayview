As I stood on the field, the weight of the game crushing me, I couldn't help but think of the young players who had given it their all. The 10th over, the one that had sealed the fate of our campaign. We had worked so hard, and in the end, it was just me, Jonathan, and a single ball. We had come so close, only to fall just short. It's just really devastating for the guys, the way we've been spoken of in the dressing room. We've been praised, but it's not the same as being part of the team that's moving forward. I felt a lump in my throat as I thought of the faces I'd seen that day, the tears I'd held back.
