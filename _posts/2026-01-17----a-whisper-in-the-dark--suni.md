In the dimly lit corridors of her mansion, Sunita Ahuja's words hung like a cold wind, a whispered warning of a dark secret. She claimed to have the proof, the incriminating document that would expose Govinda's alleged infidelity. But as she spoke, her eyes betrayed a desperation that chilled her own blood. "I will never forgive him," she gasped, the finality of her words a stark contrast to the turmoil brewing inside. The truth was out there, waiting to be unearthed, but the cost of revealing it was a price she was willing to pay.
