tle:** Sanjiv Goenka is a Convenient Punching Bag
**SEO_DESC:** LSG owner speaks out on social media as team's poor IPL run continues
**TAGS:** Sanjiv Goenka, LSG owners, IPL, social media trolling

As the IPL season grinds to a halt, the spotlight remains firmly on Sanjiv Goenka, the owner of the Lucknow Super Giants. The man who once seemed like a shrewd businessman, now finds himself at the receiving end of social media ridicule. His team's poor performance has left fans and pundits alike scratching their heads. Goenka's responses to criticism have been laced with a defensive tone, leaving many to wonder if he's truly trying to turn things around or is just running away from the truth. His words are like a punching bag, repeatedly hit and hammered, but ultimately, they leave him looking like he's getting hit repeatedly.
