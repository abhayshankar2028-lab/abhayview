"Captaincy Is A Poisoned Chalice"

Ajinkya Rahane, the stalwart leader of Kolkata Knight Riders, stood tall as the IPL 2026 season loomed closer. But amidst the euphoria, he found himself at odds with his own captaincy. His captaincy itself was a weak link, a fragile thread that snapped under the pressure.

When the going got tough, Rahane's teammates would often whisper, "Is he up to the task?" The doubts began to seep in, and his confidence waned. The spotlight shone bright on him, and he struggled to hold his ground. The IPL 2026 season would be a battle for his leadership, a test of his mettle. Only time would tell if Rahane's captaincy would prove strong enough to carry his team to the top.
