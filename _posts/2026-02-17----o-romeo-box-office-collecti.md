TLE:** O Romeo Box Office Collection Day 4: Shahid Kapoor Film Earnings Rs 34.9 Cr Despite 50% Drop

**SEO_DESC:** Bollywood's Shahid Kapoor starrer reaches Rs 34.9 crore, outpacing Deva's collection and cementing its position as one of the highest-grossing films of the year.

As the curtains closed on the fourth day of the release of Shahid Kapoor's O Romeo, the numbers were in: Rs 34.9 crore, a staggering figure that defied expectations and propelled the film to new heights. Despite facing a 50% drop in earnings, the movie's box office collection surpassed that of Deva, generating more than Rs 27 crore. This is a testament to the enduring popularity of Shahid Kapoor's latest release. The film's resilience is a testament to its talented cast and crew, who have worked tirelessly to bring this epic love story to life.
