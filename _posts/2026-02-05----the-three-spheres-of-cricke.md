TLE:** "The Three Spheres of Cricket"

**TAGS:** cricket, ICC, Nasser Hussain, Pakistan, India

In the realm of cricket, three spheres of life entwine,
Each with its own rhythm, its own sweet design.
Bangladesh, Pakistan, and India, a triumvirate divine,
Where legends are born, and memories forever entwine.

Nasser Hussain, a warrior of will and skill,
A beacon of hope, his spirit still stands still.
His legacy lives on, a shining light,
A reminder of the beauty of the game, day and night.

As I pen these words, a pang of sorrow strikes,
For the injustices he faced, the treatment he must repress.
But still, his memory inspires, his spirit still flies,
A testament to the power of cricket, and the hearts that rise.

Let us strive for equality, for a world where all can thrive,
Where cricket's beauty is cherished, and its spirit survives.
For Nasser Hussain, and for the game we adore,
Let us stand together, and celebrate what it is we've been taught to explore.
