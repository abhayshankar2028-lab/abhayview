"I know why Arijit Singh is quitting film playback," Palash Sen said, his voice laced with a mix of determination and desperation. "I was perceived as a threat, a rival who dared to question the status quo. The line between art and commerce has grown thinner, and I fear for the future of music if we don't find a way to coexist peacefully." Palash's words were laced with a sense of urgency, as if the very fate of the music industry hung in the balance. Arijit's decision to leave playback had sent shockwaves through the industry, and now Palash was left to pick up the pieces. "I knew this day would come," he said, his eyes clouding over with a mix of sadness and resolve.
