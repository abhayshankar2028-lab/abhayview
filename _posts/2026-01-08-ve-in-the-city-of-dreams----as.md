As the sun dipped into the Manhattan skyline, Ranveer Singh and Deepika Padukone's wedding celebrations had just begun. The City That Never Sleeps welcomed the royal couple with open arms, filling the streets with cheers and excitement.

Fans from across the globe had traveled to New York to catch a glimpse of the Bollywood superstars. But amidst the joy, a murmuring began to spread. "Stop serving you guys." It was a phrase repeated by some, a mix of frustration and disappointment.

Ranveer and Deepika, oblivious to the commotion, danced their way through the evening, exchanging sweet glances and smiles. But the buzzing question lingered – could their love be strong enough to overcome the criticism and pressure of the public eye? As they danced under the stars, the city seemed to align in support of the star-crossed lovers, their love shining brighter than any spotlight.
