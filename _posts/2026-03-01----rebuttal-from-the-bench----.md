In a scathing rebuke to Priyadarshan's scathing remarks, Rajpal Yadav fired back, his voice a thunderbolt on the stage. "Your education is a joke, a poor joke," he quipped, the audience chuckling at the clever jab. But Priyadarshan's ire was not to be swayed. "He's just a comedian, Rajpal," he retorted, "he doesn't know the value of a good education." Rajpal's response was a masterclass in wit, a sledgehammer to Priyadarshan's ego. "Any judgement from us doesn't hold value," he countered, the crowd erupting in laughter and applause. It was clear: Rajpal had won the battle, Priyadarshan's remarks having fallen flat.
