"I will never ask her," Abhishek Bachchan whispers to his daughter Aaradhya Bachchan, his eyes locking onto hers as if daring her to interrupt the unspoken understanding between them.

It's a moment that holds the power to silence the world, a secret shared between a father and daughter who, despite their fame, have always been bound by a code of silence. Abhishek's lips move, and he begins to speak, his voice a gentle whisper that only Aaradhya can hear.

"...if I ask her, what would she say? Would she be proud, or would she be ashamed? I don't know, but I do know that it's a thought that keeps me up at night, wondering if I've made her proud, or if I've disappointed her." Aaradhya's gaze softens, and she reaches out, taking her father's hand in hers. In this moment, the world may never know the answer to that question, but for Abhishek and Aaradhya, the truth is clear: "I will never ask her."
