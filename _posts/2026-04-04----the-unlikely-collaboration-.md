In the midst of a bustling film industry, Hansal Mehta's "Pralay" was born, a love story that would leave fans breathless. It all began when Ranveer Singh, the young actor, approached Mehta with a peculiar request. "Hansal, I want to write a story about a zombie romance," Ranveer said. Mehta, intrigued by the idea, agreed to collaborate. With Mehta's keen insight and Singh's raw talent, they brought the story to life. "The script was raw, but the passion was real," Mehta recalls. "Ranveer's energy and dedication made 'Pralay' a game-changer in the industry."
