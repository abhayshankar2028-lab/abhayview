As the music blares and the crowd erupts, Ranveer Singh stands at the edge of it all, his eyes scanning the sea of faces with a look of inner turmoil. "I'm craving calm," he says, his voice barely above a whisper. "Where I can hear myself again." The words are a confession, a surrender to the chaos that surrounds him. He's reached a breaking point, and it's not the noise that's got him - it's the emptiness that follows. "My life can be pretty crazy," he says with a wry smile, "but this is what I crave - a moment's peace."
