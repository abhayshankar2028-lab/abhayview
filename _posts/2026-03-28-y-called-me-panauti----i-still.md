ey Called Me Panauti**

I still chuckle to this day when I think about those events. It was 2003, and I was the star of Rajkumar Hirani's film, A Few Fools. As I walked onto the set, I remember being acutely aware of the whispers and snickers. "They called me Panauti," the cruel remarks still ring in my ears.

But I didn't let it faze me. I was confident in my abilities and had a plan. I gathered my crew and told Rajkumar, "I have no work." Simple, yet effective. The director raised an eyebrow, but I could see the gears turning in his head. He eventually obliged, and our film was a massive success. Little did I know, my "Panauti" reputation would follow me for years to come.
