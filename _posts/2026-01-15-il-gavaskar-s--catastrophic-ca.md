As the sun broke over the sprawling grounds, Sunil Gavaskar stood tall, surveying the vast expanse of New Zealand's attack. The duo of Mark Campbell and Graeme Swann had a seemingly insurmountable target – 285 runs to chase. Gavaskar's eyes narrowed, his mind racing with the possibilities. And yet, as the ball hurtled towards him, he couldn't help but feel a twinge of unease. It was as if the very fabric of their Test cricketing prowess had been torn asunder. Gazing out at the sea of expectant faces, he whispered a single, haunting word: "Catastrophe."
