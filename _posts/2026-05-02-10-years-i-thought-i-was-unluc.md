** I sit here, reflecting on the 10 long years that I thought were my life. The tears I cried on roads, the nights I spent without rest, the emptiness that once consumed me. But they were not just tears of sorrow. They were the tears of survival. For 10 years, I thought I was unlucky. I thought I was cursed. But then I discovered a small, humble snack that changed everything. Parle-G. Those crunchy, golden biscuits became my salvation. They were the fuel that kept me going, the comfort that brought me peace. And in that moment, I realized that I was not unlucky, I was just waiting for the right moment to discover my true self.
