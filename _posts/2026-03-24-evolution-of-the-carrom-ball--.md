In the verdant shadows of Indian cricket, a tiny, intricately crafted ball has evolved from a bygone era to a mainstream icon. The carrom ball, once the domain of secretive, underground enthusiasts, has burst onto the scene, captivating the hearts of millions. Ajantha Mendis, the pioneering cricketer who first introduced the ball's modern iteration, has long since hung up her bat. Varun Chakaravarthy, the most decorated player of his generation, continues to cherry pick the finest, most resilient balls. Today, the carrom ball is an integral part of the IPL's electric atmosphere, a testament to the power of innovation and tradition.
