"Echoes of Rebellion"

In the desolate landscape of demonetisation, Dhurandhar 2 weaves a haunting narrative that mirrors the turmoil in Hamza's world. The musician's lyrics are a testament to the resilience of the human spirit, as he channels the emotions of those who fought for their rights. In "Ghosts of Mumbai," he weaves a poignant tale of struggle and oppression, echoing the struggles of the 2016 economic upheaval. Yet, in "Resist," he inspires hope, urging listeners to rise against the forces that seek to silence them. Through Dhurandhar 2's art, Hamza's world comes alive, a testament to the enduring power of creativity and resistance.
