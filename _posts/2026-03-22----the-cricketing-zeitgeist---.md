** As I sit here, sipping my tea and pondering the cricketing zeitgeist, I'm reminded of the great Mohammad Kaif's statement: "If I had to choose, I'd go with Jos Buttler, but I'd have to replace Josh Hazlewood with someone who can bowl on a day-night match. Someone with a bit of pace, a bit of swing, and a bit of control."

I must admit, the idea of replacing Hazlewood with Buttler doesn't entirely sit well with me. But Kaif's words have stuck with me. Can someone like Ravindra Jadeja, with his varying pace and swing, fill the void left by Hazlewood? Only time will tell, but one thing is certain - the cricketing world is full of surprises.
