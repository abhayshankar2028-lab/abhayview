In the depths of Saba Azad's darkness, a tempest raged, fueled by the merciless grip of a parasitic infection. As the weight of this burden bore down upon her, Saba's once vibrant spirit began to wither, losing 4 precious kilograms in the process. The weight of her new reality was crushing, but amidst the shadows, a beacon of hope shone bright - the unwavering love and support of her friend, Hrithik Roshan. "You're my rock, Saba," he whispered, keeping her spirits up even as the world around her seemed to fade away.
