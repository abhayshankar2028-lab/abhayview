In the dead of night, a confession shook the foundations of a once-unbreakable bond. Piyush Mishra, a man whose life had been a masterclass in deception, stood before his wife, his voice cracking as he confessed his 15-year affair. The weight of his dishonesty threatened to consume him, and he broke down, overcome with shame and regret. "Took me 15 years to realize," he whispered, "she is my wife." The enormity of his mistake hung heavy in the air, a stark reminder of his failure to love and cherish her. The wound would take time to heal, but Piyush knew that true redemption lay in the unbreakable bond of his marriage, where love and forgiveness were the only currencies that mattered.
