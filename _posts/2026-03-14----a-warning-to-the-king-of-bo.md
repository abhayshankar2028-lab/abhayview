**TAGS:** Priyadarshan, Govinda, Bhagam Bhaag, Bollywood, creative differences

As I sit here, sipping my coffee and reminiscing about the golden era of Bollywood, I am reminded of a pivotal moment that changed the course of a film. It was a heated discussion between Priyadarshan and Govinda, two of the greatest actors of our time. I, Harsha Bhogle, was present that day to offer my thoughts on the script. Priyadarshan was adamant that Govinda should not add his own lines to the film, fearing it would undermine his direction. I, however, argued that Govinda's voice was essential to the character's depth. The debate raged on, with neither side willing to budge. In the end, Priyadarshan conceded, and the film was completed with Govinda's iconic lines intact.
