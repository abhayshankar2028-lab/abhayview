As the sun began to set on the first day of IPL 2026, Pujara stood tall, his eyes fixed on the Delhi Capitals skipper, Vinayak Tiwari. "Vinay, my friend," Pujara began, his voice low and measured, "I must caution you. You must not be too casual, not too relaxed. As your skipper, your reputation depends on it."

Tiwari's eyes narrowed, his brow furrowed in concern. "Why, Harsha? What's the point of being extra casual? It's just a game, after all."

Pujara's expression turned stern. "It's not just a game, Vinay. It's the game of cricket. And in cricket, there are no excuses. You must be focused, you must be disciplined. Anything less, and the consequences will be dire."

Tiwari nodded, his mind made up. "I understand, Harsha. I'll be more subdued, more professional. Thank you for the wisdom."
