As I sit in my small apartment, surrounded by cricket posters and jerseys, I'm overcome with a mix of emotions. My life is Dhoni. The captain of the Indian cricket team, the sultan of spin, the man who made me cry tears of joy. I've been a superfan since his first ODI, and every game, every match, has been a testament to his greatness. But today, it's not just the game that's ailing me, it's the thought of his last day. If he calls it a day, I'll be heartbroken. I'll be the first to give him a hug, to tell him how much he means to me. I'll be the one who'll question the world, why did it have to end like this? Dhoni, my hero, my friend, my everything.
