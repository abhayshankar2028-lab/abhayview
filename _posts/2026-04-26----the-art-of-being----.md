PrabhAnsh stood at the crossroads, torn between emulating others and forging his own path. Shreyas Iyer's words echoed in his mind, "Don't try to emulate, mimic, or be someone else." As a former Indian cricket team captain, PrabhAnsh had seen his fair share of champions and failures. With a deep breath, he steeled himself for the journey ahead. "I am not a carbon copy of anyone," he thought. "I am the sum of my experiences, the threads of my story. I will weave my own narrative, and that is the greatest game of all."
