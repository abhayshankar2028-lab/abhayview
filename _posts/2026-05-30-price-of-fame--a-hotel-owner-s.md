I remember the day I made my debut in Bollywood, a bright-eyed young actress chasing dreams in the city of lights. It was a dream come true, or so I thought. The spotlight was a harsh mistress, and soon I found myself lost in the sea of faces. The fame was intoxicating, but it came with a price. I quit the film industry, citing burnout and exhaustion. Now, I run a hotel in Bhopal, a humble abode where the only glamour is the sunset over the city. It's a far cry from the red carpet, but I've learned to adapt. As I sit by the window, watching the stars twinkle above, I wonder what could have been. A new chapter unfolds, one where I trade the glamour of Bollywood for the simplicity of Bhopal.
