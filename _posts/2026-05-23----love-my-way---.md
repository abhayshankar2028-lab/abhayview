TLE:** Love My Way
**SEO_DESC:** A romantic comedy about two pregnancies, one confusing Varun Dhawan
**TAGS:** Romantic Comedy, Pregnancy, Infertility, Unlikely Love

Varun Dhawan's life was turned upside down when he found out he was having two pregnancies. The news sent him into a tailspin, leaving him confused and uncertain about his future. His co-star, Jacqueline Fernandez, was pregnant first, and Varun was thrilled. But then, suddenly, Varun found out he was having a third child. Confused and overwhelmed, Varun struggled to navigate the chaos. As he navigated this new reality, he found himself drawn to Jacqueline's warmth and kindness. Despite the challenges, they began to fall for each other, and Varun realized that love my way might just be the best route for him.
