As the curtains closed on another Grammy performance, Arijit Singh took his final bow. The spotlight had dimmed, but the impact lingered. Amaal Mallik, a master of the playback singing realm, stood beside the departing singer with a steely gaze. "It cannot be treated like an obituary," Mallik declared. "Arijit's decision to step away is not about him, but about our collective future." The words struck a chord, resonating through the packed auditorium. It was a beautiful gesture, a testament to the artist's courage and conviction. As the curtain rose once more, the music flowed, and the nation listened, entranced.
