Was Miscast**
**SEO_DESC:** "Sai Pallavi's regret: Who could have done better on Ek Din?"
**TAGS:** Sai Pallavi, Ek Din, miscast

"Ek Din" was supposed to be a hit. A power-packed drama that had all the ingredients for a blockbuster. But as the curtains drew open, I was miscast. My character's arc seemed forced, my lines too long. The scenes where I struggled to convey the emotions felt like a chore. I was the one playing the villain, not the hero. The director's vision was clear, but mine was off. It was as if the script had been written by someone else. I felt like a pawn in a game I didn't control. The audience was invested, but I was lost. The miscast was palpable, and I was left to wonder: who could have done better on this project?
