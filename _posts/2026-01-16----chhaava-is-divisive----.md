TLE:** Chhaava is Divisive

**SEO_DESC:** In the midst of controversy, I grapple with the complexities of art and faith. "Chhaava" is a film that sparked heated debates, its message of love and acceptance deeply divided. 

As an ardent music critic, I must confess that scoring this film was an "honour". AR Rahman's compositions are a symphony of emotions, and to be a part of it was a privilege. However, I also see the film's portrayal of certain faiths as divisive. 

Words like "Subhanallah" were used in negative scenes, and I must admit that it felt cringe. The film's message of love transcends faiths, but the execution was perhaps not perfect. I hope that "Chhaava" has sparked a conversation, one that challenges our perceptions and encourages understanding.
