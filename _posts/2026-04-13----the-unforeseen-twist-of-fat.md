As the sun set over the Chennai skyline, the excitement of the IPL match was palpable. But little did the fans know, a surprise twist was in store for them. The venues of the April 26 and May 21 matches were swapped, leaving everyone wondering what had gone awry. The Chennai Super Kings' iconic M.A. Chidambaram Stadium was now the home of the Gujarat Titans, while the other way around was a jarring mix of old and new. The fans were left scratching their heads, questioning the sanity of the match organizers. "Why?" they cried out in unison. Only time would tell, but for now, the uncertainty was suffocating.
