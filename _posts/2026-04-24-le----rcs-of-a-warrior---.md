The Koli Brahmin's precision and Panaglariya's sheer strength proved too much for GT's attacking quartet, who succumbed to the onslaught with a flourish. The stage was set for Kohli's farewell, a chapter closing as RCB bid adieu to the Chinnaswamy's hallowed grounds.
