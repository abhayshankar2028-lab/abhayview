**TAGS:** Kangana Ranaut, nursing, Bollywood, distance

As I sit in my small apartment, surrounded by the familiar chaos of my life, I am reminded of the distance that once separated me from the world I knew. As a prominent figure in Bollywood, I had become a part of the bubble that surrounds us all. The glamour, the glitz, the endless scrutiny - it all took a toll on my mental health. But it was then that I made a bold decision to leave it all behind and pursue a new path. I enrolled in nursing school, and it was the most liberating experience of my life. The hands-on nature of the work, the sense of purpose, and the feeling of connection with patients - it all clicked. And so, I traded in my red carpet for scrubs, and my life changed forever.
