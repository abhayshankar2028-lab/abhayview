As he looked up to the sky, the aurora borealis dancing with colors, he knew that this was his calling. "Mera necha (son) India khelke aaega. Aap likho," his father had said. "Your India," he had replied, his voice trembling with emotion. The words echoed in his mind like a mantra, guiding him towards the cricket field. With every walk, every run, and every shot, he felt the thrill of victory and the agony of defeat. His father's words had set him on a journey, a journey that would take him to the pinnacle of the game.
