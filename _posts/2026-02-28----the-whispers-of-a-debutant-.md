**TAGS:** Manipulative, political stance, Hrithik Roshan, Dhurandhar

As I sit with a cup of steaming chai, I watch the buzz around Akshaye Khanna's Dhurandhar campaign. The whispers of a debutant, a faint hum of intrigue, all point to one thing - manipulation. The very same people who have been lauding Dhurandhar's charismatic rhetoric, now seem to be questioning his motives. "Nobody asked Hrithik" for his political stance, they say. But is it true? Did Hrithik even bother to share his thoughts on the matter? I think not. This game of cat and mouse is far from over, and I have a feeling that the truth will soon come to light. The question is, can we trust the whispers of a debutant?
