In the twilight of his years, Mohammed Siraj's eyes gleamed with a fire that had once burned bright in his son's heart. But fate had other plans, taking his son Rinku Singh away too soon, leaving behind a legacy of unfulfilled dreams. As I reflect on those fleeting moments, I am reminded of the sacrifices made for the sake of the game. His unwavering dedication to the sport, his unrelenting love for Rinku, all paid the ultimate price. Forgotten, like his son, his name etched in the annals of football history, a reminder that true greatness is not measured by achievements, but by the heart that beats within.
