** As the curtains of the auditorium parted, Arijit Singh stepped onto the stage, his eyes locked onto the sea of expectant faces. And then, the music began. The opening notes of "Ala Chalo" resonated deep within his soul, transporting him to a realm of pure joy. AR Rahman's words hung in the air: "When AR Rahman called Arijit Singh ‘reckless’ and said he is proud of him: ‘He works from his village’." Arijit's heart swelled with pride as he realized that his passion and dedication to his craft had earned him the respect of the music legend. With tears of gratitude in his eyes, he thanked AR Rahman for his unwavering support, his voice trembling with emotion.
