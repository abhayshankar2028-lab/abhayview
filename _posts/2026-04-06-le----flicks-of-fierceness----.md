As the sun dipped into the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the bustling streets of Mumbai, the city's film enthusiasts gathered at the local cinema to discuss the latest release. Ravi Kishan, the renowned film critic, took the stage to share his thoughts on Dhurandhar's latest film. "Dhurandhar's films are like a flavorful curry," Kishan declared. "They may not be for everyone, but that's what makes them special." However, not everyone was pleased with Kishan's assessment. Paresh Rawal, a prominent actor and film enthusiast, couldn't help but express his admiration for Dhurandhar's work. "Mujhe hona chahiye tha ismein," Rawal said, "these films are a testament to the power of cinema." The debate raged on, with neither side willing to concede.
