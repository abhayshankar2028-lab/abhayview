ey Called Me Panauti**

"Bad luck" was what they called me. The very surname that brought me shivers. I recall the cruel words, the whispers in the corridors, the snickers in the hallways. They called me Panauti, a fate worse than death. I tried to ignore them, to dismiss the whispers as mere gossip. But the pain lingered, a constant reminder of my so-called "lucky" status.

I remember the day I walked into the office, my head held high, but my heart weighed down by the weight of the world. I had no job, no prospects, and no one to turn to. I told Rajkumar Hirani, "I have no work." His response was less than sympathetic. "Don't you want to be a star, Panauti?" he asked, his tone dripping with insincerity. I looked at him, and for a moment, I felt like I was drowning in the sea of despair.

They called me Panauti, a shadow of my former self. But I refused to let it define me. I picked myself up, dusted myself off, and continued to chase the stars.
