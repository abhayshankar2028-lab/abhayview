**TAGS:** Ranbir Kapoor, Lord Rama, Lakshman, Bharat

In a moment of unexpected revelation, Ranbir Kapoor donned the persona of Lord Rama, the mighty king of Ayodhya. As he donned the iconic dhoti and turban, the weight of his character transformed. He spoke of innocence, of the innocence that had been stripped from him, leaving behind a harsher, more guarded persona.

"'The innocence is a little less, he suits more for Lakshman or Bharat'," Sunil Lahri whispered to himself, a mixture of pity and disdain etched on his face. Ranbir's eyes seemed to hold a thousand secrets, a thousand stories of trials and tribulations. He looked a little harsh, a little unyielding. And as he stood before the mirror, the mask of Lord Rama clung to his face, a constant reminder of the complexities he had brought to the character.
