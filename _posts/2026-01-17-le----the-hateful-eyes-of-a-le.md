"The dimly lit corridors of Stars Alliance were abuzz as Kangana Ranaut confronted the music maestro AR Rahman. Her eyes, like burning embers, seemed to pierce through the air, as she unleashed her fury. 'You even refused to meet me for Emergency, calling it propaganda,' she spat, her voice a venomous snake. The air was charged with tension as she continued, 'You have been a pillar of hate and prejudice in this industry for far too long. Your hate is almost palpable, and I'm tired of being surrounded by it.' The room fell silent, the only sound the heavy breathing of the assembled crowd. AR Rahman's eyes seemed to widen in surprise, a hint of a smile playing on his lips. 'I'm not a hate machine, Kangana,' he said, 'but I will continue to speak truth to power.' And with that, the confrontation reached its boiling point."
