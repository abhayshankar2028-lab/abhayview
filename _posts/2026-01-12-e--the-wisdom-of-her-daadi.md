I stood at the red carpet, clutching my script and nervously sipping champagne. The spotlight shone bright as I waited for my turn to present. Suddenly, I felt a gentle hand on my shoulder. It was my grandmother, her eyes twinkling with a secret. "Hamari daadiyon ne accha sikhaya hai," she whispered, "the way they did in old days." I looked around, but no one seemed to notice. "It's all about preparation and heart," she continued. I nodded, taking her words to heart. As I took the stage, I felt a newfound confidence. The audience laughed, clapped, and I felt like a true star. It was more than just a presentation, it was a family legacy.
