As I recall the days of 'Kamli', I remember Katrina Kaif's raw emotion and unbridled energy. But what I remember most is the pain she endured on set. In a scene where Katrina's character, Kamli, is bruised and battered, I recall her refusing to use a body double. "Why do I need someone else to carry my weight?" she would cry. "I'm trying to convey the vulnerability and despair of the character." Her refusal to conform to the traditional heroine template sparked a firestorm in the industry. I remember the pressure to perform like her, to convey the depth of emotion with such raw, unflinching honesty. It was a challenge that I, as a choreographer, was eager to take on.

As I watch the footage of 'Kamli', I am struck by the raw emotion and unbridled energy that Katrina brought to the role. And I am reminded of the sacrifices she made to bring this character to life. Her bruised ego and refusal to conform to traditional norms was a testament to her unwavering commitment to her craft.
