It's official. Ram Gopal Varma has spoken. The 2022 Hindi film Dhurandhar 2 has been a commercial success, but in his eyes, it's more than just a film – it's a declaration. Aditya Dhar, the director of Dhurandhar 2, has been outmaneuvered by Varma's latest project, Sholay 3. Varma's words cut deep: "Aditya Dhar has ended the era of 'godly heroes'". The phrase echoes through the corridors of Bollywood, a warning to other directors to wake up and smell the coffee. The era of the stalwart, the hero, is coming to an end. Only time will tell if Aditya Dhar will rise from the ashes to reclaim his throne.
