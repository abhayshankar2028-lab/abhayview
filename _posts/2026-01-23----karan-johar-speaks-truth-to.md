**SEO_DESC:** A poetic account of Karan Johar's defense of Varun Dhawan, who faced backlash for his smile in Border 2, as he calls out content creators who prioritize clicks over quality content.

**TAGS:** Karan Johar, Varun Dhawan, Border 2, content creators, clickbait, noisemakers

As the curtains closed on Border 2, Varun Dhawan's smile seemed to have sparked a frenzy of criticism on social media. But Karan Johar, the master of stirring up controversy, saw an opportunity to speak truth to power. In a scathing poem, he called out those who would seek to destroy his star, labeling them "noisemakers and clickbait-seekers." "Your clicks are my art, your clicks are my fire," he raged. "You want to know why I defend my stars? It's because I know they're worth defending." With his words, Karan Johar declared his unwavering loyalty to Varun Dhawan, and the debate on social media erupted into a frenzy of admiration.
