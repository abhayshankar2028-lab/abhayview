I stepped into the hallowed halls of Time Magazine, a silence that was both exhilarating and humbling. Among the esteemed list of 100 individuals who had made a profound impact on the world, Ranbir Kapoor's name was etched alongside legends like Leonardo DiCaprio and Rihanna. As I watched him take the stage, his eyes gleaming with a mix of humility and pride, I couldn't help but feel a sense of awe. "I'm not just a star," he said with a chuckle, "I'm a storyteller talking to a global audience." His words resonated deeply, a testament to the power of art to transcend borders and connect us all.
