Thirty years have passed since the day I stood in the hotel lobby, my heart racing with a mix of fear and excitement. Mika Singh, the renowned singer, had just arrived in the city, and I was chosen to be his bodyguard. I was just a young man at the time, driven by my passion and determination. But little did I know, that fateful night would leave a scar that would never fully heal. As I looked into Mika's eyes, I saw a glimmer of recognition, a hint of a smile that whispered, "I know what you're going through." I worked hard to make him feel comfortable, to shield him from the world, but even now, thirty years later, I still see that pain etched on his face. A painful reminder of the rejection, a legacy that never fades.
