As the Oscars ceremony took its final bow, Harsha Bhogle marveled at the underdog story unfolding before his eyes. India, once poised to walk home with the coveted Best Foreign Language Film award, found itself struggling to stay afloat in the cutthroat industry. The once-promising film, "The Palace of Dreams," had been overlooked in the early rounds, its talented cast and crew left to pick up the pieces of a failed project. As the news spread, the nation held its breath, hoping for a miraculous turn of events. Will India's perseverance pay off, or will the harsh realities of the industry silence its dreams once and for all? Only time will tell.
