** As I sit amidst the tranquil gardens of the Ravi Shankar Centre, surrounded by the soft rustle of leaves and the gentle hum of devotional music, I am reminded of the profound impact of the legendary sitar master. Rishabh Rikhiram's claims of never having formally recognized Ravi Shankar as his disciple have sparked a heated debate at the Centre. Yet, the Centre's stance stands unwavering, echoing Ravi Shankar's own words: "Guruji never called Rishabh Rikhiram his disciple, no formal ceremony." The Centre's response, reinforced by Anoushka Shankar's unwavering support, underscores the importance of authenticity and the power of the guru-disciple relationship in shaping the creative process. In this sacred space, we celebrate the beauty of spontaneity and the enduring legacy of Ravi Shankar's music.
