As the sun set on the Mahindra Stadium, the air was electric with excitement. SRH had just clinched their spot in the playoffs, and the players were basking in the glory of their hard-won victory. But amidst the jubilation, a quiet storm brews. Heinrich Klaasen, the SRH captain, stood at the edge of the dugout, his eyes scanning the field, his mind racing with frustration. For he had just faced a barrage of criticism from his teammates, all of whom were demanding a higher strike-rate. Klaasen's expression remained stone-faced, a mask of indifference. "I don't care," he muttered to himself. "We've got the win. That's all that matters." The team's chants of "Strike rate, strike rate" faded into the background as Klaasen retreated into his shell, his eyes fixed on the prize – the playoffs.
