Deepika Padukone's eyes welled up with tears as she spoke about the weight of being a new mom. Her daughter Dua had been just 18 months old when Deepika found herself back in the operating room, recovering from a cesarean section. The pain and exhaustion were palpable as she gazed lovingly at her little one.

I desperately need a break, Deepika confided. Being a mom is a 24/7 job, and I'm not sure I'll ever get a moment to myself again. Her voice trembled as she recalled the countless sleepless nights, endless diaper changes, and the constant worry of her daughter's safety. Deepika's words were a heart-wrenching expression of the guilt that came with being a new mom.
