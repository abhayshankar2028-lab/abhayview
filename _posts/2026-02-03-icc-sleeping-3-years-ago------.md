**TAGS: cricket boycott, ICC, Pakistan, India**

As I sit and ponder the fall of a nation, my mind wanders back to a time long past. Three years ago, the ICC was still reeling from the Indian team's defiance. The 2019 World Cup, a tournament that had brought the world together in unity, was marred by Pakistan's childish game boycott. Kamran Akmal's cries of "let India play, let India play" still echo in my mind. But the world moved on, and Pakistan's cricket woes continued. The IPL's new investment, the return of Michael Holding, and the growing influence of the BCCI all contributed to the decline. The ICC, in its wisdom, decided to extend the game boycott until 2023. But deep down, I wonder, was it a temporary measure or a permanent solution?
