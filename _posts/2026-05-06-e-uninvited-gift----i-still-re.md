I still remember the day Shah Rukh Khan walked into our office, his eyes gleaming with a mischievous sparkle. He handed me a sleek, expensive watch, the kind that would put even the most discerning horologist to shame. I was taken aback, unsure how to react. "A big cheque for Chalte Chalte?" I asked, trying to play it cool. He chuckled and said it was a "gift" from the Shah. I laughed, thinking it was a joke, but the look on his face told a different story. It was as if he was saying, "You're not worthy of such a gift." In that moment, I knew I was not the Shah's favorite player. The watch sat on my desk, a reminder of the uninvited gift that Shah Rukh Khan chose to bestow upon me.
