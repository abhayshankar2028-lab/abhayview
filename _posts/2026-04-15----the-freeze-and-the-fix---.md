In the depths of despair, Anshul Kamboj stood frozen, his cricketing dreams shattered like the ice that encased his body. The Test match had stalled, and with it, his hopes of redemption. The IPL, once his ticket to stardom, now seemed like an impossible dream. As the days turned into weeks, and the weeks into months, Anshul's spirit began to wither like the ice that held him captive. But then, a spark ignited within him. The IPL, it seemed, had been waiting patiently in the wings. With renewed fervor, Anshul set out to rebuild his cricketing career, to fix the mistakes of his past and to defy the odds that had once seemed insurmountable.
