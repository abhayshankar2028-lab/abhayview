As I sit in silence, I'm met with tears that fall like autumn rain. My daughter Priyanka Chopra, once the golden girl of Bollywood, now stands as a burden to me. Her stardom, which once set her apart, now weighs heavily on her shoulders. I see her struggling every day, the price of fame eating away at her soul.

Her mother Madhu's delicate hands tremble as she recounts the memories. "I see him struggling every day," she whispers, her voice cracking with emotion. "He's lost sight of his passions, his dreams. I fear for his future, for his happiness. I wish I could shield him from the harsh realities of the industry."

I nod in agreement, my heart aching for my child. "We must shield her from the cruel whims of fate," I say, my voice barely above a whisper. But it's too late, the damage is done. The star that once shone brightly has dimmed, and Priyanka is left to navigate the treacherous waters of fame.
