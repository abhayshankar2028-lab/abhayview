**TAGS:** Pakistan, India, T20 World Cup, Sunil Gavaskar, cricket

"I've seen some bold moves in my time, but this one takes the cake," Sunil Gavaskar said, his voice laced with incredulity. "Pakistan's decision to opt out of the entire tournament is nothing short of baffling. What's the point of playing for a tournament if you can't even take part in it? It's like they're trying to sabotage their own game. The T20 World Cup is India's biggest platform to showcase our skills, and to snub us like this is a slap in the face. We can't let them get away with this. We'll be watching, and we'll be waiting."
