Economics of the Heart

In the corridors of power, a debate rages on. Saif Ali Khan, a stalwart of the Indian film industry, finds himself torn between two ideologies. While he wholeheartedly advocates for pay parity, he believes that those who secure seats in the industry, be they stars or A-listers, should be compensated at a higher rate. "Economics work in a certain way," he'd say, "and those who sit at the top must be compensated accordingly." His words echo through the chambers of the industry, sparking a debate that few can resolve. As Saif navigates the treacherous waters of industry politics, he must confront the harsh realities of economics and the human cost of inequality.
