Shilpa Rao stepped onto the stage, her eyes locked on the expectant crowd. The music swelled, and AR Rahman took the mic, his voice laced with a calculated tone. "This young filmmaker would be lucky to work on a project with an artist of her caliber." The room fell silent, the air thick with unease. Shilpa Rao's smile faltered as she stepped forward, her voice steady. "I'd rather you asked me about my projects than my work ethic." The crowd murmured, unsure what to make of the sudden change in tone. Shilpa Rao's words hung in the air, a stark reminder that reputation is a fragile thing.

As she walked away, the words "Don't be gullible" echoed through the halls, a warning to those who would listen.
