Twenty-five years have passed since the chaos erupted in the Sports City Stadium. The MCG, as it's known, was set ablaze by the memories of the past. The air was thick with the stench of anger and frustration. I recall the cries of "Stop, stop, stop!" echoing through the stands as the umpire's call ignited a firestorm. Harbhajan Singh's majestic hat-trick, a masterclass of skill and deception, still sends shivers down my spine. Adam Gilchrist, the enigmatic wicket-keeper, his lbw decision a mere whisper of controversy. It was a game of two halves, with the referee as the referee. The curse of the "Stop crying over these things" still haunts me.
