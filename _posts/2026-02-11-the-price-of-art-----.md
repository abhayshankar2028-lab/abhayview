TLE: The Price of Art**
**SEO_DESC: A tale of luxury and loss**
**TAGS: art, value, imprisonment**

In the city of dreams, where stars are made,
A king of cinema made a reckless trade,
Karan Johar, with a wallet so bright,
Spent a fortune on art, with no clear sight.

He bought with passion, with a fervent zeal,
But some of the artists, their value did reveal,
They had no value now, can't even sell,
Their masterpieces, a mere memory to tell.

One artist, a painter, with a heart so true,
Was imprisoned for his art, what he could do,
He had no value now, just a distant past,
A reminder that some art, will forever last.
