** I stood outside the Kerala High Court, my heart racing with anticipation as I waited for the news that would change the course of my life forever. The Kerala Story 2 producer, Manoj Nair, had been waiting for this moment for weeks, his eyes fixed on the possibility of his film's release. And then, it happened. The judge had cleared the film for release, and the producers were over the moon. But as I watched Manoj's face light up with a mix of relief and pride, I couldn't help but feel a pang of doubt. Were we truly portraying Kerala in a positive light, or were we simply perpetuating the very stereotypes that we sought to avoid? The question lingered, haunting me like a ghost.
