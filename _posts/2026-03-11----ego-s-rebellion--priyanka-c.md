As I stood on the precipice of stardom, I couldn't help but feel a twinge of discontent. "I don't like staying in this sh**," I'd say to myself, as I gazed out at the glitz and glamour of Bollywood. It was a sentiment that echoed through my mind, as I navigated the ups and downs of my career. But it wasn't until I stepped foot in Hollywood that I truly found my footing. The glamour and glitz were exhilarating, but it was Mindy Kaling and Aishwarya Rai who inspired me to take control of my own destiny. They showed me that it's okay to take risks and push boundaries, and that true success comes from within. I'm not just a Bollywood star, I'm a Hollywood queen, and I wouldn't have it any other way.
