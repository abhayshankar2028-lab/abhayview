As I sat in the packed Coliseum in Kolkata, watching the inaugural T20 World Cup match between India and South Africa, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. The Indian players, many of whom had been vocal about their discontent with the tournament's format, were surprisingly enthusiastic about the competition. Rohit Sharma's confident smile, Virat Kohli's charismatic gestures, and Jasprit Bumrah's razor-sharp delivery all seemed to hint at a deeper satisfaction. Meanwhile, the Indian team's captain, Rohit Sharma, stood forthright, proclaiming that the tournament was "a proud moment" for the nation. Was it the excitement of a new tournament, or the genuine pride in their national team? Only time would tell.
