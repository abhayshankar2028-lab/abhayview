In the depths of Bollywood's dance wars, a legend was born. Farah Khan, the dance queen, stood at the precipice, her kingdom of movements on the brink of collapse. One fateful day, Vidya Balan, touted as a rising star, dared to question Khans' dancing prowess. "Can't dance," the remark cut like a knife. But Farah would not be deterred. In a breathtaking 35-40 day dance marathon, she practiced tirelessly, her body burning with a fire that would not be extinguished. When the curtains finally rose, Priyadarshan's words echoed in his mind: "The dance of defiance." Vidya, now a testament to Farah's unyielding spirit, would forever be etched in the annals of Bollywood's dance history.
