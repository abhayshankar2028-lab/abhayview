As I watched MS Dhoni's world crumble, I couldn't help but feel a twinge of disappointment. The man who had guided us to victory time and time again, now appeared lost and uncertain. It wasn't until I saw him walk off the field, his eyes welling up with tears, that I realized the depth of his frustration.

Little did I know, this was the turning point in Ashwin's journey. The pressure to perform had taken its toll, and he was on the verge of being written off. Yet, Dhoni's words of encouragement and faith in him ignited a spark within. Came the day, and Ashwin's talent was finally recognized. He was drafted into the CSK, and his name became synonymous with success.
