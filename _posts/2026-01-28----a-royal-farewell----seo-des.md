** "Arijit Singh's voice was the only thing that could interrupt the cacophony of arguments between Arijit and Salman. For years, they had been trading blows, each trying to outdo the other in the world of Bollywood. But in the end, it was Arijit's talent that shone through. The 'Sultan song' that had been written by Vishal-Shekhar for the movie 'Galwan' became Arijit's signature tune. As he sang, his voice wove a spell that captured the hearts of fans everywhere. And in the end, it was that voice that won over Salman's respect. The two had been locked in a battle of wits for years, but in the end, Arijit's talent emerged victorious."
