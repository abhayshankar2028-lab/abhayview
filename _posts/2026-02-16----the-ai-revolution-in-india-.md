"Like a siren's call, the AI revolution beckons. Prasoon Joshi, the CBFC chief, has stated that AI is not a challenge, but an opportunity for India. His words echo through the corridors of power, a reassuring whisper to those who fear the unknown. But Rana Daggubati, the actor, sees it differently. He believes AI will 'replace us quickly,' its algorithms weaving a spell of automation and obsolescence. As the lines between creator and created blur, we are left to ponder the consequences of this new world. Will we adapt, or will we succumb to the void? Only time will tell."
