**SEO_DESC:** "An Indian cricketer, caught in a scandal, struggles to clear his name amidst corruption and favoritism. A fine, a warning, and a tarnished reputation. The cracks in the system begin to show."
 
Romi Bhinder, the young Ranji Trophy manager, stood in the dugout, his eyes fixed on the pitch. The ball flew towards him, a perfect delivery from the opposing bowler. But as he watched it sail past his outstretched hand, he felt a pang of disappointment. His team's victory was short-lived, as he was soon summoned to the BCCI office.

The fine was handed down, a staggering Rs 1 lakh. Romi's eyes widened in horror as he realized the gravity of the situation. He was also warned by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) for using his mobile phone during a crucial match. The shame and embarrassment were palpable. The cracks in the system had finally begun to show, and Romi's name was at the center of the controversy.
