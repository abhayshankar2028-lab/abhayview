** The sun beat down on the empty stadium, the only sound the gentle rustling of the wind through the trees. It was a moment of introspection, a moment to confront the harsh realities of the game. Aakash Chopra stood at the edge of the pitch, his eyes fixed on the SRH bowling unit. "If they score 250, they might concede 250," he muttered to himself. The weight of his words hung in the air, a stark reminder of the vulnerability that lay at the heart of SRH's bowling. The question on everyone's mind was: how would they overcome this weakness before IPL 2026?
