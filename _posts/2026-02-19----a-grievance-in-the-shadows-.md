TLE:** "A Grievance in the Shadows of Glory"

**TAGS:** Indian players, The Hundred franchises, Pakistani players, representation, identity, cricket

As I sit in the midst of the Indian cricketing world, I am met with a growing sense of unease. The recent news that there are no Pakistani players in The Hundred franchises has sparked a heated debate about representation and identity. The absence of these talented players not only raises questions about the country's commitment to its own talent but also highlights the nationalistic fervor that often accompanies cricketing triumphs. The fact that these players, who have given their lives to the sport, are denied a platform to compete and showcase their skills is a bitter pill to swallow. It is a reminder that cricket, like politics, is a game of power and privilege, and that the marginalized often suffer the most.
