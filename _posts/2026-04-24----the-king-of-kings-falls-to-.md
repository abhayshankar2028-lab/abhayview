It was a sweltering summer evening at the Chinnaswamy Stadium, and the air was electric with anticipation. Virat Kohli, the king of kings, stood at the crease, his eyes fixed on the pitch. Devdutt Padikkal, the firecracker, took the wicket of MS Dhoni, the skipper, in the second over. Kohli, sensing the tension, took a deep breath and became one with the ball. He decided to target the GT bowlers, and with a flourish, he picked up three more wickets in the next three overs. As the final wicket fell, Kohli's voice echoed through the stadium, "I'll never be the same without this pitch!" The ball soared through the air, a perfect shot to seal the win.
