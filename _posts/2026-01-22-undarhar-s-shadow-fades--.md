In the desolate landscape of Dhurandhar's legacy, the once-vibrant tricolour now hangs limp and forlorn, a haunting reminder of a promise unkept. Border 2, a film that promised to tell the true story of India's fight against Pakistan, has been met with rejection. The Gulf countries, its intended audience, have refused to give it a chance. The CBFC's preference for sanitized narratives has left the film's gritty truths to wither in the shade. As the warships of India's imagination are renamed "Peacekeepers" and the warplanes are stripped of their tricolour, the nation's collective memory begins to fade. In this desolate expanse, the tricolour's final breath is the only sound that remains.
