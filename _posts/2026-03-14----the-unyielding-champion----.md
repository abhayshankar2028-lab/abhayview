As I watched Abhishek Sharma lift the trophy aloft, I couldn't help but notice a figure standing alone on the field, his eyes fixed intently on the prize. It was the ex-Australian World Cup winner, Mark Houlahan, who had come to India to celebrate with the Indian team. But it wasn't the medal or the adoration that drew my attention - it was the unwavering dedication in his eyes. "You'd think he's under pressure," I whispered to my assistant, "but these are the guys you want." As we watched, Mark gave India a nod of respect before disappearing into the crowd, leaving us all in awe of his unyielding spirit.
