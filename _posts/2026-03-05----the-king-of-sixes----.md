As I stood in the packed Melbourne Cricket Ground, the air was electric. The 2008 World Cup final was on the line, and Yuvraj Singh was about to rewrite history. With a fierce glint in his eye, the Indian captain unleashed a blistering 144 not out, leaving the Australian bowlers in his wake. And then, in the final over of the game, Yuvraj conjured six sixes, a feat that shook the very foundations of the game. The crowd went wild, and I couldn't help but feel a sense of awe. Sixes were a thing of beauty, a symphony of power and precision that left me in tears.
