As the sun set over the cricket stadium, Prithvi Shaw stood alone, his eyes burning with a fire that had been fueled by years of hard work and dedication. "I feel I'm giving not just 100%, but 200% to make that comeback," he whispered to himself. The weight of his teammates' expectations hung heavy on his shoulders, but Shaw was undaunted. With each passing day, he drew closer to rejoining the team that had nurtured him as a child. His journey was one of perseverance, and the outcome was far from certain, but one thing was clear – Prithvi Shaw would stop at nothing to prove himself once again.
