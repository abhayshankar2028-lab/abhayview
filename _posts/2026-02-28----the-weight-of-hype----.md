As I stood in the midst of the chaotic debate, the cacophony of opinions clashed with each other like a maelstrom. Shobhaa De, her voice a piercing cry in the din, tore into the hype surrounding Dhurandhar, her words laced with disdain. "Manipulative" was the label she hurled at the man who seemed more concerned with garnering attention than guiding the nation. The question on everyone's lips was: "nobody asked Hrithik for his political stance"? Her words hung in the air, a bitter truth that Dhurandhar's fervor had become a reflection of his true self. The weight of his hype was crushing, a heavy burden that weighed heavily on the conscience of the nation.
