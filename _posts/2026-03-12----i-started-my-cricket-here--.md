As the sun dipped below the horizon, casting a warm orange glow over the Eden Gardens cricket stadium, Jasprit Bumrah knew exactly where his heart lay. This was his home ground, the one where he had spent countless hours perfecting his craft, where he had walked among the skipsper's bench, and where he had tasted the sweet taste of victory. The T20 World Cup, and now this championship, was his. Tears pricked at the corners of his eyes as he stood on the podium, his teammates cheering in triumph. In this moment, he felt a deep sense of pride and belonging. "I started my cricket here," he whispered to no one in particular, a smile spreading across his face like the setting sun.
