As I watched from the stands, the scene unfolded like a tragic play. Imad Wasim, the former Pakistan cricketer, stood before me, his eyes filled with a mixture of guilt and defiance. His ex-wife's words hung in the air like a poison, "You never once thought about our kids, Imad. You never cared about their future." The words cut deep, a bitter reminder of his betrayal. I couldn't help but think of the countless hours he spent with influencers, the fleeting fame that brought him fleeting happiness. But at what cost? His actions had shattered the trust of one family, and now, it was too late.
