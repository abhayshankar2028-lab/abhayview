In the dimly lit room, a businessman sat, his eyes red from tears, yet unwavering in his determination. Before him stood the actor, Rajpal Yadav, his face etched with a mix of curiosity and skepticism. The businessman took a deep breath and began to speak, his words pouring out like a heartfelt plea.

"I cried before you, Rajpal, urging you to return my money," he said, his voice trembling. "I was a struggling artist, and you, my friend, were a shining star. I begged you, please, return what is rightfully yours. I was promised a place in your life, a chance to share your success with me. And now, I'm left with nothing but this, a hollow reminder of your generosity."
