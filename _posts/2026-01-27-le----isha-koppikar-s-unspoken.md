Isha Koppikar sat in her dimly lit room, the flickering candles casting eerie shadows on the walls. It was a moment she had spent years replaying in her mind, wondering what could have been if only she had taken a different path. "I should have done some jugaad," she whispered to herself, the word her lips had uttered on that fateful day. The film had been a critical and commercial success, but for Koppikar, it was a personal loss. She had given up on her dreams, and now, at 25, she was paying the price.
