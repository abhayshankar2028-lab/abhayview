As I watched Shah Rukh Khan's Kolkatacricket.com, I couldn't help but ponder the question: is there more to this man than his movies? Karan Johar, the maestro behind the Kolkatacricket.com, seemed to agree. His words dripped with conviction: "He is not just adding presence, he is obsessed." The more I saw, the more I realized that Karan's analysis wasn't just a clever marketing ploy. SRK's dedication to this project is evident in every aspect of his work, from the way he interacts with the fans to the way he portrays his cricketing persona. It's as if he's poured his entire self into this project, making it bigger than his movies.
