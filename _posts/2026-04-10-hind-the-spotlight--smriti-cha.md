** As I sit in my dimly lit studio, surrounded by scraps of fabric, threads of gold, and the faint scent of sandalwood, I recall the moment when Smriti Chauhan transformed Akshaye Khanna's performance in Dhurandhar. The viral dance moment that captured the hearts of millions. It was a masterclass in costume design, a symphony of textures, colors, and patterns that brought Khanna's character to life.

Challenges, you ask? The usual: working with a demanding actor, navigating the intricacies of Bollywood's fashion scene, and maintaining creative control in the face of tight deadlines. But Smriti Chauhan rose to the challenge, pouring her heart and soul into every stitch, every drapery, every carefully considered detail. And the result? A costume that is nothing short of breathtaking, a true work of art that has left me in awe.
