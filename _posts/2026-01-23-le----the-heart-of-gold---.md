**TAGS:** Dharmendra, Prakash Kaur, Sunny Deol, mother

As the sun set over the quiet streets of Dharmendra's old estate, a sense of stillness settled over the landscape. It had been years since the loss, but Prakash Kaur's heart still yearned for the love of her life. Her eyes, once bright with laughter, now held a hint of sorrow. Sunny Deol, who had been by her side through every trial, stood watch, his eyes warm with love. "Best son," he whispered, holding her close. In that moment, Prakash felt the ache of grief begin to heal, replaced by a sense of peace. She knew that Dharmendra was at peace, and in his memory, she found solace.
