TLE:** Anurag Kashyap's Silent Struggle
**SEO_DESC:** A gripping tale of a filmmaker's turbulent past, where the weight of his art and personal demons are laid bare.
**TAGS:** mental health, trauma, creative struggle, rehab

As I sit in my dimly lit room, the shadows dance on my walls, a constant reminder of the trials I've faced. Two heart attacks, a wake-up call that shook me to my core. I was forced to seek rehab, three times, each session a battle to reclaim my sanity. The silence that followed was deafening, a constant echo of the voices that once fueled my creativity. Netflix's "Maximum City" was a miscarriage of sorts, a cruel twist of fate that left me reeling. Anurag Kashyap's struggles are a testament to the price of artistic expression, a reminder that the lines between creator and patient are blurred.
