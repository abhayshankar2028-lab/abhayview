e Silent Chaser**

In the sun-kissed fields of Eden Gardens, a legendary encounter unfolded. The India team, led by Sunil Gavaskar, faced New Zealand in a test of wills. The score was well in their favor: 285 runs to conquer. Yet, as the sun dipped into the horizon, Gavaskar's eyes narrowed – a hint of doubt crept in. The Kiwis, though overpowered, seemed to be coasting, their opponents' spirits dwindling like autumn leaves.

Gavaskar's voice trembled as he spoke the words that would haunt him forever: "I was surprised by how easily New Zealand chased down 285 runs." The silence that followed was deafening, a stark reminder of the impossible task that lay before them. In that moment, the team's resolve was tested, and the true test of their mettle was revealed.
