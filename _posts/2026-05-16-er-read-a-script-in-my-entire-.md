I recall the day I shed my stage persona, trading my actor's skin for a pen and paper. The thrill of creating worlds, of bringing characters to life, had never felt so real. But there was a twist – a fundamental flaw that haunted me. Never read a script in my entire life. The myth of a writer not knowing their own work had always followed me. But Salman Khan, the one who penned the scripts, defied this notion. He said, "I've written scripts, but never read them." And so, I did. I devoured scripts, analyzed them, and understood the nuances of storytelling. The truth dawned on me: scripts are not just words on a page; they're a gateway to the soul. The art of scriptwriting, I realized, lies not in reading, but in writing with purpose.
