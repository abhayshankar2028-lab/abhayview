** In the midst of the 2012 T20 World Cup, a contentious decision emerged when Ryan ten Doeschate was left out of the Super 8 final match against South Africa. The reasoning behind his exclusion was rooted in a perceived 'Turkophobia' - a term that highlights the fear of South African players in international cricket. Axar Patel, a talented Indian batsman, was subsequently dropped from the Indian squad. As the era of T20 cricket unfolds, Harsha Bhogle revisits this pivotal incident. "In hindsight, it looked like the wrong decision," Bhogle laments. "A perfect storm of politics, intimidation, and biases led to a historic showdown between India and South Africa." The memories of that fateful match still linger, a painful reminder of the complexities that navigate the world of international cricket.
