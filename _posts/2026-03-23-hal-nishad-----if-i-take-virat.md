As I stood on the bowler's length, the air was electric with anticipation. Vishal Nishad, a young cricketer with a fire in his eyes, gazed out at the pitch before him. His idol, the great Virat Kohli, stood tall, his bat poised for the perfect blow. The crowd held its collective breath as the ball hurtled towards Nishad's stumps. He swung his bat with all his might, and the ball sailed through the air, landing with a thud mere inches from Kohli's feet. The stadium erupted in cheers as Nishad pumped his fist in triumph. "If I take Virat Kohli's wicket, I will touch his feet — he is my idol." The words echoed in his mind, a testament to the power of his bond with the legendary batsman.
