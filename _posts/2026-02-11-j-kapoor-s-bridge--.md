As I sit in my office, surrounded by memories of a bygone era, my mind wanders back to those tumultuous days when Anil Kapoor's career was on the cusp of a defining moment. It was Kamal Haasan who first landed the role that would catapult Anil Kapoor to stardom. But who offered it to the Tamil actor initially? The answer lies in the hands of the incomparable Raj Kapoor. "Raj saab said I could do this," Anil recalls with a smile. Ah, the impact of that simple yet profound statement. It was as if the maestro himself had given Anil the green light to bring this iconic character to life. And so, it was Raj Kapoor's bridge that connected the dots, launching Anil Kapoor's career and cementing his place in the annals of Indian cinema.
