As I sit here, reminiscing about the days when Irrfan Khan would be the voice of reason in the room, I am reminded of the fateful night when he told me, "Akshay Kumar, you won't get the money with me." The director's stubborn refusal to consider a star, a man who had played Mahabharat, was a conversation that left me speechless. I recalled the complexity of human nature, a war drama that would tackle the plight of refugees, and the complexities of war itself. Irrfan Khan's grey matter was a reflection of the times, a reminder that even in the face of adversity, we must find a way to come together. The conversation had been fleeting, but its impact would stay with me forever.
