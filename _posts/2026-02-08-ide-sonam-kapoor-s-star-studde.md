"The room was abuzz with the who's who of Bollywood, as Sonam Kapoor welcomed the arrival of her little bundle of joy. Kareena Kapoor and Bhumi Pednekar, two of the most stylish women in the industry, were the first to arrive, beaming with joy. Shabana Azmi, a legend in her own right, turned up with a warm smile, her eyes shining with love. The other guests, including Priyanka Chopra, Alia Bhatt, and Anushka Sharma, echoed their congratulations. As the festivities began, the room was filled with the sweet sound of laughter and the gentle cooing of the infant. It was a night to remember, a celebration of the mother-to-be's new life, and the love that filled the air."
