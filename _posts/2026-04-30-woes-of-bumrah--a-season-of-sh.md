e Woes of Bumrah: A Season of Shadows**

TAGS: IPL, Jasprit Bumrah, performance slump, SRH, Pollard's scathing review

As the IPL contract draws to a close, Jasprit Bumrah's season hangs precariously in the balance, a season marred by inconsistency and a precipitous decline in his form. The numbers don't lie: his economy rate has plummeted, and his ball-punching skills have become a rarity. It was a day of reckoning for Bumrah, as he took to the field to face the Mumbai Indians, only to be brutally exposed by the Sunrisers Hyderabad. As he walked off the field, defeated and demoralized, he couldn't help but wonder: "What's wrong with me? Why can't I get it right?" Pollard's parting words still ring in his ears: "You're a great bowler, Jasprit, but you're a mediocre one. You need to tighten up and sort out your game." Bumrah's response was a despairing cry: "I'll do it, I'll do it, but it's not working."
