**TAGS:** Family, Bollywood, father-daughter bond, filmmaking

"I will never ask her," a gentle resignation echoes in my mind as I sit with my daughter Aaradhya. Her eyes, like the piercing stars on a clear night, sparkle with curiosity, but I dare not dream that she'll share my passion. The thought of seeking her opinion on my films fills me with a mix of emotions – excitement, fear, and a hint of trepidation. My movies, my life's work, have been a labor of love, poured into every frame, every word. The weight of expectation rests on my shoulders, and I'm afraid of disappointing her. But, as I glance into her eyes, I see a glimmer of understanding, a hint that she knows me, and that gives me the courage to open up.
