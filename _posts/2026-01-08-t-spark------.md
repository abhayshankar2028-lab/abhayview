As I sit here, reminiscing about the past, I'm reminded of the golden days when Gautami Kapoor was riding high on her career as a television actress. Her salary was a whopping 30 lakhs per episode, and she earned thrice her fee on Ghar Ek Mandir. The spotlight shone bright on her, and her name became synonymous with glamour. But, unfulfilled with love and passion, she began to crave something more.

Her heart beats for Ram Kapoor, a man she once adored. The memories of their laughter, the way he held her hand, now seem like a distant dream. His jobless days now seem a distant memory, and she's left with the taste of bitterness. The spark that once burned so brightly has faded, leaving her with a hollow soul.
