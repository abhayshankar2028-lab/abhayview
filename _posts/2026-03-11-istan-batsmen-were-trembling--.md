**MS Dhoni's India in 2011 WC semi-final in Mohali**

By Harsha Bhogle

"Those were the moments that haunt me to this day", Shahid Afridi once said, his voice trembling with emotion. It was the 2011 World Cup semi-final between Pakistan and India at the Feroz Shah Kotla Stadium in Mohali. The stage was set for one of the greatest battles in cricket history.

I was there, alongside MS Dhoni, as he led his team to a thrilling 5-wicket victory. The atmosphere was electric, with the entire stadium electrified by the onlookers. Pakistan batsmen were trembling, sensing the gravity of their situation. Dhoni's India were the favorites, but Afridi's team refused to back down. It was a turning point in the tournament, and one that still sends shivers down my spine.
