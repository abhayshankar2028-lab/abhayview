In the scorching heat of the Indian summer, the likes of Pakistan's Mohammad Amir were once hailed as the crème de la crème of the game. But it was Abhishek Sharma who would soon test the mettle of the young fast bowler. As the Indian pacer delivered the final blow, Sharma was dismissed for a duck, leaving Amir in awe. Yet, Amir refused to break. "He kept same intent," he would later say, his voice a testament to the unyielding spirit that had driven him throughout his career. The dismissal was just a small part of a larger narrative, one that spoke to the enduring test of will and integrity that Amir had consistently demonstrated.
