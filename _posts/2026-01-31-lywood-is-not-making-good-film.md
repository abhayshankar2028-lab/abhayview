In an exclusive interview, Shahid Kapoor expressed his disappointment with the state of Bollywood cinema. He criticized the trend of manufactured marketing, where studios focus more on creating a buzz than producing genuine films. "I think we're losing sight of what cinema is about," he said. "We're churning out movies that are more about creating a brand rather than telling a story. The quality is slipping, and it's not just the films that are suffering. It's the art of filmmaking itself."

Shahid's words were met with a mixture of applause and silence, a testament to the complexity of the issue. As he spoke, he gazed out at the bright lights of the film industry, a reminder that the art of cinema is a delicate balance between creativity and commercial viability.
