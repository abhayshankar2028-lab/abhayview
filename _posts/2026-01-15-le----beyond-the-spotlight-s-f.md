** In the sweltering summer of '95, Border's Phoolwati burst onto the national stage with the chart-topping hit, 'Ae Jaate Hue Lamho'. Her voice, a melodious whisper of love, captured hearts across the nation. But behind the glittering curtains of success, a different picture emerged. As the fame grew, so did the pressures. The pressures of expectation, the constant scrutiny, the weight of responsibility. And Phoolwati, once carefree and innocent, was lost in the haze of it all. Now, in the twilight of her years, her name is a mere echo of what once was, a haunting reminder of the price of stardom.
