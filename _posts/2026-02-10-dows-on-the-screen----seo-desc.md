adows on the Screen**

SEO Desc: Harsha Bhogle's personal witness to India's superstar, Ranveer Singh's rise to stardom
TAGS: threats, WhatsApp, security, danger

I sat in the dimly lit room, sipping my tea, as the phone rang. "Ranveer, we need to talk." It was Rohan, one of Ranveer's security personnel. He told me about a threatening message on WhatsApp from an unknown number. The words "I know what you're doing" sent a shiver down my spine. Security increased outside Ranveer's residence, a sense of unease settling in. I remembered the countless times I've witnessed Ranveer's calm demeanor, the way he exudes confidence on screen. But now, it seemed, that confidence was a facade. I couldn't help but wonder what secrets Ranveer was hiding. The phone call ended abruptly, leaving me with more questions than answers.
