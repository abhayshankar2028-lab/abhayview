In the dark of night, Kamal Haasan sat in his office, staring at the film papers scattered before him. Geraftaar, his latest magnum opus, had been a game-changer, but the success had brought a weight he couldn't shake. Amitabh Bachchan and Rajinikanth had joined the cast, and with them, the fame and fortune that came with it. But Haasan's mind was elsewhere, consumed by the pressures of his own ego. As he pondered his next move, the questions began to haunt him: what was he doing? Was he truly creating something of value, or just chasing the next big thing? The doubts crept in like a thief in the night, stealing his confidence and leaving him with only one thought: what am I doing?
