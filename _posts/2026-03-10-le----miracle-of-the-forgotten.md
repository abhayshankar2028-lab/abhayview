** I still remember the day my brother woke up from the anesthesia, his eyes fluttering open like a butterfly emerging from a cocoon. He was paralyzed from the waist down, his muscles stiff and unresponsive. The doctors had given up on him, declaring it a lost cause. But I knew better. I knew that there must be something more, something divine. As he slowly opened his eyes, I saw it: a glimmer of recognition, a spark of life. It was as if God had sent us a sign, a message that we were not alone. And then, he looked at me, and I saw the same look in his eyes, the same spark of hope that had driven us all to pray for a miracle.
