conomy Class Escapades"**

I remember the peanuts and the tears, the cramped seats and the whispered secrets. Like Alia Bhatt, Ranbir Kapoor was made to travel in economy class as a child. Their parents, bless their souls, tripped them up in budget mode. But little did they know, these trips were not just about reaching the destination – they were about forging memories.

As we soared through the skies, Ranbir's eyes would light up like a kid in a candy store. Alia would snuggle up close, her face buried in a worn blanket. The in-flight meals became a staple of our family recipes, each flavor a story of love and laughter. These were not just economy-class adventures – they were escapades of a lifetime.
