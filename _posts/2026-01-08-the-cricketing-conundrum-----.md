As I stood at the crease, I couldn't help but feel a burning rivalry within me. The debate raged on – 'I prefer Arshdeep over Harshit in any circumstances.' It was a never-ending battle, a clash of titans, where one man's brilliance versus the other's brilliance seemed insurmountable. I had watched Arshdeep's moments of majesty, and the way he wove spellbinding spells, leaving opponents reeling. But then there was Harshit, the young gunslinger, with his lightning-fast reflexes and uncanny ability to unsettle the opposition's balance. The choice was clear – Arshdeep, the seasoned veteran, or Harshit, the fearless newcomer. Which one would I choose? Only time would tell, but one thing was certain – I would be watching, mesmerized, as these two titans clashed in a season of cricketing supremacy.
