ugh at me or because of me**

"Because of me, you're dead!" Rajpal Yadav yelled, his eyes blazing with fury. The courtroom fell silent, stunned by his raw emotion. His first words still echoed in the air, a defiant declaration that would become infamous in Indian media circles. But there was something more to it, something that hinted at a deeper struggle. "Because of me," he repeated, his voice dripping with a mix of anger and anguish. "Because of my starvation, my pain, my struggle." The crowd gasped, unsure how to respond to this unexpected outburst. Rajpal's demand was not just for attention, but for sympathy. He wanted the world to understand that his illness was not just a personal problem, but a cry for help.
