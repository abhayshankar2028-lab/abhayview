**SEO_DESC:** Indian cricketer Smriti Mandhana shines in Adelaide, capitalizing on a thrilling T20I victory over Australia, as she solidifies her position as a force to be reckoned with in the women's game.

**TAGS:** Women's Cricket, T20I, Smriti Mandhana, India vs Australia

Smriti Mandhana's name etched in the annals of cricket history, she took centerstage in Adelaide as India's T20I celebration turned into a jubilant victory over Australia. The Mumbai-born sensation, known for her aggressive batting style, proved to be the difference-maker, with a blistering 60 not out that put India on track for a crushing win. As she stood on the field, hoisting the trophy aloft, Mandhana's confidence soared, cementing her status as one of the most feared batswomen in the game.
