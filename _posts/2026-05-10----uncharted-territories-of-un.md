Kriti Sanon sat at her kitchen table, sipping a cup of coffee, lost in thought. The weight of uncertainty hung heavy in the air, a constant companion in her life. "I remember when I was younger, I was the one who got the call to audition for a role," she began. "But then, suddenly, I got my hands on the script, and it was one of the kid's roles. I ended up playing the lead, and it was like a switch had been flipped." The pain was palpable. "I was young, raw, and green. I thought I was ready, but sometimes, it's the unknown that makes us grow." As she spoke, the memories came flooding back - the tears, the frustration, the disappointment. But also, a glimmer of hope. "It's not just about winning or losing, it's about the journey, the growth, and the lessons learned along the way."
