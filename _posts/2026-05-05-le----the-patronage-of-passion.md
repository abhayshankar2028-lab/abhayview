As I stood at the Met Gala 2026, the air was electric with the presence of the world's most iconic celebrities. But it was a different kind of star that caught my eye - the one who had inspired generations of artists. Karan Johar, the mastermind behind blockbuster hits like Kuch Kuch Hota Hai and My Name Is Khan, took to the stage to pay tribute to his dear friend, Shah Rukh Khan.

With a sweeping gesture, Johar praised SRK, saying, "He's the reason I stand here today." His words dripped with sincerity as he gazed out at the sea of familiar faces. Shah Rukh, in turn, smiled warmly, his eyes shining with gratitude. As the applause washed over them, it was clear that this moment was not just a celebration of SRK's work, but a testament to the power of their enduring bond.

As I watched the two legends interchange warm words, I couldn't help but feel a sense of awe. This was more than just a tribute - it was a testament to the transformative impact of one's work on the world. And as Shah Rukh stepped down from the stage, his eyes locked onto Johar's, I knew that this was a moment that would be etched in the annals of pop culture history forever.
