In the realm of Indian cinema, a dynasty rose to power. Adar Poonawalla, son of the late Arun Poonawalla, sat atop the throne of Dharma Productions, a behemoth of Bollywood, with a 50% stake. As the family's wealth soared, so did their influence. The Poonawallas' opulent lifestyle was a testament to their success. With a net worth of Rs 3 lakh cr, they ruled the Indian elite. They followed in the footsteps of the Ambanis and Adanis, the richest Indians, carving out their own path to greatness. Theirs was a story of nepotism and privilege, a tale of how the Poonawallas reclaimed their place among India's elite.
