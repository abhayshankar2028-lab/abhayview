TAGS: tough, moody, Bachchan

As I sat with Raaj Kumar, I couldn't help but notice the peculiar way he addressed everyone around him. "Jaani", he'd call his dog, as if the canine's presence held a magical power. But it was in the eyes of his adversaries that I saw his true strength. Like the great Bharat Ratna, Bachchan, Raaj Kumar was moody, unpredictable, and only revealed his full fury when provoked. His tough exterior shielded a vulnerable heart, making him nearly unbeatable in the arena of politics. And yet, there was something undeniably captivating about him, a force that drew people to his side like moths to a flame.
