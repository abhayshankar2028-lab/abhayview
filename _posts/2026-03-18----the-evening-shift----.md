As the sun dipped below the horizon, the cinema hall buzzed with excitement. "Dhurandhar 2" and "Thalapathy Thalapathy" were set to start their evening run, but the news of delays sent the audience into a frenzy. Exhibitors, who had invested heavily in these shows, were at a loss. "The producers are to blame," they muttered, their frustration palpable. The cinemas, too, were reeling from the fallout, trying to manage the growing discontent of the audience. As the night wore on, the tension mounted, and the audience waited with bated breath to see which show would finally start on time. Will it be "Dhurandhar 2" or "Thalapathy Thalapathy"? Only time would tell.
