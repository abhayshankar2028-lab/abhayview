As the Zimbabwe national team stepped onto the iconic Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai, the air was thick with anticipation. But amidst the excitement, a different narrative unfolded. The Zimbabwean fans, clad in their vibrant colors, their faces etched with hope and determination, pleaded for something more - financial support to attend the ICC T20 World Cup games in India.

Their cries echoed through the stadium, a haunting melody that resonated deep within the hearts of the cricket enthusiasts. They yearned for a share of the pot, a fraction of the wealth that India had to spare. The crickets chirped in agreement, their subtle songs a poignant reminder of the Zimbabweans' plight.
