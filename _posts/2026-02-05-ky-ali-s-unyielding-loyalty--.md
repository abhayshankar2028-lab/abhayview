TAGS: Bollywood, Arijit Singh, Loyalty, Retire

In the grand tapestry of Bollywood, a thread of loyalty was woven by Lucky Ali. Post "Badtameezi", he stepped away from the limelight, his eyes fixed on a different canvas. Then, he heard the news: Arijit Singh, the maestro of melodic voices, had decided to retire. Lucky's heart skipped a beat. He remembered the countless performances of Arijit's, the way he commanded the stage with each note. He couldn't bear the thought of his friend leaving the industry. With a resolute smile, Lucky agreed with Arijit's decision. "Agree with Arijit for taking a stand," he whispered to himself, his voice barely audible. As he walked away from the music world, Lucky Ali knew he had made the right choice.
