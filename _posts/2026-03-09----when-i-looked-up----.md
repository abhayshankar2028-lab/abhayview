As I stood on that pitch, the roar of the crowd echoing through my ears, I felt an overwhelming sense of pride and elation. Fifty runs, the milestone I had worked tirelessly for, and I had achieved it. But as I gazed up at the scoreboard, my eyes fell upon the name of the one person who had given me this moment. My mother. The weight of her absence, the pain of her loss, it all hit me like a ton of bricks. I thought back to that fateful day at the Eden Gardens, when the Trinbago Knight Riders took down the New Zealand Warriors. I thought of her smiling face, her encouragement, and I felt a lump form in my throat. When I looked up, it was for her. For the love, the support, and the memories we had shared.
