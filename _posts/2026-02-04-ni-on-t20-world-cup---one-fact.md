oni on T20 World Cup: 'One Factor Scares Me'**

"India are one of the most dangerous teams in T20 cricket," MS Dhoni once said. His words still echo in my mind as I relive the unforgettable memories of India's triumph in the 2021 T20 World Cup.

The final match against New Zealand was a nail-biting thriller, with India clinching the title by a single run. The emotions were palpable as the Indian team celebrated their historic victory. But one factor that really scares me is the ability of Indian bowlers to know exactly when to unleash the ferocity of their spinners. It was that moment when the entire stadium would erupt in cheers as a batsman was out, and I would wonder how anyone can prepare for such a situation.

The rush of adrenaline and the thrill of victory still give me goosebumps. It's a testament to India's rich talent pool and the sheer determination of the cricketing fraternity.
