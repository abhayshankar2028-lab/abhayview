In the world of Bollywood, a legendary song has been immortalized in the hearts of millions. But behind the scenes, a shocking controversy has erupted, as a singer claims he was paid a paltry Rs 10,000 for one of the song's most iconic renditions. The singer, known for his powerful voice, alleged that he was paid a mere fraction of the exorbitant fees charged by A-list singers for their hits. "A-list singers charge Rs 3 crore per song," he quipped, fueling the fire. The singer's claims have sparked a heated debate, with many questioning the validity of his story. As the world of Bollywood continues to churn out hits, the question remains: what is the true cost of creating a cinematic masterpiece?
