As I stood before the hallowed halls of NSD, I couldn't help but think of the countless young minds that had walked these corridors. Naseeruddin Shah's words still echoed in my mind: "Only failures come back to teach." It was a mantra that resonated deeply within me.

As I watched the students struggle to grasp the nuances of drama, I saw the weight of their failures settle upon their shoulders. Yet, in their determination to learn, I found a glimmer of hope. Shah's words also struck a chord in my own journey, reminding me of my own role as a mentor.

His words now seemed apt, a testament to the transformative power of failure. These young actors, writers, and thinkers were not just learning to perform, but to navigate the complexities of their own humanity.
