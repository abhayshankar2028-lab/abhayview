Nikhil Advani found himself in a precarious situation, lost in an ocean of his own regret. He recalled a fateful afternoon when Shah Rukh Khan delivered a reality check to Karan Johar, gently nudging Karan towards the dark corners of his own naivety. "You don't know anything," SRK said, his words piercing through Nikhil's defenses, forcing him to confront the abyss of his own ignorance.

As Nikhil listened to SRK's wisdom, he felt a weight lift from his shoulders. He realized that true greatness lies not in knowing everything, but in embracing the unknown and learning from it. The memory of that reality check lingered, a testament to the power of SRK's words to shape the course of one's life.
