Pulled the Intruder by His Shirt, Threw Him Out**

**SEO_DESC:** "Raveena Tandon recalls the 2024 mob incident that left her shaken"

**TAGS:** mob violence, Raveena Tandon, India

In the scorching summer of 2024, I was a witness to a brutal mob violence that shook the very foundations of our city. The sound of screams and gunfire still echoes in my mind like a haunting melody. It was then that I pulled the intruder by his shirt, threw him out, and made sure he was gone for good. The memory of that fateful night still haunts me. The faces of those who were hurt, the innocent lives lost - it's a tragedy that will haunt India for generations to come. I was just a bystander, but I was there, and I remember the fear that gripped my heart.
