** In the scorching summer of 2020, two warriors from different worlds found themselves on a collision course.

Divenndu, the supreme captain of the Indian national hockey team, had been betrayed by the man he trusted most - his own teammate, Pulkit Samrat. The betrayal had shattered his world, and now he sought vengeance on the shoulders of those responsible.

As the stakes grew higher, the athletes on the ice faced a new enemy: each other. The game of hockey had become a far cry from the friendly competition it once was, as these titans of sports battled for glory and redemption.

Their rivals, a Russian sniper and an Australian gunner, set their sights on the Indian hockey team, unaware of the firecrackers waiting to explode. The stage was set for a thrilling showdown that would leave no one unscathed.

In this deadly game of sports and revenge, only one could emerge victorious. The question was, who would be the last man standing?
