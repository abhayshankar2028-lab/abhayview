The comment was laced with sarcasm, but Shah Rukh Khan couldn't help but chuckle.

As the joke continued, Shahid Kapoor began to "copy" his brother's style, albeit in a subtle way. He'd wear the same shades, the same hairstyle, and even the same accessories. The joke escalated, with both brothers trading witty barbs and playful jabs. It was a lighthearted exchange, but one that highlighted the sibling rivalry that runs deep in Bollywood.
