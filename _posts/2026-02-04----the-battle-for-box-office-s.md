As I sit here, staring at the numbers, I am reminded of the never-ending battle for box office supremacy. The film has earned a respectable Rs 285 cr on its 12th day, but alas, it lags behind Dhundhar's impressive Rs 341 cr. The gap is narrowing, but it's a slim margin. The question on everyone's mind is, can Sunny revive the film's fortunes and make it a worthy competitor to Dhundhar? Only time will tell.
