As I sit down to recount the tale of Kangana Ranaut, I am reminded of the cruel game of cat and mouse she played in Bollywood. It all began when Aditya Suman, a man she loved, targeted her with hurtful words on social media. But Kangana refused to back down, and her courage sparked a 'witch hunt' in the industry. The media, the public, and even some of her co-stars joined the chorus of criticism, threatening to silence her. The pressure was suffocating, and Kangana found herself navigating a treacherous landscape of backlash and intimidation. Her freedom of speech was under siege, and she was left to face the consequences of her refusal to be cowed.
