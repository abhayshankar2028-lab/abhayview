It was a warm summer day in Delhi, and the air was alive with the sweet scent of blooming flowers. Rajkummar Rao and Patralekhaa were beaming with joy as they sat in their cozy living room, surrounded by the people they loved most – their daughter Parvati Paul Rao. The little one was swaddled in a soft blanket, her hair tied back in a delicate bun, and a tiny smile playing on her lips. As they gazed at her, the room fell silent, filled with the warmth and love of the moment. Rajkummar took Patralekhaa's hand, and they shared a tender kiss, their hearts overflowing with love and gratitude. The world outside melted away, leaving only the two of them, lost in the beauty of this new chapter in their lives.
