As I sat in the darkened theater, the flickering lights casting eerie shadows on the screen, I couldn't help but feel a sense of unease. Ram Gopal Varma's scathing critique of Dhurandhar 2 had left me reeling. His only criticism, a mere whisper in the wind, was that the film's portrayal of Dawood Ibrahim was "incorrect". A mere slip-up, or so it seemed. But to me, it was a glaring omission. The film's writers had missed an opportunity to truly capture the complexity of Dawood's character. Varma's words hung in the air, a reminder that even the best of intentions can fall short.
