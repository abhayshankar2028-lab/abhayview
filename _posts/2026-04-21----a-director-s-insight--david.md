** "As I sat down with David Dhawan, the director of the popular Tamil show 'Hai Jawani Toh Ishq Hona Hai', I couldn't help but notice the excitement in his eyes as he spoke about his experience with AI in the show. 'It was presentable', he quipped, referring to the AI-powered script he worked on. For Dhawan, the incorporation of AI was a game-changer, allowing him to streamline the creative process and bring a new level of precision to his work. 'I was able to work on it for a long time without getting tired', he said, 'and it helped us to create something truly unique and innovative'. As we discussed the challenges of working with AI, Dhawan emphasized the importance of human intuition and creativity in the final product. 'It's not just about coding and machines', he emphasized, 'but about bringing that spark of creativity to life'. Through his experiences, Dhawan has come to realize that AI is not a substitute for human talent, but rather a tool to augment and enhance the creative process."
