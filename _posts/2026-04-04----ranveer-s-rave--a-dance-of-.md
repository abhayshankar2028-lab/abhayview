As the cameras stopped rolling on the set of Don 3, Ranveer Singh made his way to the makeshift dance floor, where a sea of rowdy crew members were waiting. But little did they know, Ranveer had other plans. With a mischievous grin, he began to dance to Farhan Akhtar's iconic song "Rockit", his feet moving in perfect sync with the pulsating beat. The crew, caught off guard, erupted into cheers and applause as Ranveer lost himself in the music. Amidst the chaos, Dhurandhar's name was being shouted out, and Ranveer, basking in the adoration, leapt onto the stage, dancing with wild abandon. It was a night to remember, and one that would be etched in the memories of everyone involved for a long time to come.
