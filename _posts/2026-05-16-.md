TITLE: "The Price of Pride"

I sat in the dimly lit room, the sound of Salman Khan's smooth voice echoing through the air. "Do you think you're some king?" he asked, his eyes gleaming with a mixture of amusement and disdain. I couldn't help but smile as I handed him a Rs 9 lakh Rolex, a gift from my wife to our dear friend Salim Khan. The weight of the money was palpable, a burden he seemed to carry with him every day. As I watched him take the timepiece, a flicker of nostalgia danced in his eyes. He was a man who reveled in his wealth, and this gift was just another manifestation of that pride.
