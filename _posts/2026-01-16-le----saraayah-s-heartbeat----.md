In the quiet hours of the night, Kiara Advani's heart beats with a secret wish. Her 15-year-old daughter Saraayah's 'favourite human', the dashing Sidharth Malhotra, has been a source of inspiration for her young daughter. Every time he sings along to his favorite song 'Baar Baar Din', Saraayah's eyes light up with delight. Today, on her husband's 41st birthday, Kiara decides to make her wish come true. She gathers all the family members and, with tears in her eyes, sings 'Baar Baar Din' for the man who has captured her daughter's heart. Her voice echoes softly, a melodic serenade to the love they share.
