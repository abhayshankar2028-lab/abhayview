In the scorching summer sun, Sanjay Mishra stood before the burning pyre, the ash-covered palm of his hand tracing the path of his own demise. As he surrendered to the flames, he felt an unshakeable sense of peace wash over him. His father-in-law, a man of immense pride, stood frozen in shock, his face a mask of incredulity. "People still asked for selfies?" he stammered, his voice barely audible over the crackling of the fire. Sanjay couldn't help but chuckle, a deep, throaty sound that shook his very being. In that moment, he knew that his sacrifice would live on, a testament to the indomitable human spirit.
