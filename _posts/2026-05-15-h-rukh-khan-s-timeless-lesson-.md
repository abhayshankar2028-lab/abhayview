ah Rukh Khan's Timeless Lesson**

**SEO_DESC:** "The Prince of Bollywood's Humble Humblety"

**TAGS:** Shah Rukh Khan, Bollywood etiquette, FaceTime, humility

I recall the day Shah Rukh Khan's daughter, Dhee, asked him to FaceTime her. He hesitated, unsure if this was the right moment. He thought of all the times he'd defied protocol, refusing to follow the rules of etiquette. But Dhee's innocence caught him off guard. He realized that sometimes, the greatest lesson is not from the world, but from within. With a newfound sense of humility, Shah Rukh Khan finally agreed to FaceTime Dhee, and the world witnessed a transformation – a man who had learned to put his daughter's needs before his own. His humble act was a testament to the enduring power of good manners.
