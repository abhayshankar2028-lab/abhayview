I stood at the precipice of greatness, but the deafening silence that followed was deafening. Dhurandhar 2's monumental success left the industry stunned, a collective "shh" that echoed through the corridors of power. The critics, too, were caught in the undertow, their usual fervor reduced to mere whispers. Varma's usually scathing tongue was silenced, his words reduced to mere inaction. I, too, felt the weight of his silence, the sting of his disdain. For in silence, there is no truth, only the crushing weight of expectation.
