** The eternal debate. As a Cricket commentator, I have had the privilege of watching some of the greatest batsmen in the game's history. Sir Don Bradman, the maestro of cricket, stands tall as the all-time highest run-scorer. But when it comes to batting ability, Sir Vivian Richards is a force to be reckoned with.

I firmly believe that Sir Vivian Richards is the greatest batsman to have ever played the game. His powerful strokes, coupled with his exceptional hand-eye coordination, made him a force to be reckoned with on the field. And, as for Mark Waugh's greatest batsman, I would give it to him hands down. The man's ability to adapt to different conditions and bat with immense power and precision is unmatched. But, in the end, it's all about the individual's style of play.
