In the realm of the Rajput Terracotta Army, a tale of intrigue unfolds. But I, Harsha Bhogle, have a different story to share. The appointment of Riyan Parag as the new captain of Rajasthan Royals, a position once reserved for the great Sachin Tendulkar. I must admit, I was surprised. Not by Parag's selection, but by the outcome.

The man who once inspired a generation with his batting, now a mere mortal, but a very capable one at that. The way he navigated the complexities of the game, a true captain in every sense. His decision-making, a testament to the evolution of the game. I, who once called Tendulkar the greatest batsman of all time, now sing the praises of Parag. The man has finally found his place at the top.
