As the sun rises over the fields, Akshay Kumar stands tall, his eyes twinkling with pride. He hands over a crisp Rs 4,500 note to Aarav, his 25-year-old son. Aarav's face lights up as he gazes at the money, a mix of excitement and gratitude. He sets off towards the village, eager to learn the art of fashion from the people who truly know it. The air is filled with the sweet scent of blooming flowers as Aarav begins his journey, one that will take him to the very heart of rural India's fashion scene.
