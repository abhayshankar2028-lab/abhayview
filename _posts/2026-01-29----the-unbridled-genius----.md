As I sat in the dimly lit room, listening to the sound of classical music drift through the air, I couldn't help but feel a sense of awe. Ar Rahman, the maestro himself, stood before us, a hint of a smile on his face. And then, the words that would leave an indelible mark on my heart: "Arijit Singh, your selflessness is truly recklessness. You work from your village, unencumbered by the trappings of success. Your music is a reflection of the beauty that lies within." I felt a lump form in my throat as I processed these words. The man I had grown to admire and respect was speaking directly to me, his words a testament to the power of art to transcend boundaries.
