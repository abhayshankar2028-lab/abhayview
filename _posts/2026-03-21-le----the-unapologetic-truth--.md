As I sat in the commentary box, the murmurs of the audience faded away, leaving only the sound of rustling papers and the weight of Gary Kirsten's words hanging in the air. "Not shocked, indeed," he said, his voice laced with a hint of disapproval. "Pakistan's under-performance at the T20 World Cup was no surprise. The fines, too, are a reflection of their failure to adapt and improve. The cricketers' inability to rise to the occasion was staggering. It's a measure of their character, really – or lack thereof. As a player, I've seen it time and time again. The arrogance of success, the complacency of failure. Pakistan's T20 World Cup campaign serves as a stark reminder of the importance of humility and hard work."
