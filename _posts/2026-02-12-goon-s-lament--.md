In the streets of Rangoon, a city torn apart by war,
A young man's heart beats for one, a love that won't part.
Jai, a soldier, and Pihu, a street urchin, meet by chance,
Their paths converge in a whirlwind of fate, a love that will withstand the flames.

As the conflict grows, their love becomes a flame,
That burns brighter with each passing day, a beacon in the stormy rain.
But fear and doubt creep in, like thieves in the night,
And threaten to snuff out the light, that guides them to the fight.

Yet still they hold on to each other, a lifeline in the fray,
A love that transcends the violence, a love that will not stray.
For in each other's arms, they find solace and peace,
A love that will conquer all, even the darkest of release.

In the end, it's not the war that wins, but love's unyielding might,
That stands as a testament to the power of the human heart's light.
