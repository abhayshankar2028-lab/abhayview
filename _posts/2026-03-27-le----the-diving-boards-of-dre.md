** The sun-kissed tracks of the Dube train became the stage for a most unlikely contest. Bishnoi and Arshdeep, two cricketing legends, engaged in a battle of wits – the Spelling Bee. Their rivalry transcended the field, as they traded words with each other, their competitive spirits burning bright. But little did they know, their opponents were watching, their social media feeds buzzing with excitement. The hashtag #CricketDoesSocialMedia trended through the nation, as fans followed the unfolding drama. The Dube train's rhythmic chug, the wickets' rattling, and the Spelling Bee's challenges became an electrifying spectacle, captivating the nation's attention. In the end, the prize – a thrilling victory – was just the beginning of a new chapter in the IPL's social media saga.
