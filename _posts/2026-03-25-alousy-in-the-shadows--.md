In the dimly lit corridors of his film set, Ryan Gosling's eyes would often wander to Hrithik Roshan, his co-star from Koi… Mil Gaya. The way he exuded confidence and charm left Ryan awestruck. As the days went by, Ryan's longing for Hrithik grew, but he didn't dare to admit it. That was until the night he penned a character for his next film, a romantic comedy-drama that would star Hrithik as the lead. And then, he had an epiphany – why not pair him with himself in a crossover film? Jaadu–Rocky, a tale of two worlds colliding, could be the perfect vehicle for Ryan to express his unrequited love. Little did he know, Hrithik was already on board, and the roads to romance would soon be paved with music and laughter.
