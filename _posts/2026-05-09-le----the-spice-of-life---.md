Jasmine Sandlas' story is one of unrelenting passion and perseverance. Born and raised on food stamps in the United States, she never let the circumstances define her. In a time when many gave up, Jasmine refused to surrender. Her talent for singing and dancing took her to the stages of India and beyond. One year, her big break came when she was discovered by a record label. After a whirlwind of success, Jasmine's name became synonymous with dance. Though her father's passing weighed heavily on her heart, Jasmine's determination only intensified. Now, she travels the world, leaving a trail of wonder and inspiration in her wake. Her story is a testament to the power of the human spirit.
