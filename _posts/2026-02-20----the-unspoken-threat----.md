As the lights of the Eden Gardens stadium cast a golden glow on the players' faces, the tension was palpable. Abhishek Sharma, the young opener, stood tall, his eyes fixed on the pitch. But it was his teammate, Mohammad Amir, who had other plans. With a stern expression, Amir leaned in, his voice low and menacing.

"The game is not just about batting, Abhishek. It's about exposing yourself. International cricket will expose you, and I will be the one to show you." Sharma's eyes widened, taken aback by the warning. Amir's words hung in the air, a challenge to his teammate's confidence. Will Sharma rise to the occasion, or will he crumble under the pressure? Only time would tell.
