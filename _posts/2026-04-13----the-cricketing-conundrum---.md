As the IPL stage lights danced with excitement, the cricketing world held its breath. The debate raged on - should Rajat Patidar replace Tilak Varma in the Royal Challengers Bangalore? But Patidar's emergence as a force to be reckoned with has sparked a renewed interest in the debate. "He's been brilliant for India," said host Kamran Akmal, "but RCB needs a more experienced, match-winning player. Patidar's got the skills and the confidence to carry the team to the top." As the cameras rolled, the question on everyone's mind was - can Patidar lead RCB to glory?
