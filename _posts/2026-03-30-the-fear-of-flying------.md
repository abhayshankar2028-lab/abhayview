TAGS: Aerophobia, Flight Anxiety, Fear of Flying

By Harsha Bhogle

As I sit here, pen in hand, I'm taken back to the day Tiger Shroff first revealed his secret to struggling with aerophobia. The actor, who has become a household name, confided in me that he starts feeling anxious a couple of days before every flight. The fear of turbulence, the fear of crashing – it's a daunting thought that sends shivers down his spine. "It's frustrating," he admitted, his voice laced with emotion. "Every time I board a flight, I'm left wondering if I'll ever be able to escape this constant dread. But I've learned to face my fears, one flight at a time." Tiger Shroff's courage is inspiring, and his story serves as a reminder that even the most unlikely individuals can overcome their anxieties.
